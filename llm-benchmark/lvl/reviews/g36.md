# g36 review: A = Alurentell (on the draw), B = Boros Aggro (on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn). A was at 13, facing 13 creatures (Ocelot Pride with city's blessing copied Ajani's and Voice's tokens), so the win had to happen that turn. A seq 41-44: Ancient Tomb, Aluren (to 11), Acererak. B seq 48: Swords to Plowshares on Acererak with its ETB trigger on the stack. In the same window B cast Amped Raptor and Spider-Woman free through A's Aluren, and Raptor's flip hit a second Raptor. Swords resolved (A 11 to 16), the trigger still ventured, and A cast the second Acererak, drawn that very turn (seq 41 draw). Lost Mine loop: 83 Acererak casts, 21 Dark Pool drains, B 21 to 0, A ended at about 37.
- **What decided it:** A's turn-6 draw of the second Acererak. With one copy, B's Swords would have ended the loop after one venture and B's board kills A next turn. B's Swords was correct (see 2); B lost to the draw, not to a misplay.
- **The enabler: A seq 10-14 (turn 4).** Brainstorm with the only untapped land (Hedge Maze) found Ancient Tomb. Without Tomb, A had 3 mana on turn 6 and no Aluren. A then played Flooded Strand, not Tomb, which kept Tomb out of reach of B's 4 Wastelands until the combo turn (playbook: "cast the key spell first with the lands that Wasteland can hit"). Correct.
- **Was A's turn-6 line safe against the free casts?** Not fully, but it was forced. Aluren lets B cast creatures of mana value 3 or less from hand at instant speed. B's real outs were (a) Swords on a lone Acererak, beaten by the second copy, and (b) an Amped Raptor cast from hand flipping Goblin Bombardment (3 in the list; 2 energy pays its MV 2) with about 15 creatures to sacrifice against A at 11, which is lethal. B put Raptor above Swords, so the flip resolved before the +5. It hit a Raptor; a Bombardment there wins for B. Phlage free (3 damage) would not have killed. Spider-Woman only made A's Goblin tokens enter tapped, which did not matter for a drain kill. A had no Force or Veil (Veil does nothing against R/W) and could not wait a turn, so there was no safer line. The risk was a single Raptor flip, roughly 1 in 10.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A, seq 6 (turn 2, Hedge Maze surveil): kept Stock Up on top (medium).** Hand: Strand, 2 Misty, Brainstorm, Stock Up, Aluren, Acererak. The plan was Aluren on the third land, so A needed a mana source (4 Tomb, 4 Petal, City) far more than a second 3-mana cantrip it could not cast before the combo turn (turn 4 had 2 mana at most without Tomb). Binning it makes the turn-4 draw a fresh card (about 1 in 6 to be Tomb, Petal or City) and lets Brainstorm see three new cards as well. It worked out because Brainstorm hit Tomb anyway. Both Stock Ups stayed uncast all game.
- **A, seq 38 (minor): fetched Savannah at B's end step.** Hedge Maze (also an Island) gives the same G, adds U and surveils, and enters tapped harmlessly at end step. That is an existing playbook rule (t22, t25), so it is a missed rule, not a new one. No impact this game.
- Otherwise A was clean: land drop every turn, main-phase Brainstorm, Veil put back (dead against Boros), Strand held to B's end step to shuffle away the put-backs, and the Swords was allowed to resolve for +5.
- **B: no real mistake.** The keep (Plateau, Heath, five cheap threats), Ajani plus holding W for Swords on turn 5, and the trigger order at seq 34 (Voice's sacrifice trigger first, so Pride copied the mobilize Warriors before the originals died) were all good. Swords with the trigger on the stack was the only window that could break a one-Acererak loop. B's report calls it a mistake and says A "looped in response". That is wrong: the loop started after Swords resolved, with B's hand empty. B lost to variance (A's second Acererak plus a Raptor flip that missed).

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Against a white deck with mana open (Swords to Plowshares, Solitude), start the Acererak loop only with a second Acererak in hand when you can afford to wait. Removal on Acererak with his ETB trigger on the stack exiles him, and the loop ends after one venture." Evidence: B seq 48 / A seq 52-59; A survived only because of a turn-6 draw. Confidence: medium; on a forced turn like this one it changes nothing. Related to the open question "whether to hold Acererak until a lock piece shows up", but this covers removal, not lock pieces. No contradiction.
2. "With Aluren and Acererak already in hand, surveil and Brainstorm for mana (Ancient Tomb, Petal, the third land), not for more cantrips. Bin a Stock Up you cannot cast before the combo turn." Evidence: A seq 6. Confidence: low-medium. It does not duplicate Brady's Stock Up pick rule ("take the strong cantrip first" applies when no combo pair is in hand) and does not contradict it.
3. Correction, not a new rule. The playbook says the Lost Mine cycle is "three Acererak casts per point of life". It is four: Cave Entrance, Goblin Lair, Dark Pool, Temple of Dumathoin. Temple draws a card each lap (83 casts for 21 drains; library 48 to 28). Against a high life total (Ocelot lifelink, Phlage), check that the library holds about as many cards as their life before relying on Dark Pool. Confidence: high on the count.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
1. "Against Aluren plus Acererak: cast Swords on Acererak while his ETB trigger is on the stack (the only window, and it wins if they have one copy). In the same window, cast your hand's creatures free through their Aluren, Amped Raptor last so its flip resolves above the Swords life gain; a Goblin Bombardment flip with a wide board is lethal." Evidence: B seq 48-55. Confidence: medium-high. This extends the g10 note on free casts through the opponent's Aluren.
2. "Ocelot Pride plus Voice of Victory: at end step put Voice's sacrifice trigger on the stack first so Pride's city's-blessing copies of the mobilize Warriors are made before the originals die; the copies stay." Evidence: B seq 34 (done right; two permanent Warriors). Confidence: medium.
