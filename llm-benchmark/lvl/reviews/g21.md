# Review g21: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

**TAINTED: exclude from win-rate counts.** B's report says a stray `show g21a` printed A's opening seven (Savannah, Flooded Strand, Misty, Ancient Tomb, Lotus Petal, Brainstorm, Force of Will) before B's first decision. B's play is judged here only for obvious mistakes. A's decisions were not affected by the leak and are reviewed in full.

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 12 (B's 6th turn). Dragon's Rage Channeler (delirium by turn 4 from fetches, Ponder, Bauble and the DRC it surveilled into the graveyard; later equipped with Cori-Steel Cutter, so 4/4 flying, haste, trample) did 3+4+4+4 damage and finished A at 4. A never cast a spell that put a combo piece on the stack. Its only real play was a hard-cast Force of Will on Murktide (turn 10).
- The game was decided by flood plus a loose keep. A saw Ancient Tomb six times (counting re-seen copies) and drew two Petals, and no Show and Tell, Acererak or Atraxa in about 20 cards. But A also threw away card selection four times. Twice it threw away Aluren, the one combo piece it found:
  - Turn 3, A prompts 26-29. Ponder showed Misty, Ponder, Aluren, and A kept them in the order Ponder, Aluren, Misty. It drew Ponder. Then it played and cracked Flooded Strand for Tropical Island so it could cast the second Ponder this turn. The shuffle sent Aluren, the known next draw, back into the library. Better: play Savannah (or Strand and leave it uncracked), keep Ponder, and draw Aluren on turn 5.
  - Turn 6 end step, A prompt 82. Hedge Maze surveil showed Aluren and A put it in the graveyard ("no creature"). A's hand had no combo piece at all (lands, 2 Petal, 2 Force of Will). Aluren on turn 7 (Trop + Savannah for GG, Force of Will x2 as backup) turns every one of the 4 Acererak into a win. A random card was a land about half the time, and it was one: Ancient Tomb.
- Honest verdict: even with clean selection, A needed Aluren plus one of 4 Acererak (or Show and Tell plus Atraxa) before turn 12. That is maybe a 40-50% game rather than a lock. The loss is mostly variance, but A's errors gave away a lot of those chances.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "When you hold no combo piece, a known Aluren or Show and Tell on top is your best draw. Keep it on top with Ponder and with surveil, and do not crack a fetch that would shuffle it away. 'No creature yet' is a reason to keep the enabler, not to bin it."
   - Evidence: turn 3 prompts 26-29, turn 6 prompt 82. Confidence: medium-high.
   - Overlap: this extends "the real harm is cracking after a Ponder or Brainstorm that put a good card on top". It also limits "With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle", which A seems to have stretched into binning Aluren. That rule is about a hand that already has the enabler.
2. "After Brainstorm leaves bad cards on top, crack a fetch before casting Ponder, so Ponder looks at three new cards. Use Ponder's own shuffle only when no fetch is available."
   - Evidence: turn 5, prompts 58-66. Confidence: high. The same error was proposed in the w1g4 review, so this is its second occurrence.
   - Overlap: this sharpens "Fetch away the cards you put back with Brainstorm if they are bad". It partly contradicts "Cast Brainstorm before Ponder so Ponder's shuffle clears the put-backs", which was written for Omniscience, where mana and land drops do not matter.
3. "Ponder with no payoff and no enabler in hand: shuffle unless one of the three cards is a combo piece. A Brainstorm on top of two known blanks digs only one new card."
   - Evidence: turn 3, prompt 34. Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: this is close to the existing creature rule. Merge them if Brady prefers.
