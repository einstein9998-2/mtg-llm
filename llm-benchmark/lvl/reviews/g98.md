# Review g98: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 9 (B's 5th turn) with 10 attackers into A at 1 life. It was decided on turns 5 and 7. B's attacks did 6 and then 11. A's life went 20, 19 (turn 3, Guide), 13 (turn 5, a 4/3 flying Cat plus two 1-power creatures), 12 (A's own Misty crack, turn 6), 1 (turn 7). The Misty point was the only life A paid itself, and it was the cheapest way to make 3 mana on turn 6: Tomb would have cost 2, which would have put A at 11 and dead to the 11-point attack on turn 7. At 1 life on turn 8, A's Ancient Tombs, Misty and Force of Will were all lethal to use. Show and Tell for Atraxa (Maze, Tropical, Petal) was A's only remaining play. B answered with Erode on the Atraxa trigger from one open Plateau (Veil could not stop a white spell). Even an Atraxa that survived only blocks one of 13 creatures, so the game was lost after turn 7. The verdict is mostly the clock and variance: A's seven had no payoff and it found Atraxa only with the turn-6 Stock Up.
- The caller's summary has two small errors. The first Ponder (A prompts 9-11) saw Strand, Ponder and Force of Will and kept Ponder and Force on top without shuffling. The Aluren/Strand/Petal look came from the second Ponder (prompts 21-23). B Wastelanded Tropical on game turn 5, which was B's own third turn.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A's keep (prompt 2) was fine: Tropical, Hedge Maze, Misty, Ponder, Stock Up, Show and Tell and Veil, on the draw against an unknown opponent. Two cantrips and a surveil land are a route to a payoff (playbook: "A keep needs a route to a payoff"). It lost to a fast curve, not to the keep.
- A, turn 4, prompts 21-23: the second Ponder saw Strand, Petal and Aluren, and A put Aluren on top and did not shuffle. A already held Show and Tell and had no creature. Aluren without Acererak is a second enabler that does nothing. This breaks the existing rule "With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle". A shuffle gives about 8 in 49 to draw Atraxa or Acererak, plus cantrips. Its cost is the known Petal, which did turn out to be the third mana on turn 6. Medium confidence that this was a mistake; it did not decide the game by itself.
- A, turn 4, prompt 19: Ponder plus a tapped Hedge Maze (dig 3) against Ancient Tomb plus Stock Up off Tropical (dig 5, 2 life, with the Maze held back). A Stock Up on turn 4 could find Atraxa two turns earlier, and then Show and Tell on turn 6 lands at about 11 life. The cost is a Tomb exposed to Wasteland. This is close (flagged).
- A, turn 2, prompt 7: playing Tropical Island first instead of Misty fetching the basic Island gave B's later Wasteland its best target. This is minor, because the Maze would have been the target instead.
- A's other choices were correct. The Stock Up picks (Atraxa and Petal over Misty, Ponder and Force) were right. On turn 7, Force of Will on Ocelot Pride would have pitched Show and Tell or Atraxa, A's only blue cards. Atraxa was the only possible put-in, since Aluren with no creature does nothing. In the reveal, Hedge Maze was taken over Misty (a crack would cost 1 life at 1 life), then Acererak, Brainstorm and Show and Tell. Declining the second Aluren only saved a discard.
- B: no mistake found. The keep (Mesa, Heath, Parlor, Guide, Ajani, Swords, Erode) and the curve were good. On turn 3, Heath was needed for Ajani (a Wasteland tapped for mana cannot also destroy). On turn 5, B cast Sand Scout first, then Wasteland on Tropical before A untapped, which made a token and gave Guide energy. Erode was correct over Swords, because Swords would have put A back to 8 life. The turn-7 "miscount" in B's report is wrong. Ocelot Pride and the new Sand Warrior were summoning sick, and Guide pumps once per attack, so the maximum was 1+1+1+4+(2+2) = 11, with no 12th point available. Lethal on turn 7 was impossible.
