# Review g33: Alurentell (A, on the play, mulligan to 6) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 10 (B's 5th turn): Dragon's Rage Channeler (3/3 flier) attacked and Lightning Bolt finished A at 4 life. A's report says the game ended on turn 8 right after Veil. It did not. On A's turn 9 A had only Island and Savannah, and nothing in hand cost 2 or less (Show and Tell x2, Stock Up, Acererak x2; no black source), so the engine auto-passed the whole turn and A never saw a prompt.
- Turn 6 (A prompt 68): B cast a 6/6 Murktide (delve exiled its whole graveyard and turned off delirium) into A's open Savannah. A cast Force of Will, and the only blue card it could pitch was Atraxa, its one Show and Tell payoff. That bought four turns but left A with Acererak as the only thing to Show in. Close, and flagged; I think it was right, since a 6/6 plus DRC kills in about three turns and A had no enabler in hand.
- Turn 8 (B prompts 86-88): Wasteland on Hedge Maze. A had just Brainstormed into Show and Tell x2 plus Stock Up (A 81) and was now held at 2 mana, with every spell in hand costing 3. B then Bolted face twice (10 to 4), and turn 10 was lethal. This is the decisive play. B flagged it itself.
- Verdict: mostly variance. The 6-card hand had 2 lands and no enabler. A's first enabler arrived on turn 7, one turn before the Wasteland. A drew no land after turn 5 except the Hedge Maze it fetched.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, prompt 3 (bottom after the mulligan): A bottomed Omniscience and kept Veil of Summer. With no Show and Tell or Aluren in the six, Veil protects a combo A did not have yet. Omniscience was both a payoff (Show and Tell into Omniscience, then free Acererak loops, per the playbook) and the second blue card for Force of Will. Without it, Ponder was the only pitch until Atraxa arrived, and Force later cost Atraxa. Medium confidence, flagged as close.
- A, prompt 16 (turn 3): A played Savannah before casting Ponder. That breaks "cast cantrips ... before playing the land" (Brady). It cost nothing here: Ponder found Misty, and either land order worked.
- A, turn 7 (prompt 78), Brainstorm in main 1: A's report calls this its mistake (it left 2 mana, so no Show and Tell). I disagree. A had no land in hand, and Brainstorm in main 1 was about 70% to find a land drop that keeps 3 mana for Show and Tell or Stock Up. Holding it gained nothing: the only payoff was Acererak, so Show and Tell could not win that turn anyway. This follows Brady's cantrip-before-land rule.
- Hands. The 7 (Strand, Petal, Brainstorm, Ponder, FoW, Show and Tell, Acererak): Brady's rule says "One land with Ponder is a mulligan", and A followed it. The hand has more than the rule pictures: Petal plus two cantrips, Force with a pitch card, and a fetch for a basic that dodges Wasteland. But it still needed two lands and a real payoff, since Acererak through Show and Tell is weak. I lean slightly toward keep. It is a genuine Brady question; A already flagged it (a 1). The 6 was a clear keep.
- Ponder shuffles. Turn 1 (a 11): the look was Tropical Island, Force of Will, Acererak, with a hand of Savannah, Force, Veil and Acererak. Keeping that order means three draws with no enabler. Shuffling was right, by a little. Turn 3 (a 22): Misty, Ponder, Atraxa; kept, correct. Turn 5 (a 47): Ancient Tomb, Omniscience, Atraxa, with Atraxa already in hand; shuffled, correct. A fourth mana is worthless without an enabler, and the known order would have meant two more dead draws.
- B: no real mistake. Murktide on turn 6 (B 62): B's report calls it greedy. It traded for Force of Will plus Atraxa, and the lost delirium came back by turn 8. The two Bolts in main 2 on turn 8 (B 101/108) could have waited until A's end step, but A could not use the information, so this was harmless.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Bottoming after a mulligan with no Show and Tell or Aluren in hand: keep the blue payoff (Omniscience, Atraxa) over Veil of Summer. It turns the Show and Tell you draw into a win and gives Force of Will a pitch card; Veil only protects a combo you already have."
   - Evidence: A prompt 3, then prompt 68, where Force had to pitch Atraxa.
   - Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: none. It is close to "Keep one extra blue card as Force of Will pitch fodder when discarding to hand size (a40)", which covers discard to hand size, not the bottom choice.
2. "Ponder with no Show and Tell or Aluren in hand: shuffle unless the three cards include an enabler or a cantrip. A land alone (or a fourth mana) is not worth two known dead draws."
   - Evidence: A turn 1 (prompt 11, shuffled Tropical/FoW/Acererak), turn 3 (prompt 22, kept Misty/Ponder/Atraxa, right), turn 5 (prompt 47, shuffled Tomb/Omniscience/Atraxa).
   - Confidence: medium. It might be wrong on one land; this game had 2 lands both times.
   - Overlap: it extends "With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle" (a33) from creatures to enablers. No contradiction.
