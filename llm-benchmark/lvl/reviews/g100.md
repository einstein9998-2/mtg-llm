# Review g100: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn): Misty to Savannah, hard-cast Aluren (Tomb, Tropical, Savannah: exactly 4), free Acererak, Lost Mine macro. B's 21 life (20 plus 1 from Ocelot's lifelink on turn 3, its only life gain) fell to 0 at instant speed; the log has 22 Dark Pool picks and 87 Acererak casts. A's "ended at 23" is a mid-loop reading (A prompt 52, B at 9); A finished at about 32.
- Decisive play, A prompt 18 (turn 4): with B tapped out (Plateau and Mountain spent on Ajani), A jammed Show and Tell for Atraxa (A flagged it as 17). The reveal (prompts 21-25) gave Aluren, a spare Acererak, Force of Will, Show and Tell and Flooded Strand, so the loop was set up a turn early. The turn-1 Stock Up (prompt 9, Tomb + Petal, 2 life) made this possible by finding Atraxa for the Show and Tell already in hand.
- B never had a real out: it was stuck on 2 lands with a 6-card hand, and its only removal spell was spent into a Force of Will it had seen in the reveal.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none of consequence. Checked:
  - Keep (Tomb, Tropical, Petal, Brainstorm, 2 Stock Up, Acererak): a clear keep.
  - Stock Up (prompts 10-11): Atraxa completed the Show and Tell pair, and Misty was the 3rd land and the 2nd green source that Aluren needed on turn 6. Correct.
  - Life: Tomb 2 three times, Force 1, Misty 1 and Ocelot 1 took A from 20 to 11. Boros never had reach, so this was safe.
  - Land before Show and Tell (prompt 17): Tomb + Tropical was exactly 3 mana, so no cantrip fit alongside it. Brady's rule ("make the land drop before a committal spell") applies. Tropical over Misty saved 1 life and kept Misty for Savannah. The mistake A reports here is not a mistake.
  - Reveal and cleanup: taking Show and Tell over Ponder kept a 2nd blue Force pitch, and the 10-card discard (Veil, which is blank vs Boros, Stock Up, Strand) was free. This is consistent with "do not take 0 cards" and "keep one extra blue card as pitch fodder".
- A, prompt 31 (A flagged it): Force of Will (pitching Brainstorm) on B's main-phase Swords on Atraxa. This breaks the existing rule ("not on Swords for a Show-and-Tell Atraxa"), but it cost nothing:
  - B had tapped Plateau, its only white source and with no third land, so neither Swords nor Erode could hit Acererak on A's turn 6.
  - A held a second Acererak.
  - The 7/7 lifelink deathtoucher stopped all B attacks (B prompts 33-37). Letting Swords resolve would have put A at 22, facing 7 power.
  - So A had no Force left for the loop turn, and only a free Amped Raptor discover could have hit Acererak (see B below).
- B: the mulligan is verified as a mulligan to 6, not "kept 7" as B's report says. B saw 7 (Heath plus 6 spells, 1 land, on the play), took a mulligan, drew a new 7 and bottomed Sand Scout (B prompts 1-3; the log says "you kept (bottomed 1)"; at prompt 5 B holds 6 cards). B flagged the mulligan itself. The 6 (Plateau, Mountain, Ocelot, Ajani, Spider-Woman, Swords) is a fine keep; 2 lands with a 1-drop and a 2-drop is normal for Boros. It drew no land on turn 3 or 5, which was variance.
- B, Swords on Atraxa (B flagged it as 29): it is defensible as Force bait. The reveal had shown B Force, Aluren, Show and Tell and Acererak. Holding Swords for Acererak's trigger loses the same way to Force plus the spare Acererak.
- B's real error is at prompts 44-51. B passed with Guide of Souls and Amped Raptor in hand while A's Aluren was in play, so both were free at instant speed, and A had no Force left. The right line was Guide first, then Raptor with Acererak's trigger on the stack. That discovers off 2-3 energy and could hit Swords, Erode or Bombardment. It is a low-equity out (A held a second Acererak), but it was B's only one, and the existing note covers it ("Under their Aluren, cast your MV 3 or less creatures free"). B's report misreads the menu as an engine quirk ("listed castable spells while my Plateau was tapped").
- B's other choices:
  - Ocelot/city's blessing: B had 7 permanents (needs 10), so it never applied. Ocelot made one token (turn 3 lifelink).
  - No attack into an untapped Atraxa on turn 5: correct.
  - Ajani over Spider-Woman on turn 3 (prompt 15): fine. Spider-Woman would only have made Atraxa enter tapped, and it went in through Show and Tell anyway.
