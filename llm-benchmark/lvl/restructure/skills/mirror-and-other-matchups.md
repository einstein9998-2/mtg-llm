# Mirror and other matchups

Use when: the opponent is another Aluren or Show and Tell deck (the mirror), Reanimator, Storm, or a deck with no file of its own; or when choosing sideboard cards.

## Mirror (P12, P13)

- **MM1** [P12] Aluren and Show and Tell are symmetric, so whoever blinks first loses (you are down the card and the mana), and whoever resolves more Stock Ups usually wins. If an Aluren is in play, somebody wins at instant speed almost immediately, so check whether the OPPONENT's Aluren lets me win first: example, the opponent resolved Aluren, cast Acererak in my upkeep so I would not draw; I cast my own Acererak in response, they Forced, I Forced back and won. (Brady, tips 2026-10-06)
- **MM2** [P13] Prismatic Ending does nothing against an Aluren in play; keep it out of the mirror. (Brady, tips 2026-10-06)

## Reanimator (P9, P13)

- **MM3** [P9, P13] Against Reanimator keep one Show and Tell for the high-resource line (Show in Atraxa, Chant them so they cannot answer, win next turn); otherwise Show is risky because they put in something big. (Brady, tips 2026-10-06)
- **MM4** [P6, P13] Against Reanimator keep City of Traitors over a basic: two extra mana means one land fewer for the combo. (Brady, tips 2026-10-06)
- Orim's Chant against Reanimator is a time walk on their turn (their upkeep, or in response to Dark Ritual): protecting-the-combo.md PR14.
