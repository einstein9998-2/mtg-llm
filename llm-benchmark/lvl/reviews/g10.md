# g10 review: A = Alurentell (on the draw), B = Boros Aggro (on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 8 (A's own 4th turn). A was at 3 and then 1 life (Ancient Tomb for Aluren) and looped Acererak through Lost Mine of Phandelver: 82 free casts, 20 Dark Pool drains took B from 21 to 1, and a final Tomb of Annihilation Trapped Entry finished it. The whole loop ran on A's turn 8, with A as active player. B's report calls it "opponent's turn", which is correct from B's seat. B's permanents were tapped because B tapped them on its own turn 7. That is normal: B never got a turn 9.
- **Deciding error: B seq 49 (and 32 more prompts through seq 135), pass.** Aluren is symmetric: any player may cast creatures with mana value 3 or less for free, as though they had flash. B's menu offered "Cast Phlage, Titan of Fire's Fury" with A at 1 life, and Phlage's enter trigger deals 3 damage, which kills A. A stayed at 3 or less until B's seq 138. B read the offer as an engine oddity ("listed as castable while my lands were tapped") and passed 33 times. Any one of those casts would have won the game.
- **Earlier lethal, probably missed: B seq 36 (turn 7, A at 12, B had 2 lands).** B cast Sand Scout precombat and used both lands. Better line: attack with everything first (9 damage, A to 3), then in main 2 cast Erode (R) on its own Cat token. Ajani, Nacatl Pariah flips. Avenger's 0 ability makes a Cat Warrior, and with a red permanent (the red mobilize Warrior tokens) it deals damage equal to B's creature count (7) to A. Erode also ramps B a basic. I have not verified the card text or the engine's support for the flip; Brady should confirm.
- A's side: on turn 8 the line was forced. A had no route that avoided a free Phlage: Veil gives hexproof only from blue and black. A's real risk came earlier (see 2).

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A, seq 9 (turn 2): fetched basic Island over Tropical Island.** Hand: Savannah, Boseiju, City of Traitors, Misty, Brainstorm, Veil, Show and Tell. Aluren costs {2}{G}{G}. With Island as the first land, three land drops cannot make GG plus 2: A had Island, Hedge Maze and Ancient Tomb on turn 6, which is 4 mana but only one G. Tropical Island plus Hedge Maze or Savannah plus Tomb/City would have cast Aluren on turn 6. Aluren was drawn that turn and Acererak was already in hand, so A would have won at 10 life, before the 9-point turn-7 swing, and even a free Phlage would not have killed. The cost was Wasteland exposure against a 4-Wasteland deck (B never drew one), so this is close rather than clear. Everything else A did was sound. The Brainstorms in main phase followed playbook rules, Misty was held until B's end step to shuffle, Aluren stayed on top at seq 33 (correct: Omniscience was 2 of 45 cards), and A did not cast a raw Show and Tell on turn 6. The turn-6 Ancient Tomb instead of Savannah (A's "menu slip") was harmless: Aluren was impossible either way, and both orders give the same turn-8 mana.
- **B: seq 36** (missed Ajani-flip lethal, medium confidence), then **seq 49** (the free Phlage, certain). The keep, the curve (Ocelot, Ajani, Voice) and the attacks were fine. Missed land drops were forced: no land in hand on turns 5 and 7.
- Not variance: B lost a game it had won twice over.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Aluren is symmetric: once it resolves, the opponent may cast creatures of mana value 3 or less for free at instant speed. Against Boros, a free Phlage deals 3 the moment Aluren lands. Do not plan to cast Aluren at 3 life or less (count the 2 from Ancient Tomb); arrange the colours so Aluren comes a turn earlier at a higher life total." Evidence: B seq 49-135 (33 windows, A at 1-3 life). Confidence: high on the fact, medium on the advice. Related to Brady's mirror tip ("check whether the OPPONENT's Aluren lets me win first") but different: that tip covers the opponent's Aluren, this one covers what my Aluren gives the opponent. No contradiction.
2. "Count GG for Aluren from the first fetch. With Ancient Tomb or City of Traitors in hand, fetch Tropical Island (or play Savannah) as the first land unless two other green sources are already certain to land by the third land drop. A first-turn basic Island makes a third-land Aluren impossible." Evidence: A seq 9 (above). Confidence: low-medium. This is in tension with "Wasteland takes Ancient Tomb and Tropical Island: prefer basics or fetches when I am short on lands" and "I can fetch basics with Misty, Strand and Delta and keep nonbasics for later". The new rule applies when not short on lands and the opponent is a fast clock.

Not a rule, but a correction: A's report says Tomb of Annihilation could have saved casts earlier in the loop. It could not. Once in Tomb you cannot restart Lost Mine until Tomb is completed, and completing it ends the loop (Acererak stays). Trapped Entry only as the last point, with the opponent at 1 and me above 1, is what A did and is right. The existing "do not complete Tomb" rule already covers this.
