RESULT: WON
TURNS: 9 (my turns 2, 4, ... 18; on the draw, mulligan to 6)

SIDEBOARD: Drew several Carpet of Flowers but never cast one (they had 0 to 2 Islands, so it was weak early), saw several Veil of Summer (one cast on the winning turn before Aluren, one more taken off an Atraxa reveal), and drew 1 Orim's Chant (see below). Veil was the best boarded-in card: it made Aluren and every Acererak cast uncounterable and I won on the spot. Carpet was never needed but is fine as a Wasteland-proof mana source once they have 2 Islands. I would keep all of it, and I would keep Veil at 4.

CHANT_ENDING: Drew one Chant (the first) on turn 8 (my 4th turn); a second one was revealed by Atraxa on turn 14 and left behind. No Prismatic Ending was drawn or revealed (I never had it to judge; Tamiyo, its target, flipped and used her minus once but never mattered).
- Chant turn 8: they had Wasteland, Wasteland and an uncracked Flooded Strand and 7 cards in hand but no colored mana, I had Island, Misty (cracked for Savannah) and Ancient Tomb. I cast Chant (W from Savannah) as step 1 of Chant then Show and Tell with 4 mana, protecting against Force of Will/Negation/Stifle. They responded by Wastelanding Ancient Tomb with Chant on the stack, so Show and Tell was no longer castable, and then in my second main with Ponder on the stack they Wastelanded Savannah. Result: Chant stopped nothing they wanted to cast (they could not cast anything anyway), I used the safe window to Ponder, and it cost me 2 lands for their 2 Wastelands. It was a bad use: Chant needs 4 total mana for Chant+S&T and any Wasteland response breaks it.
- In hindsight: Chant is hard to cast here because white sources are only Tundra and Savannah (Carpet is the third). The Chant+S&T line costs exactly 4 mana from 3 lands, which Wasteland (they had 4) punishes. I would play 2 Chant at most against this deck, and 0 to 1 Ending: I never saw an Ending castable, and Tamiyo (the Ending target) never mattered because Veil+Aluren won the same turn. Veil/Force were what actually beat their Force of Negation, Force of Will, Stifle, Pyroblast and Swords.

Key sequence: they Forced my first Show and Tell with Force of Negation, I resolved the second by Forcing their Force of Will (pitching Atraxa); they Swordsed that Atraxa in response to its trigger but the reveal still gave me Atraxa, Show and Tell, Veil, Carpet and a Strand; the next Show and Tell was Pyroblasted and put Wasteland in, but its Atraxa reveal found Ponder, Brainstorm and lands and the Ponder found Aluren. Turn 18 (they were tapped out): Veil of Summer, Aluren, then the free Acererak Lost Mine loop, about 15 laps with the auto macro, from 17 to 0.

- Mistake: turn 8, I played Ancient Tomb and cast Chant into two untapped Wastelands, losing Tomb and Savannah. Better would have been to hold Tomb and fetch the white source only on the turn the whole line was castable.
- Surprise: the opponent kept a hand with almost no colored mana and discarded to hand size for several turns, and they Wastelanded in response to Chant rather than in my upkeep.
