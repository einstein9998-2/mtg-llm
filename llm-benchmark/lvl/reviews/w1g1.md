# Review w1g1: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 15 (A's 8th turn): Atraxa hit for 7 lifelink on T13 and T15. The game was decided on T9, when Show and Tell resolved into Omniscience.
- T9 (A prompts 63-77, B prompts 70-76): A fetched Savannah, cast Show and Tell with Hedge Maze + 2 Tropical, and held Savannah + Petal. B had Daze, FoW, FoW: two usable counters, since one FoW has to pitch the other or the Daze. A's two spare mana beat both. B Forced (pitching Daze), Veil went on in response and drew a card, and Omniscience came in. No B line at prompt 70 works: Daze first gets paid, then Veil answers the Force.
- The real decision came two turns earlier. On T7 (A prompt 52), A had 4 mana and Veil, Show and Tell, Aluren, Acererak. It cast Ponder instead of jamming Veil + Show and Tell for Aluren. B held exactly Daze + FoW + FoW then, so the jam (Veil as the only spare) loses to Daze on Show and Tell plus Force on Veil. Waiting a turn for a fifth mana was right. B did nothing on T8.
- After Omniscience: Aluren was cast as bait first, then Acererak, which B killed with Bolt + Unholy Heat in response to its enter trigger (B prompts 83-85), so there was no loop on T9. On T11 a free Stock Up found Atraxa + Acererak, the Atraxa reveal took Force, Omniscience, Atraxa, Boseiju and Show and Tell, and 7 Acererak laps drained 2 and made 2 Goblins. B was down to 1 card.

Petal is not an instant, so it cannot come down in response. Casting Petal first (option [1] at prompt 12) makes Daze cost B a card for nothing. A did this right on T5 (prompt 24). - A's later choices were sound. T5: Petal, Brainstorm before the land, Stock Up with Petal spare. T7: the wait (see 1). T9: Show and Tell first while holding Veil, Omniscience over Aluren as the put-in, and Aluren as bait before Acererak.

On T4 B played Polluted Delta and cracked it before Brainstorm, with a known Daze on top and Volcanic Island in hand as the land drop. That shuffled the Daze away. On T9 B needed exactly one more blue card: FoW pitching Daze on Show and Tell, then a second FoW (with a pitch) on Veil, stops the combo. - B's good plays: Daze on the tapped-out Stock Up (T3), Wasteland on Ancient Tomb (T6), and Bolt + Heat on Acererak in response to its trigger (T9). B also cast DRC for free through A's Aluren (prompt 95, no lands tapped).
