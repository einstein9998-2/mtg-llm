# g51 review: Alurentell (A, on the play) beat UR Cutter (B) on game turn 7

## 1. Result and turning point
- A won on turn 7 (A's 4th turn): Savannah, Veil of Summer (A 66), Aluren off 4 lands plus Petal (A 69), free Acererak (A 72), then the Lost Mine macro took B from 19 to 0 (A ended at 29).
- Decisive, B turn 6, B 53/56: B fetched its 3rd Volcanic, cast Cori-Steel Cutter, then Bolted A's face and tapped out. A already had 3 lands, a Petal on the battlefield and 7 cards, so A was clearly on 4 lands plus Petal next turn. Holding all three Volcanics left Bolt plus Unholy Heat (5 damage, no delirium needed) for Acererak with his trigger on the stack. Veil gives hexproof only from blue and black, so the red removal still works through Veil. The existing note already says this ("Do not tap out on your own turn for Murktide or Cutter once they can reach 4 mana").
- With hindsight, holding RR probably only delayed the loss: A held Force of Will plus Stock Up as a pitch, and after Veil A's Force could not be countered. B could not know that.
- The real cause was earlier. B bottomed both Dazes with Preordain (B 12 on turn 2, B 22 on turn 4). On turn 7 A had exactly 5 mana for Veil plus Aluren. A Daze on Veil makes A pay 1, which leaves 3 mana and no Aluren that turn.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- B: B 12 (turn 2). The scry showed Volcanic and Daze. B kept Volcanic on top and bottomed Daze, but Volcanic on top with Daze second keeps both. That breaks the existing note "Never ... Preordain-bottom away Daze ... against a combo deck". B then bottomed the second Daze plus a Bolt at B 22 (B flagged that one itself). The turn-6 tap-out came after these.
- A: none found. Every choice either matches the playbook or is reasonable:
  - Ponder (A 6-8): A kept Misty, Stock Up, Atraxa without a shuffle. Aluren and Acererak were already in hand, so a land plus a cantrip is the right look to keep. Atraxa was redundant, and the end-step fetch shuffled her away for free.
  - Hedge Maze fetch at B's turn-4 end step (A 28): this follows the existing Hedge Maze rule. It untapped on A's turn, its surveil found Veil, and it gave the second green source for GG.
  - Stock Up with no spare (A 36): Daze into it was a fine trade with a second Stock Up in hand. The picks were Tundra and Petal ("with Aluren and Acererak already in hand, dig for mana").
  - Veil, then Aluren (A 66): correct with B's lands all tapped. Only a free Daze or Force was possible, and Veil first covers both (A flagged this itself). Aluren first with a G spare and Veil held for the response was the alternative; it also beats a lone Force and draws a card.

## 3. B's keep and passes
- B's keep (B 2): a one-land seven on the draw (Volcanic, 2 Preordain, 2 Bolt, Cutter, Murktide). On the draw with two Preordain it is a reasonable keep; the sim gave about 80% to find a second land by turn 2, and B did hit every land drop. Its real weakness against combo is that it has no Daze or Force, and it threw away the Daze it found. B already flagged it, and it is a good close spot for Brady.
- Pass on Stock Up (B 38): correct. B's only counter was a single Force, and countering a cantrip 2-for-1 against itself is what A wants.
- Pass on Veil (B 68): it breaks the existing note "Force a Veil cast on their own turn". It did not matter here: B had 0 mana and no second counter, so Aluren and the loop would have resolved anyway. I am not flagging it, because the tap-out made it moot.
- B passes 71, 74, 75 and 76 were forced (Veil made A's spells uncounterable).

## 4. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
none. Every A decision is already covered (Veil first into Force or Daze, Hedge Maze at their end step, Stock Up digging for mana, looping as soon as Aluren resolves).

## 5. Candidate UR Cutter notes
- "Veil of Summer stops blue, not red: once they can cast Veil plus Aluren, keep RR for Bolt plus Heat on Acererak's trigger even before U. Cast Cutter on the turns they cannot combo (turn 4 here, instead of Preordain plus holding a Bolt), not the turn before they reach 5 mana."
  - Evidence: B 18 and B 53/56.
  - Confidence: medium.
  - It refines, rather than contradicts, "hold U (and R) for Prismari Charm ... or Unholy Heat" and "Bolt plus Unholy Heat (delirium) on Acererak ... ends the loop". Delirium is not needed against a 5/5.

## 6. Process and engine flags
- No engine bugs.
- Neither side skipped a land drop, and nobody cast a cantrip in upkeep.
- B's own flag seq 76 is a forced pass. The decision it describes is B 53 (Cutter) and B 56 (Bolt), so I re-flagged it at 53 so Brady can answer it.
- The B macro stopped after 40 picks mid-loop, which was harmless. A's macro took 2 x 200 picks, as expected.
- Tool calls: A made 2 opp-odds calls, which did not change its play. B made 2 sim calls on the keep, which were useful.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on turn 7 on the play: Veil, Aluren, then the Acererak Lost Mine loop while B was tapped out.
TOP MISTAKE: B bottomed both Dazes with Preordain (B 12, B 22) and then tapped out on turn 6 (B 53/56). A Daze on Veil, or Bolt plus Heat held up, was B's only out.
TOP RULE: none new for Alurentell. UR note: Veil does not stop red, so hold RR for Bolt plus Heat once they can cast Veil plus Aluren.
