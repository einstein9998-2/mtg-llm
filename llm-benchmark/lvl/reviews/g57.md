# Review g57: Alurentell (A, on the play, mulligan to 6) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 12 by casting Bolt to A's face at 2 life. A never got past two mana sources until a Lotus Petal on turn 11. The game was decided in two places:
- Turn 4 (B prompts 17-19): B's Wasteland as its second land, used at once on A's only dual (Tropical). That is the signed-off note "their only land on turn 2 against a one-land keep". A's Hedge Maze had entered tapped, so A went back to one land. The Tropical that A's surveil had kept on top only got A back to two lands, and its next three draws were Aluren, Omniscience and Petal.
- Turn 8 (A prompt 65, B prompt 67): A cast Force of Will on a 7/7 Murktide, pitching the spare Stock Up, which was right. B Forced back, pitching Brainstorm and keeping Daze (A had two untapped lands, so Daze would have been paid). Murktide plus DRC took A from 11 to 2. On turn 11 A cast Show and Tell (Petal + 2 lands, no spare) into B's one card, which was Daze. Nothing better was castable at 3 mana, so the loss was locked once the turn-10 attack landed.
- Verdict: mostly the keep plus variance. A's play after the keep was sound; B played close to its notes.
