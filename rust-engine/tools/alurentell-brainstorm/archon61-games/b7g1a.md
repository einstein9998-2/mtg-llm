ARCHON: I never drew Archon of Cruelty (it was somewhere in the 45 cards left in my library) and never saw it, so it had no effect. I cannot say how well it plays. On a card-text basis it is castable only through Show and Tell or Omniscience (8 mana, and my deck has no black source for the BB). Against this deck its enter trigger is strong: they must sacrifice a creature, discard, lose 3 life, and I draw and gain 3. Snuff Out cannot target a black creature, and Fatal Push cannot reach MV 8, so only bounce or Kaito's tap can touch it. As a 61st card it would be a worse draw than Atraxa in the opening hand, because it is a pure Show and Tell payoff with no mana route. I would leave it out, or at most cut a spare Atraxa for it. Atraxa blocks and gains life, and this opponent kills me with fast beaters, so the lifelink and flying body matters more than Archon's drain.
