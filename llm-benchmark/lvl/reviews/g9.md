# Review g9: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 7 (A's 4th turn) without B getting another turn: Aluren, then Acererak recast for free 77 times (19 Lost Mine laps, one Dark Pool drain per lap, 20 -> 1), and the Tomb of Annihilation first room (Trapped Entry) for the last point.
- **Decisive: B turn 6, prompts 17-24.** B played Wasteland and never activated it, although it was offered on every prompt from 17 to 24. A's board was Ancient Tomb, Hedge Maze, Tropical Island: its only two green sources, and Aluren costs {2}{G}{G}. Alurentell has no basic Forest, so Wasteland on Tropical Island (or Maze) leaves A unable to cast Aluren on turn 7 (A's hand: Tomb, 2 Aluren, 2 Force, Veil, drew Acererak; no third G source). Wasteland was not needed for mana: Ajani was paid with Parlor + Quarry. B could have cast Ajani AND Wastelanded.
- **Second: B prompt 17, Ajani tapped Parlor and Quarry (B self-flagged).** With both tapped, B had no white for Swords to Plowshares on A's turn. Swords on Acererak with its ETB trigger on the stack ends the loop (the trigger cannot return him). Kept open, Parlor plus Quarry (sacrificing Voice) gives two Swords against A's one usable Force (the second Force was its only pitch card).
- **A prompt 25:** A saw B's Parlor and Quarry tapped and only Wasteland untapped, cast Aluren (Tomb + Maze + Trop), then Acererak. Correct, and the existing playbook rule ("when the opponent taps out on its own turn its lands stay tapped through my turn") covers it.
- Verdict: not variance. A's hand was fragile (exactly two G sources against a four-Wasteland deck) and B let it through.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none that mattered. Keep at prompt 1 (3 lands, 2 Force, 2 Aluren, Veil, no cantrip, Force without a blue pitch) contradicts Brady's mulligan rule ("a 7 with no cantrips and no blue card for Force of Will ... is a mulligan"); A self-flagged it and it worked, but its weakness was exactly the one B failed to punish. Passing turn 5 with Aluren castable and no creature in hand (prompt 15) was right: Aluren is symmetric and Boros' creatures cost 1-3. Holding Tropical Island instead of playing it on turn 5 (prompt 14) would not have helped: B would Wasteland the Maze instead and A still has one G source.
- A misread Voice of Victory (flags at prompts 25 and 632, report: "Voice stops them casting on my turn"). Voice stops A from casting during B's turn, not B on A's. The loop was safe because B was tapped out, not because of Voice. No decision changed, but the misreading could cost a game when B has W open.
- B: prompt 17-24 (Wasteland, above) is the first and decisive mistake. Keep (prompt 2), Parlor turn 2, Quarry + Voice turn 4 were fine.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Against Boros, Voice of Victory stops me from casting spells during their turn (Force, Veil, Brainstorm, free creatures under Aluren); it does not stop them on mine. Before starting the Acererak loop count their untapped white (Plateau, Parlor, Savannah, and Lazotep Quarry with a creature to sacrifice) as Swords to Plowshares: Swords on Acererak with the ETB trigger on the stack ends the loop. Start it when they are tapped out, or with a Force for each white source."
   - Evidence: A prompts 25 and 632 (flags and report misread Voice); B's two possible white sources were tapped only because of Ajani (B prompt 17). Confidence: high on the card facts, medium on the "Force per white source" count.
   - Overlap: extends "When the opponent taps out on its own turn its lands stay tapped through my turn" (written for UR); no contradiction.
