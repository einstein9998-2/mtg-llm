# Engine and serializer oddities seen while playing Alurentell vs UR Cutter

These are engine or serializer behaviours, kept apart from my own misplays (those are in report.md). Games: a01 to a06, seeds 201, 203 to 207 (seed 202 was aborted, see item 1). Patch for items 1 to 5, 10, 11, 13: `choice-visibility.patch` (diff over `serializer/src/silo/SeatController.java`, tested only in these games, original serializer untouched). Which games had which fix: a01 none; a02 items 1 and 2; a03 items 1 to 3; a04 items 1 to 3 plus the first Atraxa fix, which did not work (see item 4).

## Fixed in the patch

1. **Stock Up picked its cards without asking me.** Forge asks "which two do you keep" through `chooseEntitiesForEffect` with no reveal list, so the five looked-at library cards were labelled "a hidden card", collapsed to one forced option and auto-resolved to an arbitrary pair. Seen in a01 (the first Stock Up; the second was countered) and in the aborted seed-202 game. Fix: the Dig spell's controller sees the cards by name and picks.
2. **Ponder asked "shuffle?" without showing the three cards and reordered them itself.** `orderMoveToZoneList` was not exposed. Fix: one prompt per card, first pick = top of library, then the shuffle question.
3. **Scry and surveil were decided by the Forge AI** (a01 had none; in a02 Hedge Maze's surveil binned Omniscience before I could act). Fix: one keep/discard prompt per card, then order of the kept cards.
4. **Atraxa's "choose a card of each type from the top ten" showed "a hidden card".** The ten cards were revealed to everyone, but the serializer treated them as hidden, so I picked blind in a02, a04 and a06. Two earlier fixes failed (imprint-based; then a hook in `chooseSingleEntityForEffect`). Cause found by replaying a06 (seed 207, my recorded picks): Forge asks through `chooseCardsForEffect`, not the single-entity method. Fix: in `chooseCardsForEffect`, library cards that I own and that are offered by a ChooseCard ability I activate count as shown. Verified by replay and then live: the prompt lists the cards of that type by name in every game since a07.
5. **Dungeon and room choices were made by the Forge AI** (a02 and a04, where it also entered Tomb of Annihilation). Now prompts: dungeon (`chooseSingleCardFace`) and room (`chooseSingleSpellForEffect`). Used live in a06 (about 14 dungeon/room prompts, Lost Mine every time, Dark Pool for the drain).
10. **Venture room effects with a cost (Veils of Fear, Sandfall Cell) were decided for me**, and 13. **Force of Will's pitched card was chosen for me.** Both fixed with one mechanism: the cost decision maker (`getCostDecisionMaker`) now asks me for discard, exile and sacrifice costs. Pitch prompt seen live in a06 (I pitched a spare Show and Tell).

## Still open (not fixed)

5 (old note, superseded by the entry above). **Dungeon and room choices are made by the Forge AI, not offered to me.** Correction to my earlier note: the loop is meant to avoid Tomb of Annihilation so Acererak keeps returning to hand and Aluren recasts it free, and that is what happened in a02. Forge entered Lost Mine of Phandelver (twice), then Dungeon of the Mad Mage, never Tomb, so the loop ran as intended (about 20 free casts; Dark Pool drained the opponent 5 to 2 and the rooms gave Goblins, Treasures and cards). The oddity is only that I could not choose: the AI could just as well enter Tomb of Annihilation and end the loop, and I could not pick Dark Pool or other rooms myself. Needs a dungeon pick and a room pick prompt, but it is not blocking.
6. **London mulligan order.** The prompt to bottom a card comes before the keep or mulligan question for that hand ("you will bottom 1" appears afterwards). Harmless but easy to misread; the keep decision is made on the already-reduced hand.
7. **Mana payment is still delegated (open, now low priority).** In a03 the Forge AI paid Stock Up's {2}{U} with Hedge Maze, Island and Tropical Island and left Ancient Tomb and a Petal, so a Show and Tell that turn would have had one coloured source only. A payment prompt, or at least "pay with colourless first", would help the combo deck most.
8. **Display of tapped state during the opponent's turn.** My lands show "(T)" all through the opponent's turn because they untap only on my untap step. Correct, but it reads like a leak or a bug on first sight.

9. **WITHDRAWN: Omniscience "missing from the menu" was my miscount, not an engine bug.** Omniscience costs {7}{U}{U}{U}, ten mana. In a04 turn 12 I had six lands (two Ancient Tombs) plus a Petal, which is nine; in a06 turn 11 eight, turn 13 nine and then ten only by tapping both Tombs at 3 life (four damage, which is lethal, so leaving it off the menu was right). Everything below this line in this item is the original (wrong) diagnosis and is kept only for the record. The patched `ComputerUtilMana` (lets a player tap Ancient Tomb at low life) was never needed for these cases; it stays behind the `SelfHarmOk` switch and is off for the Forge AI baseline.

Original text: A castable spell can be missing from the menu: Omniscience at 7 life (a04, turn 12). I had 9 mana of sources including two Ancient Tombs, enough for Omniscience, but it was not offered, while Aluren (4 mana) was. The menu uses Forge's AI `ComputerUtilMana.canPayManaCost`, which refuses to tap sources whose drawback the AI judges unsafe, so two Tomb taps at 7 life were rejected. Later at 9+ life with Treasures Omniscience (and hardcast Atraxa) appeared. Legality is understated by AI self-preservation. The AI then never offered me the cast, so I lost a turn and took 3 more damage. This one decided how a04 went. Fix needed: payability check without the AI's life-safety filter, plus the payment prompt (item 7).
10 (old note, fixed). **Venture room effects with a cost are decided for me.** Veils of Fear (discard or lose 2) and Sandfall Cell (sacrifice or lose 2) chose City of Traitors and an Island for me without a prompt.
11 (old note, fixed). **Dungeon pick not mine, and sometimes Tomb.** In a04 the AI chose Lost Mine, Mad Mage, Lost Mine, Mad Mage, then Tomb of Annihilation (Trapped Entry, Veils of Fear, Sandfall Cell). Tomb completes after the next room and would end the Acererak bounce (the edge case Brady mentioned). I could not choose a different dungeon, so this is exactly the choice the policy should have.
12. **Priority prompts for every trigger on the stack.** Three Dragon's Rage Channeler triggers on the stack cost three separate pass prompts, plus one per room trigger. Harmless but inflates prompt counts.

13 (old note, fixed). **Force of Will's pitched card is chosen for me.** In a05 the engine exiled Omniscience from my hand as the blue card, with no prompt (I had a spare Atraxa and a second Omniscience, so no harm, but it is my choice to make).

## Found in the a07 to a21 batch

14. **Invalid pick counts silently fall back to the Forge AI.** In a07 I answered a two-card cleanup discard with one index; the serializer did not reject it but let the Forge AI choose, and it binned my only Acererak and a spare Atraxa. `play.py` now rejects wrong counts, duplicate indices and out-of-range indices before anything is sent. The serializer itself should answer "invalid pick" or re-ask.
15. **Mana payment wastes sources (open, low priority).** a20: Aluren ({2}{G}{G}) was paid with Ancient Tomb (two) plus Forest, Tropical Island and Island, one more mana than needed, so a spare mana I was holding for Daze was gone. a03 had the opposite problem (it kept Tomb and Petal but tapped coloured sources). Both show the AI's payment choice matters for a deck that keeps Daze mana; a "pay with" prompt would fix it. a37: Aluren ({2}{G}{G}) with Tomb, Forest, Island and a Petal was paid with all four, though Tomb + Forest + Petal sufficed and I wanted Island open for Daze; no cost that game (the opponent used Force of Will, not Daze).
16. **Daze with no untapped mana.** Daze's "pay {1}" has no way to pay when every land is tapped; the engine just counters the spell. Correct per the rules, but I only learned that Daze is live by reading their hand in the post-game review, because the prompt does not mark it. No serializer change needed.
17. **Stale-menu risk is structural.** Every action changes the menu indices, and the first prompt of a game is a die-roll question in some games and a Mulligan question in others (a16: my "draw first" answer 1 hit Mulligan). Players should always read the newest menu before picking; `play.py pick` could print the option text it is about to send. Not an engine bug.

18. **Ponder ordering prompt lists identical card names once (found in a33, cost a game).** The prompt for ordering the top three showed 'Aluren, Show and Tell' for three cards, because the two Show and Tell cards collapsed into one line; the third card then disappeared as a forced pick. Menus print duplicates as '(x2)', this prompt does not. A '(2 cards)' marker or one line per card id would have shown me that two of the three were the same card. Brainstorm put-backs and Stock Up lists have distinct card ids and were fine. Fix in the serializer: list cards by id in `orderMoveToZoneList`.

## Checked and worked

Show and Tell with both sides choosing (the AI put in Murktide Regent, then Mishra's Bauble), Veil of Summer making Show and Tell uncounterable, Aluren casting Acererak free at instant speed, Force of Will with a blue pitch card, Runestone Caverns "play from exile" prompt, legend rule prompt, Brainstorm put-backs, fetch land shuffle, Wasteland on my nonbasics in response to which I could still use the land.
