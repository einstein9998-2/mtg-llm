RESULT: WON
TURNS: 4 (my fourth turn, on the play; won in my second main phase)
EUREKA: I drew one Eureka, on my turn 2, as one of the two cards I took from a Stock Up. I never cast it. It sat in hand to the end (I kept it over the second Atraxa when discarding to hand size).
- Why I did not cast it: on turn 3 I had 4 mana (Island, Tropical, 2 Petals) and either Eureka or Show and Tell. Eureka would have used all 4 mana with no Daze spare. It would also have let the opponent dump their whole hand of permanents, one round at a time. Show and Tell cost 3, left a Petal spare, and gave them only one put-in. The opponent used that put-in on Flooded Strand, fetching an Underground Sea. I put in Atraxa.
- Atraxa's trigger found Aluren, Acererak, Ancient Tomb, Petal, Brainstorm and a second Show and Tell. On turn 4 I attacked with Atraxa, cast Aluren (they Forced it, I Forced back), then looped Acererak for the win.
- Eureka was only a backup here. The hand would have worked with Eureka as a 4-mana Atraxa plus Atraxa (two Atraxa triggers at once), but it cost too much mana, gave a symmetrical hand dump, and had no Daze protection.
- Keep count: I would keep 1 Eureka in this matchup, not 2. The opponent has Wasteland, Daze and Force of Will, which punish a 4-mana sorcery with GG that is worse than Show and Tell. The second copy would come out for the Ponder (or an extra Stock Up). Eureka looks best once their hand is empty or they are tapped out with few permanents, and in a hand with GG and spare mana and no Show and Tell. Compared with Show and Tell: it costs 1 more, needs GG, and helps them more.
