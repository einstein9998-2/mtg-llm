# g73 review: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won in combat on game turn 12 (B's 6th turn): DRC 3/3 flier + Monk 2/2 trample = 5 into A at 4 (3 + 1 from Mad Mage's Yawning Portal). Truth check of A's report: nothing killed A "right after the venture". After a123 A had no untapped land and only Veil in hand, so the engine auto-passed the rest of A's T11 and B's T12 windows; the game ended in B's T12 combat. Engine order was right: Heat (cast with Acererak's ETB trigger on the stack) resolved first, Acererak died, the trigger still ventured and the bounce did nothing. No bug.
- Deciding decision: T9 a85/a86 Brainstorm put-back. Hand after the draw: Misty, Aluren, Omniscience, Boseiju, Acererak x2. A put back Omniscience and the SECOND Acererak and kept Misty and Boseiju. Better: put back Omniscience and one land (Boseiju on top, play Misty and keep it uncracked until after the T11 draw; or play Boseiju, put Misty on top). T11 mana is identical (Island, Maze, Trop, Boseiju = 2GG), but with two Acererak in hand: Heat kills the first, the second is cast free under Aluren and loops. B's hand after Heat was Polluted Delta only (third Force was on the library bottom), so this was a win. A named the mistake itself but did not flag it.
- T7 a63/a68 (Show and Tell for Aluren, Petal held, Veil in response to Force; B Forced the Veil too, pitching Charm then Murktide): correct, and the loss of that exchange was variance. B was tapped out (only Force/Daze live), Tomb was certain to be Wastelanded on B's next turn, and A held protection ("Show and Tell: when you hold protection, jam it now"). Waiting only gave B untapped Heat/Charm and a Wasteland. Veil first instead of in response loses the same way to two Forces. The Veil-in-response cost B four cards (2 Force, Charm, Murktide) for A's two and left B with one card.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: T1 a6, small. Cracked the only fetch to cast Brainstorm in main 1 with no reason to hurry, then put back Strand and Boseiju with no shuffle left, so the T3 draw was the put-back Strand. Brainstorm at B's T2 end step off the Island, then crack the T3 Strand, sees a fresh card ("wait as long as possible if waiting is free"). The real loss was a86 above.
- Keep (a1, A flagged): fine on the play. 4 lands + Petal + Brainstorm + Aluren needs one creature; a 6 is worse against Force/Wasteland.
- A's own mistake list: (1) the T7 jam was right (above). (2) "Feeding Wasteland" was not avoidable: the deck has one basic Island (fetched T1) and no Forest, so every later fetch was a nonbasic; Hedge Maze at end step is playbook, and Tomb on T7 was the only way to have Show and Tell plus Petal for Veil. (3) The damage (11 combat, 4 fetch, 2 Tomb) is a result, not a decision. (4) The spare Acererak: yes, decisive. The T10 end-step Misty crack (a108) shuffled away Omniscience and the put-back Acererak, but without it A draws Omniscience T11, so the put-back was the error. T11 a114 Aluren with no spare was forced (Veil does not stop red Heat).
- Ponder T7 a56-a58: correct (Acererak on top, Brainstorm next, no shuffle). Hedge Maze surveils fine.
- B: no real mistake. Wasteland on Hedge Maze T6 (A's only green source on board, B self-flagged) and on Ancient Tomb T8 (playbook: Tomb when Show and Tell is the threat), both on B's own turn before A untapped. Force on Show and Tell, second Force on Veil (required: a resolved Veil makes the first Force fail). Pitch order was moot after the second Force; in isolation Murktide was the better first pitch (Charm bounces Aluren). Bauble cast on its own turn under a DRC trigger, surveil Bauble #2 for delirium; Heat with the ETB trigger on the stack was exact.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "With Aluren as the route and two Acererak, keep both through Brainstorm and Ponder; put back Omniscience or a land you will redraw instead. Against Heat, Bolt or Swords the second Acererak is the backup that wins after they kill the first on his trigger." Evidence: g73 a86 (and a108 crack). Confidence: high. Refines "Brainstorm, fetch and Ponder: put back duplicates (a second Atraxa or Show and Tell, extra lands), never your only Aluren or Acererak", which reads as allowing the second Acererak; consistent with "take every card Atraxa offers, a spare Acererak first".
2. "When you put a land back with Brainstorm to redraw it, do not crack a fetch before that draw." Evidence: a86-a108 line. Confidence: medium. Complements "hold the fetch uncracked until their end step so it shuffles before my draw (t25)", which is right only when the put-backs are bad.
