# Review g53: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 8 (B's 4th turn): Ponder, Unholy Heat on Atraxa (A Force, B Force pitching Daze), Bolt on Atraxa, then DRC 3/3 flier + a 6/6 prowess Monk hit A at 4.
- Turn 3, A prompt 22 (decisive): with Ponder in hand and only a colourless Ancient Tomb out, A fetched Hedge Maze. It always enters tapped (Oracle, and playbook "Hedge Maze fetched at their end step enters tapped"), so A could not Ponder that turn. A basic Island (also the anti-Wasteland pick) lets A Ponder at once. In the game the shuffle put Aluren, Ancient Tomb, Lotus Petal on top (the Maze surveil binned that Aluren), and a different fetch may not have changed that order. Ponder takes Petal now and leaves Tomb for the turn-5 draw. Turn 5 then has Island + 2 Tomb + Petal: Veil (Petal G), Show and Tell (Island + Tomb) and a spare Tomb. B held only one Force (pitch Preordain) and no Daze until turn 8, so Atraxa would very likely have resolved on turn 5 against a 1/1 DRC with A at about 17. In the game, the turn-5 Ponder had to use the Maze, which left one coloured source (Petal) and no Veil + Show and Tell until turn 7. By turn 7 Cutter, the Monk and a 3/3 DRC had put A at 12.
- Turn 7-8: once Atraxa arrived a turn late, B's hand (Heat, Bolt, Force, then Daze and a second Bolt off Ponder) killed it and A in most lines. That part was variance.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: the turn-3 Hedge Maze fetch above, a rules misread (A's report says it expected the land untapped). It cost a turn, and in this game probably the game.
- A, the one-land keep (prompt 1, already self-flagged): the seven had two Atraxa (creatures) but no Show and Tell or Acererak, so it was half of each combo. Brady's "one-lander is a keep if it has the combo" does not cover it. The batch-2 rule "Stock Up on turn 1 that likely finds a permanent mana source is a keep (close)" does: about 90% to find a land and about 33% to see Show and Tell in the five. It is defensible, not a mistake. The hidden cost was colour: Tomb makes none and the Stock Up ate the Petal, so A had no coloured source until turn 3 and needed two for Veil + Show and Tell.
- A, the turn-7 tap-out for Stock Up (prompt 88, 10 -> 8 via Tomb): sound. Veil had made it uncounterable, Aluren was uncastable that turn (one green source untapped), and it found Acererak for a turn-9 Aluren win. The odds call showed 42% for Acererak in 5 cards against 27% with Brainstorm. The 2 life did not decide the game: B's turn-8 attack dealt 9, and at 10 life a held Bolt still finds 9 against 7. The real alternative was in the pick (prompt 91, self-flagged): Petal + Veil instead of Acererak + Petal. A Petal-paid Veil in B's turn wins the Heat/Force war and keeps a 7/7 lifelink blocker (survive at about 6, no Acererak). That is close; A never weighed it.
- Tomb life: 6 life from three Tomb payments (turns 1 and 7). Each was the only way to cast that spell and none decided the result. No overpayment.
- B: no mistake. Not Forcing Veil (B prompt 78) was right with one Force: Show and Tell was still castable off Tomb + Petal, so the Force was better kept for the Heat war on turn 8, which it won. The end-step Bolt to face (8 -> 5, prompt 103) set up exact lethal through any block. Heat first to draw out the Force, then Bolt, was the right order. Daze or Preordain as the pitch was a coin flip.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Hedge Maze always enters tapped, also when you fetch it in your main phase. If you want to cast a spell this turn (Ponder off a Tomb-only board), fetch the basic Island or a dual instead." Evidence: turn 3, A prompt 22. Confidence: high. Overlap: it sharpens "from hand, play it on a turn where the untapped land's mana would go unused" and the end-step note. Those rules imply it, but A misread the card, so the main-phase fetch case needs saying.
2. "Count coloured sources for the Veil + Show and Tell turn: it needs two (G and U); Ancient Tomb and City give none. With a Tomb-only start, the first fetch should be the coloured land that lets you cantrip now." Evidence: turns 1-5 (prompts 1, 22, 39). Confidence: medium. Overlap: it extends "Count mana before planning", which counts amounts but not colours.
