RESULT: WON

ARCHON: I drew Archon of Cruelty on turn 4 and held it; it never reached the battlefield. My only Show and Tell that turn went to Atraxa, because Atraxa (7/7 lifelink, deathtouch) blocks the two must-attack Channelers and her ten-card reveal found Aluren, Force of Will, Ponder and City of Traitors. Archon's edict, discard, 3 drain and draw would have been strong against two Channelers, but at 6 toughness it dies to a single delirium Unholy Heat (their Heat is red, so Veil does not stop it), while Atraxa needed two Bolts after blocking. Archon is uncastable without a second Show and Tell or Omniscience (neither was available), so it was a dead card in hand all game and Aluren plus Acererak won on turn 5. I would not keep it in the main deck against this opponent: Atraxa already does the job, and when Aluren or Omniscience is online Acererak wins by himself. I would swap it back for the Ponder it replaced (a second choice is to cut a Veil of Summer, which is weak against Pyroblast and Heat). Compared with Atraxa, it is a faster clock (about 9 per hit, edict every attack) but has no card selection and a much worse body.
