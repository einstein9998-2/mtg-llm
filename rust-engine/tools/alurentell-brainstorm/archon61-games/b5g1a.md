ARCHON: I never drew Archon of Cruelty (it was somewhere in the library the whole game; I saw about 14 of my 61 cards) and never got a Show and Tell to cast. So it did nothing here. Judged on card text only: it is a strong Show and Tell target (the enter trigger edicts a creature or planeswalker, strips a card, drains 3, draws a card, gains 3, and the 6/6 flyer attacks for the same again). Against this deck it would be a good second payoff to put in, because Dimir has few answers that handle a big black flyer cleanly. Against Atraxa it is weaker, since it is a worse body and gives no card selection, but its life swing helps against their small fliers and Moonshadow, and Atraxa can only be answered by Force, Daze or bounce. It is also a worse payoff than Omniscience or Aluren, because those win outright. Over a 60-card deck it only works with Show and Tell (or Omniscience), so as a 61st card I would leave it out, or cut one Atraxa for it, but not add it on top of the deck. In the games where I draw Show and Tell the deck already has enough payoffs, and in the games where I do not it is a dead card.
