# Opening hand and mulligan

Use when: deciding whether to keep an opening hand, and choosing what to bottom after a mulligan.

## The test (P1)

- **OH1** [P1] A keep needs a route to a payoff. A seven whose only spells are Show and Tell and Acererak, or one land plus Ponder with no creature, is a mulligan. Exception (Brady): if you have the combo you do not need cantrips and can keep a one-lander. (Brady, LvL sign-off)
- **OH2** [P1] Hands that need two more cards (a creature plus an enabler) are the ones that lose to Force of Will, Daze and Wasteland. (observed, a07 to a30)

## Keeps and mulligans Brady has ruled on

- **OH3** [P1] A 7 with no cantrips and no blue card for Force of Will, slow even if it finds Acererak, is a mulligan. A 7 with two Force of Will (the second pitches to the first), Hedge Maze surveil and Ponder / Stock Up to dig toward a fast combo is a keep. (Brady, tough spots batch 1)
- **OH4** [P1, P2] Two surveil lands toward Acererak or Atraxa or more cantrips, with live Force, is a keep (close). Stock Up on turn 1 that likely finds a permanent mana source is a keep (close). (Brady, tough spots batch 2)
- **OH5** [P1, P13] One land with Ponder is a mulligan unless you know the opponent is Storm or Reanimator (then keep). No permanent coloured source, or no cantrips, with one land is a mulligan. (Brady, tough spots batch 2)

## Observed

- **OH6** [P1] A seven with no blue source and no payoff creature (a30: Tomb, City, Forest, Ponder, Show and Tell, two Omniscience) is a close mulligan; the keep would have won there, but the decision was reasonable. (a30)
- **OH7** [P1] One-land keeps with Petal and three cantrips on the draw were not punished early but never found a payoff; a creature (Acererak or Atraxa) in the opening seven matters more than extra cantrips. (a36)

Conflict: OH7 (observed) says a creature in the seven matters more than extra cantrips, while OH3 and OH4 (Brady) call some creature-less dig hands keeps. OH3 and OH4 are Brady's rulings on specific hands; OH7 is an observation about one-land hands, which OH1 and OH5 already make mulligans unless you have the combo or face Storm or Reanimator.

## After a mulligan (P3, P13)

- **OH8** [P3, P13] After a mulligan, bottom the card that is dead in the matchup (Veil of Summer against Boros) and keep the Show and Tell payoff (Omniscience, Atraxa). (Brady, LvL sign-off)
- See also vs-boros.md BO1: against Boros after a mulligan, Stock Up should put one card back.
