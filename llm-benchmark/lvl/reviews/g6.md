# Review g6: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs UR Cutter (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 14 (A's 7th turn): The Atropal (4/4) and Acererak attacked an opponent at 4 life, after Aluren + Acererak went through Tomb of Annihilation on turn 12-13. B had A at 3 life on turn 8 with three 3/3 flying DRCs.
- Turn 8 (A prompt 62-63): at 4 life A cracked Misty (to 3) for the third mana and cast Show and Tell for Atraxa. B could not answer: its hand was Scalding Tarn, Force of Will and DRC, with no blue card to pitch (B prompt 64). Atraxa blocked a DRC every turn, gaining 7 each time; lifelink and combat damage are simultaneous, so A survived at exactly 3 + 7 - 6 = 4 (A prompt 71).
- Why B had no pitch: B's Brainstorm put back both blue cards (Murktide, Daze) and then Delta shuffled them away (B prompt 19, B's own flag), and DRC's surveil binned the next Murktide for delirium (B prompt 42). Delirium gave the fast clock; it also left Force of Will dead for the whole game.
- Verdict: A won on skill plus a little luck (no Bolt for B on turns 9 and 10). A's turn-8 pick of a second Atraxa over Acererak (A prompt 65) delayed the win from turn 10 to turn 14; the turn-12 choice of Tomb over the Lost Mine loop (A prompt 122) gave B one more turn.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, turn 8, prompt 65 (Atraxa reveal, creature slot): A took a second Atraxa with Atraxa #1 already on the battlefield; the other creature option was Acererak, and Aluren was taken in the same reveal (prompt 67). The second Atraxa is legendary and only useful as a spare against a bounce. With Acererak, turn 10 (4 lands) was Veil, then Show and Tell for Aluren, then a free Acererak through Aluren (blue counters dead under Veil), then the Lost Mine loop: a win on turn 10. Instead A had to spend a second Show and Tell on turn 10 to dig up Acererak (prompts 82-95).
- A, turn 12, prompt 122: chose Tomb of Annihilation over the Lost Mine drain loop with B at 5 life. This breaks an existing rule ("Default: do not complete Tomb of Annihilation"); B has no lock pieces. A flagged it itself. Then, at prompt 130, A picked Oubliette over Veils of Fear. Oubliette hits only the player who ventures (discard, then sacrifice an artifact, a creature and a land), so A lost its own Atraxa, Hedge Maze and Flooded Strand. Veils of Fear and then Sandfall Cell would have made B (at 4) give up a card or a permanent or die, at no real cost to A (at 20 life).
- Not mistakes, despite A's report: the turn-10 Show and Tell for a second Atraxa (prompt 91) was good with no Acererak in hand. The legend rule only cost a copy, and the new enter-the-battlefield reveal got Acererak, Veil, Petal and Strand. The report's "did not crack Misty at the turn-4 end step" is also wrong: A's land drop that turn was Ancient Tomb, and no Misty was on the battlefield.
- B, turn 3, prompt 19 (B flagged it): with Force of Will as the only answer to a combo deck, B put back Murktide and Daze, its only blue cards, and shuffled them away. It then binned Murktide to surveil at prompt 42 (a close trade: delirium meant +4 flying power a turn). The clearest pure mistake was turn 10, prompt 81: B passed on A's Veil of Summer while holding Daze and Force of Will, then tried Daze on the Show and Tell that Veil had made uncounterable (prompt 83). A had exactly 3 mana left after Veil. Cracking Tarn and hard-casting Force (5 lands) on Veil, with Daze kept for Show and Tell, would have countered both; even Force pitching Daze on Veil was better than a dead Daze.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Atraxa reveal with Atraxa already on the battlefield: take Acererak as the creature, not a second Atraxa. Aluren plus Acererak wins; a second Atraxa dies to the legend rule."
   - Evidence: A prompt 65 (cost the turn-10 win). Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: extends "Take the green source and Force of Will, then Aluren, and win" (batch 2). No contradiction.
2. "If you do go through Tomb of Annihilation, take Veils of Fear and then Sandfall Cell, never Oubliette. Oubliette makes only you discard and sacrifice an artifact, a creature and a land; it cost me Atraxa."
   - Evidence: A prompt 130. Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: adds Oubliette to the existing note "Tomb rooms hurt both players (...Veils of Fear and Sandfall Cell...)".
3. "The Lost Mine loop is free and infinite under Aluren. The number of casts is never a reason to pick Tomb; Tomb leaves the opponent a turn."
   - Evidence: A prompt 122 (B got turn 13 at 4 life with 2 cards). Confidence: medium-high.
   - Overlap: sharpens the existing default ("do not complete Tomb of Annihilation"); A broke that rule rather than lacking one.
4. "With Atraxa on board and no Acererak in hand, Show and Tell for a second Atraxa is a fresh 10-card reveal (the legend rule only costs the copy). It beats putting in Aluren alone."
   - Evidence: A prompts 82-99. Confidence: low-medium (one game; ask Brady).

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "When the combo player casts Veil of Summer, counter the Veil, not the spell behind it: once Veil resolves, Daze and Force of Will cannot counter anything that turn. Crack a fetch to hard-cast Force of Will if you can, and keep Daze for the follow-up when they have no spare mana."
   - Evidence: B prompts 81 and 83. Confidence: high.
