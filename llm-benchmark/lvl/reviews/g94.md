# g94 review: Alurentell (A, on the draw, 7 cards) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play, mulligan to 6)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 9 (B's 5th turn): 18 attackers plus 2 mobilize Warriors (Ocelot 3/3 flier, the rest 1/1, over 20 damage) into A at 3 life with no creatures, tapped out after a do-nothing Aluren. Life: 20, T3 19, T5 14 (5 attackers), Misty 13, T7 4 (Ocelot 3 + 6), Misty 3.
- Decided at A prompt 4, the keep: Island, Tundra, Tropical, Misty, Petal, Force, Show and Tell. Mana was fine, but there was no payoff, no creature and no cantrip, and Force had only Show and Tell to pitch. The odds call (26 outs in 53, 94% in 4 draws) is the right arithmetic for the wrong question. It counted Aluren (useless without a creature) and Omniscience (needs a creature too). The cards that actually win from this hand: Atraxa (4) for Show and Tell, about 21% in 3 draws, and Karakas bounces her anyway. Cantrips only dig. A close keep, leaning mulligan against a turn-1 Ocelot. A flagged it itself.
- After the keep the loss was variance. In 5 looks (draws Hedge Maze, Misty, Misty; surveils Tomb, Force) A saw none of its own 26 outs, then drew Aluren on T8. That is the 6% case.
