# g44 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn, on the draw). T4 (A19): Ancient Tomb, then Show and Tell for Atraxa; B put in Ajani. Atraxa (A27-30) took Omniscience, Show and Tell, Force of Will and Savannah. T6 (A39-40): Savannah, then Island + Savannah + Petal for Show and Tell into Omniscience. Atraxa attacked (20 to 13), then free Acererak looped Lost Mine with the triggers resolving until B was dead (about 323 auto picks).
- **What decided it:** B never had a white source. Its whole game was one Wasteland and two creatures put in by A's Show and Tells. A's line was fast and clean. Even with a Plains on T3, B's Swords would have run into two free Forces of Will under Omniscience, so the result was mostly variance plus the keep.

## 2. Judgements requested, then first real mistake per side
- **B's keep (B1): lean mulligan, close, high tough-spot value (B flagged it itself).** The hand was Mountain, 2 Wasteland, Erode, Voice, Guide, Ajani: zero castable spells. 17 of the 53 cards left make W (fetches, Plateau, Plains, Savannah, Parlor, Karakas). On the play that is 32% by B's turn 2 and 54% by turn 3, so a 2-drop on T3 is a coin flip against a deck that goldfishes T4-T6. B's "79% for a land in 3 draws" counted Wastelands. Two Wastelands are the case for keeping, but against a Show and Tell deck they only delay, and with no clock A gets every turn it needs. A six with a white source and a 1-drop is better.
- **B's Wasteland (B21): wrong timing, cost nothing.** B killed Ancient Tomb in response to Show and Tell, after its mana was already spent. That breaks the existing note "Use it before they untap, not after a spell". Killing it at A's end step denies the same mana and keeps the option open. Tomb was still the right target. B's choices for Show and Tell (Ajani, then Voice) were fine.
- **B T5 no attack (B36): small miss.** Attacking with the Cat Warrior token alone into untapped Atraxa was free. If Atraxa blocked, a Cat dies and Ajani flips to Avenger at no cost. Unblocked, it deals 2.
- **A's Atraxa picks (A27): Omniscience over Aluren was correct, high confidence.** Count the T6 mana: Island + Petal + one land drop. Aluren ({2}{G}{G}) was not castable (Savannah route is 3 mana; Tomb route has one G). So Aluren, like Omniscience, needed the second Show and Tell, and there Omniscience is strictly better against Boros: free Force for Swords on Acererak, no free creatures for B, and Atraxa's 7 lifelink plus 44 cards of library made the resolve-everything lap safe. The other picks (Show and Tell over Ponder, Force over Veil, which is blank against Boros) were right. Savannah over Strand was fine because A tapped it at once (A39-40).
- **Petal and discard (A31, A33): correct.** A cast Petal on T4 instead of discarding to hand size. That follows the Boros note "Spider-Woman makes Petals enter tapped (cast Petals a turn early)", and the Petal paid for T6's Show and Tell. Discarding Hedge Maze (tapped land) over Ancient Tomb, Savannah or a Force pitch was right.
- **A's first real mistake, A46 (T6, harmless):** A attacked with Atraxa before starting the loop. The signed-off rule says "loop now ... Do not cantrip, attack or take Tomb first." B had no W, so it cost nothing. A minor earlier slip (A5): A cracked Misty in T2 main 1 rather than at B's end step. The playbook says "Keeping Misty uncracked until the turn I need the mana costs nothing" (a41). No effect here.
- **A's Brainstorm put-back (A15, self-flagged):** Hedge Maze + Ponder rather than Petal. It goes against "put back Lotus Petal, not a land" (batch 2), but it is moot: Atraxa took Ponder off the top and the Petal paid for T6.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules

## 5. Process and engine flags
- **Land drops and upkeep:** none skipped (A: T2 Misty, T4 Tomb, T6 Savannah; B: T1, T3, T5). No upkeep cantrips. One odds call each, neither decisive.
- **Engine:** no bugs. Free and pitch Force of Will appear as two menu entries under Omniscience (A48), which is correct. The auto macro stopped at its pick budget twice (A56, B55), which is benign.
- **A's report is wrong on one point:** the loop did not drain "faster than 1 per lap". 60 picks is about 5 laps (12 picks per lap) for 5 life, exactly one Dark Pool per lap. A's report also misses its own attack-before-loop slip.
