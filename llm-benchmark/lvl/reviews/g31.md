# Review g31: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 10 (B's 5th turn): DRC turn 2, delve Murktide 7/7 turn 6, Cutter + Bolt + Monk turn 8 took A from 14 to 1, lethal attack turn 10. A never cast Aluren or Show and Tell: its draws were Show and Tell, Veil x2, Ancient Tomb x2, Acererak x3 (uncastable: no black source, and Show and Tell on Acererak only bounces him), and no Atraxa, Omniscience, Aluren or cantrip until a Brainstorm on turn 9. Mostly variance, but two A decisions on turns 8-9 threw away a real out.
- **T8, A prompt 97 (deciding).** Bolt on the stack aimed at A (14 life), with Murktide 7, Monk 2 and DRC 1 about to attack (the surveil had just shown no delirium). A passed and went to 1. At 1 life both Ancient Tombs (2 life per tap) and Force of Will (1 life) are dead, so A's turn 9 had only Island + Savannah. Force of Will on the Bolt, pitching Show and Tell (the only blue card, with no payoff to pair it with), would have left A at 4. In hindsight that wins: on turn 9 A drew Brainstorm, B Dazed it, A's Veil in response drew a card and made A's spells uncounterable for the turn, and Brainstorm found Lotus Petal, a second Show and Tell and Aluren. At 4 life, Tomb (2 colorless) + Petal (U) casts the uncounterable Show and Tell for Aluren, then the free Acererak loop wins (B's two Forces were blank under Veil). At prompt 91, before the surveil, delirium was still possible and the hit could have been lethal (3+7+3+2 = 15 vs 14), so the Force was even stronger there. Caveat: playbook line 23 says the engine refuses Tomb taps "at 4 life or less" in the Omniscience context (two taps); a single tap at 4 should be legal, but confirm.
- **T9, A prompt 123.** A passed main 1 and then main 2 holding Lotus Petal + Brainstorm, with its land drop unused, while dead on board next turn and its spells uncounterable this turn. Petal into Brainstorm (3 new cards) looks for City of Traitors (Petal U + City CC = Show and Tell for Aluren) or a land plus another Petal. That is a small out (~10%), but passing is a 0% line.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A:** prompt 97 above (medium confidence: it costs the only blue card, but at 1 life A had essentially no outs). Before that, the turn-1 Ponder keep (prompt 7: Show and Tell, Veil, Ancient Tomb on top, with Acererak as the hand's only creature) is close, not a clear error. Holding Show and Tell on turns 5 and 7 was right: the only thing to put in was Acererak, and it gives B a free Murktide. Report errors: A's report says Tomb taps cost it life; A never tapped a Tomb.
- **B:** prompt 110. A cast Veil of Summer in response to Daze on its own turn at 1 life. B held Force of Will x2 and passed. A resolved Veil makes every A spell uncounterable for the rest of the turn and draws a card, so B's Forces were dead for exactly the turn A could combo. Force on the Veil (pitch the other Force) keeps Daze countering Brainstorm and A draws nothing. It did not cost B the game, because A found no third mana. Everything else B did was clean: Bauble on A's library revealed Acererak, Murktide after combat with two Forces as backup, and the all-in Bolt to face put A below Tomb range (B's report notes this).

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Life is mana: Ancient Tomb costs 2 life per tap and Force of Will costs 1. When burn or an attack would take me to 2 or less, Force the burn (or chump) if that keeps a Tomb usable next turn. At 1 life my Tombs and Forces are blank cards." Evidence: T8 prompts 91/97. Confidence: medium. New: the playbook only mentions the Tomb life cost for Omniscience hardcasts (line 23), and no rule covers defending my life total.
2. "When I am dead next turn, spend everything this turn: crack Petal into a cantrip and keep digging until I have no mana or no cards left. Passing with castable cards is a certain loss. After my Veil of Summer resolves, every spell I cast that turn is uncounterable, so that is the turn to dig for the combo." Evidence: T9 prompts 123/124. Confidence: high. It extends Brady's "waiting is only right when it is free" (line 82) and does not contradict it.
3. "Acererak is a payoff only with Aluren (or Omniscience) in play: we have no black mana, and Show and Tell on him just bounces him. When Ponder shows no Aluren, Atraxa, Omniscience or cantrip and my only creature is Acererak, shuffle, even if one of the three is Show and Tell." Evidence: T1 prompts 5-7. Confidence: low to medium. It refines line 41 ("With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle"); ask Brady whether Show and Tell alone justifies a keep.
