RESULT: WON

EMRAKUL: Opening seven had Emrakul (plus 2 Strand, Misty, Tomb, Stock Up, Veil). get_card could not find Emrakul ("not in the card pool"), so I used my own knowledge of the card. Turn 3 I cast Show and Tell (Tomb + Hedge Maze, Tropical Island spare). They Forced it, pitching Kaito. I answered with Veil of Summer, which made Show and Tell uncounterable and drew a card. I put Emrakul in and kept Atraxa in hand as Force of Will pitch fodder. Emrakul blocked and killed a Nethergoyf. On turn 4 she attacked: annihilator 6 took all their lands and creature, plus 15 flying damage (18 to 3). On turn 5 she attacked again for lethal. Their colored removal (Push, Snuff Out, Borrower, bounce) cannot target her, and Daze and Force cannot stop a Show and Tell put-in. I would keep her in the main against this opponent in place of a second Veil (or the 4th Stock Up or 4th Lotus Petal if Veil is wanted). She needs Show and Tell or Omniscience to be live, so she is a dead card in hands without those.
VEIL: Drew the one Veil in the opening seven. It won the game: cast in response to their Force of Will on Show and Tell, it made Show and Tell uncounterable and drew a card (Misty). A second Veil would also be good against this opponent (Force of Will and Daze both beaten, blue/black removal irrelevant to a Veiled board), but a Show and Tell with Emrakul on the battlefield wins on its own, so I would rather have Emrakul than a second Veil as the payoff. Emrakul and Veil together are the strongest pairing; Veil is the protection and Emrakul the payoff that only needs one resolved Show and Tell.
