# g86 review: Alurentell (A, on the draw) beat Boros Aggro (B) on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn)

## 1. Result and turning point
- A won with the Lost Mine drain on turn 6. The log shows 16 Dark Pool picks in the auto macro, and B was on exactly 16 (17, then 1 from its own Marsh Flats crack). The kill was verified: B's Swords on the first Acererak (A seq 78) exiled him (A 20 to 25). The enter trigger still ventured (A seq 80), and A cast the second Acererak from hand with the room trigger on the stack (A seq 81, after the exile). No engine problem.
- Decision 1, turn 4, A seq 25/27/30. B had tapped out for Voice on its own turn, so A cast Show and Tell (Island, Savannah, Petal) and put in Atraxa; Acererak was the only other creature and there was no Aluren. The reveal took one card of each available type (Omniscience, a second Show and Tell, Brainstorm, Petal, Misty) and set up the next turn. Correct, and there was no line to win that turn.
- Decision 2, turn 6, A seq 52 to 72. Show and Tell put in Omniscience. Aluren was not in hand yet, so "Omniscience over Aluren" was not a real choice; Acererak alone does nothing. Before the first Acererak, A used the free Brainstorm (found Aluren and Atraxa) and the free Atraxa (took the second Acererak). That backup was the card that beat B's last Swords. This departs from the playbook's "loop now, do not cantrip", but it was correct here: the dig was free, and B had one card in hand and an uncracked Marsh Flats (W).
- On B's side, the game turned on turn 5 (B seq 43/46). B already knew Omniscience and Show and Tell were in A's hand from the Atraxa reveal. B still used one Swords on the Atraxa that came in with Show and Tell, then cast Amped Raptor and tapped all three Plateaus. The Marsh Flats that B put in with A's second Show and Tell is the only reason B had W for the last Swords.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none that changed anything. The first slip was the menu at seq 56. The first "Cast Brainstorm" entry was the paid cast, so it sacrificed Lotus Petal #30; the second entry was the free Omniscience cast. It cost nothing. A second small slip was at seq 67: after taking only Acererak and Omniscience from the second Atraxa reveal, A chose Done. That left Stock Up, Petal, a land and a spare Show and Tell in the library, against the existing rule "On the combo turn, take every card Atraxa offers". It would have mattered only if a third answer had stopped the loop.
- Notes on other A choices. The keep was clear: 3 lands (2 blue), Ponder, Show and Tell and Atraxa. Leaving Veil unused was right, since it is a blank against Boros in game 1 and went back with Brainstorm. Fetching a basic Island at seq 8 was a good Wasteland-proof pick. Ponder kept Petal, Acererak, Petal (the duplicate Petal was shown as one entry), which gave the draw toward the creature. Both Petals were cast early, as the existing Spider-Woman note says. They exposed nothing, because B's main deck has no artifact removal.
- B: seq 46, casting Amped Raptor and tapping out on its own turn, when A was known to hold Omniscience and Show and Tell. The existing Boros note already says "Keep W open ... for Swords on Acererak". Skipping the Raptor would have left 2 Plateaus open for Swords on A's turn (and 3 open if B had also skipped the Swords on Atraxa). Even so, the loss was mostly structural: with one Swords left against two Acereraks under Omniscience, B could not win once Show and Tell resolved.
- B's other decisions. Voice of Victory as the put-in at seq 28 was a reasonable choice, but Marsh Flats (a fourth mana on turn 5) was close; see the flags. Putting in Marsh Flats at seq 55 was its only option and correct. Holding the Flats until A cast Aluren and then fetching an untapped Plains (seq 69) was good. Swords on Acererak with his trigger on the stack (seq 77) was correct; B flagged that one itself.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Under Omniscience without Aluren, cast the free cantrips and a free Atraxa before the first Acererak when the opponent can still answer him (open W, an uncracked fetch, cards in hand): find Aluren and a second Acererak first, then loop." Evidence: A seq 56 to 72, where the backup Acererak beat Swords. Confidence: medium. It refines "With Aluren or Omniscience in play ... loop now ... Do not cantrip, attack or take Tomb first". That rule should stay for Aluren alone, where cantrips cost mana.
2. "Under Omniscience the menu lists each spell twice: the first 'Cast X' is the paid cast (it taps lands and sacrifices Petals), the later duplicate is the free cast. Pick the later entry." Evidence: A seq 56, where Petal #30 was lost; at seq 72 A picked the later Acererak entry and kept its Petal. Confidence: high. This is new; the playbook has only general menu-discipline notes.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Harness issue worth fixing: duplicate "Cast X" labels under Omniscience (A seq 56, 62, 68, 72) carry no cost hint. Add "(free: Omniscience)" or "(free: Aluren)" to the alternative-cost entry.
- A never wrote flags/g86a.tsv (its report says "Flags: 0"). B's report misread the end ("they presumably answered/looped in response"); the log shows a second Acererak.
- No engine bugs. Venture on an exiled Acererak, the Swords life gain, and Raptor's discover into the second Raptor all behaved correctly. No land drop was skipped and nothing was cast in upkeep. A made no wasted tool calls (0 odds or sim calls).

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on game turn 6: Show and Tell for Atraxa on turn 4, then Show and Tell for Omniscience, a free dig to Aluren and a second Acererak, and 16 Dark Pool drains through B's last Swords.
TOP MISTAKE: B seq 46, tapping out for Amped Raptor (after Swords on Atraxa) while A was known to hold Omniscience and Show and Tell; only the Flats from Show and Tell gave B its last Swords.
TOP RULE: Under Omniscience, the first duplicate "Cast X" entry is the paid cast; pick the later one. Also dig with the free cantrips for a second Acererak before looping into open W.
