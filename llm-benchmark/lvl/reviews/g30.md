# g30 review: A = Alurentell (on the draw), B = Boros Aggro (on the play, mulligan to 6)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 8 (A's 4th turn): Misty -> Tropical, Show and Tell with Tundra + Tropical + Petal putting in Aluren
(B could only add Plateau, emptying its hand), then free Acererak through Lost Mine for 17+ laps, B 22 -> 0, A 8 -> 25+.
- Decider (A p17, turn 4): Show and Tell resolving, A put in **Atraxa**. Aluren was the winning choice. A could see
  "YOUR LIBRARY TOP (known): Acererak" (A itself had stacked it with Brainstorm, p8-p10) and still held Lotus Petal and
  Ponder. Line: Aluren in, Petal for U, Ponder (p28 shows Ponder castable off Petal), draw Acererak, cast it free, loop.
  B was tapped out (Karakas and Wasteland tapped for Ajani) with Parlor, Strand, Spider-Woman in hand: nothing could
  stop it. Atraxa instead found Tropical, Force, Acererak, Stock Up, but left A with no way to cast Aluren ({2}{G}{G},
  one G source), and Karakas bounced Atraxa on B's turn 5 (Atraxa is legendary). The win slipped by four turns and A
  fell from 17 to 8 life against a board that would have threatened lethal around turn 11.
- Second decider (A p65-p68, turn 8): Show and Tell for Aluren rather than Atraxa at 9 life was right and A saw it
  (A's flag p61; agree, nothing to add).

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, p8 (turn 2 Brainstorm): put back Acererak and Ponder, kept both Atraxas. Show and Tell uses one Atraxa; the second
  was a dead card, Acererak a combo piece. With Acererak in hand, p17's Aluren put-in wins with no cantrip needed.
- A, p17: Atraxa over Aluren (above), the costly one.
- A, p42 (turn 6): played Tropical Island first; City of Traitors was sacrificed unused (A's own report). With Tundra +
  City + Petal untapped, Stock Up off City + Tundra before the land drop was free value (2 of 5 cards) and kept Petal.
  The engine never lists mana abilities, so the paper line (tap City, float CC, play Tropical, Aluren off CC + Trop +
  Petal, Acererak in hand: win on turn 6) was not available here. Breaks two existing rules (City last; cantrip
  before land). A's flag p43 (Stock Up vs keep Petal after the land) only follows from this.
- B: no clear mistake. Mulligan of a one-lander on the play was standard (B's own flag). p14 (turn 3): cast Ajani rather
  than Wasteland on Tundra, A's only land after a turn-2 Brainstorm. Close: A would still have Show and Tell'd on
  turn 4 (City + Petal) but without the Petal-Ponder kill and short of mana for Aluren for several turns. p5 Karakas
  on turn 1 instead of the tapped Elegant Parlor (B's report) cost nothing in practice. B's loss was mostly A's draw
  (Show and Tell off the top on turn 8) plus no Swords drawn; with A's p17 error B was not even supposed to get there.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Before choosing what Show and Tell puts in, check whether Aluren wins right now: Acererak in hand, or a known
   Acererak on top plus a cantrip and a spare mana (Petal) to draw it. If yes, put in Aluren. Atraxa only digs, and
   against Karakas (Boros, Death and Taxes) it is bounced on their next turn because it is legendary."
   Evidence: A p17 (turn 4, lost a turn-4 win); A p68 (turn 8, got it right). Confidence: high. Sharpens, does not
   contradict, Brady's "Show and Tell versus Aluren: decide by how much mana I have and which other combo pieces are in
   my hand". Not covered by "Put Omniscience in ... when I have Atraxa, Aluren or Acererak in hand" (no Omniscience).
2. "Brainstorm put-back: keep Acererak and put back a second Atraxa first. Show and Tell uses one Atraxa; Acererak is
   the piece that turns Aluren (in play or Show and Tell'd) into a win."
   Evidence: A p8-p9. Confidence: medium-high. New; consistent with the existing put-back rules (Petal not land; not
   Veil or Show and Tell for Bauble).
3. "With City of Traitors in play, spend its mana before the land drop (Stock Up, Ponder, Petal-free Show and Tell):
   the engine cannot float mana, so a land played first sacrifices City for nothing."
   Evidence: A p42 (turn 6; City lost, Stock Up never cast, Atraxa discarded at cleanup p45). Confidence: high. Partly
   duplicates "City of Traitors is sacrificed as soon as I play another land (a31). Play it as the last land" and
   Brady's "cast cantrips ... before playing the land"; propose it as an addendum to the City line.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes (in place of UR Cutter notes)
1. "Against a blue deck on one land after a turn-2 cantrip, Wasteland its only dual on your turn 2 instead of a
   2-drop: Show and Tell and Aluren need 3 and 4 mana, and the tempo cost is one creature a turn later."
   Evidence: B p14 (Tundra was A's only land; A held City, Misty, Petal). Confidence: low-medium (A's City + Petal
   still cast Show and Tell on turn 4; it mainly removes the Petal-Ponder kill and delays Aluren).
2. "Save Karakas for Atraxa (legendary; bounce it on your turn before it blocks) and play the tapped land on a turn
   with no 1-drop. Karakas does nothing against the Acererak loop (it returns itself anyway)."
   Evidence: B p5 (Karakas turn 1, Parlor held to turn 7), B p31 (bounce was right). Confidence: medium.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Engine limitation, not a bug: menus never offer mana abilities (Karakas lists only "ability 1"), so floating mana
  before a land drop (City of Traitors, A p42) is impossible. In paper A wins on turn 6 there. Worth an RFC or a
  playbook note; it changes City lines.
- A's auto macro stopped three times on pick budget (after p75, p77, p79; 676 auto picks). Lost Mine laps end in
  Temple of Dumathoin (draw 1): library 43 -> 26 in one loop. Fine at 22 life; against lifegain or a higher total
  count laps against library size.
- A p30: discarded Ponder to hand size on turn 4; it was the card that won that turn under the Aluren line.
- Tools: A none; B one sim call (mulligan). Neither report spotted the turn-4 kill.
- Land drops: none skipped by either side. Upkeep cantrips: none. Menu slips: none. Force of Will correctly held
  through Spider-Woman (A p39) and Guide of Souls (A p51).
- Engine: no bug seen. Checked: Phlage from Show and Tell dealt 3 / gained 3 and was sacrificed (B p19-p20); Karakas
  bounce of a legendary creature; Show and Tell paid with Tundra + Tropical + Petal (A p65, no overpay); Aluren let A
  cast Acererak free; life totals consistent (A 20-17-14-9-8).
