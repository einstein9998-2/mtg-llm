# Review g75: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, one-land keep on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 5 (A's 3rd turn). Aluren resolved through a Force war, the free Acererak survived Daze thanks to a spare Petal, and Lost Mine drained B from 17 to 0. Both reports agree on the result.
- Turn 3 (A 20, flagged by A): exactly 4 mana (Island, Savannah, 2 Petals in hand), Force in hand but no blue pitch (Omniscience on top). B's only land was a tapped Volcanic, and B was holding 2 Daze. Aluren then would have been Dazed with no way to pay, costing A its only Aluren. Waiting was right and follows "If Daze is live and I cannot keep a spare, wait a turn". Waiting cost nothing: B was on one land and only cantripped.
- Turn 5, decisive (B 48-49): A cast Petal, Petal, then Aluren with one Petal spare. B Forced Aluren pitching Daze, although both Volcanics were tapped and Brainstorm and Preordain could not be cast that turn. Pitching a cantrip keeps both Dazes, and two Dazes beat one spare Petal. After A Forces back, Daze on A's Force (A pays with the Petal), then the second Daze on A's Force (A cannot pay), so Aluren is countered. Alternatively, let Aluren resolve and Daze the Acererak twice. Either line stops the turn-5 kill and leaves A with Acererak alone (or Aluren alone).
- The loop (log): 134 picks = the prompt-59 Lost Mine choice + 133 macro picks. Each lap took 8 picks and 4 Acererak casts: Lost Mine, cast, Goblin Lair, cast, Dark Pool, one pass (resolves only the drain), cast (Temple, no choice), cast. There were exactly 17 Dark Pool drains (17 laps, 17 Goblin Lairs) and 67 casts (prompt 55 + 66 macro); the last lap stopped after its drain (16x8+6 = 134). That is one point per lap, as designed. A's 280-pick estimate was simply wrong (about 16 picks per life point); the macro was not faster than the technique allows. It is not 15 laps: 17 life needs 17 laps. No other damage was dealt.
