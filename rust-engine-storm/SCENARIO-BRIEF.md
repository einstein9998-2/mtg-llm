## New action spellings
SCHEMA.md has no spelling for: bargaining, suspending, choosing a card from the sideboard, choosing a card to imprint, choosing which exiled card to cast free. Follow the Chant precedent (direct keys on the action, e.g. `bargained: true|false` on `{t: cast, ...}`, `{t: suspend, card: "@will"}`, and for choices use the schema's existing pick/choice mechanism for searches). Write each spelling you invent in your notes so the runner adapter can be taught.

## Cover, for each card
About 4 to 8 scenarios per card (fewer for plain cards): the card's normal effect, edge and negative cases (what it must NOT do), interaction with the cards in the Storm deck and the Alurentell deck (Aluren, Omniscience, Force of Will, Force of Negation, Show and Tell, Lotus Petal, Acererak, Atraxa, Brainstorm, Ponder, Stock Up, Carpet of Flowers, Prismatic Ending, Veil of Summer, Orim's Chant, Consign to Memory, Faerie Macabre), and cost, timing, stack and priority details. Each scenario must assert something an implementation could get wrong.

## Engine simplifications: do NOT write scenarios that depend on these (documented divergences)
- Hexing Squelcher: "Other creatures you control have 'Ward—Pay 2 life'" is modeled as a triggered ability of the Squelcher itself ("whenever a creature you control other than this becomes the target of a spell or ability an opponent controls, counter it unless its controller pays 2 life"). Test outcomes (a targeting spell or ability is countered unless 2 life is paid), not Stifle on the ward trigger of another creature, and not Squelcher leaving the battlefield in the same moment as the targeting.
- Giant's Boulder: {1} of its mana ability is paid from mana already in your pool; which floating mana is spent is chosen by the engine, not by the player. Do not write scenarios that depend on which colour is spent.
- Burning Wish: "outside the game" is the player's own sideboard (15 cards in a real game). Scenarios give a sideboard explicitly. Cards in the sideboard are hidden from the opponent.
- Urza's Saga: the abilities chapters I and II give are modeled as abilities that exist once that chapter ability has resolved (not before). Test the observable behavior.
- Echo of Eons and Runehorn Hellkite shuffle libraries: do not assert which cards are drawn; assert counts, zones and that the graveyards and hands were emptied first.
- Gaea's Will: the cast at the end of suspend is a cast without paying its mana cost (CR 702.62); the engine has no way to decline it.

## Oracle text (Scryfall data via Savecraft, 2026-10-06; Giant's Boulder from three store pages, not Scryfall: mark its quotes `verified: false`)
**Burning Wish** {1}{R} Sorcery: You may reveal a sorcery card you own from outside the game and put it into your hand. Exile Burning Wish.
**Gamble** {R} Sorcery: Search your library for a card, put that card into your hand, discard a card at random, then shuffle.
**Beseech the Mirror** {1}{B}{B}{B} Sorcery: Bargain (You may sacrifice an artifact, enchantment, or token as you cast this spell.) Search your library for a card, exile it face down, then shuffle. If this spell was bargained, you may cast the exiled card without paying its mana cost if that spell's mana value is 4 or less. Put the exiled card into your hand if it wasn't cast this way.
**Tendrils of Agony** {2}{B}{B} Sorcery: Target player loses 2 life and you gain 2 life. Storm (When you cast this spell, copy it for each spell cast before it this turn. You may choose new targets for the copies.)
**Empty the Warrens** {3}{R} Sorcery: Create two 1/1 red Goblin creature tokens. Storm (When you cast this spell, copy it for each spell cast before it this turn.)
**Peer into the Abyss** {4}{B}{B}{B} Sorcery: Target player draws cards equal to half the number of cards in their library and loses half their life. Round up each time.
**Boomerang Basics** {U} Sorcery — Lesson: Return target nonland permanent to its owner's hand. If you controlled that permanent, draw a card.
**Echo of Eons** {4}{U}{U} Sorcery: Each player shuffles their hand and graveyard into their library, then draws seven cards. Flashback {2}{U} (You may cast this card from your graveyard for its flashback cost. Then exile it.)
**Chrome Mox** {0} Artifact: Imprint — When this artifact enters, you may exile a nonartifact, nonland card from your hand. / {T}: Add one mana of any of the exiled card's colors.
**Mox Opal** {0} Legendary Artifact: Metalcraft — {T}: Add one mana of any color. Activate only if you control three or more artifacts.
**Giant's Boulder** {1} Artifact: When this artifact enters, scry 2. / {1}, {T}: Add one mana of any color. / {7}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact: Destroy target permanent.

**Haywire Mite** {1} Artifact Creature — Insect 1/1: When this creature dies, you gain 2 life. / {G}, Sacrifice this creature: Exile target noncreature artifact or noncreature enchantment.
**Taiga** Land — Mountain Forest: ({T}: Add {R} or {G}.)
**Runehorn Hellkite** {5}{R} Creature — Dragon 5/5: Flying / {5}{R}, Exile this card from your graveyard: Each player discards their hand, then draws seven cards.
