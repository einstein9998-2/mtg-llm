# g78 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B), A won on game turn 8

## 1. Result and turning point
- A won on game turn 8 (A's 4th turn, on the draw): Show and Tell for Omniscience (A #70, #72), free Atraxa (#74; took Aluren, Force, Show and Tell, Petal, Hedge Maze at #77-81), free Aluren (#82), Acererak into Lost Mine; 21 Dark Pool drains took B from 21 to 0 (log). A's report agrees with B's.
- A's life went 20, 19 (Ocelot, t3), 18 (Misty), 17 (Force), 13 (4 combat, t5), 4 (9 combat, t7). A never had a creature that could block: the only Atraxa entered tapped under Spider-Woman (#44) and was Eroded on B's turn before combat (B #57-58). So no chump block was ever possible.
- The deciding play was A's Force of Will (pitch Brainstorm) on the Goblin Bombardment that Amped Raptor discovered (A #35, flagged by A). Without it B wins on t7: 9 combat damage plus about 9 creatures to sacrifice against 13 life, or Bombardment in response to the t8 Show and Tell at 4 life. Passing on Spider-Woman (#14) and on the Raptor itself (#33) to keep that Force was right.
- The first Show and Tell (#41-49) was also right, even though Atraxa entered tapped and died: the reveal gave Omniscience, Petal, Ponder, Atraxa and Misty. That made t8 a win at any life total. Waiting instead would have lost to the clock.
- The second Show and Tell for Omniscience versus a hardcast Aluren was not a real choice: A had no Aluren until the free Atraxa found it, and with 4 mana Show and Tell for Omniscience was the only line that won. A skipped the t8 land drop (Tomb costs 2 at 4 life, Misty 1): correct under "Life is mana".

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: no costly mistake. The small one is the t2 Brainstorm (#8) with no fetch to shuffle. It put back Veil and City, and A drew the known City on t4. Ponder (it can shuffle) with Brainstorm saved for t4, when Misty was played, was better; the existing rule "weave the fetch" already covers it. A's report calls the t6 Petal (#50) wasted. It was not: the playbook says to cast Petals a turn early under Spider-Woman, the Petal was untapped on t8, and the Veil discarded at cleanup is a blank against Boros. Boseiju over Ancient Tomb on t6 (#40) saved 2 life and lost only a spare mana with nothing to cast; that was correct.
- B: Erode on a tapped, summoning-sick Atraxa (B #57) when Karakas was untapped and can bounce a legendary Atraxa for free at instant speed. B knew A held Omniscience and a second Atraxa (the reveal was shown). Holding Erode for the Acererak trigger was the only possible out. It was probably not enough, because A's free Atraxa found Force of Will, so the loss was mostly variance plus the Forced Bombardment.
- B's curve was close to the best possible: Ocelot t1, Spider-Woman t3 (it made the Atraxa tapped and the Petal slow), Raptor t5, Sand Scout through Show and Tell. Its attacks were the maximum: 1, 4, 9; the Sand Warrior token was summoning sick. B could not have won a turn earlier once Bombardment was countered. Holding back creatures would gain nothing: B had no Swords to hold (its hand on t7 was Plateau and Erode), and Ascend plus Ocelot needed the board. Parlor (tapped) over Plateau on t5 (B #29) cost nothing that turn.
- Correction to the task premise: Sand Scout was B's put-in (B #43, the only creature in hand), not Spider-Woman; Spider-Woman had been cast on t3.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- (medium) "Against Boros, Force the Goblin Bombardment (cast or found by Raptor's discover) when their creature count plus their power on board reaches your life within a turn. It is the only card that kills you in response to Show and Tell; keep the Force through Spider-Woman and the creatures for it." Evidence: A #14, #33, #35. This extends "Life is mana" (Bombardment = creature count) and "Spend Force of Will on what breaks the loop" without contradicting them.
- (low) "Under Spider-Woman, a Show-and-Tell Atraxa is a card-draw spell, not a blocker: take Omniscience or a second Show and Tell, plus Petal, and plan to win next turn at whatever life you have left." Evidence: #44-49 and the t7 attack for 9. This only refines the existing Spider-Woman rule; skip it if Brady thinks it is obvious.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes

Once Atraxa has revealed Omniscience, their next turn is the kill turn, and only removal held for Acererak, or Bombardment, matters." Evidence: B #57. This partly contradicts the signed-off note "Swords the Show-and-Tell Atraxa before attacking"; add "unless Karakas can bounce her".
- (low) "Show and Tell also allows a land: put your spare land in for free." Evidence: B #73 (B chose Done holding Plateau).

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Not a harness bug: Show and Tell lets each player put in "an artifact, creature, enchantment, or land card", so listing Plateau (B #43, #73) is legal. B's report is wrong on this point.
- A #60: the Erode "may search for a basic" prompt arrived after Atraxa was already in the graveyard (the stack showed "targeting (gone)"). "Yes" only shuffled, because A's only basic, Island, was already on the battlefield. This is display ordering, not a bug (A was unsure).
- Menus list identical cards twice (Cast Ponder x2, Cast Acererak x2 at #74, #85); this is harmless. The 431-pick Lost Mine macro worked as Brady specified. No upkeep cantrips; the only skipped land drop was A's on t8, which was deliberate.
