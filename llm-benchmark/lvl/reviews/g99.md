# Review g99: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 9 (A's 5th turn): City of Traitors, then Aluren with B tapped out, a free Acererak and the Lost Mine macro (141 picks). B was at 18 life, not 7: it lost 1 to Tarn and 1 to its Force. 7 was A's life. The log has exactly 18 Dark Pool drains.
- Mana on the combo turn (A prompt 119): Island, Tropical, Hedge Maze and City (2) made 5 mana, with GG from Tropical and Maze. The Maze came from the Misty cracked at B's end step (A prompt 112). Without it A had one green source and no Aluren. Playing City as the last land, with Misty kept in hand, was correct.
- Decisive exchange, turn 8:
  - B prompt 94: B attacked with DRC (A down to 8), then tapped both Volcanics in Main 2 for a delve Murktide. Main 2 was fine because Murktide was summoning sick anyway. Casting it at all broke the existing note: do not tap out on your own turn once they can reach 4 mana.
  - A prompt 101: A Forced it, pitching its 2nd Force and keeping Brainstorm.
  - B prompt 104/106: B Forced back, pitching Daze, and was left with Force of Will plus 2 Cori-Steel Cutters. Cutters are red, so B had nothing blue to pitch and the last Force was dead. That is why B had no legal action and auto-passed until it lost. It was not an engine bug and not a hidden combo, as B's report suspected.
  - If B had let Murktide be countered, it would have kept Force (with Daze to pitch) plus Daze against A, who had no Force left. A's Aluren on turn 9 would almost surely have been countered.
- A's Force on Murktide was right. Without it, DRC (3) plus Murktide (6) was lethal on A's 8 life. Saving the Force for Aluren meant one Force against B's possible 2 Force + Daze. Forcing on B's turn made B either lose the 6/6 or spend its counters. A's report ("a 2-for-1 that bought nothing") is wrong: it bought the win.
- Pitch choice: Force over Brainstorm was correct. A Force kept with only Brainstorm to pitch would have been one live card either way, and Brainstorm was the castable one.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- "If they tap out on their turn for a threat that kills you before your combo turn after next, Force it: either it dies or they spend their counters on their own turn, and your combo turn is clear." Evidence: A prompt 101, B prompts 104-108. Medium confidence. New: the existing rule only says "spend Force on what breaks the loop... not on face burn when tapped out".
- "With only fetches as lands against a Wasteland deck, fetch the basic at their end step; Hedge Maze is the land they will Wasteland." Evidence: A prompt 27, B prompt 47. Low confidence. It conflicts with the LvL rule "Hedge Maze: at their end step fetch it over a dual or basic" and sharpens the a36 rule "fetch basics... keep nonbasics for later". Brady should say which rule wins.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
- "Do not Force back to save your own threat if it leaves your last Force with no blue card to pitch. Against combo, a countered Murktide costs less than an Aluren you cannot answer." Evidence: B prompt 104/106. High confidence. It extends "keep one blue pitch card per Force of Will".
