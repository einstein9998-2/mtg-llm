RESULT: WON
TURNS: 4
PROTECT: My deck had two Orim's Chant. I got one via a Ponder on turn 1 (put on top, drawn turn 3). It was awkward for three turns: I was taking 5 to 9 a turn from Moonshadows, Bowmasters and Kaito, and the white mana (Tundra, Savannah) was needed to keep it as a kicked time walk on their turn, which I never could afford. On turn 4, at 3 life with Show and Tell resolved and Omniscience in play, I cast Chant for free targeting the opponent (in response to their Bowmasters put-in trigger). It resolved. They could not cast Force of Will, Daze or removal for the whole turn, and I ran the Acererak/Lost Mine loop unopposed (about 20 laps, opponent 17 to 0).
Would I rather have had Veil of Summer in those slots? No. Veil only protects Show and Tell from blue counters and does not stop the Bowmasters put-in or the Temple draw pings. Chant locked the whole loop turn and was castable for free under Omniscience; it was the better card here.
- Possible mistake (turn 2 to 3): I kept a slow 6 (two fetches, Ponder, Stock Up, Omniscience, Acererak after a zero-land 7) and had no castable play on turn 2, so I took about 14 damage before turn 4. Two Moonshadows grew from their fetches and Brainstorms, and Kaito ninjutsu on an unblocked attacker hit for a lot. I won only because Ponder found Show and Tell on the one turn they were nearly tapped out (one Underground Sea open).
- Surprise: Kaito ninjutsu returned Bowmasters to hand, and Show and Tell gave them Bowmasters back as their put-in (ping 3 to 2, plus a ping on every Temple draw). I avoided Ancient Tomb that turn, because the ping would have killed me at 1 life; each Dark Pool gain offset each Temple-draw ping, so life stayed at 2 to 3 the whole loop.
- Engine: no bug found. Under Omniscience the loop macro worked as written (about 354 auto picks).
