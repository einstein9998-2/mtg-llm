# Alurentell sideboard plans, for Brady's 75 of 2026-10-06 (decks/alurentell.txt).

# 2 Orim's Chant, 2 Prismatic Ending, 2 Veil of Summer.
#
# [Brady] = locked by Brady in the project chat, 2026-10-06. [draft] = my adaptation, not reviewed.

vs dimir-tempo

-2 Omniscience
+2 Veil of Summer
+4 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Orim's Chant

+2 Faerie Macabre
+2 Veil of Summer
+1 Force of Negation
