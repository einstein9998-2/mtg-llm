# g70 review: A = Alurentell (on the draw), B = Boros Aggro (on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 11 (B's 6th turn): Cat 2 + Spider-Woman 2 + Sand Scout 2 + Ajani 1 = 7 into A at 6. A saw about 28 distinct cards (opening 7, draws, two Stock Ups, Ponder, two Brainstorms, two surveils) and none of its 8 enablers (4 Show and Tell, 4 Aluren). That happens about 0.4% of the time (C(52,28)/C(60,28)); it would be about 3% with 20 cards. No single decision decided the game. It was variance on a keep that needed an enabler.
- **Life:** 20 -> 18 (Tomb, Stock Up T2) -> 17 (Misty T4) -> 15 (Tomb, Stock Up T6) -> 7 (combat) -> 6 (Strand at B's T9 end step). Both Tomb payments bought a Stock Up, the only way to find an out. The Strand crack (seq 53) did not matter: 7 power was lethal at 7 as well. If A had paid no life at all, it would have lived to turn 12 at 5 life with about 8/37 live draws. That is not worth giving up three digs for.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A keep (seq 2, self-flagged; Brady to rule).** Misty, Tomb, Petal, Ponder, Veil, Atraxa, Acererak. It has two payoffs but one cantrip, no enabler, a Veil that is blank against Boros, and a Tomb that Wasteland hits. Odds of an enabler: about 39% in the next 3 cards and about 65% in 6 (Ponder plus draws by turn 4), against a deck that kills on turns 9-11. Brady's exception ("keep a one-lander if it has the combo") does not cover this hand: it has the payoffs, not the combo. My read is a close mulligan, not a clear mistake.
- **A T2 (seq 4-6): Tomb, Petal, Stock Up on its first turn.** Fine. Boros plays 4 Wasteland and the note says it hits Tomb, so using the Tomb at once was the best value it could get (B used Wasteland on it the next turn, B seq 12-14).
- **A Stock Up picks (seq 7-9): Stock Up, then Petal; bottomed Hedge Maze, Misty, Misty.** Taking the second Stock Up first is right (batch 2: "take the strong cantrip first"). Petal over a land is close. With Tomb exposed and only Misty in hand, a Misty (it fetches a basic, so Wasteland cannot touch it) or Maze gives permanent mana. But Petal paid for the T6 Stock Up, and the T4 Misty crack shuffled the three bottomed lands back in (Misty #20 showed up again in the Ponder). Net cost: about zero. The existing rule "Stock Up with two or fewer lands and none in hand: take a land first" does not apply, because Misty was in hand.
- **A Ponder (seq 19-21): kept Brainstorm, Strand, Misty, no shuffle.** Correct. A had one land in play, none in hand, and the look held two lands plus a cantrip. The existing rule: "A lone land is not enough unless you are stuck on one land with none in hand (then shuffle any landless look)". This look had lands, so keep.
- **A T6 (seq 26-35): Brainstorm (Island), put back Veil and Veil, then Tomb, Petal, Stock Up**, which looked at the two Veils. That wasted 2 of the 5 looks, but Brainstorm was what found the Tomb that paid for Stock Up. Doing it this way saw 6 fresh cards this turn; the alternative (Strand, Petal, Stock Up) saw 5. Fine. T8 Brainstorm put back a duplicate Acererak and Strand, then A held Strand to shuffle them away at B's end step (self-flagged seq 43). Both follow existing rules.
- **B: no mistakes.** The two-land keep (Plains, Karakas, Ajani, Sand Scout, Swords, Erode, Phlage) is a normal keep on the play. Every card costs 2 or less except Phlage, and both lands make W. On T3 (seq 12-14) B played the newly drawn Wasteland over Karakas and used it on A's only land (Tomb) right away, giving up an on-curve Ajani. The existing note covers this ("Hit Ancient Tomb ... Use it before they untap"), and it held A to one land for two turns. Spider-Woman on T7, then Sand Scout on T9: correct order. On T7 A had 2 lands to B's 2, so Scout's land search would not have triggered. On T9 A had 3 lands, and Scout fetched Lazotep Quarry (the only Desert offered, seq 49). Holding Swords and Erode was right with Acererak in A's deck.

- No engine bug. Ponder drawing as part of resolution (A's report) is correct card text, not a quirk. With Lazotep Quarry in play, B's menu lists 20-25 sac-for-mana entries (seq 58-62). That is harmless, but it is noisy.
