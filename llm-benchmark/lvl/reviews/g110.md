## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 7 (B's 4th turn), A at 7 life, B at 33. A never cast a spell after Ponder (turn 2).
- Decided at the keep (A P2): Island, Tropical Island, 2 Ancient Tomb, 2 Lotus Petal, Ponder on the draw. No payoff, no enabler, one cantrip: it needs three specific draws (Show and Tell or Aluren plus a creature) from Ponder and three natural draws. A London mulligan to 6 was the alternative; any six with a cantrip plus one combo piece is better. - A's life path: 20, 19 (Guide attack T3), 16 (Phlage), 8 (combat), 7 (Strand crack). Lethal T7: five attackers (3/4 flier, 3/3 flier, 1/2, two 1/1 cats) = 9 into 7; energy 2, so no pump; Bombardment was never activated. A had no out: no creature in hand, Force could only stop Bombardment (not needed), and an Atraxa via Show and Tell would also have faced Karakas (played that turn).

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: the keep (P2), already self-flagged. The rest was reasonable given the hand: Ponder kept Show and Tell on top (playbook: keep a known Show and Tell without an enabler in hand); Strand played T4 as a Wasteland decoy and cracked at B's T5 end step, which also shuffled away the known third Petal before the T6 draw; binning Aluren to surveil (P51) was right with no creature in hand and Aluren symmetric against a creature deck (self-flagged). Not Forcing Phlage (P28) was defensible: the only pitch was Show and Tell, and Force would have saved about 5 (3 burn plus one EEE pump) but B still had 7 power plus 5 Bombardment sacrifices against about 11 life on T7.
- B: none. Held Wasteland correctly (A's only nonbasic targets were an uncracked Strand, then Hedge Maze/Trop when lethal was already on board). Played Arid Mesa for Plateau on T5 to cast Phlage (needed R).

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- R1: "A seven that is only mana plus one cantrip (lands, Petals, Ponder; no Show and Tell, Aluren or creature) is a mulligan, most of all against Boros. Extra mana is not a route to a payoff." Evidence: g110 P2 (zero payoff in 15 cards, dead on T7). Confidence: medium-high. Mostly extends "A keep needs a route to a payoff. A seven whose only spells are Show and Tell and Acererak, or one land plus Ponder with no creature, is a mulligan" and "Hands that need two more cards (a creature plus an enabler) are the ones that lose": this hand needs three, and the existing text names only the one-land case, so the bot read it as not covering a four-lander. Propose as a one-clause extension, not a new rule.
- Otherwise none.
