# Review g62: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn), looping Acererak through Lost Mine from 19 in one macro run. B was at 19 with Voice, Spider-Woman and 4 cards.
- Turn 3 (A 12): A cast Brainstorm in response to B's Voice of Victory. That was the last window, because a resolved Voice stops A's spells on B's turns. It found Ancient Tomb, Atraxa and Lotus Petal.
- Turn 4 (A 16-27): A played Tomb and cast Show and Tell with Island + Tomb while B was tapped out, kept the Petal in hand, and put in Atraxa. The reveal took Savannah, Acererak, Aluren, Petal and Stock Up. A then cast both Petals at once into Spider-Woman's enters-tapped effect. That Petal-saving line is the only reason Aluren was castable after B's Wasteland on Tomb: Island + Savannah + 2 Petals = exactly {2}{G}{G}.
- Turn 6 (A 43, 46, 52-53; B 49): A tapped out for Aluren and cast a free Acererak. B cast Erode on the enter trigger and A cast Force of Will on it, pitching the Karakas-bounced Atraxa. Verdict: A played it cleanly, and B had no out. B needed a second answer to Acererak, or an answer A could not Force, and the 7 cards it saw had one Erode.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none found. The keep (5 lands, Ponder, Show and Tell; A's own flag 2) had blue, a cantrip and an engine, so it was close but defensible on the draw. Playing Island on turn 2 kept a basic away from Wasteland. Holding Brainstorm over Ponder on turn 2 was fine because Voice's cast gave a response window (close, flagged). On turn 4, Ponder first (Island, then Tomb + Petal for Show and Tell) would have spent the Petal and lost the turn-6 Aluren. Reveal picks: Savannah is the untapped green source, and Acererak was the right creature over Atraxa 2. Veil is a blank vs Boros and A would have discarded it at cleanup anyway, since A already discarded Tundra. On turn 6, waiting gave B another unanswerable turn under Voice plus a clock of about 5 a turn. Tapping out with Force + Atraxa as backup was right (A's flag 43). Atraxa was the correct pitch: it was dead without Show and Tell, and Ponder and Stock Up could matter if the loop broke.
- B: no real mistake. Wasteland on Tomb (B 31) was right: Wasteland on Island leaves Tomb + Savannah + 2 Petals, so A has a spare mana. Karakas on Atraxa (B 34) was better than Erode on Atraxa. The bounce costs no card and Atraxa cannot come back without another Show and Tell, while Karakas cannot stop Acererak (he bounces himself anyway). So the only card that could fight the loop was Erode, and B kept it. Erode on the turn-6 trigger (B 49) was forced and on time ("kill the first Acererak on its enter trigger"). The idea in the task, using Erode on Atraxa while Voice made it Force-proof, would have meant a free loop on turn 6. Minor misses that cost nothing here: Arid Mesa instead of Wasteland as the turn-1 land (the note says Wasteland when you have no 1-drop; A only had an Island to hit), and Spider-Woman as the Show and Tell put-in. Its tap effect was dodged by casting the Petals early. Putting in Wasteland would have saved the turn-5 land drop for Lazotep Quarry (flagged close).

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
none. A's choices already follow existing rules: "Spider-Woman makes Petals ... enter tapped (cast Petals a turn early)", "Voice of Victory stops your spells on their turn", "Spend Force of Will on what breaks the loop", and the Atraxa-reveal land rule. Two things the game confirms, not new rules: keep Petals in hand rather than spend them to cantrip on the Show and Tell turn when Wasteland can still cut your Aluren mana; and against Voice, the response to Voice's own cast is your last instant window on their turns.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
1. "With Karakas, bounce the Show-and-Tell Atraxa on your own turn and keep Swords or Erode for Acererak's enter trigger. Atraxa cannot return without another Show and Tell, and removal spent on her leaves the loop free." Evidence: turn 5, B prompts 33-34, and turn 6, B 49. Confidence: medium-high. This refines the existing note "Swords the Show-and-Tell Atraxa before attacking ... count your removal against their Force": when Karakas is available, use it and not a removal spell.
2. "Against Alurentell with Force of Will, one removal spell for Acererak is not enough. When you know Aluren and Acererak are in their hand (Atraxa reveal), the turn before is for cutting their mana below Aluren's {2}{G}{G}. If that is impossible, deploy creatures that make their loop turn need more than one answer." Evidence: B 29-41 (A had exactly 4 mana after Wasteland). Confidence: low. It is mostly a statement that B was beaten.
