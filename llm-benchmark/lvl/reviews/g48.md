# g48 review: Alurentell (A, on the draw) beat Boros Aggro (B, on the play), game turn 6

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 6 (its 3rd turn) at 8 life, B at 29. B tapped out on its own turn 5 (B p24-26: Quarry, Guide, Raptor discovering Ocelot Pride), so its lands stayed tapped through A's turn. A p34: Tomb, Show and Tell; Omniscience in (p36), B put in Phlage (3 to A, A 11 -> 8). Free Stock Up x2 (p41-50) found Aluren, free Aluren (p53), Acererak (p55), Lost Mine macro with triggers stacked killed from 29.
The game was decided by A's draw (S&T, Stock Up into Omniscience, Acererak off the top on turn 6) plus B having no Wasteland and no Bombardment. B could not race: A was at 13 entering turn 6, out of reach of a turn-5 board.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none. Keep (p2: Trop, 2 Tomb, Ponder, Stock Up, FoW, S&T) is a clear keep: Show and Tell plus Stock Up and Ponder is a route to a payoff, and Brady calls Stock Up solid in a blind three-land hand. Risk: Tropical is the only blue and green source against 4 Wasteland; it was not punished. Turn 4 Stock Up (p16) was the only play (raw S&T without a payoff breaks a playbook rule); taking Omniscience + Stock Up and discarding Veil (blank vs Boros) were right.
- A's ~5 combat damage (plus 4 Tomb, 3 Phlage: 20 -> 8) was unavoidable: no blocker or removal in hand, and no faster line existed (with Petal kept on top at p9 turn 4 still had 4 mana, not enough for Stock Up plus S&T). Life stayed above B's reach (no Bombardment, everything tapped or sick).
- A deviated from the playbook at p41: with Omniscience and Acererak it cantripped first instead of looping now (triggers resolving: library 47 vs 28 life was enough). Free and harmless here, but casting Aluren gave B free MV<=3 creature casts (B used it for Voice #2, p57). See the rule question below.
- B: none that mattered. Mountain + Voice on turn 3 follows the note; turn 5 ordering was correct (Guide trigger resolved first, 3 energy, discover 3 hit Ocelot). Phlage into Show and Tell (p37) was right (Voice does nothing on A's turn), aimed at face. Holding W for Erode instead of Guide (Raptor only, Plateau up) would have cost Guide, the 3/5 flying pump and about 3 damage, and lost anyway to two free Force of Will under Omniscience. Loss = matchup and draw variance.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- (low) "Under Omniscience without Aluren, free cantrips that dig for Aluren are fine when nothing they hold is untapped, so you can loop with triggers stacked. Against Boros prefer the Omniscience-only loop when your library exceeds their life: Aluren lets them cast Raptor (discover Swords/Erode) and Phlage for free." Evidence: p41-p53. Contradicts/refines "With Aluren or Omniscience in play ... loop now ... Do not cantrip, attack or take Tomb first." and "Aluren is symmetric". Question for Brady rather than a new rule.
- Nothing else new. The S&T window when Boros taps out on its own turn is the existing "When the opponent taps out on its own turn its lands stay tapped through my turn" (UR section), which holds against Boros too.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
- (low-medium) "On your third turn with three lands and no Wasteland, develop (Guide, then Raptor with Guide's trigger resolving first) rather than hold one W for Erode or Swords; one removal does not beat Show and Tell into Omniscience with free Force. Start holding W once the clock is on board." Evidence: B p24-26. Qualifies "Keep W open (or Quarry plus a creature) for Swords on Acererak with his trigger on the stack".
- (medium) "Show and Tell: put in Phlage (3 to face, 3 life) over Voice of Victory or any 2-drop; Voice stops spells only on your turn." Evidence: B p37. New.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- A sent two picks in one shell line once (report); the SEQ check rejected the second. Still breaks "one pick per command".
- Lost Mine macro: 458 auto picks in 8 chunks of 60 (B at 28-29 from Guide and Ocelot lifelink); worked, Dark Pool also gained A life (to 34).
- Possible engine bug, B p32: Ocelot Pride's end-step trigger resolved first (Voice sacrifice ordered below it); the Cat made 10 permanents (3 lands, Voice, Guide, Raptor, Ocelot, 2 Warriors, Cat), yet no token copies were made and city's blessing appeared only afterwards. Ascend on a permanent is static (702.131b), so copies of the Cat and both Warriors look owed. Verify.
- A p55 lists "Cast Acererak" twice (Omniscience vs Aluren cost); Ponder shuffle shows as "May / Do it?" (yes = shuffle). B got no prompt during the loop after p57 (no castable card: Erode, no W), as expected.
- No land drops skipped (A turns 2/4/6, B turns 1/3/5), no upkeep cantrips.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on game turn 6 on the draw: S&T for Omniscience into tapped-out Boros, free Stock Ups found Aluren, Lost Mine loop killed from 29.
TOP MISTAKE: none decisive; A's p41 cantrip-first under Omniscience broke the "loop now" rule harmlessly; B's tap-out was correct.
TOP RULE: Boros puts Phlage in with Show and Tell; Alurentell question for Brady: Omniscience-only loop vs digging for Aluren against Boros.
