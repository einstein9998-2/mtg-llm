# Review g27: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 10 (B's 5th turn): Murktide Regent 7/7 + Dragon's Rage Channeler 3/3 attacked for 10, then Lightning Bolt for the last 2 (B prompts 75-77). A was stuck on one land (Tropical Island) from turn 4 on.
- Turn 3 (A prompts 21-25): A played Ancient Tomb, then Lotus Petal and Aluren, tapping out, with Acererak in hand. B (6 cards, only a tapped Thundering Falls) cast Force of Will pitching Murktide (B 26).
- Turn 4 (B 27-31): B played Wasteland instead of one of its two Volcanic Islands and destroyed Ancient Tomb at once. After that A saw about six new cards (Brainstorm, Ponder, Aluren, Petal, Veil, Stock Up) and none was a land, about a 6% draw from roughly 18 lands in 50 cards. - Verdict: mostly variance after a defensible jam. A's alternative on turn 3 was Stock Up with Tropical + Tomb, keeping Petal. In hindsight that sees Brainstorm, Ponder, Aluren, Petal, Veil (the next five cards, no shuffle happened), so still no land, and B Wastes the Tomb either way. The one-land six and B's Force + Wasteland draw decided the game more than any single pick.

- B, turn 10, prompt 63: B played Volcanic Island, then Brainstorm found Wasteland with no land drop left (B's report notes this). Brainstorm first, then Wasteland the last Tropical. It was harmless because that turn was lethal.
- B's other choices were good. It did not Force the Petal (24), Forced Aluren (26), attacked with the 3/3 DRC before delving it out of delirium (54-55), and its delve maximized Murktide's counters (four instants or sorceries, 7/7).

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Daze needs any land with the Island type, tapped or untapped: Volcanic Island, Underground Sea and Thundering Falls (Island Mountain) all count. Only Wasteland, fetches and non-Island lands make Daze impossible. Read the land's types, not its name."
   - Evidence: turn 3, A prompts 22-25 (A's flag note "Daze impossible" with only a tapped Thundering Falls out); B prompt 51 (Daze cost offered Falls). Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: this corrects the wording of "Look at what the opponent has untapped: with only a Wasteland out, Daze is impossible" and "When the opponent has only Wasteland (no Island), Daze is impossible". Those rules are right, but the word "untapped" misleads, and they say nothing about dual or surveil lands. Suggest replacing them with this one.
2. "Stuck on one land with no land in hand, shuffle any Ponder that shows no land, however good the spells are. Do not spend Lotus Petal on a Ponder you will not shuffle."
   - Evidence: turn 5, A prompts 37 and 40. Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: this extends "With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle" and "Cast Brainstorm before Ponder so Ponder's shuffle clears the put-backs" to the mana-screw case. It does not contradict either.
