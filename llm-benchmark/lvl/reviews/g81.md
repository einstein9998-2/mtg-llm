# g81 review: A Alurentell (on the play) vs B UR Cutter. A won on game turn 9 inside one Aluren loop

## 1. Result and turning point
- **Result**: A won on turn 9 (A's 5th turn). A topdecked Aluren at 10 life, then played Tomb #2, cracked Misty for Tropical and cast Aluren (Tomb 2 + Maze G + Trop G, Island spare). Life went 10 to 9 to 7. The Lost Mine loop drained B from 18. B held FoW #74 with no blue pitch, plus Bolt, which cannot kill a 5/5 Acererak. B had no outs.
- **Turning point: b60 (t6). DRC's surveil put Prismari Charm, B's next draw, into the graveyard.** It gave nothing toward delirium: B's graveyard would hold only instants (two FoW and Charm). Kept, Charm is the t8 draw. On t9, with three Volcanics open, B bounces Aluren in response to the free Acererak (the existing UR note says exactly this). A's FoW #20 had no blue pitch, so A was tapped out at 7 life facing 6 power plus Bolt. B wins that game. B had already thrown away two other routes:
  - b19 (t2): B Pondered into Charm, Daze and Island, then shuffled them away. B had Delta and Wasteland as its next lands.
  - b28 (t4): B put back Daze with Brainstorm and shuffled it away with Delta. B kept Bolt instead. Daze is a blue card: held, it pitches to FoW #74 and counters Aluren.
- **A's FoW on Murktide (a54, A flagged it)** turned out well only through B's later errors. A holds only one Force cast (Atraxa and FoW #2 are its only blue pitches), and A had no enabler in hand. Had B let Murktide die (b57), B keeps FoW plus Preordain and draws Charm, and A has no protection on t9. Had A held its Force, B casts no spell, so DRC never surveils and B still draws Charm. So the trade was not "Atraxa and FoW for nothing": it drew out B's last blue pitch and set up the surveil error. On information it was still a close call, which A's own flag covers. It gives no new support to the g79 candidate ("hold Force without an enabler"), and no evidence against it either.
- **a82 (Aluren at 10, Tomb payment): correct.** At 10, Murktide plus DRC kill A in two attacks. Without Tomb, A had only 3 mana. The spare Island beat Daze. Waiting gained nothing.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A: a40/a41 (t5), Boseiju channel on a tapped Volcanic.** B searched up an untapped Volcanic. The real cost was the graveyard: the dead Volcanic was B's 4th delve card. Murktide cost {1}{U}{U}, exactly B's three t6 lands. Without it, Murktide costs 4 and does not come down on t6. A also lost its second green land. That forced the Misty crack and the Tomb payment on t9 (3 life). Kept as a land, Boseiju plus Maze pay GG without Tomb. B's report is right that it got an untapped Volcanic, but B never used it (b47 passed with Bolt).
- **Other A decisions were fine:** the keep (4 lands, Brainstorm and Force); the t1 Brainstorm while B had no land, so Daze was impossible; the a10/a11 put-backs of Acererak #2 (a duplicate) and Boseiju, at the cost of one known redraw on t3; the Misty crack at B's t4 end step, which shuffled away the known Acererak #2 (playbook t25 rule); and the Hedge Maze surveil of Tropical. A's report's "flood from the put-backs" is mostly the Tomb #2 and Misty draws.
- **B: b19, the Ponder shuffle of Charm, Daze and Island** (above). It was followed by b28 (Daze put back), b60 (Charm surveilled away, which was decisive) and b70: Wasteland on Ancient Tomb rather than Hedge Maze. A's green sources were Maze and Misty. Hitting Maze leaves one G, so Aluren (GG) is off, while Show and Tell stays castable either way. A's t9 Aluren was then uncastable. The timing (B's own turn, before A untapped) was right.
- **B's mulligan (b2, B flagged it)**: a one-lander on the draw, without a cantrip, so mulliganing was reasonable. Bottoming Unholy Heat over Bolt is equal against Acererak: only both together (5 damage) kill him.
- **Bolt question:** B's "Bolt face at A's t7 end step" was impossible. All three Volcanics were tapped from the t6 Murktide until B's t8 untap. A t8 Bolt to face puts A at 7, then 4 after Misty and Tomb. The loop still wins, since Dark Pool gains 1 per lap. B had no line on t9: FoW had no pitch and Bolt cannot kill Acererak. The cards that win are the ones it binned: Charm, Daze or Heat.
