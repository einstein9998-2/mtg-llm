# g32 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 6 (its third turn) with the Aluren + Acererak loop, draining B from 21 to 0 through Lost Mine's Dark Pool (about 84 free Acererak casts; A finished at 31 life, library 25).
- **How B died (checked in both transcripts).** A cast Aluren (A p50), then Acererak #58 (A p52). With the ETB trigger on the stack, B sacrificed all five creatures to Goblin Bombardment, each time targeting Acererak (B p56 to p69). A did **not** respond: its macro passed, all five pings resolved, and the 5/5 Acererak died. Then the ETB trigger resolved: Acererak could not come back from the graveyard, but A still ventured (Lost Mine, Cave Entrance). A then cast its **second Acererak (#11, in hand since its turn-2 draw)** for free through Aluren and looped. Under Aluren A could also have cast #11 in response at instant speed; it did not need to. B never got another turn and died to the 21st Dark Pool drain. B's report ("A looped in response with my abilities on the stack") is wrong.
- **Was racing better? No.** The loop is deterministic and unbounded (each lap: scry, Goblin, drain 1, draw 1). If B did nothing, Acererak #58 alone kills B this turn. "Attack plus 5 sacrifices = 11 next turn" assumes a next turn B would never get. Killing the first Acererak was B's only out (it works if A has no second copy and does not find one with Ponder off the untapped Tundra). The first ETB trigger was the right moment, because each later lap draws A a card. B's play was correct and simply lost to the second copy.
- **The real deciding decision was A's, on turn 4 (A p17):** Show and Tell put in Atraxa, while A held **Aluren and Acererak**. Aluren put in, then a free Acererak, wins that turn. B was tapped out (Savannah and Plateau tapped for Sand Scout on its own turn 3), so Erode was impossible. B's best put-in, Goblin Bombardment, had 3 creatures, which is 3 damage against a 5/5. B's hand was Marsh Flats and Erode, with no creatures to cast free under the symmetric Aluren. Atraxa instead died to Erode next turn (which also ramped A an Island). The win came two turns later and cost A 6 life. It did not matter here; against a Swords or a second Bombardment it could have.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A: p17 (turn 4), Atraxa over Aluren in the Show and Tell.** A could see Acererak in hand and B's tapped lands. Second, smaller: p23, the Atraxa reveal ended with Done, leaving Acererak #2 and Aluren #2 on the bottom to avoid a second discard. Acererak #2 as a backup against Erode or Swords beat keeping Boseiju (A then discarded Stock Up at p27 anyway). No cost here, because A drew another Acererak.
- **B: no clear mistake.** The mulligan of the 1-lander (B p1) is close and B flagged it itself. The Bombardment put-in (p18), Erode on Atraxa (p28; a 7/7 lifelink flier ends the race), and the all-in kill attempt (p56 to p69) were all right. The loss came from A's fast draw: Show and Tell on turn 4, then Aluren with two Acererak on turn 6. B's report draws the wrong lesson ("keep the creatures and race").

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "When Show and Tell resolves and Acererak is already in hand, put in Aluren, not Atraxa: cast Acererak for free right away and loop. This wins on the spot unless they can kill a 5/5 at instant speed. Atraxa is for hands without Acererak."
   - Evidence: turn 4, A p17. B was tapped out with 3 creatures. A won two turns later instead.
   - Confidence: high when the opponent is tapped out; medium against Boros with mana up, because Aluren is symmetric (a free Amped Raptor can discover Swords).
   - Overlap: it makes Brady's "Show and Tell versus Aluren: decide by how much mana I have and which other combo pieces are in my hand" concrete. It does not contradict "Put Omniscience in with Show and Tell when I have Atraxa, Aluren or Acererak in hand"; it covers the case with no Omniscience in hand. Earlier reviews (g28-era) flagged the same choice as close; this game adds a clean data point.
2. "Atraxa reveal, creature slot: take the second Acererak when one is already in hand and discard a spare land instead. Removal on the first copy is how they break the loop."
   - Evidence: A p23, low impact.
   - Confidence: low to medium. It refines the existing rule "The only reason to take fewer than allowed is to put back cards you would discard to hand size anyway".

## 4. Candidate Boros Aggro notes
1. "Against Aluren plus Acererak there is no race: the loop drains you on their turn. On the first Acererak ETB trigger, kill it with everything (5 Bombardment pings, Swords or Erode). It only fails if they hold a second copy, but it is the only out. Do it on the first cast, because each lap draws them a card."
   - Evidence: B p56 to p69, correct, lost to the second copy.
   - Confidence: high.
2. "Once the opponent has shown Show and Tell or Aluren, keep W plus Swords or Erode open on their turn when the board allows it. One exile on Acererak saves the board that Bombardment would otherwise eat, and Bombardment then handles the second copy."
   - Evidence: here Erode had to go on Atraxa (p28), so this is a general note, not a mistake.
   - Confidence: medium.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- **Engine payment (A p50 to p52):** Aluren was auto-paid with Ancient Tomb (-2 life), and 1 colorless floated unused while Tundra stayed untapped. Tropical, Petal, Island and Tundra paid it with no life loss. Same Tomb-preference payment issue as before; it cost 2 life.
- **Possible hidden-information leak (B p49):** after Erode's basic search shuffled A's library (A p32), B's "opponent cards you know in hand" listed Acererak right after A's turn-6 draw. Atraxa had revealed an Acererak and put it on the bottom before the shuffle, so revealed-card knowledge seems to survive a library shuffle. The engine needs a check.
- Display: "1 damage to (gone)" and "Erode targeting (gone)" show the name lookup after the object left; cosmetic.
- A's auto macro hit its pick budget three times (p70, p71, p72): harmless re-prompts. B used 2 sims on the mulligan, no other tools.
- No skipped land drops and no upkeep cantrips by either side. A held Ponder and Strand unused through the loop (harmless). B's report misread the stack and the race; A's report is accurate apart from Brainstorm ordering confusion (choosing Stock Up first at p11 put it on top, and it was drawn on turn 4).

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on turn 6 with Aluren + Acererak (Lost Mine drain). B's five-Bombardment kill on the first Acererak was correct but A recast its second copy for free.
TOP MISTAKE: A p17 (turn 4): Show and Tell put in Atraxa while holding Aluren + Acererak against a tapped-out opponent. Aluren wins that turn.
TOP RULE: With Acererak in hand, Show and Tell puts in Aluren (free Acererak, loop now), not Atraxa.
