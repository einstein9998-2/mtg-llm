## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 7 (A's 4th turn): Ancient Tomb, Aluren through one Daze (paid), then the Acererak / Lost Mine loop drained B from 19 to 0 with 19 Dark Pools.
- **Turning point: B's turn 6 (B prompts 35-40) plus A's turn 7 (A prompts 47/48).** B played Scalding Tarn as its land, then Cutter and Preordain, tapping out with Daze as its only counter. Preordain then found Wasteland, but B had already used its land drop, so A's Tropical Island (A's only green land on the battlefield; Aluren needs GG) survived. On turn 7 A played Ancient Tomb (6 mana: Island, Tundra, Tropical, Tomb, Petal), cast Aluren with 2 spare and paid for the Daze. With Tropical Wastelanded, A would have had exactly 4 mana with GG only by playing the second Tropical, so Daze would have countered Aluren.
- A's own line was sound on turn 7. Tomb over Tropical (A's self-flag 47) gave 2 spare against double Daze for 2 life, which was right with B tapped out and holding 6 cards.
- Not variance-driven: B never drew Force of Will. A's turn-5 error (below) cost a Veil of Summer but nothing else, because B had only one Daze.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Land drops: none skipped (A: Strand, Tundra, Tropical, Tomb; B: Falls, Volcanic, Tarn). No upkeep cantrips.
- **Auto macro (338 picks: 332 auto lines in the log plus 6 launch picks).** Verdict: it behaved correctly once the rules were reordered; the first rule list was wrong.
  - Launch 1 (prompt 54, `last:Cast Acererak` first): legal but useless. Every pick was a Cast Acererak (flash via Aluren) or a room choice; it never passed priority. After 30 picks, 18 Lost Mine room abilities sat unresolved and the opponent was still at 19.
  - Launches 2-6 (prompts 55-59, `stack:Pass priority` first): each room resolved before the next cast. Every lap went Lost Mine, Cave Entrance, Goblin Lair (never Mine Tunnels), Dark Pool (never Storeroom or Fungi Cavern), Temple of Dumathoin. That made 19 Dark Pools for 19 drains (B 19 to 0, A 15 to 33). The macro found Cast Acererak by label as its index moved (1, 2, 3, 4) and never paid mana.
  - Cave Entrance scry always chose "Done" (kept the top card), even City of Traitors and lands. Harmless here, but the macro does not filter scries.
  - The pick budgets (30/40/60/80/120) stopped the macro 5 times, so it took 6 launches. A larger first budget would save prompts.
- Possible rules deviation (verify, no effect here): at A prompt 55 the engine counted 4 Lost Mine dungeons as completed while their Temple of Dumathoin abilities were still on the stack. Under CR 309.7 / 704.5t a dungeon is completed only after its last room's ability has left the stack. Acererak checks only Tomb of Annihilation, so the loop was unaffected.
- Payment oddity (minor): Aluren (A prompt 48) tapped Island, Tropical, Tomb and Petal (5 mana) for a 4-mana spell and left 1 floating. The float happened to pay for the Daze; outside a Daze window it would have been lost.
- B's report quotes the footer ("338 auto picks, odds calls 3") as if it were B's; those numbers are A's. B again saw no events after its Daze (the loser has no game-over log, as in g11 and g14).
- A used 3 odds calls (opponent model, ~40% Force/Daze); they supported the turn-7 jam. B made no tool calls.
