# g34 review: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 11 (B's 6th turn). Combat took A from 10 to 2. Then a second Ajani went to the graveyard by the legend rule, the remaining Ajani flipped into the Avenger, and its 0 ability (B prompts 65 to 74) dealt 6 damage, one per creature. B controlled a red permanent, the Sand Warrior token. A never had 3 mana: its land count went 1, 0, 1, 2, 1, 2.
- The game was decided by A's mulligan keep (A prompt 3) and B's two Wastelands, which hit A's only lands.
  - B prompt 11 (turn 2): B played Wasteland as its turn-1 land and fired it at Tropical Island in response to Brainstorm. A was left with no land.
  - B prompt 47 (turn 9): Wasteland on Savannah, rather than the uncracked Misty, which could have dodged by fetching a basic in response. A stayed on 2 lands.
  - Both B plays were correct. A could not have dodged either one: its only land was a nonbasic, and on turn 8 cracking Misty for a basic Island would have left the same land count after the Wasteland.
- The mulligan sequence, judged on what A could see:
  - **The 7 (prompt 2)** was Boseiju, Lotus Petal, two Brainstorm, Stock Up and two Atraxa, with no Show and Tell or Aluren. Mulliganing it was right, though it was close.
    - It had one land and no blue land. The Petal pays for exactly one blue spell, so the second Brainstorm and Stock Up (2U) needed a blue land the hand did not have.
    - It held two of the four pieces the combo needs (a third mana and Show and Tell were missing), against a fast clock with Wasteland.
    - Brady's batch-2 rule covers it: "One land with Ponder is a mulligan unless you know the opponent is Storm or Reanimator".
  - **The 6 (prompt 3)** was Tropical Island, Brainstorm, Force of Will, Veil, Show and Tell, Atraxa and Aluren. It is close and leans mulligan.
    - The same rule applies: one land, one cantrip, opponent not Storm or Reanimator.
    - The only land is a dual facing 4 Wastelands. Show and Tell needs two more lands, so A had to draw about 2 lands in roughly 4 fresh cards (19 lands left in 53) and dodge Wasteland (B is about 44% to see one in its first 8 cards).
    - The 6 also had less mana than the 7 A had just thrown back: no Petal and one cantrip instead of three. Keeping it is inconsistent with the first mulligan.
    - Going to 5 against Boros is also poor (my estimate is 25 to 30% for either choice), so this is not a blunder. It is the flag to put in front of Brady, and A already self-flagged both prompts (g34a.tsv seq 2 and 3).
- **Bottom (prompt 4):** A bottomed Aluren. Veil of Summer was the better card to bottom.
  - In game 1 Boros casts no blue or black spells and has no counterspell, so Veil only offers a draw that never triggers, plus "can't be countered", which no Boros card tests.
  - Aluren is a second route: Aluren plus Acererak needs 4 mana, but A drew Acererak on turn 2. Neither card got cast, so this did not decide the game.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: the 6-card keep at prompt 3 (close, see above), then the Veil-over-Aluren bottom at prompt 4 (minor).
  - Land draws were about average (3 lands in 7 fresh cards). The loss came from the fragile keep meeting two well-timed Wastelands, which was partly variance.
  - Force of Will on Ocelot Pride (prompt 31, A self-flagged) was fine. A was at least two land drops from Show and Tell, Boros has no counterspell for the spare Force to stop, and Pride is their best snowball 1-drop.
  - At prompt 66 (2 life, second Ajani on the stack), Force of Will would only have delayed the loss by one turn: B had 6 attackers and A had 2 lands.
- A's report is wrong about one thing: fetching Hedge Maze (prompt 54) was not a mistake.
  - A needed its third land. Surveil 1 raises the chance that the next draw is a land from about 33% to about 56%. A binned Stock Up and drew Tropical Island.
  - Island would have been safe from Wasteland but gave no selection.
- B: none.
  - The keep (2 Plains, Wasteland, 3 Spider-Woman, Sand Scout) was fine against a combo deck.
  - Wasteland as the turn-1 land, Ocelot Pride first as Force of Will bait (prompt 30), Wasteland on the dual rather than the fetch, and Ajani after combat were all good.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- **Sim tool at the mulligan prompt (A, log lines 4 to 6).** A ran 3 queries, all returning 0.0%: "turn; brainstorm putback lands; goal lands_playable >= 2", "turn; goal lands_playable >= 2" and "turn;turn; goal lands_playable >= 3". A concluded the tool "did not model my pre-mulligan hand".
  - That is unverified. At this prompt it is B's turn 0 and A is on the draw. If `turn` stops at the end of B's turn 1, the first two queries give A no land drop, and `turn;turn` gives exactly one. In that case 0% is the true answer to the question asked, if lands_playable counts battlefield lands.
  - If it counts lands in hand or reachable lands, 0.0% is wrong (Petal plus Brainstorm should give about 50%). Then the sim probably does not resolve the pending Mulligan decision as a keep.
  - Tool owner: reproduce on g34 at A prompt 2. Make the sim warn at Mulligan prompts and print which turn it stopped on and how many own land drops it simulated.
  - Either way, 3 calls were wasted and no decision changed.
- **Menu bloat (B).** Every B priority menu from turn 7 lists 15 to 20 "Tap Lazotep Quarry for X, sacrificing Y" mana options (for example prompts 42 to 46). That is noise and a misclick risk. Suggest hiding sacrifice-mana options unless a spell needs them.
- **Legend-rule menu (B prompt 67).** It shows two identical "Choose Ajani, Nacatl Pariah" options with no ids (B reported this). It should show #id and the summoning-sick/tapped state.
- Engine rules looked correct: the legend rule put the old Ajani in the graveyard, which triggered the new Ajani's flip, and the Avenger's 0 ability counted 6 creatures with a red token out. B's report misreads the log summary ("sim calls 3", "75 prompts"). That line is from A's side.
- No skipped land drops (A had no land in hand on turn 6) and no upkeep cantrips.
- Minor: A cast Brainstorm in main phase 1 at prompt 9 into B's untapped Wasteland. Holding it until B's end step, or until B activated Wasteland (Tropical Island could tap in response), was free and kept information. Brady's rule: "wait as long as possible to cast a spell if waiting is free". The outcome was the same.
