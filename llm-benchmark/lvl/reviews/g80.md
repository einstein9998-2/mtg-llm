# g80 review: A = Alurentell (won), B = Boros Aggro

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn, on the draw): Show and Tell #2 for Omniscience (A prompt 47/50), free Acererak, Lost Mine loop took B from 21 to 0 (21 laps, about 330 macro picks). The setup was A prompt 17 (turn 4): with B's Plateau and Wasteland both tapped from its own turn 3, Show and Tell for Atraxa could not be answered, and the reveal (prompts 20-24) gave A Show and Tell #2, Acererak, Tomb, Petal and Brainstorm. B's best chance came earlier, at B prompt 12 (turn 3): Hedge Maze was A's only land, and B tapped Wasteland for mana to cast Voice of Victory instead of Wastelanding it. Without the Maze, A has Savannah plus Petal on turn 4 (2 mana), so no Show and Tell until turn 6, and B gets two more attacks before the first one lands.

## 2. Mistakes
- A: no real mistake. The keep (4 lands, Petal, Brainstorm, Show and Tell) needed only a payoff and drew Atraxa on turn 2. Hedge Maze on turn 2 was right: nothing else was castable, and the surveil kept Stock Up. Jamming on turn 4 into a tapped-out Boros was correct, and so was every reveal pick (one card of each type: land, sorcery, creature, artifact, instant). Taking Acererak over a second Atraxa was close but right: the existing rule says "a spare Acererak first", and it won as soon as Omniscience was drawn.
- A's self-reported mistake is wrong. Brainstorm does not change hand size: cast it (-1), draw 3 (+3), put back 2 (-2). With 8 cards A would still have discarded one. Brainstorm in main 2 or at B's end step (with the Petal) would only have dug 3 cards deeper. It would have found Omniscience a turn sooner, which did not matter. Discarding Misty was fine, though with two Brainstorms in hand Ancient Tomb was the better discard: the fetch shuffles away put-backs, and Tomb is a Wasteland target that costs life against Boros.
- A's risk: A was at 18 entering turn 6. B had 14 power for turn 7 (12 on board plus 2 mobilize) and Goblin Bombardment in its deck. A needed the turn-6 combo or a stabiliser. Its outs were Omniscience (2), Aluren (4: 5 mana available, then the free Acererak) or Atraxa via Show and Tell, with Brainstorm x2 and Stock Up to dig. Drawing Omniscience made it trivial, and B had no W open on A's turn 6 (only Wasteland), so there was no risk on the combo turn itself.
- B, first real mistake: prompt 12 (Voice over Wasteland on the only land; see section 1). Everything else B did was sound or forced:
  - Ocelot Pride line: excellent. It was the turn-1 play, the solo turn-3 attack gained life for the Cat, city's blessing came on turn 5, and the triggers were ordered correctly (prompt 43: Voice first, so Ocelot copied the mobilize Warriors) for 14 permanents.
  - Turn-5 attack: all five attackers into an empty board was correct.
  - Swords on Atraxa (prompt 32/33): correct and matches the existing note ("Swords the Show-and-Tell Atraxa before attacking even though they gain 7"). Atraxa is a vigilant lifelink blocker, and B's precombat main on turn 5 was the first window with W untapped, because Voice had tapped Plateau on turn 3. Holding Swords for Acererak needs a loop enabler that B had not seen (the reveal showed B Tomb, Brainstorm, Show and Tell and Acererak).
  - The +7 life is what made turn 7 non-lethal (14 power against 18, not "23+" as B's report says). Without Swords, though, Atraxa blocks and gains life every turn. It was moot: A won on turn 6.
  - Turn-5 mana: forced. Plateau paid for Swords, Parlor entered tapped, and every other spell in hand needed W (after Swords the menu offered only Wasteland's ability).
  - Held Wasteland on turn 5: harmless. A had Hedge Maze, Savannah, Petal and a land drop (Tropical or Tomb) for a 3-mana Show and Tell, so no Wasteland target stopped it. B's report blames itself here, wrongly; the real Wasteland error was turn 3.
  - Put-ins: Ajani on turn 4 was fine. Spider-Woman on turn 6 was irrelevant.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- "Brainstorm does not lower hand size (cast, draw 3, put back 2 leaves the count unchanged); only a permanent or a spell without draw does. When discarding to hand size with Brainstorm in hand, keep a fetch over Ancient Tomb or a basic." Evidence: A prompt 27/28 and A's report (which drew the wrong lesson). Confidence: medium. Not a duplicate. It extends "Hand size: ... discard lands and duplicates at cleanup" and "with a fetch in hand, weave it in".

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
- "Turn 2 against Alurentell: if their only land is a blue or green source (Hedge Maze, a dual), Wasteland it even if that costs your 2-drop; a Petal plus one land cannot cast Show and Tell. Do not tap Wasteland for mana that turn." Evidence: B prompt 12; A cast Show and Tell on turn 4 off Maze, Savannah and Petal. Confidence: medium. This sharpens the existing notes "Hit Ancient Tomb or their only blue or green dual" and "Voice of Victory first among 2-drops against Force decks". The land-kill outranks Voice when it denies their Show and Tell turn.

- No skipped land drops, no upkeep cantrips, no wasted tool calls. A used only the auto macro (658 picks).
- A's note is correct rules, not a bug: under Omniscience without Aluren, Acererak is a creature at sorcery speed, so the room triggers had to resolve between casts (as Brady's override says). Library 45 against 21 laps was safe.
- B: the menu offers one attacker per prompt (tedious, fine). The trigger-order prompt worked. No engine bugs seen.
