RESULT: WON
TURNS: 5
SIDEBOARD: Drew and used Carpet of Flowers x2 (cast T1 and T2, about 4 to 8 mana a turn once they had 3 to 4 Islands, it carried the whole game and Wasteland never mattered), Prismatic Ending (T2, X=1 with Savannah W plus Carpet blue, exiled Lavinia while they were tapped out), and both Orim's Chant (cast one on T5, hard-cast Force of Will countered it and tapped them out). Veil of Summer was not drawn. I would keep all of it in this matchup, Carpet most of all.
PETAL: Never drawn. I saw it once, as the third card of a turn-3 Ponder (Tropical, Tomb, Petal), and shuffled it away. With 2 to 4 Carpets worth of mana a Petal would only have been a spare Daze or Force payment or a white or green pip, so it was low-impact here and I did not miss it. They run no Daze in the list I saw. I would keep none, or at most one as the trimmed fourth Carpet-adjacent mana piece. Against this deck Carpet makes the Petal redundant, and the real cost of the slot is the cantrip or payoff it replaces.
CHANT_ENDING: Drew both Chants and Prismatic Ending. Ending was cast T2 on Lavinia (needed two colours, W from Savannah plus blue from Carpet, no problem). Chant was cast T5 as protection for hard-cast Aluren: they responded with a hard-cast Force of Will (all 5 lands tapped, 1 card left in hand), so with their hand and mana gone I did not need the second Chant. White was never a problem: Carpet can make white, and I also had Savannah and Tundra.
- Possible mistake T3: took both Carpet triggers (U and W) before Ponder, then Ponder found only lands and Petal, so 5 or 6 mana was wasted. Not much alternative, but I could have kept one trigger for main 2.
- Surprise: the opponent sat on 3 to 5 open mana with 2 cards for several turns doing nothing but attacking with Phelia (blinking its own Tamiyo to grow Phelia), then hard-cast Force of Will on my Chant and tapped out, which opened the Aluren turn. Aluren plus Acererak looped 17 life in 271 auto picks, no response possible.
