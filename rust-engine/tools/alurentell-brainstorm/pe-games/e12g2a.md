RESULT: WON
TURNS: 5
SIDEBOARD: Drew and used 1 Orim's Chant, 1 Veil of Summer, 1 Carpet of Flowers (from Atraxa reveal), 0 Prismatic Ending (not in list), 1 Lotus Petal (from Atraxa reveal). Chant and Veil won the game on turn 5 (details below). Carpet was cast turn 3 off the Petal and then Prismatic Ending'd by them before it ever made mana (a fine trade: it ate a removal). I would keep Chant and Veil in this matchup.
LIST: Draws: Orim's Chant #1 in the turn-3 draw (Chant #2 was in my first, mulliganed, seven; the third Chant never showed); Petal #1 taken from the turn-3 Atraxa reveal (the second Petal never showed); Aluren #1 found by turn-4 Stock Up, Aluren #2 drawn turn 5 (so three Alurens were not a constraint, I held two at the end and cast neither, because Show and Tell for Aluren was better than hardcasting it: only one green source left after Veil). The decisive turn was turn 5: opponent had 2 untapped Tundra, 1 card + Force in hand after Brainstorm, I had 6 mana. I held Chant until I had the whole turn lined up, played Ancient Tomb, cast Chant (Tundra) first so that Force of Will would be aimed at the Chant and not at Aluren. They Forced it (pitching Stifle, which was the real danger to Acererak triggers). I answered with Veil (Tropical Island, G) in response: Chant became uncounterable, the Force fizzled, Veil drew a card (Flooded Strand). With Chant live they could not cast any spell for the rest of the turn (no Swords on Acererak, no Stifle, no second Force), so I cast Show and Tell (Island + Tomb) for Aluren, and looped Acererak with Lost Mine from 16 to 0 in about 266 picks. Chant plus Veil as a pair is what beat a Force-and-Stifle hand; either alone would have been much worse (Chant alone loses to Force, Veil alone loses to Swords/Stifle on the Acererak trigger). A third Chant, a second Petal and the third Aluren did not decide anything this game; the Petal's value was mostly letting me cast Carpet, which I cannot say was needed. I never missed Prismatic Ending: their only targets were Tamiyo (a 0/3 flyer on turn 1 and a flipped walker), neither of which hurt me. If anything, Ending on the first Tamiyo would have denied them the Brainstorm/Tamiyo recursion engine (they recurred Brainstorm with Tamiyo SS), but it did not matter. I would want the 3rd Chant over the 2nd Petal: Chant is the card that beat the opponent's Force plus Stifle plan; I would not want fewer Chants, and the third Aluren is fine (it was never the bottleneck, finding the first one was).
- Mistake (turn 3): I cast Carpet of Flowers into their two untapped-next-turn Tundras, using my Petal; they answered it with Prismatic Ending. Not fatal, but I also took a Show-and-Tell Atraxa pick of Misty (binned on surveil) and kept a slightly slow mana; Cleaner would have been holding Petal for a Daze/Veil spare. Also turn 1: I bottomed Stock Up from my seven, which turned out fine, but I did not bring Force of Will pitch fodder into account for the Chant turn.
- Surprise: the opponent Forced Orim's Chant (pitching Stifle) instead of saving the Force for Aluren or Acererak, and Swords on a freshly resolved Atraxa plus Prismatic Ending on Carpet in the same turn rather than holding mana up for my next turn.
- Surprise 2: their Tamiyo/Phelia line recurred Brainstorm and flipped Tamiyo repeatedly with Phelia blinking Tamiyo (Phelia 3/3 by turn 4), which is a real clock-less card-advantage engine.
