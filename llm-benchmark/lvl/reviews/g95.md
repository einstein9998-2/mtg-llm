B won on game turn 8 (B's 4th turn), 14 combat damage into A's 12 life, A tapped out with no creatures.
- Decided at A prompts 47-48 (T7, S&T #45 on the stack under B's Force pitching Murktide). Probably worse odds after B's turn-8 attack, but strictly better than spending Force and Atraxa for nothing.
