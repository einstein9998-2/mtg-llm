# g91 review: A (Alurentell) beat B (UR Cutter) on turn 7 (A's 4th turn, A on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
- A won on T7 main 2: Atraxa (Show-and-Telled on T5) hit for 7 lifelink (B 18 to 11, A 17 to 24), second Show and Tell (a59) put in Omniscience (a62), free Aluren (a64), free second Atraxa (a67, legend rule, reveal of 10) took Stock Up, Brainstorm, Tundra, the free Stock Up (a78) took Acererak and Atraxa, then the Lost Mine macro. Kill check: exactly 11 Dark Pool picks for B's 11 life (42 Acererak casts); no issue.
- Decisive moment, B's side: T5 b29, with A's Veil of Summer on the stack, B passed. A had only Island and Ancient Tomb left (Tropical tapped for Veil), exactly the Show and Tell cost. Daze on Veil forces A to pay 1 (no Show and Tell this turn) or lose Veil and run Show and Tell into Force of Will; Force on Veil plus Daze on Show and Tell stops both. After Veil resolved, B threw Daze (b31) and Force of Will pitching Prismari Charm (b34) at an uncounterable Show and Tell. Veil's effect was real: Daze resolved with no payment prompt for A and Show and Tell still resolved (a36).
- Decisive moment, A's side: the wait at T3 (a19, self-flagged). Verified from B's hand: Volcanic, Delta, Misty, Daze, Bolt, Cutter, plus Force of Will drawn off Bauble in A's upkeep (Daze its only blue pitch). With only Island plus Tomb (or City), a T3 Show and Tell had no spare, so Daze or Force (pitching Daze) would have stopped it for sure. Waiting cost nothing (B's board was empty) and gave Veil plus Show and Tell with 4 mana on T5. Right call, and the existing rules already say so.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none. Keep (a1): Strand, Tomb, City, Ponder, Aluren, Veil, Show and Tell has a blue/green fetch, a cantrip, and two enablers, so it needs only a creature. That is a keep. T1 Strand for a basic Island (a5) was Wasteland-proof (B's deck runs 4 Wasteland) but left the hand with no green until Ponder found Tropical (close, flagged). Ponder (a7) kept Atraxa, Tropical, Misty with no shuffle: correct. T5: Tomb as the land, Veil from Tropical, Show and Tell from Island plus Tomb: correct (the playbook's jam line). Atraxa reveal took one card of every type (6 of 10). Discards City and Misty, keeping Tropical for the T7 land drop: fine.
- A, attack before Show and Tell (a58): correct. B had 3 Volcanics up but only Bolt, Island and Misty in hand, no flier, and Atraxa has vigilance. The 7 damage made the loop 11 laps instead of 18, and Acererak was not yet in hand, so the "loop now, do not attack first" rule did not apply.
- B: b29 (pass on Veil), then b31/b34 (counters into a resolved Veil). Its own report admits b34 but misses that b29 was the real error. The existing note "Force a Veil cast on their own turn" covers it.
- B, other checks: keep (b2) was 4 lands, not 5 (Island, Volcanic, Delta, Misty) plus Preordain, Daze, Bauble. Low action but a normal tempo keep. Bauble on A (b18) was fine: B's own top was a known Force of Will from Preordain. Tapping out for Cutter on T4 (b23) goes against the note, but it cost nothing here: Veil's hexproof from blue blanked Charm on A's T5 anyway. Putting in a land at both Show and Tells (Delta b37, Misty T7) was right: B's hand held only lands and Bolt, no creature or permanent. T6 doing nothing was forced (hand Island, Misty, Bolt; Monk into a deathtouch Atraxa is no attack).
- Atraxa answer: after the pitch, none. The deck's only bounce is the single Charm. Bolt plus a delirium Unholy Heat (B had 4 types in the graveyard by T6) would kill her, but B held no Heat. Keeping the Charm meant bouncing Atraxa on T6. A still held Show and Tell, Omniscience, Aluren, Atraxa and Force, so B was probably lost anyway, but more slowly.
- Note: B's report says A's hand "had FoW, Lotus Petal etc."; those were A's cards revealed by Atraxa, not B's.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- "Under Omniscience with Atraxa already in play and no Acererak in hand, cast the second Atraxa anyway: the legend rule costs nothing and the reveal digs for Stock Up or Acererak." Evidence: a67 to a78 (reveal took Stock Up, which found Acererak). Confidence: medium. It is new. It extends "Cast the free Atraxa second" and does not contradict it.
- Nothing else is new: wait-for-Veil (a19) and the Veil, then Show and Tell line (a28/a30) are existing rules, and A followed them.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
- "When they cast Veil of Summer, count their untapped mana: if what remains is exactly the Show and Tell or Aluren cost, Daze the Veil (they must pay and lose the combo turn). Once Veil has resolved, cast nothing at their spells that turn, and above all never pitch Prismari Charm, your only Atraxa or Aluren bounce." Evidence: b29, b31, b34. Confidence: high. It refines the existing note "Force a Veil cast on their own turn" (same idea, adds the Daze count and the Charm rule).

## 5. Process and engine flags
- No engine bug. Daze and Force were offered under a resolved Veil (legal, just useless). A state line such as "opponent's spells can't be countered this turn" would have prevented b31/b34. The duplicate "Cast Lotus Petal" entries under Omniscience are the normal and free-cast options.
- All land drops made by both sides, no upkeep cantrips, and one odds call by A (a19). B's report counts that call as an engine oddity; the summary line is shared between the seats. The macro ran cleanly for 268 picks.
