## Result and turning point
- Decisive, t7 (A prompts 84-89). Hand Tropical, Tundra, Petal, Brainstorm, Omniscience; B had shown Wasteland #62. Brainstorm drew Misty, Ponder, Acererak. A put back BOTH lands from hand (Tundra, Tropical, p84/85), kept the dead Omniscience, played Misty and fetched the put-back Tropical (p89), and that shuffled away the known Tundra. A went into B's turn with zero lands in hand. B played its second Wasteland on t8 and killed Hedge Maze #43 and Tropical. Better: put back Omniscience plus Misty, play Tropical, and keep Tundra in hand. Then on t9 Island + Hedge Maze + Tundra + Petal = 4 mana with GG (Maze, Petal), so Aluren, then a free Acererak loop. B at t9 had one untapped Island and a hand of Unholy Heat, DRC and Cutter: no counter, and no red mana for Heat. That line very likely wins on t9.
- Second error, t9 p102 (A's own flag): hardcasting Acererak for a one-card scry spent the Petal, which was A's Aluren mana. Keeping the Petal, any land drawn on t13 gave Aluren plus the loop before the t14 lethal (about 12 lands in 38 unknown cards, and t11 was a known Atraxa). After the scry A needed two lands, one of them green. That move cost roughly 20 points of win chance, and it is the playbook's "Acererak is plan F".

## First real mistake per side
- A: t7 p84, the land put-backs and the fetch that shuffled (above). Everything earlier was sound. The keep (5 lands, Ponder, Omniscience) is a close, defensible keep. Fetching the deck's only basic Island on t1 was right. Holding Strand and cracking it in response to B's Bauble (p45) wasted the Bauble. FoW on the delved Murktide while B was tapped out, with Veil as backup (p71, p76), was good: Veil made A's Force resolve and drew a card. Small slip: Hedge Maze surveil binned a second Lotus Petal (p47); with 4 lands in hand that was fine, but in hindsight a second Petal would have been the t9 Aluren mana. "Nonbasics untapped into Wasteland" was not a separate error: no basics were left in the library, and the fix is keeping a land in hand.
- B: no clear mistake. The one-land keep on the draw (2 Preordain, Brainstorm, 2 Bauble) was risky but hit a land every turn. Wasteland timing was ideal. B played the second Wasteland and cracked both on its own t8, before A's untap, taking out Hedge Maze and the green dual (Brady: use it before they untap). Close spot, t6 p74: Force back on A's Force (pitching Daze, its last blue card) while A had Hedge Maze untapped for Veil. B lost FoW, Daze and Murktide to A's Veil. Not Forcing back would have kept FoW and Daze for the combo. In exchange, B stripped A's only protection, so this is close rather than a mistake. Baubles aimed at A (p43/p51) did nothing beyond delve fuel, since A cracked a fetch in response. Holding one for DRC delirium was available (Brady's note), but it cost little here.

## Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Against Wasteland decks, end every turn with a land in hand: put back dead cards with Brainstorm (Omniscience without Show and Tell, a second Atraxa, Misty) before your last lands, and never fetch one put-back land if that shuffles away the other." Evidence: t7 p84-89, and t9 Aluren was otherwise castable. Confidence high. Related rules: "Brainstorm put-back with plenty of lands ... put back Lotus Petal, not a land" (consistent) and "put back duplicates (..., extra lands)". This adds the Wasteland exception: two lands are not "extra" when the opponent has shown Wasteland.
2. "When short of mana for Aluren, keep a Lotus Petal on the battlefield as the Aluren mana; never spend it to hardcast Acererak for a scry." Evidence: t9 p102. Confidence medium-high. Extends "Never cast Acererak instead of Show and Tell ... plan F" and does not contradict it.
