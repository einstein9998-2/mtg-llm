# g28 review: A = Alurentell (on the draw), B = Boros Aggro (on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 4 (A's 2nd turn): City of Traitors + Savannah + Lotus Petal cast Aluren (A p20), then Acererak looped
through Lost Mine (Goblin Lair, Dark Pool drain) from 20 (23 after Guide of Souls) to 0 in one main phase.
- Decider 1 (variance): A's keep needed one of ~10 cards (4 Aluren; Atraxa/Omniscience for Show and Tell) and drew
  Aluren on its first draw (A p7). Everything else in A's hand (City, Petal, green source, Acererak) was already there.
- Decider 2: B p11 (turn 3, 2 Plateaus, Ocelot on board) cast Ajani and tapped out. B's only way to stop the loop was W
  open for Swords to Plowshares on Acererak with its ETB trigger on the stack (A held one Acererak; the loop dies and A's
  Aluren then gives B free MV<=3 creatures). B's hand had no 1-mana play besides Swords, so "hold Swords" meant casting
  nothing on turn 2 against an opponent that had shown only an uncracked Misty Rainforest. With what B could see this is
  a close call that most aggro pilots would make the same way (Ajani on curve); it is only clearly wrong if B was told it
  faced Alurentell. B's report calls it a mistake; I rate it close, leaning correct without archetype knowledge.
- B's in-loop line (A p22 onward) was right in principle: free Amped Raptors through A's Aluren, hoping to discover
  Swords or Erode (both MV 1) with energy while Acererak was on the battlefield. It hit Guide of Souls and Spider-Woman.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none that mattered. Minor: A p16 fetched Savannah at B's end step instead of Hedge Maze. Maze would have entered
  tapped, untapped for A's turn, given G (Aluren still castable: Maze G + City CC + Petal G) plus blue for Show and Tell,
  and surveilled 1 before the draw. The playbook already says this (t22/t25 Hedge Maze line), so it is a missed rule, not
  a new one. The keep (A p2) is A's own flag; my view: keep is fine against Boros, which has no counterspells or
  Wasteland pressure on a hand that needs only one card, and whose mana already casts Aluren on A's turn 2.
- B: none clear. Small inefficiency: B passed at B p23 (A's first Acererak on the battlefield, ETB trigger on the stack),
  which was the first window where a free Raptor discovering Swords could exile Acererak; B first acted at p27, the
  same window one loop later. It cost A only one extra room, so no impact, but it is the habit to fix.
- The loss was mostly variance plus information: A drew its one missing piece on time, and B could not know it needed
  turn-2 Swords mana.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Against a creature deck, remember Aluren is symmetric: once it resolves, the opponent can flash in any MV<=3
   creature for free, including Amped Raptor, which can discover Swords to Plowshares or Erode onto Acererak while its ETB
   trigger is on the stack. Loop anyway when they are tapped out, but count their lifegain (Guide of Souls gained 3 here)
   and keep a second Acererak or a Show and Tell line in hand if possible."
   Evidence: A p25 to p36, B p27 to p35. Confidence: medium. Not in the playbook; Brady's mirror note ("Aluren and Show
   and Tell are symmetric") covers only the mirror. It would not have changed a decision here; propose it as a note.
2. "A seven with no cantrips is a keep against a deck without counters or Wasteland (Boros) if it already has the mana
   to cast Aluren on my second turn (City or Tomb plus Petal plus a green source) and Acererak: it needs only Aluren."
   Evidence: A p2 (A flagged it). Confidence: low (one game, it hit). Partly contradicts "a 7 with no cantrips and no
   blue card for Force of Will, slow even if it finds Acererak, is a mulligan"; this hand is not slow, so ask Brady.
   (No new rule for the Hedge Maze fetch: existing "Hedge Maze fetched at their end step enters tapped, untaps on my
   turn and still surveils" already covers A p16.)

## 4. Candidate Boros notes (in place of UR Cutter notes)
1. "Against a known Aluren/Acererak deck, keep W open for Swords from their second turn on when the alternative is a
   2-drop on a 2-land turn; Swords Acererak with its ETB trigger on the stack ends the loop, and their own Aluren then
   lets you cast your creatures for free." Evidence: B p11. Confidence: medium if the archetype is known, low otherwise
   (against an unknown deck showing only a fetch, Ajani on curve is standard).
2. "When the opponent's Aluren is in play and you are tapped out, start casting free Amped Raptors at the first window
   where the target creature is on the battlefield with its trigger on the stack, not while the creature spell is still
   on the stack (Swords cannot target a spell). Order Guide of Souls' energy trigger to resolve before Raptor's discover
   (B p34 did this right)." Evidence: B p22, p23 (passed), p27 (acted). Confidence: medium.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- A's auto macro rule order put "last:Cast Acererak" before "stack:Pass priority", so 35 Lost Mine venture triggers
  piled up on the stack (A p36) before resolving; A noticed and swapped the order (A report). Harmless here; with an
  opponent holding instant-speed removal it would still be fine, but it makes the state hard to read.
- Macro stopped three times on pick budget (A p36, p37, p38), forcing re-reads; ~384 auto picks for one loop. Consider a
  larger budget or a "loop until opponent dead" macro for the Acererak loop.
- Land drops: none skipped by either side. Upkeep cantrips: none. Menu slips: none.
- Engine: no bug seen. Checked: Aluren let B cast its creatures free at instant speed (correct); Ocelot Pride token at
  B's end step after lifelink damage, and B's "city's blessing" at 10 permanents (Ocelot Pride has ascend) are correct;
  City of Traitors + Petal + Savannah paid {2}{G}{G} correctly; life totals consistent.
- Tools: no odds/sim/consult calls by either player; none would have changed a decision.
