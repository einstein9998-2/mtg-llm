EUREKA: Drew one Eureka in the opening seven (hand: Misty, Petal, Brainstorm, Veil, Show and Tell, Atraxa, Eureka). I never cast it. My Turn-1 end-step Brainstorm (no shuffle) found no land, and I put Eureka back as the least useful card. I redrew it on turn 3 and then held it. I was stuck on two mana (Tropical + Petal) for turns 2 and 3, so it was uncastable anyway: Eureka needs 2GG, and I only had green from Tropical and Petal. On turn 4 I drew Savannah and cast Show and Tell off Tropical, Savannah and Petal, with Force of Will as backup. Show and Tell was Dazed and I Forced the Daze (pitching Stock Up), then put in Atraxa. They put in a Volcanic Island. On turn 5 (Tomb, Tropical and Savannah = exactly 4 mana) I had both Eureka and Show and Tell in hand and chose Show and Tell. Reasons: Show and Tell costs 3, so Savannah stayed up to pay for Daze or cast Veil in response to Force; Eureka at 4 mana would have tapped me out into three open Volcanic Islands and one unknown card, with no Daze payment, and Veil would not have fit. Show and Tell put in Omniscience (they put in a Scalding Tarn). Then free Veil, free Acererak, and the Lost Mine loop drained them from 12 to 0 in about 360 auto picks. Eureka was never needed. What it would have done: with 4 mana it could have put in Omniscience + Acererak + Island + Tomb together (extra land drops), but the opponent also gets repeated permanent drops (Murktide, lands, DRC), and it is uncounterable-by-Veil only if I have 5 mana. Verdict: against UR Cutter I would keep 0 or at most 1 Eureka. Its hard cast is 4 mana (2GG, so it needs two green sources, which this mana base often lacks early) against Show and Tell's 3, and the opponent's Daze and Force of Will are the real fight, which a 4-mana tap-out loses. If one slot is kept as a cheat for a hand that already has 4+ lands, I would cut the same card Ponder was replaced for; I would still prefer the Ponder back (cheap dig, shuffle with fetches) in this matchup, and cut Eureka rather than a Show and Tell, Stock Up or Veil.
