# g87 review: Alurentell (A, on the play) beat UR Cutter (B) on game turn 5

**1. Result and turning point.** A won on turn 5 (its 3rd turn). The log has 19 "Dark Pool" drains, matching B's 19 life, and the summary line says "auto picks 445". A's report says 545, which is wrong. At A prompt 53, A had 36 cards in library and B had 11 life, and each Omniscience lap draws one card (Temple), so A could not deck. The game was decided at A p27-30: Ancient Tomb, then Veil of Summer (G from Tropical), then Show and Tell (Tomb plus Hedge Maze U) for Omniscience. B's whole hand was Volcanic, FoW, Heat x2, Murktide, Cutter. It had **no Daze, no Bolt and no Wasteland**, and its only counter was the one FoW, with Murktide as its only blue pitch card. Neither order of Veil and Show and Tell could lose to that hand. Veil first was the right line given what A could see: B had an untapped Volcanic, so Daze was live, and Veil first risks only a turn to Daze rather than a card. If A had cast Show and Tell first and held Veil, Veil in response to FoW would also have won and drawn a card. A flagged this spot itself (a27).
**2. First real mistake per side.**
- A: none. The two-land keep (Tropical, Hedge Maze, 2 Brainstorm, Omniscience, Atraxa, Acererak) needed one of 8 enablers, with 6 Brainstorm looks plus draws to find one. It was close but fine, and A flagged it (a1).
  - T1 Brainstorm (p4) came while B had no land, so Daze was impossible. Brady's "cast it when waiting costs a Daze window" applies; this was a small spend of information, not an error.
  - On T3, A passed in upkeep and cast Brainstorm before the land (p15). It put back Strand and Misty, played Hedge Maze tapped with Tropical already used, and surveiled the Strand away. That kept Ancient Tomb out of Wasteland range until the combo turn, which was good sequencing.
  - A's Atraxa picks were Force, Ponder, Petal, Island, the maximum of one per type.
- B, first mistake at B p29: it **passed with FoW while Veil of Summer was on the stack**. Forcing the Veil (pitching Murktide) would not have stopped Show and Tell, because A still had Tomb plus Maze for {2}{U}. But FoW plus Murktide in the graveyard, next to Delta and Bauble, gives delirium. Two Unholy Heats for 6 then kill Acererak on his enter trigger, and A had only one free FoW from Atraxa. That was B's only line with any chance. Passing guaranteed the loss, because once Veil resolved, FoW on Show and Tell was dead. The playbook's UR note already says "Force a Veil cast on their own turn".
  - Minor (B p9-13): B cracked Delta, then cast Bauble targeting itself. That order wasted the Bauble look. It should have looked first, then cracked or held the fetch.
  - B's other choices did not matter. Not Forcing the T3 Brainstorm (p16) was correct. DRC on T4 with 1 open was fine. Cutter instead changes nothing against a turn-3 combo.
- Otherwise the loss was variance: a counter-light hand facing a turn-3 Veil plus Show and Tell.
**3. Candidate Alurentell rules:** none. Every A decision followed existing rules: Veil first around Daze, land before the committal spell, cantrip before the land, and taking every card Atraxa offers.
**4. Candidate UR Cutter notes**
- "When Veil of Summer is cast with your only counter in hand, Force the Veil and pitch a creature if you can. Once Veil resolves, your Force is dead, and the pitched creature plus Force can turn on delirium so Unholy Heat kills Acererak on his trigger." Evidence: B p29. Confidence: medium. It extends the existing notes "Force a Veil cast on their own turn" and "Bolt plus Unholy Heat (delirium)".
- "With Bauble and an uncracked fetch, cast Bauble on yourself first and crack the fetch only if the top card is bad." Evidence: B p9-13. Confidence: high. It refines the existing Bauble note.
**5. Process and engine flags.**
- No land drops were skipped and there was no upkeep cantrip.
- Under Omniscience the menu lists free and paid copies under the same name ("Cast Lotus Petal" twice at A p41, "Cast Ponder" twice). It does label the paid FoW, so this is a labelling gap rather than a bug.
- The A macro ran out of its 100-pick budget once and was restarted.
- Both reports contain errors:
  - A's report counts 545 macro picks; the log says 445.
  - B's report says the engine hid FoW once Veil had resolved. FoW was offered at B p31.
  - B's report credits B with a Bolt it never had.
- B's Volcanic #18 staying tapped on A's turn is correct: B tapped it on its own turn.

TOP RULE: none for Alurentell. UR note: Force a Veil with your only counter, pitching a creature for delirium.
