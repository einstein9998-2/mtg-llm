SIDEBOARD: In: 3 Consign to Memory and 2 Force of Negation. Out: 3 Fatal Push, 1 Snuff Out, 1 Island. I drew one Consign and one Force of Negation. On turn 4 I cast FoN by pitching that Consign (the only blue card in hand) and exiled their first Aluren. Consign never got cast for its own effect, so I could not stop the later Atraxa/Acererak triggers. FoN is worth keeping. Consign is marginal. The cut removal hurt: nothing in my deck answered a resolved Atraxa, a 7/7 lifelink flier that swung the race by 14 life per turn cycle. I would keep at least 2 Snuff Out or Fatal Push for the Atraxa/Acererak bodies.
EUREKA: Cast against me (I run none). Their turn 5, postcombat main, with Atraxa already on the battlefield from Show and Tell, 4 lands plus 2 Carpets of Flowers mana, and me tapped out with no counter. Round 1 they put Atraxa, I put Orcish Bowmasters (free ping and an Army). Round 2 Atraxa, I declined. Round 3 Misty Rainforest, I declined. Round 4 Acererak, I declined. Hand went from 3 to 0. The legend rule ran only after Eureka resolved, so three Atraxa triggers (30 cards) all resolved, and Acererak bounced itself. They took Aluren, a second Aluren, Show and Tell, Veil of Summer, Orim's Chant, Strand and Ancient Tomb, which refilled their hand to 8 cards. Next turn they cast Aluren and looped Acererak for the win. For me Eureka gave one Bowmasters ping and an Army; for them it was the game. Eureka is excellent against me. I would run 2 Eureka in a deck like theirs.
- Mistake (turn 3): I let the first Carpet of Flowers resolve and Dazed a Stock Up. Two Carpets (mana equal to my Islands) gave them 6-8 mana a turn and powered Show and Tell, Eureka and Aluren. Saving FoN/Daze for the Carpet or for Show and Tell would have been better. I also had no hand answer to Show and Tell on turn 4 (FoN spent on Aluren on turn 4).
- Surprise: Brainstorm tucked back an Atraxa and Acererak, and Eureka then dropped three Atraxa at once. Atraxa lifelink pushed them from 10 to 17 to 19 life in two turns, so racing with Bilbo, Moonshadow and Nethergoyf never worked.
- Daze on Veil of Summer (turn 6): they did not pay and the Veil was countered, but I had nothing left for the Aluren cast that followed.
