RESULT: WON
TURNS: 6 (5 normal turns plus the extra turn Emrakul gave; I was on the draw and the opponent played first)
EMRAKUL: Drew it off a free Ponder on turn 5, after Show and Tell had put Omniscience in on turn 4. It was cast free with Omniscience (the engine offers only the free cast; the 15 mana is unpayable). The cast trigger gave an extra turn, which the engine implemented correctly. The opponent was tapped out and had no cards, so on the extra turn Emrakul and Atraxa attacked. Annihilator 6 made them sacrifice all 6 of their permanents, so there were no blockers and 15 plus 7 damage killed them from 18. I would keep it in the main deck: it is uncounterable, which matters against Force of Will and Daze, and with Omniscience it wins on the spot. Against this opponent I would cut the 20th land (City of Traitors is out already) or one Hedge Maze, not a combo card.
MANA: I never felt short on lands. Fetches, Hedge Maze, Tomb and Tropical gave 4 to 5 mana every turn and I never had a dead turn. I lost Tropical Island to Wasteland, but that only cost one source. The Emrakul swap was fine, and I would not want City of Traitors back.
- Mistake candidates: Turn 4, casting Acererak into 3 open red/blue mana. They Bolted and Unholy Heated it while its trigger was on the stack and it died, a 2-for-1 for them. Casting Atraxa or waiting would have been safer, but the Acererak cast was free and baited out their last two cards.
- Surprise: They held 3 untapped Volcanic Islands and 3 cards on turn 4 and did nothing until I committed. Their second Murktide (5/5) tapped them out on their own turn, which let me untap into a turn where they had no responses at all.
