# Review g12: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 26 (Acererak, Atropal and Zombie beats into B at 2). It should have ended twice before that.
- **Turn 8, A prompt 76 (the strategic error).** A had Aluren on the battlefield and Acererak in hand. B (15 life) had **0 cards in hand**, one untapped Plateau and no lock piece, so nothing could interact. Taking Lost Mine of Phandelver on every venture (a Dark Pool drain each lap, plus Goblins and card draw) wins on the spot in about 3-4 free casts per point of life, roughly 50-60 casts. A chose Tomb of Annihilation "to set up a lethal Atraxa swing next turn". The Tomb rooms cost A 5 life (18 to 13) and B only 3, and B had Spider-Woman (a flier) to chump Atraxa. B then drew Voice of Victory and Goblin Bombardment, killed two Atraxas with sacrifice pings, and the game turned into an 18-turn race. This breaks the existing playbook default (quoted in section 3).
- **Turn 17, B prompt 361 (the decider).** After B's alpha strike, A was at 8 and B had exactly 8 creatures plus Bombardment, which was deterministic lethal (A could not respond: Voice of Victory, and Bombardment is an ability). B had scripted the picks in a shell loop. On the 8th activation the target menu read `[1] opponent, [2] The Atropal`, and B picked [2]. A survived at 1. B now had no creatures, A's Omniscience (on the battlefield since t14) gave a free Atraxa (A P377) and a free Force of Will on B's Guide of Souls (A P389), and A won.
- Verdict: not variance. A threw away a locked win, and B threw away a locked win. B's slip came later and decided the result.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A, turn 4 cleanup, prompt 30**: discarded Acererak to hand size while holding Aluren. It kept Atraxa #2 (no Show and Tell in hand) and two Stock Ups. This cost nothing here, because A could not make GG for Aluren before t8 and Brainstorm (P66/68) found another Acererak, but the discard threw away half of the combo. Its Atraxa reveal just before that (P26-29) took only land, instant and sorcery and left the creature and enchantment picks empty.
- **A, decisive: P76** (above). Other A errors:
  - P251 (t14): hardcast Omniscience at 30 life with nothing to cast (hand: Tomb, Force, Veil), paying 4 life for two Ancient Tomb taps. B's t17 lethal was exactly A's life. With those 4 points, the correct pings would have left A at 4.
  - P379-380 (t18, at 1 life, Omniscience out): Atraxa reveal took only Ponder and pressed Done. It left Veil of Summer, Tropical Island, Lotus Petal, Aluren, Omniscience and Show and Tell, with only 3 cards in hand. This breaks Brady's existing reveal rule.
  - P2 (one-land keep; A flagged it) goes against Brady's batch-2 "one land with Ponder is a mulligan unless Storm/Reanimator". It worked because Brainstorm found Ancient Tomb.
  - P338 (A flagged it) was correct. Atropal cannot block the flying 6/6 Spider-Woman. Blocking Sand Scout prevents the most damage (2) and removes a Bombardment body. Blocking Voice prevents only 1 and leaves 8 bodies against 7 life.
- **B: P361** is its first real mistake. Earlier play was sound:
  - Swords on Atraxa into a known Force of Will (P35) was an unavoidable 2-for-1, since Force is free.
  - Bombardment as the answer to Atraxa was very good both times: 4 pings after combat damage (P142-153), and 7 pings at the end step using mobilize Warriors that were dying anyway (P281-301).
  - Minor: at P187, B declined to sacrifice a summoning-sick Cat to Acererak's attack trigger, which gave A a 2/2 Zombie blocker.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "With Aluren on the battlefield and Acererak in hand, and no lock piece on their side, choose Lost Mine on every venture and loop until they are dead. Never stop the loop for a combat plan, and never start Tomb of Annihilation to 'set up' an attack."
   - Evidence: P76 (B had 0 cards; A was later saved only by B's misclick). Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: this sharpens the existing rule "Default: do **not** complete Tomb of Annihilation ... the loop keeps producing rooms". The bot treated that default as optional when an Atraxa swing looked close. Also check the playbook's "about three casts per point": Lost Mine has four rooms (Cave Entrance, Goblin Lair, Dark Pool, Temple), so it is about 3-4.
2. "When discarding to hand size with Aluren in hand, keep Acererak. Discard a second Atraxa when Show and Tell is not in hand, or a spare Stock Up."
   - Evidence: P30. Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: compatible with "Keep one extra blue card as Force of Will pitch fodder when discarding to hand size (a40)", because Atraxa and Stock Up are both blue.
3. "Against Goblin Bombardment, their reach is combat damage plus one point per creature they still control after combat. Count it every turn, and do not pay Ancient Tomb or fetch life for a spell that does nothing this turn (a hardcast Omniscience with nothing to follow)."
   - Evidence: P251, B P339-361. Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: an application of Brady's "wait as long as possible to cast a spell if waiting is free". It also extends the g11 candidate about attack math under Voice of Victory, which is not in the playbook yet.
