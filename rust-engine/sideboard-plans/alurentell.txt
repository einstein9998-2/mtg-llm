# Alurentell sideboard plans, for Brady's 75 of 2026-10-06 (decks/alurentell.txt).
# Sideboard (15): 4 Carpet of Flowers, Force of Negation, 2 Consign to Memory, 2 Faerie Macabre,
# 2 Orim's Chant, 2 Prismatic Ending, 2 Veil of Summer.
#
# [Brady] = locked by Brady in the project chat, 2026-10-06. [draft] = my adaptation, not reviewed.

vs dimir-tempo
# [Brady] Veil is good because of Force of Will, Force of Negation and Daze, not because of any one
# sideboard card. Prismatic Ending (always +2) answers their 1-mana 3/3.
-4 Lotus Petal
-4 Force of Will
-2 Omniscience
+2 Veil of Summer
+4 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Orim's Chant
+2 Prismatic Ending

vs ur-cutter
# [Brady] Baseline. Their clock is faster, so the combo has to fit into one turn: keep 2 Force of
# Will, cut Boseiju and one Aluren, no Petal (4 Stock Up stay: very good when Carpet is out and they
# are threat-light but interaction-heavy). Variants are in sideboard-variants/.
-2 Force of Will
-2 Omniscience
-1 Boseiju, Who Endures
-1 Aluren
-4 Lotus Petal
+2 Veil of Summer
+4 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Orim's Chant
+2 Prismatic Ending

vs uwx-control
# [Brady] Similar to UR: they kill slower but their mana denial is stronger. Two Aluren out because
# they have many Swords to Plowshares: go for Atraxa first and set up several Acereraks. Ponder and
# City of Traitors stay (City's two-mana jump matters when they attack the mana). Prismatic Ending is
# a little worse here, but it has to hit Tamiyo and Lavinia.
-4 Lotus Petal
-2 Force of Will
-2 Omniscience
-2 Aluren
+2 Veil of Summer
+4 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Orim's Chant
+2 Prismatic Ending

vs doomsday
# [draft] A race between two combos that both fight with Force of Will and Daze: Veil and Force of
# Negation in, slow cards out. Carpet: Underground Sea x4, Snow-Covered Island.
-4 Stock Up
-2 Omniscience
+3 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Veil of Summer
+1 Force of Negation

vs reanimator
# [Brady] Show and Tell is risky here because they have big things to put in (Griselbrand, Archon,
# Koma), so three of four come out. One stays as the high-resource line: Show in Atraxa, Orim's
# Chant so they cannot answer, win next turn; their discard makes high-resource games unreliable.
# City of Traitors stays over the Island: two extra mana lets the combo go with one land fewer (one
# more spell, combo or interaction). Faerie Macabre answers Reanimate and Animate Dead; Veil beats
# Thoughtseize and Unmask and draws; Force of Negation covers their spells. Orim's Chant here is a
# time walk cast on THEIR turn (upkeep, or in response to Dark Ritual): they are a non-blue combo
# deck faster than you, unlike the blue interactive decks where Chant is for your own turn.
-2 Omniscience
-1 Stock Up
-3 Show and Tell
-1 Island
+2 Veil of Summer
+2 Faerie Macabre
+1 Force of Negation
+2 Orim's Chant

vs boros-aggro
# [Brady, tentative] Prismatic Ending is an easy +2 for Veil: it also answers their hate pieces
# (Deafening Silence, Teeg, Containment Priest). Orim's Chant covers Swords to Plowshares, Pyroblast
# and Flute. All 4 Force of Will and all 4 Petal stay: with 3 Pyroblast around Force and Chant are
# wanted, and speed matters. No Carpet (no Islands) and no Consign (no colorless static prison).
# Stock Up stays valuable but after a mulligan against Boros it should put 1 card back (play tip):
# the game must be won the turn after it. Many variants in sideboard-variants-boros/.
-2 Veil of Summer
-1 Stock Up
-1 Aluren
+2 Prismatic Ending
+2 Orim's Chant

vs alurentell
# [Brady, baseline] Aluren and Show and Tell are symmetric: whoever blinks first loses, and whoever
# resolves more Stock Ups usually wins. Ending does not matter against Aluren (with an Aluren in play
# someone wins at instant speed in all but the most corner of cases). Same boarding in the white mirror.
# Cutting City of Traitors is unsure: variants keep it, or play 61 cards (more data, but flawed).
-1 City of Traitors
-4 Lotus Petal
-4 Show and Tell
-2 Omniscience
+4 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Orim's Chant
+2 Veil of Summer
+2 Consign to Memory
+1 Force of Negation
