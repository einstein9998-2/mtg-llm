# Review g88: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn): Tropical Island, Lotus Petal, hard-cast Aluren (A p33-36), free Acererak looped through Lost Mine with only Dark Pool resolved. B went from 22 to 0 in the macro chunks (B p44-47). A was at 30 at its last prompt (B at 5) and 34 with B at 1, so the "finished at 30" summary is a little low.
- **A p36 (Aluren, not Show and Tell): right, and more than a mana question.** B's hand was 2 Goblin Bombardment, Guide of Souls and Sand Scout. Show and Tell would let B put Bombardment in for free. Then 4 creatures on board plus 2 free creatures under A's Aluren is 6 pings on the 5/5 Acererak with his trigger on the stack, more than A's single Force could stop. Hard-casting kept Bombardment in B's hand. B's lands were tapped from its own turn and it held no Swords or Erode, so nothing could touch the loop.
- **B p24 (Amped Raptor, not Goblin Bombardment): the only decision that could have mattered.** Bombardment is an enchantment, so it had to be cast on B's own turn. With it out, B has Guide, Ajani and Cat, plus free Guide and Scout under A's Aluren: 5 sacrifices, exactly Acererak's toughness. A's Force on one free creature cuts that to 4, so B was still behind. But the Force came only from A's Ponder shuffle (A p21; keeping the known top would have drawn Aluren #2), so the line was a live out.
