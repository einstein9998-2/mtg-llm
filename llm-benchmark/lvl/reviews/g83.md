# g83 review: Alurentell (A) vs UR Cutter (B), B won on turn 10

## 1. Result and turning point
- B won on game turn 10 (its 5th turn, on the draw). A spent the whole game on Island plus Petals and never cast Show and Tell.
- **No engine bug** (A's report is wrong). After A's turn-8 Force on Murktide (a113: 13 to 12 life), B Forced back (b114, pitching Ponder) and Murktide resolved. That second Force went to the graveyard as an instant and restored DRC's delirium (the delve had dropped it to 1/1). On A's turn 9 A drew a known Atraxa. A had 2 mana (Island, Petal) and only 3-mana spells (Stock Up, Show and Tell), so the engine auto-passed the turn with no prompt. On turn 10 B cast Cutter, then Bolt to face (12 to 9; the second spell made a hasty Monk token), then attacked with Murktide 6/6, DRC 3/3 and the Monk at 2/2 from prowess: 11 damage into 9. Bolt plus Murktide plus DRC (12) was already exactly lethal.
- **Decisive decision: a62/a64, turn 4 end step.** A cracked Misty for Hedge Maze while B's Wasteland was untapped. B Wastelanded it in response to the surveil trigger (b65). At that moment A held Show and Tell, Atraxa, Force and Stock Up, had Island and Misty in play, and the known top card was Lotus Petal (put there by the turn-3 Ponder at a52). Holding Misty was the better line. On turn 5 A draws the Petal and casts it, then cracks Misty in main 1 for Tundra or Tropical. A keeps priority after the fetch resolves, so it can cast Show and Tell right away, paying Island + dual + Petal before Wasteland can answer. Force (pitching Stock Up) backs it up. B held exactly one Force and no Daze then (b76 hand). The crack also shuffled the known Petal away (turn 5 drew Ponder instead).
- Playbook rules A broke: "Keeping Misty uncracked until the turn I need the mana costs nothing and gives Wasteland a decoy"; "Do not play a nonbasic into an untapped Wasteland"; "the real harm is cracking after a Ponder or Brainstorm that put a good card on top". A instead followed the LvL Hedge Maze rule ("at their end step fetch it over a dual or basic"), which does not account for an untapped Wasteland. The deck's only basic Island was already in play, so no basic could be fetched.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A: turn 1 (a10/a13, a16-17).** A cast a Petal to Brainstorm on turn 1 on a one-land, two-Petal hand. Brainstorm then put back both remaining Petals. Net effect: one combo-turn mana spent for one card of depth, and the next two draws locked to Petals with Misty already used up as the land drop. The second Petal was spent on Ponder on turn 3 (a48). That Ponder found Show and Tell, so it is defensible. The decisive mistake is the turn-4 Misty crack above.
- Keep (a1): Island, Misty, 2 Petal, 2 Ponder, Force with no payoff is a close keep, not a mistake. It had assembled Show and Tell plus Atraxa plus Force by turn 3.
- Turn-5 Ponder (a73, already flagged by A): keeping Petal, Brainstorm, Omniscience on top was reasonable. The Petal is a mana source. A shuffle gives about 37% land per draw (17 lands in 46 cards) and is not clearly better.
- Turn-7 Brainstorm put-back (a102-103, both Atraxas): not a mistake. With no fetch left, any two put-backs lock the next two draws, and both Atraxas were dead without three mana anyway.
- Turn-8 Force on Murktide pitching Stock Up (a112-113): correct. Without it A dies on turn 10 regardless (13 < Bolt 3 + 6 + 3 + 2), and the known Atraxa draws meant Show and Tell was not castable before turn 11 at the earliest. Stock Up and Omniscience were equal pitches.
- **B: no real mistake.**
  - One-land keep (b2): fine. Two cantrips plus two Baubles on the draw, and it found land 2 on time.
  - Wasteland in response to the surveil (b65): right. It cut A's only second land before A untapped, as the existing opponent note says.
  - Delve 5 (b110): forced. UU from two lands needs delve 5, and with 8 cards in the yard only 3 remain, so delirium could not be kept. Exiling all three instants and sorceries made Murktide 6/6.
  - Force-on-Force (b114): obvious.
  - Small slip: both Baubles cracked on turn 2 with no DRC in play (b30/b33). The note "hold it for a DRC or Cutter trigger" already covers it.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- R1 (high): "Against an untapped Wasteland with no basic left to fetch, do not crack a fetch at their end step. Cast Petal first, crack it in your main phase on the turn you need the mana, then cast the spell at once, paying with the fetched land before they get priority." Evidence: g83 a62/a64, turn 4. This refines (adds an exception to) "Hedge Maze: at their end step fetch it over a dual or basic when either gives the colours you need". It extends "Keeping Misty uncracked until the turn I need the mana costs nothing". The new part is the immediate-spend timing.
- R2 (medium): "With one land on the battlefield, do not spend Lotus Petal on a cantrip unless the combo turn is this turn or the next: a Petal is the third mana for Show and Tell. Do not put Petals back with Brainstorm when you are short of mana." Evidence: a10/a13 and a16-17 (turn 1). This is new. It partly contradicts "Brainstorm put-back with plenty of lands: put back Lotus Petal", which applies only with 4+ lands.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
- N1 (medium): "On the kill turn, cast Cori-Steel Cutter before the burn spell, so the Bolt is your second spell and makes a hasty Monk. Count Bolt plus attackers first; the Monk is insurance." Evidence: b119-b127. This is new.
- Wasteland timing (b65) is already covered: "their only land on turn 2 against a one-land keep ... use it before they untap". Not re-proposed.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Not a bug: auto-passing a turn with no castable spell (A's turn 9) leaves no prompt, so the player never sees its own loss. A misread this as an engine bug. Suggestion: print "turn N auto-passed (nothing castable)" and a final life/damage line to the losing seat.
- A's report also misstates the end board ("DRC 1/1, a countered Murktide"). It never saw B's Force-back.
- No land drops skipped. No upkeep cantrips (A correctly passed Brainstorm in upkeep at a36/a37). No menu slips seen.

RESULT: B (UR Cutter) won on turn 10 with Cutter, Bolt to face and an attack from Murktide, DRC and the Monk from 12 life. No engine bug: A had 2 mana and only 3-mana spells on turn 9, so its turn was auto-passed.
TOP MISTAKE: A cracked Misty at B's turn-4 end step for Hedge Maze into an untapped Wasteland (a62/a64). That shuffled away a known Petal and cost a turn-5 Show and Tell for Atraxa with Force backup.
TOP RULE: Against an untapped Wasteland, hold the fetch and crack it in your main phase on the combo turn, spending the fetched land at once.
