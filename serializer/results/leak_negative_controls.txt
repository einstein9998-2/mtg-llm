Negative controls for the non-interference test (hybrid policy, 2 games, seed 4, leak-test on).
A deliberate leak is injected into the serializer with -Dsilo.injectLeak=<kind>; the test must fail on (almost) every check.
  opphand   prints the name of the first card in the opponent's hand      52 checks, 49 failures
  libtop    prints the name of the top card of your own library           52 checks, 46 failures
  forgeid   prints Forge ids of the opponent's hand cards                 52 checks, 49 failures
Without injection: 1964 checks over 30 games, 0 failures (summary_hybrid_leak.json).
Failures < checks happens when the random perturbation leaves the leaked value unchanged (e.g. empty hand, one-card library).
