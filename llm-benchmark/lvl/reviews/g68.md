# g68 review: A = Alurentell (on the draw), B = Boros Aggro (on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 6 (A's 3rd turn). B tapped out on its own turn 5 (second Amped Raptor), so on turn 6 A played City of Traitors (Island + City = {2}{U}), resolved Show and Tell (seq 46) and put in Omniscience (seq 49); B put in Ajani. Free Atraxa took Veil, Acererak, Ancient Tomb, Petal, Aluren (seq 58-62); then an Omniscience-only Lost Mine loop: 20 Dark Pool drains (log), about 80 casts, B 20 to 0, A 12 to about 32.
- **Deciding play, A seq 17 (turn 4):** the second Ponder showed Petal, Show and Tell, Acererak. A ordered Show and Tell, Acererak, Petal, did not shuffle, and left Flooded Strand uncracked (seq 19/20) so the known Acererak stayed on top for turn 6. Correct, and it is what won.
- **Omniscience-only loop (the open question), seq 63:** A cast free Acererak with three Aluren in hand and never cast one. Under Omniscience alone Acererak is sorcery speed, so every room resolves: one lap = 4 casts, 1 drain, 1 Temple draw. Library check: 42 cards when the loop began (after Atraxa), 20 life to drain, so about 19-20 draws; the library was 26 with B at 3 and about 23 at the end. No deck-out risk (margin about 22). A's report says 17 draws; the count is closer to 20. The engine enforced the timing correctly: with a room trigger on the stack only Veil was offered, never Acererak (seq 64, 66, 70, 72). Not casting Aluren was right against Boros: Aluren is symmetric, and B had Guide of Souls and Voice of Victory in hand (free at instant speed) plus Goblin Bombardment in play with 5 creatures, A at 12. Free bodies were Bombardment damage; no draw risk existed to trade it for. Casting Aluren only makes sense when library is below their life plus a margin.
- B never had a window: no white source all game, both lands tapped on A's turn, Bombardment (5 damage max) could not stop a 5/5 Acererak or kill A.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A: none that mattered.** The keep (seq 2, self-flagged) was thin: 3 lands, Ponder, 2 Aluren, Omniscience, needing both Show and Tell or Acererak and luck against a clock. It worked because B stumbled. Ponder shuffle (seq 10, self-flagged as seq 8) followed the existing rule "With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle", with a second Ponder in hand; correct. Small error: Atraxa's instant slot (seq 58) took Veil of Summer over Brainstorm. The playbook already says "Against Boros, Veil of Summer is a blank in game 1"; Brainstorm under Omniscience is 3 free cards. No impact here.
- **A seq 6, basic Island over Tropical (close, not a mistake):** after Island, A's GG for Aluren needed Strand plus a later green source; Tropical first plus Strand to Savannah plus City casts Aluren on turn 6 too and still gives U for Show and Tell. Basic dodged B's Wasteland (which B held). Same question as g10 seq 9; still unsigned.
- **B: the keep (seq 1, self-flagged).** Mountain, Wasteland and five spells of which only Goblin Bombardment was castable without white (Guide, Voice, Ajani, Swords all need W). The deck has about 17 white sources in 53 cards, so about 32% to see one by its second turn and 54% by its third. Then variance: it drew two Raptors, and Raptor #2 exiled Marsh Flats, Flooded Strand and Wasteland on the way to Bombardment. The play after the keep was fine:
  - Raptor turn 3 (discovered Voice) and turn 5 (discovered Bombardment) were its best plays; tapping out cost nothing because Swords had no white source ("holding Swords mana" was moot).
  - Wasteland was played on turn 3 as its second land and tapped for Raptor. Its only targets were A's basic Island and an uncracked Strand, so using it as mana was right. Turn-1 Mountain over Wasteland went against the existing note ("with no 1-drop, play it as the turn-1 land") but changed nothing, because A fetched a basic.
  - Sacrificing the mobilize Warriors to Bombardment (20 to 12) was free value. Ajani was a fine Show and Tell put-in.
- Verdict: B's loss is mostly variance on a risky keep; A played cleanly.
