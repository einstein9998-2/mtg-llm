- Rule 2: check a keep on the combo assembled by the turn you must win (Show and Tell plus 3 mana with U by turn 6 on the draw against Boros), counting Aluren or Omniscience only with a creature.

## P2 See new cards
- Rule 3: in a Ponder look, count a creature or enabler only if your hand can use it (Atraxa needs Show and Tell; Aluren needs Acererak).
- Rule 4: with no shuffle coming, Brainstorm put-backs are your next draws: put back the lands for your next drops, not Force or its pitch. (Its last clause, "do not crack a fetch before redrawing a put-back land", is now covered by Brady's ruling CS19 from the interactive match m02, 2026-10-08: with a card you want known on top, fetch after the draw.)
- Rule 5: Brainstorm does not lower hand size; when discarding with Brainstorm in hand, keep a fetch over Ancient Tomb.

## P3 Keep what is unique or pays for Force
- Rule 7: with Aluren as the route, keep both Acererak through Brainstorm, Ponder and discards; put back Omniscience or a land you will redraw.
- Rule 8: with no Acererak in hand, keep the spare Atraxa at cleanup and drop a land or the second Stock Up.
- Rule 9: Atraxa reveal, rank within a type by what you hold (Show and Tell over Ponder or Stock Up; Omniscience over a second Aluren; Brainstorm over Veil against Boros).
- Rule 10: Atraxa land pick: take the land that makes next turn's combo castable with your one land drop (City of Traitors over Tropical Island when GG is out or Wasteland threatens the dual).
- Rule 11: under Omniscience with Atraxa in play, cast the free second Atraxa for a fresh reveal.

## P4 Land every turn
- Rule 13: when Daze is live, the cantrip needs your only untapped land and the land choice does not depend on the look, play the land first if it gives a Daze spare.

## P5 Wait while free
- Rule 15 (all but the Wasteland part, which Brady accepted as TS10): wait for a Daze spare only when the wait can make one and their clock allows it; cast now when nothing in hand makes mana or they just spent their counters.

## P6 Count mana and life
- Rule 16 (strategy part; the engine fact is EF1): at low life keep Ancient Tomb in hand if your other mana pays, and keep at least 3 life for a combo spell with Tomb on the battlefield.
- Rule 17: count colours, not only amounts (Veil plus Show and Tell needs G and U; Tomb and City make neither; Aluren needs two green sources); Hedge Maze always enters tapped, also when fetched in your main phase.
- Rule 20 (strategy part; the engine fact is EF3): at a Stock Up pick take Petal over Veil when Petal is what pays Show and Tell plus a spare.
- Rule 21: "no Aluren at 3 life or less against Boros" counts your life after the Tomb payment, and yields when their board kills you next turn anyway.
- Rule 22: against UR with Cori-Steel Cutter out, pay no optional life: shuffle with Ponder, not a fetch; play the dual from hand.

## P7 Mana safe from Wasteland
- Rule 24: against Wasteland, end each turn with a land in hand by putting back dead cards first, and never fetch one put-back land if that shuffles away the other.
- Rule 25 (all but "do not channel Boseiju on a dual", which Brady ruled as ML12): also not on any land with a basic type; play it as a green land; channel it at Karakas, Cori-Steel Cutter or a lock piece.

## P9 Show and Tell
- Rule 27: with Show and Tell, Aluren and Acererak in hand and exactly 3 mana into a possible Daze, cast Show and Tell (Aluren in), not Stock Up; bait with Stock Up only before a counter is seen or while a piece is missing.

## P10 Loop
- Rule 30 (defence part; Brady's own hardcast use is AL9): when dead on board with no enabler, hardcast Acererak into Mine Tunnels (the Treasure pays the next cast), then Fungi Cavern on their biggest attacker.

## P11 Force on what breaks the combo

- Rule 32: against Boros, Force Goblin Bombardment when their creature count plus their power on board reaches your life within a turn.
- Conflict 1, the part Brady did not address: Force Voice of Victory only when your combo is two or more turns away (he agreed "mostly" with the spare-blue part, BO4).
