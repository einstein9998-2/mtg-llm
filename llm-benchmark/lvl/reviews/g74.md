# g74 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B)

1. **Result**: A won on game turn 2 (A's first turn, on the draw). Truth log: 21 Dark Pool picks = 21 drains, B 21 -> 0 (B was 20, +1 from Guide of Souls on the Show-and-Tell Ocelot). Line: Boseiju, Petal, Petal, Show and Tell (A: Omniscience p12; B: Ocelot Pride b13), free Atraxa (p14), Aluren + Force of Will taken (p16-17), free Aluren (p19), Acererak at instant speed via Aluren (p21), macro 434 picks. B had a tapped Plateau and no instant; nothing B saw could change it.
2. **Keep (a2)**: clear keep. It is a complete turn-1 kill that needs one resolved Show and Tell; Force or a second land are unnecessary (Brady: one-lander with the combo is a keep). On the draw vs Boros, turn-1 Wasteland has no target (A has no land yet), so Wasteland only matters if A fails to win on turn 1. The only game-1 Boros cards that hurt it: untapped Swords on Acererak with his enter trigger on the stack (Atraxa finds Force, and Force is free under Omniscience), or a turn-2+ Spider-Woman (Petals enter tapped). Postboard Deafening Silence or Pyroblast beat it; game 1 neither exists. A mulligan to 6 is far worse.
3. **Show and Tell target (a12)**: Omniscience was right: no Aluren in hand, so the playbook's "put in Aluren with Acererak" does not apply; Omniscience makes Atraxa, Aluren, Acererak and Force all free.
4. **Atraxa picks (a16-18)**: first mistake by A, but it changed nothing. A took Aluren and Force, then chose Done and left Ponder, Lotus Petal, Ancient Tomb/Misty and a second Atraxa on the table. That breaks the existing rule: "On the combo turn, take every card Atraxa offers". Hand size does not apply, and the extra cards are the backup if the loop is broken.
5. **Aluren (a19)**: only insurance. Omniscience alone loops Acererak at sorcery speed with the triggers resolving (about 21 Temple draws from a 50-card library is safe). The cost is that Aluren is symmetric: B could cast MV 3 or less creatures free at instant speed (Amped Raptor could discover Swords for Acererak, Phlage does 3). A held two free Forces, so it was harmless here. Better order: Acererak first under Omniscience, with Aluren only if instant speed is needed.
6. **B keep (b1)**: Plateau, 2 Arid Mesa, Wasteland, Guide, 2 Ocelot is a normal keep. Correction to the brief: B did hold Wasteland. **Turn 1 (b3-4)**: Plateau + Guide is correct. On the play, Wasteland has no target on turn 1. Hold it for A's first land, used before A untaps (playbook 142). No mistake from B; the loss was variance: a turn-1 kill on the draw.
7. **Put-in (b13)**: Ocelot Pride is right (Guide trigger +1 life, a body). Wasteland put in could have killed only an already-tapped Boseiju, and B's hand was all 1-drops, so ramp did nothing.
8. **Playbook rules**: none new. The Atraxa miss is covered by line 112 and line 99; Aluren vs Boros is covered by line 120. Possible low-confidence addition to 120: "With Omniscience already in play against Boros, loop Acererak without casting Aluren; Aluren gives them free instant-speed creatures."
9. **Boros notes**: none new (turn-1 Wasteland on the play has no target; line 142 already covers holding it).
10. **Engine/process**: no bugs. B's "Cast Ocelot Pride with a tapped Plateau" at b22-25 is correct: Aluren gives B flash and free casting. "Completed dungeons" list spam is harmless. The macro stopped at 200 picks (a26) and was restarted correctly. No wasted calls and no skipped land drops.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won game turn 2 on the draw: one-land Show and Tell -> Omniscience -> Atraxa -> Aluren -> Acererak loop, 21 Dark Pool drains; B never had a window.
TOP MISTAKE: a18, A stopped the Atraxa reveal after 2 of 6 types (left Ponder, Petal, a land and Atraxa), against the existing "take every card" rule; it did not matter.
TOP RULE: none new; at most a low-confidence addition: with Omniscience in play against Boros, loop Acererak without casting the symmetric Aluren.
