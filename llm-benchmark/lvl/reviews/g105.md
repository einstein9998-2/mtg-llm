# g105 review: Alurentell (A) beat UR Cutter (B) on game turn 9 (A's 5th turn, A on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
- Kill verified: the log has exactly 10 Dark Pool picks, and B was at 10 after Atraxa's T9 hit. B's life: 20, 19 (Tarn, T4), 18 (Strand, T6), 17 (FoW, T7), 10 (Atraxa, T9), then 0. A's life: 20, 19 (Strand, T2), 16 (DRC, T4), 14 (Tomb for Stock Up, T5), 6 (T6: Bolt 3, Monk 2, DRC 3), 4 (Tomb for Show and Tell, T7), 8 (T8: Monk 3 unblocked, Atraxa lifelink +7), 15 (T9 attack), 13 (Tomb for Aluren). The two reports agree: "Opp went to 4" in B's report means A was at 4.
- What decided it: T7, A seq 92-96. A played Boseiju and cast Show and Tell with Tundra, Petal and Boseiju still open. B was tapped out, so only FoW or Daze could answer, and A could pay for Daze. B Forced, pitching Murktide (b94). A answered with Veil (a96): Show and Tell became uncounterable and A drew a card (Aluren). Atraxa came in and revealed Acererak, Veil, Savannah and Ponder (a106-109), which were the right picks. On T8 Atraxa blocked DRC and gained 7. On T9 A attacked, then cast Aluren and Acererak and ran the loop. B had one Bolt and could not stop it.
- Show and Tell first, with Veil held as backup, was the right order here. B could only pay for Force or Daze (no open mana), A had a spare mana for Daze, and Veil cast in response also drew a card. This fits Brady's "Veil later to surprise and draw".

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, seq 25/26 (T3, main 1 and main 2): A had Trop + Tomb untapped and Stock Up castable, and passed both phases. A's report says it meant to cast it at B's end step, but Stock Up is a sorcery, so A wasted the whole turn's mana. Cast on T3, Stock Up digs a turn earlier (the T5 Stock Up found Atraxa). Then T5 is Tundra, Veil, and Show and Tell for Atraxa at about 14 life, before the Cutter, the Bolt and the 8-damage swing on T6. This mistake is why A fell to 4.
- A, minor: on T9 (seq 136) A attacked before looping. The rule "Do not cantrip, attack or take Tomb first" says loop first. It cost nothing here: Atraxa has vigilance, B had no flier, and B held 1 card with 1 R open.
- B, seq 68 (T6, close): B played Flooded Strand rather than Wasteland while A had Ancient Tomb out. B's existing note says to Wasteland the Tomb when Show and Tell is the threat. It would not have stopped T7: Trop, Tundra, Boseiju and Petal still make exactly Show and Tell plus Veil. It would have cut A's Ponder. B's FoW on Show and Tell (b94) was right even into open green. A was at 4, B had lethal pressure on board, and Atraxa was A's only out. Surveilling the second FoW away (b100) was fine: Murktide had already been exiled to the first Force, so B had no blue pitch card, and B had only 4 mana, so that FoW was dead. B self-flagged both of those spots, and seq 31 too.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- "Stock Up and Ponder are sorceries: only Brainstorm, Veil and Force wait for their end step. If you pass your main phase with Stock Up and three open mana, the turn's mana is gone." Evidence: g105 A seq 25/26, which cost a full turn and about 10 life. Confidence: high. This is an instance of the existing rule "when waiting costs you something (... mana that would go unused, a turn), cast it", so it could go in as a note under that principle rather than as a new rule.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
- "Against a lifelink Atraxa blocking at low life, burn to the face does not win, because the lifelink is simultaneous. The lethal line is to Bolt your own blocked attacker after blocks, so Atraxa deals no damage while the unblocked Monk (prowess +1) tramples in." Evidence: B T8 plan (b120/b130). A was at 4, the Monk would have been 4/4, and A held 2 FoW plus Stock Up, so A could have countered the Bolt. Confidence: low. It only works once the engine gives a priority window after blockers (see 5).

## 5. Process and engine flags
- No priority after blockers (known harness item, confirmed): log prompts 130 (B attacks), 131 (A blocks DRC), 132 (B in Main 2). Neither player got a prompt in the declare-blockers or combat-damage step, although B held Bolt with Volcanic #67 untapped. This is a real engine gap, and it removed B's only lethal line. The outcome probably holds anyway, because A could have Forced the Bolt.
- Auto-payment taps Ancient Tomb first. T5 (Stock Up, seq 43): unavoidable. T7 (Show and Tell, seq 92): Tundra + Trop + Boseiju could have paid, but then Ponder would have had no blue source. T9 (Aluren, seq 137): avoidable and also an overpayment. The engine tapped Trop, Tomb, Boseiju and Island for {2}{G}{G}, left 1 mana floating and cost 2 life; Trop + Boseiju + Tundra + Island would have cost 0. That is 6 life over three casts. At 6 instead of 4 on T8, B's self-Bolt line would no longer have been lethal.
- A's report is wrong about "Cast Acererak" (seq 134): the hard cast is legal, because Lotus Petal makes B. A's discard pick at seq 119 ran over 120s; the re-sent pick was rejected as stale (bad SEQ), safely. B's auto macro stopped at its 60-pick budget (b146), which did no harm. Neither side skipped a land drop or cast a cantrip in upkeep. Small slip: on T5 A played Tundra before Stock Up, though Trop + Tomb could pay for it. No effect.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on turn 9 with Show and Tell into Force answered by Veil, Atraxa, then Aluren and Acererak (10 Dark Pool drains = B's 10 life).
TOP MISTAKE: A seq 25/26, Stock Up held through T3 (a sorcery), which wasted the turn and left A at 4 life.
TOP RULE: Stock Up and Ponder are sorceries: if you pass the main phase with Stock Up and three open mana, that turn's mana is gone (an instance of the existing "cast it when waiting costs mana" rule).
