# g82 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn). A was at 11 facing Ajani 3/4 flier, Cat Warrior, Guide, Voice, Ocelot and a Cat, which is lethal next turn, so jamming was forced. The deciding sequence: T4 Brainstorm (A p19), Misty crack to shuffle the put-backs (p25), then Ponder kept Acererak on top and drew it (p29-30). On T6: Savannah, Show and Tell for Aluren (p45), free Acererak (p47). B fetched an untapped Plateau with the Windswept Heath that Show and Tell had just given it and cast Swords on Acererak with the trigger on the stack (B p50-53). A answered with Force of Will (p54), pitching Brainstorm and keeping the second Force (p55). The Lost Mine macro then ran.
- Macro check: 189 picks = 1 by hand + 188 auto. Each lap is 8 picks (Cast, Goblin Lair, Cast, Dark Pool, Pass, Cast, Cast, Lost Mine) and the log has exactly 24 Dark Pool picks. That is 24 drains against B's 24 life (25 minus 1 for the Heath). 95 Acererak casts, all by Brady's technique.
- Show and Tell choice: A held no Atraxa. The menu offered only Misty, Aluren and Acererak, so the real comparison was Aluren versus Acererak, and Aluren is clearly right (playbook rule). A hardcast Aluren needed 4 lands and A had 3.
- The free land: Show and Tell let B put in Heath (B's hand was Heath plus Swords; Karakas would have done the same). That turned B's tapped-out board into Swords mana. A could not have avoided it: waiting meant dying, and A had two Forces. "Should have counted it" changes nothing here, but it belongs in the count (rule below).
