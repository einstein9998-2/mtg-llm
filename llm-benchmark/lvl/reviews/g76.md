# Review g76: Alurentell (A, on the draw, mulligan to 6) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 9 (its 5th turn). How A died, per the log and B's transcript (A never saw it): A ended its turn 8 at 10 life. B attacked with everything for 9 (Voice 1, Ajani 1, Cat 2, Sand Scout 2, Sand Warrior 1, two mobilize Warriors 1+1), which put A at 1 (B prompts 69-74). In main 2 B sacrificed the Cat to Lazotep Quarry (75), which flipped Ajani, and used Avenger's 0 (78, "ability 2"). That made a new Cat, and with the red mobilize Warriors as the red permanent it dealt 6 (the creature count) to A (80). A had no blockers, and Voice of Victory stopped every spell on B's turn, so A was dead on board when its turn 8 began: 3 lands, no 4-mana Aluren, 9 visible power against 10 life, and a Quarry plus Cat to flip Ajani.
- What decided it was mana. A's line needed 4 mana with GG for Aluren and Acererak. It made every land drop (Maze T2, Tropical T4, Strand T6, Tropical T8), but B's turn-5 Wasteland on Tropical (B 29-31) held it to 3 on turn 8. Ajani plus Voice mobilize dealt 3, then 6, then 9.
- The Lotus Petal would have been the 4th mana. On turn 2 (A prompt 6) A cast Petal to Ponder while its land drop was a tapped Hedge Maze. Ponder off the untapped Maze before the turn-4 land drop would have dug just as deep by turn-4 main (draw plus 3), and the Petal would have been kept. On turn 8 that gives Maze, Maze, Tropical and Petal: Aluren plus a free Acererak, with Force (pitch Atraxa or Stock Up) against B's one open W (Swords). That was a live win on A's turn 8.
