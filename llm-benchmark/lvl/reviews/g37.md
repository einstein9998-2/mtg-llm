# Review g37: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 7 (A's 4th turn): Show and Tell for Atraxa on turn 5, then Boseiju, Aluren and an Acererak / Lost Mine loop with an Atraxa attack (18 to 10, then the drain).
- **Turn 5, A prompt 49 (A's self-flag 48):** Ancient Tomb, then Show and Tell with Island + Tomb, keeping Petal for Daze. Atraxa took Boseiju, Force of Will, Show and Tell and Omniscience (prompts 56-60). That was right with the information A had: a payoff in hand, a spare against Daze, and B tapped out (Volcanic used for DRC). B held no Force of Will or Daze (hand: Delta, Misty, Preordain, Bolt, Cutter) and put in Cori-Steel Cutter.
- **Turn 7, A prompt 99:** Aluren with Tomb + Boseiju + Petal, then Acererak. B had an untapped Volcanic but only Force of Will with no blue card to pitch, so nothing could stop it.
- B's best chance was gone on turn 6 (B prompt 80): it surveilled its only Daze into the graveyard (section 2). Mostly the game came down to B's draws: from turn 2 to turn 7, B held no counter it could cast.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, turn 3, prompt 38 (close, not clear): after Brainstorm, A played Tropical Island and passed. Ancient Tomb + Lotus Petal (blue) was exactly {2}{U}, so Show and Tell for Atraxa was castable that turn. A's report says the opposite ("Brainstorm with my only blue source before the land made Show and Tell uncastable"). That is wrong twice: A had no land in hand before Brainstorm, so cantrip-first followed Brady's rule, and the Tomb + Petal line still existed. Waiting without a spare follows the playbook (B had 6 cards). If A waits, the land to expose is the one-mana dual, not the Tomb (section 3, rule 1), and A did play the dual. The cost was Tropical lost to Wasteland and two turns. In hindsight the jam would have won on turn 5.
- A, keep (prompt 1, A's self-flag): one land (Misty) + Petal, Brainstorm, Ponder, Aluren, Show and Tell, Acererak. Brady's batch-2 rule says "One land with Ponder is a mulligan unless you know the opponent is Storm or Reanimator". The keep broke that rule and worked. Brady should rule whether Petal + two cantrips + both combo halves is an exception.
- A, prompt 60 (minor): Atraxa reveal: "Done" without the second Acererak (A's hand was 7, so taking it meant one discard). B runs Unholy Heat (6 damage with delirium, which was on) and Bolt. Killing the only Acererak with its return trigger on the stack breaks the loop. Close.
- B, turn 6, prompts 72 and 80 (likely mistake): B knew A held Show and Tell, Omniscience, Force of Will and Boseiju (Atraxa reveal). B drew Bauble off Preordain and left Daze on top, then binned Daze with DRC's surveil. That turned on delirium, which forced DRC to attack into an untapped 7/7 deathtouch lifelink Atraxa (B lost DRC, A gained 7). Keeping Daze on top (the Bauble targeted A, so the draw came in A's upkeep anyway) would have given B a free counter against Aluren. A probably still wins (Force of Will + Brainstorm pitch in hand, Show and Tell + Omniscience as a second route), but B threw away its only out.
- B, keep (prompt 2): three fetches, Ponder, Bolt, Bauble, Cutter. That is fine for a tempo deck, but it has no Force of Will or Daze, so it depended on draws. The turn-4 Wasteland on Tropical (B prompt 42) was good: it cut A's only green source.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "When I wait a turn against Wasteland, play the one-mana nonbasic and keep Ancient Tomb in hand. A Tomb played on the combo turn is used before Wasteland can hit it, and if they Wasteland the dual I still have the Tomb's 2 next turn (Island + Tomb + Petal = Show and Tell plus a spare)."
   - Evidence: A prompt 38 (Tropical played, Wastelanded on turn 4, prompt 48 shows it), prompt 49 (Tomb played and used at once, Petal spare). Confidence: medium. Overlap: refines "Wasteland takes Ancient Tomb and Tropical Island: prefer basics or fetches when I am short on lands" and "Opponent Wasteland: cast the key spell first with the lands that Wasteland can hit". It does not contradict them. Caveat: the a36 lesson ("a second green source matters"). Here Boseiju was known on top.
2. "Count Lotus Petal as a coloured mana and Ancient Tomb as two before deciding a spell is uncastable: Tomb + Petal casts Show and Tell. Decide jam or wait on purpose, not by miscount."
   - Evidence: A prompt 38 and A's report (said Show and Tell was uncastable). Confidence: medium (a process rule). Overlap: none. The Omniscience mana-count note covers only the hardcast.
3. "With Show and Tell, Omniscience and Aluren all in hand and Daze live, prefer Show and Tell for Omniscience (3 mana) over Aluren (4 mana). The engine's auto-payment taps every land for Aluren, so it leaves no spare. Show and Tell leaves one, and Omniscience makes Aluren and Acererak free afterwards."
   - Evidence: A prompt 99 (Aluren tapped Island too: 1 colourless left floating at prompt 100, Volcanic untapped on B's side). Confidence: low (it worked; B had no Daze). Overlap: consistent with Brady's "Show and Tell versus Aluren: decide by how much mana I have and which other combo pieces are in my hand" and "the engine's payment taps all colours".

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "Against a combo deck whose hand you have seen, never surveil or bottom Daze or Force of Will to turn on delirium. Draw the counter first (pick it with Preordain), and do not turn on delirium when it forces DRC to attack into a bigger untapped blocker."
   - Evidence: B prompts 72 and 80, combat on turn 6 (DRC died to Atraxa, A +7 life). Confidence: high.
2. "Against Alurentell, Wasteland the green dual (Tropical) when it is their only green source. It delays Aluren until they find another." Evidence: B prompt 42 (A had no green land until Boseiju on turn 7). Confidence: medium.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Engine payment: Aluren (A prompt 99) was paid with Island + Tomb + Boseiju + Petal although Tomb + Boseiju + Petal was enough. 1 colourless floated (prompt 100), and the Island that could have paid for Daze was tapped. This is the known auto-payment over-tap and it is still there. A manual payment option, or least-cost payment, would fix it.
- A's report says "hand stayed at 8 with no discard prompt". In fact A ended turn 5 with exactly 7 cards (Brainstorm, Force of Will, Aluren, Omniscience, Boseiju, Show and Tell, Acererak). A miscounted; no bug.
- B's report says the summary line lists odds/sim calls it did not make. The final "YOU WON ... odds calls 1, sim calls 5" line is A's (A's report confirms 1 odds and 5 sim calls). No bug.
- No land drop skipped by either side, no upkeep cantrips. The auto macro handled about 294 loop picks cleanly, with Lost Mine chosen every lap.
