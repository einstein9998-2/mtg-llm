# Petal/Chant list again, with a land trimmed instead of a Ponder (2026-10-15)

Brady's follow-up to `REPORT-pe-list.md`: "if I want 3 chant I feel like I want some petal so I have enough white sources. Maybe don't need more mana sources total though because it plays around daze, try trimming boseiju instead of ponder". Same list as before (2 Petal, 3 Chant, 0 Prismatic Ending, 3 Aluren, 4 Veil, 4 Carpet of Flowers), post-board game 2, 12 LLM-vs-LLM games (4 per opponent, 2 on the play and 2 on the draw), Sonnet agents on both seats, same briefs, rulings and menus as the first Petal/Chant wave. No same-build control arm.

## What changed from the first wave
The first wave kept Ponder as the only card that gave way when the numbers did not add to 60. Here the extra cut is a land instead (`plans/plan-pe2-*.txt`):
- **UR:** the usual UR plan already boards Boseiju, Who Endures out, so Boseiju could not be cut a second time. The extra cut is the basic Island. Ponder stays at 4. (First wave: Ponder 3.)
- **UW:** cut Boseiju and one Ponder instead of two Ponder. Ponder is 3. (First wave: Ponder 2.)
- **UB:** unchanged from the first wave, because the numbers already added up to 60 with no extra cut. So the 4 UB games here and the 4 UB games there are the same list.

Those are my reading of "trim Boseiju instead of Ponder", the lands being the only thing that Boseiju could be cut for; if Brady meant Boseiju out of every matchup's 60 (including UR, where it was already out) say so.

|---|---|---|---|

Twelve games cannot give a win rate (95% interval about ±28 to 37 points). 5-7 against 8-4 is within noise, but the whole 13-11 across 24 games is a list near a coin flip against these decks, and the drop is in UW (3-1 to 1-3) and UB (2-2 to 1-3). The only like-for-like comparison is UB, 3-5 over 8 games.

## What the changed cards did
- **Orim's Chant (3):** in hand in 6 of 12 games (n2, n5, n7, n10, n11, n12), cast in 4 (n2, n7, n11, n12). The three games where it was cast and the loop followed were wins: n2 (UR), n7 (UB, two cast) and n11 (UW, Chant then Show and Tell, then Acererak) each had Chant resolve into a tapped-out or one-counter hand, with the loop finishing it. n12 cast it and lost, because Phelia was flashed in response (Chant stops casting, not a spell already on the stack). In the two games where it was held and never cast (n5, n10) there was no white source: the Savannah was Wastelanded in n5, and in n10 it came on the last turn. Both of those were losses. This matches the first wave: Chant is the best card when it can be cast and a dead card when white is gone.
- **Lotus Petal (2):** drawn in 7 games (n1, n3, n4, n5, n6, n7, n12), used as the Daze spare or Veil backup on the combo turn (n1, n6, n7), the green for Carpet (n4, n12), the third mana for Show and Tell (n3, n12), or cast and left (n5). The players mostly said one Petal did the work and the second did nothing. n8 and n9 drew none and said they wanted one. Brady's reason for keeping Petal with 3 Chant was white sources: Petal makes any color, but no player cast a Chant off a Petal. The n10 player dug for a second Petal to make WW for a kicked Chant and missed.
- **Aluren (3):** one copy seen in most games, the other two stayed in the library. All five wins ended in the Aluren + Acererak loop (hard cast on Carpet mana in n1, n2, n4; put in with Show and Tell in n7, n11). The n11 player saw all three copies by turn 3 and said the problem was green sources, not copies. No player said three was too few; the n8 player would play fewer, since a symmetrical enchantment is poor when behind against a creature deck.
- **No Prismatic Ending:** five of the twelve players said they missed it, and all five games were losses: n6 and n8 (UB: Nethergoyf, Moonshadow, Bilbo), n9, n10 and n12 (UW: Tamiyo, Phelia, Lavinia). The first wave had one such game in twelve. The other seven said it was not missed (five of them wins: n1, n2, n4, n7, n11). UW is where the Ending gap is largest: the UW decks board Lavinia, Erode and Pyroblast against this list, and Lavinia caps Aluren and Show and Tell at the Alurentell side's land count.

## Land trim
Nobody named the Island or the Boseiju as the missing card. But four of the eight UR and UW players (n3, n4, n9, n10) described the mana as thin or stuck on lands (Wasteland on Ancient Tomb and Tropical Island in n3 and n10; a Stifled Flooded Strand and no land until turn 7 in n9; "two lands plus Carpet all game" in n4, a win). In each of those losses the lands were killed by Wasteland or Stifle rather than missing from the deck. I cannot show the trim made it worse. What the data does show is that the deck has little room for lost land in these matchups: "Maybe don't need more mana sources total" is not contradicted, but nothing here supports cutting a land either.

## Wins and losses
- Wins (5): Aluren + Acererak on Carpet mana (n1, n2, n4), Chant + Show and Tell (n7, n11).
- Losses (7): n3 (UR) three Wasteland hits and a Force of Will on Show and Tell, then Murktide; n5 (UB) Savannah Wastelanded, no way to cast Chant, took 4 and 8 in two turns; n6 (UB) Show and Tell into a free Force of Negation, Aluren into a free Force of Will, then Bilbo, Nethergoyf and Kaito; n8 (UB) Daze on Stock Up, Thoughtseize on Acererak, Wasteland on the Savannah, died to Goyf and Bowmasters; n9 (UW) Stifle on the only fetch, two lands for five turns; n10 (UW) Lavinia on turn 2, Wasteland on the Tomb and the Tropical, one land all game; n12 (UW) Show and Tell for Atraxa let the opponent put in a second Quantum Riddler, and Phelia, two Riddlers and Pyroblast on Atraxa killed on turn 6.

## Notes from the games
- **Show and Tell gives the opponent a permanent too:** n12's UW opponent put in a second Quantum Riddler, and n11's UW opponent put in a Flooded Strand. This again matches Brady's earlier correction.
- **Carpet of Flowers:** best card in the wins (n1, n2, n4, n7), where it made 2 to 10 mana off the opponent's Islands. It made little against boards with few Island-typed lands (n8: 1 to 2 mana in main 2 only; m7 in the Eureka wave: none until turn 5), and it feeds the opponent when the Alurentell side holds Island-typed lands (n12's UW player fetched Snow-Covered Plains for that reason).
- **Orim's Chant never offered a kicker in n12.** Kicked Chant costs {W} more, and the player had one white source, so I believe it was simply not payable; I did not check the log.
- **Engine and menus:** nothing that looks like a bug. Same menu items as the other waves: `;` in a pick reason runs as a shell command, a fetch-land menu with the Activate option at index [0] and no Pass line (two players, m10b and n5b, passed by accident), and the Ponder and Brainstorm order prompts do not say the first pick goes on top.

## Read
5-7 is not distinguishable from the first wave's 8-4 or from the usual plan, and the one like-for-like comparison (UB, 3-5 in 8) is not good. What repeated in the reports: Chant wins when white survives, Petal is a one-of, the third Aluren is not the problem, and the missing Prismatic Ending was named in five of the twelve games, all losses, mostly against UW and UB. If the list gets another try, putting back the two Endings is the change the players asked for, and a same-build control arm (the usual plan) would tell whether 13-11 is the list or the matchups.
