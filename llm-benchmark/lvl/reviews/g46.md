# g46 review: Alurentell (A, on the draw) beat Boros Aggro (B, on the play), game turn 10

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 10 (its 5th turn). The game was decided on turns 6 to 9:
- T6, A p32-40: Show and Tell put in Atraxa; B put in Karakas from hand (B p34). The reveal had no creature. A took Ponder (sorcery), Petal, Force of Will, Boseiju and Aluren. Karakas bounced Atraxa at A's end step (B p52), and A discarded two Alurens to hand size (p55-56).
- T7, B p57-70: Raptor (its free spell hit Swords, which had no creature to target), Phlage to face, and an attack took A from 14 to 5.
- T8, A p71-86: A drew Show and Tell (about 3 left in 38 cards). Boseiju channel on Karakas, which B had tapped for mana on T7 but which would untap and bounce Atraxa before the T9 attack. Then Show and Tell for Atraxa; the reveal gave Acererak, Petal, Brainstorm and Stock Up.
- T9, A p99 (decisive block): B attacked with 8 power (Raptor first strike, Cat, Ajani, Voice plus 2 mobilize Warriors). Atraxa blocked Raptor: 5 - 6 + 7 lifelink = 6.
- T10: Aluren, free Acererak, Lost Mine, Atraxa attack for 7, then the macro drained 14 to 0.

Why B "lost with no visible cause": its transcript ends at B p98. The block, the lifelink and A's whole T10 loop happened with no B prompt, so B saw nothing.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, p36 (likely mistake, decisive in expectation): the sorcery pick took Ponder over Show and Tell. Karakas was already on B's board (in RECENT EVENTS at p36), so Atraxa would be bounced. A second Show and Tell re-deploys her, or puts in Aluren once Acererak shows up. Ponder found Strand plus two Hedge Maze, shuffled and drew a 3rd Aluren. Without the ~8% Show and Tell draw on T8, A at 5 life with Aluren and no Acererak dies on T9.
- A's other reveal picks were right: Force of Will over Veil (Veil is a blank against Boros), Boseiju over Misty/City (it killed Karakas), and the second reveal's Acererak first. Note on the brief: A did not take 3 Aluren from the reveal (Atraxa gives one card per type). It had #47 in hand, took #76 and drew #82 off Ponder.
- A, p99 block: correct and not obvious. A Cat block flips Ajani with Raptor (B's only red permanent) alive, and Avenger's 0 then deals 6 to A at 6. Warrior or Voice blocks lose the same way after a Quarry sacrifice of the Cat. Only the Raptor or Ajani blocks were safe.
- A's report says "Boseiju'd Karakas earlier" (T6, 2 Petal mana at p44). That would not have helped: Karakas was untapped and could bounce Atraxa in response. At most it saves 2 mana on T8, which is still short of Aluren.
- B: no decisive mistake. Show and Tell into Karakas (p34) and the end-step bounce (p52, it forced 2 discards) were strong. Its self-criticism is wrong:
  - Tapping Karakas on T7 cost nothing, because A's Boseiju (known to B) killed it before Show and Tell.
  - Phlage to face (p65/67) was right: holding it never reaches the "free Phlage at 3 life" window.
- B T9 all-in (p94-98, self-flagged): right. A held Aluren + Acererak (known to B) and B had no interaction, so not attacking just loses. B could not win against the Raptor block: Phlage via Quarry first (A to 2) leaves exactly one surviving block, the Raptor, which A found anyway.
- B minor, p75: took Plains over Plateau from Boseiju's search.

No effect here (Raptor was dead, so no red permanent), but after a Warrior or Voice block it would have hidden a lethal line. The loser again saw no final events.
