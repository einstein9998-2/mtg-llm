## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 8 (A's 4th turn). On A prompts 85-89, A played City of Traitors, cast Show and Tell (City plus Island) for Aluren, then cast Acererak for free. The Lost Mine macro (A prompts 93-97, five macros of about 200 picks) drained B from 56 to 0. B could not respond at any point on turn 8: its hand was empty and its Plains, Quarry and Parlor were all tapped from its own turn.
- What decided it: A drew Ancient Tomb (turn 6) and City of Traitors (turn 8) off a one-land six, and B never drew Swords to Plowshares, Goblin Bombardment or a second Wasteland. B's lifegain (to 56) did not matter against an unbounded drain. Mostly variance; neither side's mistakes changed the result.
- A turn 6 (prompts 31-38): Show and Tell for Atraxa, not Aluren, was right: A had no Acererak, and Aluren alone gives Boros free creatures. Atraxa found Boseiju, Acererak and Petal, which set up turn 8. On turn 8 Aluren (with Acererak in hand) follows the playbook rule "Show and Tell with Acererak in hand: put in Aluren".

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, prompts 2-3 (both flagged by A): the mulligans went the wrong way round. The seven (Strand, 2 Brainstorm, Force of Will, Show and Tell, 2 Atraxa) had the combo, which is a keep under Brady's rule "if you have the combo you can keep a one-lander". The six (Misty, Ponder, Stock Up, 2 Force of Will, Show and Tell, Aluren) had no creature, which the playbook calls a mulligan: "one land plus Ponder with no creature is a mulligan". For tough-spot value, the six is the better question: going to five against Boros is bad too, and B's Wasteland plan does not cut a one-lander off by itself. It cost A about four turns (stuck on one land on turns 2-4 and 20 to 13 life). Bottoming Aluren (prompt 4) was fine.
- A, prompt 18 (close, not flagged by A): B cast Ocelot Pride on turn 3. A held 2 Force of Will plus 2 Show and Tell and was stuck on one land. Forcing it with the spare Show and Tell as pitch would have stopped the token and lifegain engine and still left one Force for Swords on Acererak. Guide of Souls on turn 1 (prompt 7) was not worth two cards.
- Prompt 13: the Ponder shuffle followed the playbook (stuck on one land, no land in the look). Prompt 82: blocking the 9/9 first-strike Ocelot with Atraxa was right (A stayed at 13, not 4, and the combo was next turn). At prompt 38, A declined the instant and sorcery picks at 7 cards in hand, which the "put back what you would discard" exception allows.
- B, prompts 68-71 (likely mistake): B attacked with Ocelot Pride alone and stacked all four Guide triggers on it, keeping Guide x2, Sand Scout and a Cat at home. No blockers were needed (A's only creature was Atraxa, and B was at 35). With everything attacking, Atraxa must still block the 9/9 or A takes 14. A ends at 8, not 13; spreading the counters gets A to about 2. Not lethal, so the result does not change, but it is the wrong habit against combo.
- B's Wasteland on Ancient Tomb (prompt 45, turn 7, before A untapped) was correct. B knew A held Boseiju. Wasteland on Island would have left Tomb, Petal and Boseiju, which is Aluren mana (2GG). Wasteland on Tomb left 3 mana: Show and Tell, but not Aluren.
- B's end-step macro was NOT a mistake (B's report blames itself wrongly). `auto g60b 84 15 0 "Put the trigger"` made 1 pick plus 8 auto picks (log lines after "auto macro started at prompt 84"). Those were Guide of Souls enter triggers from Ocelot's end-step token copies. It then stopped because the next prompt was A's turn 8. The 918 is `auto_total` in llmgame.rs: one game-wide counter covering both seats (A's turn-7 pass macro 5, B's 9, A's Lost Mine macros 904, each counting its first pick). That summary line is written to both players. A did not go off at instant speed; A won in its own main phase.
