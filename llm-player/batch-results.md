# Counted batch: Alurentell (me) vs UR Cutter (Forge AI), games a07 onward

Same setup as report.md (a01 to a06 are in that file). Serializer: final build with dungeon, room, cost and Atraxa fixes. Blunders judged after each game with full information (`review.py`).

## Summary (40 counted games, a07 to a46, seeds 208 to 247)

- **Result: 34 wins, 6 losses (85%).** Wilson 95% interval 71% to 93%. (The first 15 games, a07 to a21, were 12-3; a22 to a46 were 22-3.) Counting a01 to a06 as well (3-3) it is 27-9 (75%), but a01 to a05 ran before the serializer fixes, so the batch above is the number to use.
- **By seat: 24-2 on the draw, 10-4 on the play.** The draw sample is large because the opponent won the roll in several games and I picked the draw whenever offered; a20, a24, a29, a37, a38, a39, a41, a42, a43 and a44 were chosen play-first games (all won). Four of the six losses were on the play (a08, a10, a12, a33); a30 (mulligan to 6) and a36 (one-land keep, three Wastelands) were on the draw. Too small to say the seat matters.
- **How games were won:** 33 of 34 wins used Aluren (or Omniscience) plus the Acererak loop (Lost Mine, Dark Pool drain; `loop.py` drove it); in a07 and a09 Atraxa and Goblins finished after a few laps. a17 was won by Atraxa alone. Almost every game turned on whether the first Aluren or Show and Tell resolved against Force of Will and Daze.
- **How the first three losses happened (a30 is in its row):** a08 Aluren into Force of Will with exactly four mana, then Murktide; a10 Show and Tell traded into two Force of Will and Daze, then eight blank draws against Dragon's Rage Channeler and Bolt; a12 kept a two-lander on the play and Wasteland took the Tropical Island.
- **Blunders (judged with full information after each game):** 6 in 40 games (a33 added: misread Ponder prompt, see its row): a07, a14, a16 (moderate, process), a22 (major, process: two picks chained, the second hardcast Acererak instead of a Petal, which left me at 3 life facing two Murktides before I recovered) and a25 (minor: let Prismari Charm bounce Atraxa). None decided a game, but a22 came close. Plus no-cost slips. Chaining picks is the recurring failure: always one pick per call and re-read the menu.
- **Cost:** about 2,660 prompts in 20 games (about 130 per game; 38 to 209). The loop games are the long ones (about 150 prompts of mechanical Acererak casts); `loop.py` made those picks without my reasoning, using fixed rules (Lost Mine, room that drains, scry keep only Veil/Show and Tell/Force of Will, stop on any opponent spell).
- **Recurring counterplay that worked:** keep one spare mana for Daze (Petal), Force of Will back only when the spell is the one that wins, let Force of Will pitched on a bait spell go, Veil of Summer first when I have the green, and cast key spells while their blue mana is tapped (free counters are the only risk then).
