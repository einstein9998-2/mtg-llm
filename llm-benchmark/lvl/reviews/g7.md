# Review g7: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 11 (A's 6th turn): Atraxa, Goblin, Atropal and Acererak attacked B, who was at 5 life with no cards in hand. Both players mulliganed to 6.
- **Decisive: turn 5, A prompts 41-48.** A played Tropical Island, cast Veil of Summer (Hedge Maze), then Show and Tell (Tomb + Trop) for Omniscience. Under Omniscience A cast Ponder, Stock Up (took Atraxa + Acererak), Atraxa (took Stock Up, Boseiju, Force, Acererak) and three Acererak. B (FoW + Preordain + 2 Bolt) could not interact: once Veil resolved, Show and Tell could not be countered, and a Force on Veil would have met A's own Force (pitching Ponder). This was textbook, and it follows Brady's jam-with-Veil rule.
- **The game should have ended right there, and it did not.** At prompt 89 (turn 5, opponent at 18, Omniscience in play, two Acererak in hand, no Tomb completed) A passed with "Cast Acererak" still on the menu. Under Omniscience (and from turn 7 also Aluren) Acererak bounces itself and can be recast free as many times as you like. Each Lost Mine lap (Cave Entrance, Goblin Lair, Dark Pool, Temple) drains 1, so about 72 free casts would have won that turn. A repeated this at turn 7 (prompts 122/126: opponent at 18, Aluren + Omniscience + 2 Acererak, passed) and at turn 9 (prompt 172: opponent at 8 with no hand; A completed Tomb of Annihilation for 5 drain, which ends the loop because Acererak then stays, and left B at 5).
- Cost of the miss: B got two more turns. On turn 7 B blocked Atraxa with a 3/3 delirium DRC, then at end step cast Bolt + Bolt and Force of Will (pitching Force) to kill the 7/7. A needed free Force, Veil of Summer and luck on the damage count (Atraxa survived with 6 damage on it) to keep its only fast clock. If B had drawn a third burn spell or Prismari Charm, the game would have gone longer. The game was never really at risk, because the loop stayed available, but A showed no sign that it knew this.

Forcing it would have cost B its only Force, but Veil later made that Force useless anyway (flagged below). B's turn-7 all-in on Atraxa was its only out and it self-flagged it.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "With Omniscience or Aluren in play, Acererak in hand and Tomb of Annihilation not completed, you have won: keep casting Acererak through Lost Mine (Cave Entrance, Goblin Lair, Dark Pool, Temple of Dumathoin) until the opponent is at 0 this turn. Never pass the turn while the loop is available."
   - Evidence: A prompts 89 (T5, opp 18), 122/126 (T7, opp 18), 172 (T9, opp 8). Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: extends "Default: do not complete Tomb of Annihilation. Acererak then bounces to hand every time, and Aluren recasts him for free, so the loop keeps producing rooms". That rule describes the loop but never says to run it to the kill. It also corrects "the three-cast cycle that drains once per lap, so about three Acererak casts per point": in this engine a lap is four casts. Dark Pool was "room 4", and the next cast completed Lost Mine at Temple (prompt 162).
2. "Even when the opponent is low, do not complete Tomb of Annihilation while the Lost Mine loop is available. Completing it keeps Acererak on the battlefield, which ends the loop and leaves them alive. Go through Tomb only for the lock-piece exception."
   - Evidence: prompt 172 (opp 8, no hand; Tomb took them to 5 and the game ran two more turns). Confidence: medium-high.
   - Overlap: answers the open question "Whether Brady wants Tomb completed when the opponent is low and Dark Pool is not available" for the case where Dark Pool is available. Consistent with Brady's exception (tax/lock pieces), which did not apply here.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Loop length: about 72 free Acererak casts (3-4 prompts each) is a lot of menu traffic. That may be why A stopped. Consider an engine or tool shortcut, "repeat the Acererak loop N times", so that a deterministic win does not cost hundreds of picks.
- Possible engine bug: no priority window for either player in the combat damage step (B 125 -> 127 and A 124 -> 126 both go from declaring blockers or attackers straight to end step or main 2), even though B held a castable Lightning Bolt. B's block-then-Bolt plan only worked because damage stays marked until cleanup.
- Tool inconsistency (B report): get_card says Tomb of Annihilation is not in the pool, but the engine offers and runs it.
- Not a bug: Boseiju's channel let B search for Volcanic Island (it has basic land types). The Ponder shuffle question is an unlabeled "Do it?" (A 55, B 32); both players handled it.
- Duplicate cast entries under Omniscience: the first entry is the paid one (A 104 tapped Hedge Maze). A used the free entry correctly for Force of Will at 133.
- Land drops: A skipped turn 7 with Boseiju in hand (deliberate, since it was channeled at the turn-8 end step on Cori-Steel Cutter; harmless under Omniscience). No upkeep cantrips. 0 tool calls on either side.
- Report accuracy: A's report calls the Tomb completion a deliberate redundancy play and does not mention that the loop could have killed on turns 5, 7 or 9. B's report is accurate.
