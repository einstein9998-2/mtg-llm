# g104 review: Alurentell (A) beat Boros Aggro (B) on game turn 4 (A's 2nd turn, B on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
- A won on turn 4 at 41 life (took 1 from a Guide attack). A kept Trop x2, Hedge Maze, Petal x2, Aluren, Atraxa. T2 Maze surveil showed Acererak and A kept it on top (seq 6, right). T4 (seq 13-17): Tropical Island, Petal, Petal, Aluren (Maze G + Trop G + 2 Petals, no Tomb life), Acererak drawn and cast free, then the Lost Mine macro.
- Kill verified: the log has 22 Dark Pool picks, which matches B's 22 life (20 + 2 from Guide of Souls triggering on Ajani and on the Cat token; Ajani gains no life itself). The halfway point checks too: at B seq 24, B was at 11 and A at 30.
- What decided it: B tapped out for Ajani on its turn 3 (seq 10) and held no Swords, Erode or Bombardment, so the turn-2 Aluren met no resistance. Sequencing: Trop rather than Tomb was right. Tomb + one Petal also casts Aluren but costs 2 life, and the spare Petal would have been worth nothing once the loop was running.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, seq 7/8 (T2, Petal offered, passed twice; small): A had no other use for its mana that turn. Against Boros, both Petals should go down the turn before the Aluren turn. Boros runs 3 Spider-Woman, and B held one. Boros has no game-1 artifact removal (Abrade is sideboard only), so playing the Petals early cost nothing. A did not get punished (see B below). This is the existing rule "Spider-Woman makes Petals ... enter tapped (cast Petals a turn early)", which A applied only to a Spider-Woman it could see.
- A's keep (seq 2, A flagged it itself) was fine. The hand is a turn-2 Aluren with 8 direct outs (4 Acererak, 4 Show and Tell for Atraxa) plus Maze surveil.
- B, seq 10 (T3, two lands, options Spider-Woman / Guide / Ajani; chose Ajani): close, and probably wrong against a Hedge Maze deck holding 7 cards. Spider-Woman would have made both Petals enter tapped. A then has only 2 or 3 mana from lands, so no Aluren that turn, and B gets one more draw at Swords, Erode or Bombardment (about 9 outs in 52) with W possibly open. B's Boros notes already say "Spider-Woman early makes their Petals and Atraxa enter tapped."
- B's free casts (seq 18-24, Spider-Woman and Guide offered while B's lands were tapped): the menu was correct. Aluren lets any player cast creatures with MV 3 or less for free at instant speed; B's report guessed "unfiltered options", which is wrong. Casting them would not have mattered. Guide triggers give at most +3 life against an unbounded drain, and a Spider-Woman cast now only taps an Acererak that bounces anyway. The "no Karakas, no Wasteland" question is moot: B's lands were Plateau, Savannah, Strand and Heath, and no fetch finds either card. B's keep and T1 Savannah + Guide were fine.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- "Against Boros, cast your Petals the turn before the Aluren turn whenever that mana is otherwise unused, even before Spider-Woman is on the battlefield. They play three and cast them on turn 2." Evidence: g104 A seq 7/8 (B held Spider-Woman and cast Ajani instead). Confidence: medium. This sharpens the existing line "Spider-Woman makes Petals and the Show-and-Tell Atraxa enter tapped (cast Petals a turn early)"; it does not contradict it. It only makes the rule apply before Spider-Woman is seen.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
- None new. The existing "Spider-Woman early makes their Petals and Atraxa enter tapped" covers seq 10. B did not apply it to an unknown hand that had shown Hedge Maze.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- No engine bugs. The free-cast menu under Aluren is correct. B's auto macro stopped once at its 100-pick budget (seq 24) and resumed harmlessly. Neither side skipped a land drop, cast an upkeep cantrip, or wasted a tool call.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on turn 4 with a turn-2 Petal-Petal Aluren into Acererak (22 Dark Pool drains = B's 20 + 2 Guide life); A took 1 damage.
TOP MISTAKE: B seq 10, Ajani over Spider-Woman on turn 2; Spider-Woman would have tapped both Petals and pushed Aluren back a turn (A's matching slip: Petals held on T2, seq 7).
TOP RULE: Against Boros, cast your Petals the turn before the Aluren turn even before Spider-Woman is on the battlefield (sharpens the existing Spider-Woman line).
