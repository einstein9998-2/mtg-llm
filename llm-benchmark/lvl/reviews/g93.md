# g93 review: UR Cutter (B) beat Alurentell (A, on the play) on game turn 12 (B's 6th turn)

## 1. Result and turning point
- B won with 7 damage into A's 6 life on turn 12: Monk #95 3/3 (two prowess triggers, Heat and Ponder) + new flurry Monk #118 2/2 with Cutter moved onto it + Murktide 2/6 (Fungi Cavern's -4/-0 still active). One point over lethal. A saw no prompts on that turn because it held no instant (Omniscience, Atraxa, Acererak), so there is no engine problem.
- The game was decided on turns 3 to 7 by B's three answers. Each was correct, and A's play around them was mostly sound:
  - T3 (B seq 26): Daze on A's tapped-out Stock Up (A seq 25, Island + Tomb, zero spare; A flagged it as seq 24). Daze cost B its only land.
  - T4 (B seq 30-32): Wasteland played as B's only land and used at once on the tapped Ancient Tomb. This was the key play. Without it, on T5 A has Island + Tomb + City = 5 mana: Stock Up (found Atraxa + Petal), Petal, and Show and Tell for Atraxa. B's hand then was Volcanic x2, Strand, Misty, Brainstorm x2, Heat, with no counter, so A wins.
  - T7 (B seq 52/53): Force of Will on Show and Tell, pitching Brainstorm and keeping Murktide (correct pitch). A had Petal spare for Daze (B had no Daze) and had no Veil or Force, so the jam was right: A had a payoff, no protection, and no cantrip left to dig with.
- Stock Up pick (A seq 35/36, A's own flag): Atraxa + Petal over Atraxa + Veil was correct in this engine. Veil + Show and Tell on T7 needs 4 mana: City CC + Island U + Tropical G. The engine lists no mana abilities, so City cannot be floated across the land drop that sacrifices it. Without the drop, the Veil hand had 3 mana. (In paper you tap City in response to its trigger, and Veil would have won.)
- After the Force, A had Atraxa, Acererak and lands with no enabler. B's Murktide (T8, delved Delta, Misty, Brainstorm, Daze, Force: 3 spells, so 6/6) plus Cutter, Bolt to face and the flurry Monk took A from 17 to 6 on T10.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: no clear mistake; the loss was mostly B holding Daze, Wasteland and Force for A's only three plays.
  - The keep was reasonable: 4 lands (Strand to a basic Island), Brainstorm and 2 Stock Up on the play, a triple dig toward a payoff.
  - The T1 Brainstorm was correct: B had no land, so it could not be Dazed. Brainstorm drew Show and Tell and Acererak. Putting back City + Stock Up with no shuffle left meant redrawing both; the cost was small.
  - The T3 jam (A's flag) was defensible. Waiting would not have built a spare, because B's Wasteland was coming for Tomb or Tropical either way. And the Daze bounced B's only land, which meant there was no Daze window on T5, when Stock Up resolved.
  - A's report has two errors. City's mana was not lost: it paid for Show and Tell at seq 51 before Hedge Maze sacrificed it. And the game ended on turn 12, not 11.
  - T9 (seq 65): hardcasting Acererak with Petal for a scry looked like a waste, but that room is what let T11 reach Fungi Cavern (see flag).
  - T11 (seq 81/83): Mine Tunnels, then a second cast with the Treasure, then Fungi Cavern on Murktide was the only line that survived Murktide alone. Goblin Lair leaves 2 mana and no second cast, and Murktide alone is 6. Dark Pool puts A at 7 against 8+ damage. Fungi forced B to find two spells, and it had exactly Heat + Ponder.
- B: none significant.
  - The Daze, Wasteland and Force sequence was textbook.
  - Murktide's delve kept Bauble + Wasteland in the graveyard (artifact and land for delirium).
  - The lethal turn was precise: Unholy Heat had to target its own creature (A had none). B chose Murktide, which survives 2, not the Monk, and cast Heat before Ponder: Ponder (a sorcery) would have turned on delirium and made Heat kill Murktide.
  - Minor: Brainstorm on its own T6 main phase with one land (the note says end step). It cost nothing here.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "When you are dead on board to a big attacker and have no enabler, hardcast Acererak toward Mine Tunnels (Treasure pays the next cast), then Fungi Cavern on their biggest attacker: -4/-0 until your next turn makes them find extra damage." Evidence: seq 81-84; B needed two spells to deal exactly 7 into 6. Confidence: medium. New: the playbook says nothing on Fungi Cavern or on Acererak as defence. It does not contradict "Never cast Acererak instead of Show and Tell" (here there was no Show and Tell).
2. "City of Traitors cannot be floated across your land drop in this engine. At a Stock Up or Atraxa pick, count next turn's mana as City + untapped lands with no land drop, and take Petal over Veil when Petal is what pays for Show and Tell plus a spare." Evidence: seq 35/36 (Veil + Show and Tell needed 4 mana and only 3 were reachable). Confidence: medium. It refines "Spend City of Traitors' mana before the land drop" and "Veil + Show and Tell = 4 mana".

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "With no opposing creature, Unholy Heat must target your own: cast it as the first spell, before the card that completes delirium, on the creature that survives 2 (Murktide), not on a prowess Monk." Evidence: B seq 86/87 (lethal by 1). Confidence: medium. New.
2. "Wasteland their Ancient Tomb even as your only land when they are tapped out and holding City or Petal: Tomb + City + Petal is a same-turn Stock Up into Show and Tell." Evidence: B seq 30-32 (cut A from 5 to 3 mana on the turn Stock Up found Atraxa + Petal). Confidence: high. ## 5. Process and engine flags
- Engine: no manual mana abilities or floating mana, so City cannot be tapped in response to its own sacrifice trigger (seq 49/54). This blocks a legal paper line; worth noting in engine-oddities.
- Both sides made every land drop; there were no upkeep cantrips. A made one opponent-odds call (seq 24: 37% Daze / 37% Force), which did not change its choice.
- B's delve menu always shows "remaining: 1", and its graveyard display is stale while delving (5 separate picks, seq 59-63). Cosmetic.
- Fungi Cavern correctly lasted through B's turn (Murktide shown as 2/6 at B seq 85).

RESULT: B (UR Cutter) won on game turn 12 with exactly 7 damage into 6 (two Monks + a Fungi-shrunk Murktide) after Daze, Wasteland on Tomb and Force of Will on Show and Tell stopped A's three plays.
TOP MISTAKE: No decisive mistake. The closest is A seq 25 (self-flagged), a T2 Stock Up with zero spare into Daze, and that was defensible because Wasteland was coming either way.
TOP RULE: When dead on board with no enabler, use Acererak's Mine Tunnels, then Fungi Cavern on their biggest attacker, to force them to find extra damage.
