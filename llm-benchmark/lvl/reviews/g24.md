# Review g24: Alurentell (A, on the draw, mulligan to 6) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 8 (A's 4th turn). A cast Show and Tell for Omniscience, then cast Stock Up, Atraxa, Aluren and Acererak for free. The Lost Mine loop drained B from 21 to 0 with 21 Dark Pools (83 Acererak casts). B finished at 21 life with 9 power on board, and A was at 12.
- Turn 5 (B prompts 26-30): B cast Swords on the Show-and-Tell Atraxa, then Wasteland on Ancient Tomb. Both were right. The Wasteland cost A a full turn. With Island + Tomb + Tomb + Petal on turn 6, A could have cast Stock Up (which found Omniscience) and then Show and Tell in the same turn.
- Turn 6 (A prompts 35-37): Stock Up (no payoff in hand, so it was correct) found Omniscience. Turn 8 (A 55-57): A played Tropical, cast Show and Tell and put in Omniscience. B could only add Lazotep Quarry and held one Swords.
- Decisive moment (A 77-82): the loop macro cast the second Acererak while the first one's bounce trigger was on the stack. The legend rule killed one copy (A 79). B then cast Swords on the last Acererak (B 81). Only the free Force of Will that Atraxa had revealed (A 68, cast at A 82) kept the loop alive. Without it, the game would have been Atraxa plus Omniscience against a wide board, likely still a win but no longer certain.
- Verdict: a fair win. B played well, but its main deck has no way to interact with Show and Tell.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, turn 4, prompt 22: on the first Atraxa reveal, A chose Done with Acererak still on offer. Taking it meant 9 cards and two discards at cleanup (Veil, which A discarded anyway, plus Misty or a Stock Up), and it gave A a loop creature for Aluren. The cost was small because A drew Acererak on turn 6 anyway.
- A, turn 8, prompt 70: this one mattered more. Under Omniscience, with the loop about to start, A again chose Done with Acererak, Stock Up and Misty/Boseiju still on offer. Hand size cannot matter on the combo turn, because the loop draws a card every lap. A spare Acererak would have covered both the legend-rule loss (prompt 77) and the Swords (B 81), so the Force of Will would not have been needed. Both Done picks break the existing rule "Atraxa reveal: do not take 0 cards. The only reason to take fewer than allowed is to put back cards you would discard to hand size anyway."
- A, prompt 77: the macro's cast rule fired before its pass rule (A's report says the same). This was a process error, not a judgement error.
- A's other choices were sound:
  - The mulligan followed Brady's rule ("One land with Ponder is a mulligan").
  - Island + Brainstorm on turn 2, holding Misty.
  - The turn-4 jam, which A flagged itself: B was tapped out, Daze is impossible for Boros, and the Atraxa reveal netted Tropical, Show and Tell, Petal and 7 life.
  - Discarding Veil against Boros.
  - Casting Atraxa as bait before the loop, which follows the playbook.
- Minor: on turn 8, Island + Tropical + Petal could have paid for Show and Tell instead of Ancient Tomb (2 life, 14 to 12 in front of 9 power). It did not matter here.
- B: no real mistake. Swords on the 7/7 lifelink Atraxa before combat was necessary; B's report doubts it, but Atraxa blocks and swings 7 lifelink every turn. Wasteland on the only nonbasic (Tomb) delayed the combo by one turn. B cast Sand Scout before cracking Flooded Strand to get the Sand Warrior token, and kept Swords for the next big threat. Swords on Acererak with the trigger on the stack (B 81, already flagged by B) was the best target. A's Force was known and free, so no target would have worked.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Loop macro: put the pass rule before the cast rule. While an Acererak trigger is on the stack, pass so it bounces first; with Aluren out you can cast at instant speed, and a second Acererak cast now dies to the legend rule."
   - Evidence: A prompt 77, then the legend rule at 79, then B's Swords at 81 against A's last copy.
   - Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: none. The playbook covers neither the macro nor the legend rule.
2. "On the combo turn (Omniscience in play or the loop about to start), take every card Atraxa offers, a spare Acererak first: hand size never applies, and the second Acererak is the answer to Swords or Bolt on the looping one."
   - Evidence: A prompts 70 (and 22).
   - Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: this sharpens "Atraxa reveal: do not take 0 cards. The only reason to take fewer than allowed is to put back cards you would discard to hand size anyway." The bot read "fewer than allowed" as permission to stop after two picks, so the rule should name Acererak and the combo turn.
- Correction to an existing line (not a new rule): the playbook calls Lost Mine a "three-cast cycle" that drains once per lap. Here each lap took 4 casts: Cave Entrance, Goblin Lair, Dark Pool, and Temple of Dumathoin, which draws a card (83 casts for 21 drains). Each lap draws 1, so the drain loop needs a library at least as large as the opponent's life. Here it was 39 cards against 21 life.

## 4. Candidate Boros Aggro notes

   - Evidence: B prompts 29-30. Without that Wasteland, A had 5-6 mana on turn 6 together with Stock Up, Show and Tell and Omniscience (found).
   - Confidence: high.

   - Evidence: B prompts 27 and 81.
   - Confidence: medium. This confirms B's choices; B's report doubted the first.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- The macro behaved apart from prompt 77:
  - It chose only Lost Mine, Goblin Lair and Dark Pool, and Done on the Cave Entrance scry (it kept the top card every time).
  - It never cast a stray Petal, Atraxa or Show and Tell (81 casts, 169 passes).
  - It stopped four times: at 79 (no rule for the LegendRule prompt), at 84 (40-pick budget used up), and at 85 and 86 (150-pick budgets used up). Each time A re-chose Dark Pool and restarted it. After 86 it ran to the win.
- A skipped no land drops (Island, Tomb, Tomb, Tropical). No upkeep cantrips. The 2 sim calls at the mulligan did not change the decision, as A itself reports.
- A prompt 79: the LegendRule menu lists "Choose Acererak the Archlich" twice with no ids. A kept the original (#113). Both ETB triggers still resolved (two ventures, A prompt 83). This is correct rules behaviour, not the bug B's report suspects.
- A prompt 68: the library-top view shows "Force of Will #0" three times, and B's known-hand view also uses #0 ids. This is cosmetic. The reveal menu lists Force of Will once, which matches the known duplicate-name behaviour.
- B prompts 66, 80: Lazotep Quarry adds 35 mana entries (7 creatures x 5 colours) to every priority menu, which invites index slips. Suggest collapsing them to one entry.
- A's report rightly lists the Tomb life payments as minor. B's report counts its own prompts as about 31; that is fine.
