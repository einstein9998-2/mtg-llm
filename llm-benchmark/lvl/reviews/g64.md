# Review g64: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 8 (A's 4th turn), in main phase 1, at 5 life. Ancient Tomb, Hedge Maze and Tropical Island paid for Aluren (A 5 -> 3, prompts 39-40). A then cast a free Acererak (41) and chose Lost Mine (44). The macro looped 23 Dark Pool drains: B went 23 -> 0 and A went 3 -> 26. A's report says "3 to 16"; 16 was its life at prompt 47 with B at 10.
- B's report is wrong about how it died. It did not die "during its own declare-attackers on turn 7 from 22 life". Its last prompt was 36 (turn 7, attack with Ajani). That attack took A from 12 to 6 and B to 23. A cracked Misty at B's end step (37-38), drew Aluren and won on its own turn. B had no response: its hand was Flooded Strand, Arid Mesa, Windswept Heath and Lazotep Quarry, so the engine auto-passed all of its priority windows during the loop. B saw only GAME OVER.
- Deciding factors: B drew three lands in a row (Strand, Quarry, Mesa) and never had Swords or Bombardment for the first Acererak. A's Misty shuffle at the end step (37), done before the draw as the playbook says, turned up Aluren with A one turn from death (7 power against 5 life). This was mostly variance; the one avoidable B error is in section 2.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- B, turn 3, prompt 7: B played Plains and held Wasteland. By then B had seen Hedge Maze and a surveilled Omniscience, so it knew A was a combo deck and that Maze was A's only land and only blue source. Wasteland plus the first Plains could cast Ajani ({1}{W}) just as well. Then B could fire Wasteland in A's turn-4 upkeep on Maze, before A untapped. A would have had only Ancient Tomb (colourless) that turn: no Stock Up, and the dig falls a full turn behind against a clock that killed A on turn 9. This is the existing Boros note ("hit ... their only blue or green dual ... before they untap") applied to a second-land Wasteland. B's turn-5 Wasteland on Tomb (19) was a fine use of the card; it was just a turn late.
- B's other choices were right. Attacking with everything every turn was correct: A had no creatures and wins without combat, so holding back blockers does nothing. B never held Swords, so there was no Swords mana to keep open. The keep (5 lands, Pride, Ajani; Wasteland counts as a spell here) was fine.
- A made no clear mistake.
  - Keep (prompt 2: Maze, Strand, Tomb, 2 Stock Up, Veil, Acererak): sound. Brady: "In a blind three-land hand Stock Up is solid". Two Stock Ups see 10 cards for 8 enablers, about 84%, and Acererak is the payoff once Aluren or Show and Tell lands. Close only because Veil is a blank against Boros.
  - Turn 4, Tomb plus Stock Up at 19: forced. With Maze alone, no 3-mana spell was castable without Tomb.
  - Stock Up picks (12-13: Ponder and a second Acererak over Maze, City, Veil): fine. A had three lands in hand. The spare Acererak is insurance against B's 4 Swords. A's report regrets not taking mana, but no pick lets A cast Ponder and Stock Up in the same turn: one land drop, and Maze is the only blue source.
  - Veil discarded at cleanup (16): right.
  - Ponder shuffle of Atraxa, Ponder, Atraxa (25-27): right (see rule 2).
  - Misty kept uncracked as a Wasteland decoy, then cracked at B's end step: textbook.
  - Tomb for Aluren at 5 (39-40): forced. Maze plus Tropical is only 2 mana, and Strand could not make 4. B's board (7 power) killed A next turn anyway, so the free-Phlage risk (B had one Phlage in 50 cards, and its hand was all lands) had to be taken.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "The 'no Aluren at 3 life or less against Boros' rule counts your life after the Tomb payment, and it yields when their board kills you next turn: then cast it anyway, since waiting is certain death and a free Phlage is only possible." Evidence: turn 8, A prompts 39-40 (A's flag 39). Confidence: medium-high. It refines the existing "Aluren is symmetric ... Do not cast Aluren at 3 life or less against Boros" and does not contradict it.
2. "In a Ponder look, count a creature only if your hand can cast it (Atraxa needs Show and Tell; Acererak needs Aluren or Show and Tell). With Acererak in hand and no Show and Tell, Atraxa is a blank, and a cantrip second from the top does not save a look whose top card is dead: shuffle." Evidence: turn 6, A prompts 25-27 (Atraxa, Ponder, Atraxa; shuffled, correctly). Confidence: medium. It sharpens "shuffle any look with no enabler, creature or cantrip", which taken literally says to keep this look.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
1. "Against Alurentell with a 2-mana play and one Plains already out, play Wasteland as the second land (it pays the generic mana) and fire it in their upkeep on their only blue source (Hedge Maze or a dual). That stops their turn-2 Stock Up or Ponder." Evidence: turn 3, B prompt 7. Confidence: medium. It extends the existing note "Wasteland: with no 1-drop, play it as the turn-1 land ... Use it before they untap", which covers only the hand with no 1-drop.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- No land drop was skipped (A: Maze, Tomb, Misty, Tomb; B: Plains, Plains, Wasteland, Quarry). No upkeep cantrips. Neither player made any odds, opponent or sim calls. The macro stopped at its pick budget twice (after 12 picks, then after 200) and re-prompted cleanly. Not a bug.
- B's report claims of engine oddities are not bugs. The tapped Hedge Maze on B's turns is correct: A tapped it on its own turns 4 and 6, and it untaps only in A's untap step. The background pick of more than 120s was A's 361-pick macro (wall time 361s).
- UX: the losing player gets no event log for a turn in which all of its windows were auto-passed (236 here), so B made up a wrong account of its death. Suggest that the GAME OVER screen show the last turn's events.
- Ponder OrderTop listed 2 entries for 3 cards (prompt 25; Atraxa is a duplicate). This is known (playbook a33), and A handled it.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on turn 8 (A's 4th turn) at 5 life: Aluren via Tomb, Maze and Tropical, then a free Acererak with 23 Lost Mine drains (B 23 -> 0). B died in A's main phase, not in its own attack step, and had no response (hand of four lands).
TOP MISTAKE: B turn 3 (prompt 7) played Plains instead of Wasteland. Wasteland on Hedge Maze in A's turn-4 upkeep would have stopped A's Stock Up and put A a turn behind a clock that was one turn from lethal.
TOP RULE: "No Aluren at 3 life or less against Boros" counts life after paying Tomb, and it yields when their board kills you next turn; then cast it anyway.
