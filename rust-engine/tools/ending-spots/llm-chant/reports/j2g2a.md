I bottomed it on the mulligan because my lands were two fetches and City, with no way to make WW for a kicked Chant, and a 6-card hand with no payoff wanted pitch/protection that works with the mana I had (I kept Veil and Force of Will). In hindsight that is the one thing I would redo: they showed 2 Pyroblast plus Daze and Force of Will, and Chant (cast on my turn, opponent cannot cast spells, so no Pyroblast, Daze or Force) is exactly what beats that, but only if I have WW (Tundra, Savannah, or Tundra plus Savannah). It was also a fine Chant turn on turn 5 (tapped-out opponent, I had Stock Up). Prismatic Ending: none drawn (their DRC arrived on turn 5, not turn 1, and I was never going to have W for it anyway; my base 75 has only Tundra and Savannah). Net: Chant and Ending are only as good as the white mana. With 4 Chant and 2 Ending in the main I needed 3 or 4 white sources (I have 2 plus City, which only makes colourless). More Chant is only right if I also add white sources or fetch Tundra/Savannah early; otherwise fewer. Veil is weaker here than expected: Pyroblast (they played two) ignores it. Force of Will did the actual work: it won Stock Up (Pyroblast) and Show and Tell (Pyroblast), each time pitching Atraxa or Brainstorm.
