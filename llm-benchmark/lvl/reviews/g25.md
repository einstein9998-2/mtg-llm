# Review g25: Alurentell (A, on the play, kept 7) vs UR Cutter (B, mulligan to 6)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 13 (A's 7th turn). Atraxa hit B from 11 to 4 (A 120). A then cast Aluren in main 2 with four lands, keeping a Lotus Petal spare for Daze and Force of Will as backup (A 121-125), and the Acererak / Lost Mine loop drained the last 4. Life at the start of turn 13 was A 30, B 11. The caller's summary has the two numbers swapped, and B's report has the right ones.
- Decisive turn 7 (A 73-82, B 76-83): A played Tropical Island and cast Show and Tell with exactly 3 mana. Its hand was Force of Will x2 and Atraxa. B Dazed (returning its tapped Volcanic), and A Forced the Daze by pitching the other Force. B's own Force was then dead, because Daze had been its only blue pitch card. Atraxa came in, and its reveal took Flooded Strand, Brainstorm, Aluren and Show and Tell.
- The jam was right (A flagged it, a 73). It matches Brady's "Show and Tell: when you hold protection, jam it now". It was also the only line: B could stop Show and Tell only with Daze plus Force of Will, and two Forces beat that pair in either order.
- After turn 7: Atraxa blocked and killed DRC on turns 8 and 11 and gained 7 on every swing, so B's burn plan was gone. B's Wasteland on Tropical (B 90) cut A to one green source and pushed Aluren back to turn 13. Verdict: A won cleanly. B lost mostly to A's cards (two Forces beat Daze plus Force), not to its own errors.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, minor: on turn 1 (A 6), A cast Brainstorm in main 1 with nothing else to do. A had no fetch, so it locked Omniscience and Petal on top. Waiting until B's end step was free. This breaks the existing rule "wait as long as possible to cast a spell if waiting is free" and cost little.
- A, first meaningful mistake: turn 9, A 93-97. Brainstorm drew Ponder, Show and Tell and Stock Up. A put back Aluren and a Show and Tell, then cracked Flooded Strand, which shuffled both away.
  - A's hand still held a second Show and Tell, a second Atraxa (dead without Show, since the first was already on the battlefield) and Hedge Maze.
  - The right put-back was the duplicates, Atraxa #2 plus Show #2. That keeps the only Aluren, the piece the Acererak loop needs.
  - Ponder then happened to find Acererak and Aluren together (A 100). Without that luck A has no loop piece in hand on turn 11.
- A, turn 11 (A 106), close, not a mistake: A chose Stock Up (finding Force and Petal) over Show and Tell for Aluren followed by a free Acererak. See the flag below.
- A, turn 7 (A 52, Stock Up pick): already flagged by A. Ponder plus Tropical worked, because Ponder drew Show and Tell.
- B, turn 12 (B 118): the Thundering Falls surveil binned a second Force of Will. B's hand was Volcanic Island plus Force of Will with no blue card, so the top Force was the one card that would make the Force in hand live. It changed nothing (A won before B's next draw), but it is a clear error.
- B, before turn 12: no real mistakes.
  - The keep, both Wastelands (Tomb on turn 4, Tropical on turn 8), Bolt face for delirium (A to 11) and Daze-first on Show and Tell were all reasonable.
  - B's report blames itself for "Bolting rather than holding a blue card". Bolt is red, and B's hand (Island, Daze, Force, Cutter) had no other blue card to keep.
  - The only blue card B spent was the turn-4 Preordain (B 36), and that Preordain is what drew the Force. B's loss was mostly variance against double Force.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Before cracking a fetch after Brainstorm, put back the cards you want gone: a second Atraxa, a second Show and Tell, extra lands. Never put back your only Aluren or Acererak, because the shuffle sends the put-backs to a random place."
   - Evidence: A 93-97 (Aluren put back, then the Strand crack). Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: sharpens "Fetch away the cards you put back with Brainstorm if they are bad" (batch 1) and "Brainstorm put-backs are drawn next turn unless a crack shuffles first" (t19-t26). Neither says which cards count as bad. A put back a needed piece and then fetched it away.
2. "Count the opponent's live counters from the cards you know. Force of Will needs a second blue card, so a hand of one known land plus one unknown card holds at most Daze. With the whole combo in hand (Show and Tell or Aluren plus Acererak) and only Daze possible, decide whether one card being Daze is worth a turn."
   - Evidence: A 106 (B held Volcanic, known from the Daze bounce, plus Force of Will) and A 121-125. Confidence: low.
   - Overlap: tension with "If Daze is live and I cannot keep a spare, wait a turn" (t07-t18). Brady should rule first (flag a 106).
3. Repeat of g24 candidate 1, still not in the playbook: "Loop macro: put the pass rule before the cast rule."
   - Evidence: A 128. The macro cast Acererak 15 times while Lost Mine triggers sat on the stack.
   - It was harmless only because A held a single Acererak; with two, the legend rule bites, as it did in g24. Confidence: high.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "When your Force of Will has no blue card to pitch, keep a second Force or any blue card on top when you surveil or scry. A pitchless Force is a blank against Show and Tell and Aluren."
   - Evidence: B 118. Confidence: high.
2. "Against Alurentell, Wasteland Ancient Tomb first: it is the Stock Up plus Show and Tell mana. Once Aluren or Show and Tell has been seen, Wasteland their green dual: Aluren costs GG."
   - Evidence: B 34 cut A to 3 mana on turns 5-7. B 90 left Savannah as A's only green source and delayed Aluren to turn 13. Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: confirms g24 Boros note 1 and covers B's own flags 32 and 88.
