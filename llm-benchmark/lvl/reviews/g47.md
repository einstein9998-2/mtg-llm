# g47 review: Alurentell (A) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 9 (A's 5th turn) with Aluren plus Acererak looping Lost Mine, 15 Dark Pool drains from 15 to 0, at 28 life.
- Turn 5, A prompt 35 / B prompts 37-43: A cast Show and Tell (Tomb + Island, Tropical up as Daze spare) into 7 cards. B Forced (pitch Daze), A Forced back (pitch Stock Up), B Forced again (pitch Murktide). Both sides played it correctly: A held payoff (Aluren + Acererak) and protection, which is the playbook "jam it now" case; B's Daze was dead against the untapped Tropical, so pitching it first was the only line that kept two Forces fed. A could never cast a second Force here: its only other blue card was Stock Up, so a kept second Force (the prompt-21 put-back A regrets) would have had no pitch. B came out of the war with Wasteland, two Volcanics and no threat.
- Turn 9, A prompt 56: Aluren with all four lands tapped, B holding 2 cards and two untapped Volcanics. B's hand was two Volcanic Islands, so nothing could happen. Given A's information it was a close call, not a gamble: about 13% that 2 unknown cards hold one of the 3 remaining Dazes (Force also needed a blue pitch). Waiting needed a land or Petal off the top (16 of 45, about 36%) while B drew too, which comes out about even. B had cast nothing but Cutter in two turns, which points to lands or counters but does not show which. Reasonable. A broke the a05/t18 playbook rule, but it fits Brady's "waiting is contextual" note.
- The game was really decided by B's flood: a 5-land keep with no threat, then Volcanic, Volcanic, Cutter, Misty off the top.

## Lost Mine macro check (first game with the rework)
- Library 45 before Aluren (prompt 57), 45 at prompt 60 and 45 at prompt 61: no cards drawn, no library loss. Cave Entrance, Goblin Lair and Temple of Dumathoin triggers piled up (47 on the stack at the end); only Dark Pool resolved, once per lap (opponent -1 / you +1 each time). That matches Brady's technique.
- Each Acererak went Stack -> Battlefield -> Hand before the next cast, so there was never a second Acererak and no legend-rule event. Lap = 4 casts (Cave, Goblin Lair, Dark Pool + pass, Temple) then a new Lost Mine. Goblin Lair was chosen over Mine Tunnels every time. B had no castable response and was auto-passed throughout.
- The macro stopped twice at its 60-pick budget (prompts 60, 61, "pick budget used up"). A re-read the prompt and resumed correctly. This is harmless, but a 15-life kill needs about 125 picks; raise the budget to about 200 or have the macro restart itself.
- Cosmetic: "completed dungeons" goes up when the marker leaves Temple even though Temple's draw trigger is still pending. Only Tomb completion matters to Acererak, so this has no effect.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: turn 5, prompt 33. Library top was a known Force of Will #28 (A put it back with Brainstorm on turn 3 on purpose), and A cracked Misty main phase for a Tropical, shuffling the Force away. Show and Tell only needed Tomb + Island. An uncracked Misty is still a Daze spare (crack it in response to Daze), and Misty would also have stayed a Wasteland decoy. A drew Omniscience on turn 7 instead of Force. Impact was small, because Force would have needed a blue pitch later. It breaks the existing rule "the real harm is cracking after a Ponder or Brainstorm that put a good card on top".
- B: no clear mistake. The keep was weak (see flags), and B passed on Dazing A's tapped-out turn-3 Brainstorm (close). The rest was flood. Pitching Murktide was correct: a resolved Show and Tell from a hand with Aluren and Acererak ends the game.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "An uncracked fetch is your Daze spare. When a Brainstorm or Ponder left a card you want on top, cast the spell with your other lands and crack the fetch only in response to Daze."
   Evidence: turn 5, prompt 33. Confidence: medium. Partly duplicates "the real harm is cracking after a Ponder or Brainstorm that put a good card on top" and "Keeping Misty uncracked until the turn I need the mana costs nothing". What is new is counting the fetch as the spare. The engine offers fetch activations at every priority window (for example prompts 8 and 12), so this should work; check it once.
2. "Wait for a spare only when the wait can produce one: a land, Petal or cantrip in hand, or a fetch to crack. With none of those, no clock on you and the opponent at two cards or fewer, cast Aluren now; each turn you wait gives them a draw as well."
   Evidence: turn 9, prompt 56, about 13% risk to jam against about the same risk to wait. Confidence: low (one game, and it worked). Qualifies "Do not tap out for Aluren when Daze may be in hand and I have no spare mana (a05)" and "If Daze is live and I cannot keep a spare, wait a turn". Consistent with Brady's "only if waiting helps you more than the opponent".
