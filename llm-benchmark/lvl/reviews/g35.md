# g35 review: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 1, before B got a turn. A kept 7 (Hedge Maze, Ancient Tomb, Lotus Petal, Aluren, Boseiju, Show and Tell, Acererak).
At A prompt 3 to 5 it played Ancient Tomb, then Petal (U), then Show and Tell. At prompt 6 it put in Aluren. B put in Murktide Regent (B prompt 7).
At A prompt 8 it cast Acererak for free and chose Lost Mine (prompt 11). The auto macro then looped Goblin Lair and Dark Pool and drained B from 20 to 0.
The decision that won was the turn-1 jam at A prompt 5. It was right:
- Daze was impossible because B had no land. Wasteland could not touch the Tomb before the spell. The only possible answer was Force of Will: 4 copies, about 40% to be in a 7, and B also needs a blue card to pitch. A's own opp-odds tool gave 40%.
- Waiting had no upside. A's hand had no Force or Veil to find, and the 7 could not hardcast Aluren on turn 1 (3 mana available, Aluren costs 4).
- The turn-2 alternative was Tomb, Boseiju and Petal into a hardcast Aluren. By then B would have a land and Daze would be live, Wasteland could hit Tomb, and Force would still be possible. Every risk is the same or worse a turn later.
- Aluren over Acererak at prompt 6 was the only correct put: with Aluren in play, Acererak loops for free. Tomb over Hedge Maze as the land was forced, since Maze enters tapped.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none. The line was the fastest one possible and the one most resistant to interaction.
- B: none. The keep (B prompt 2) was a normal UR 7: Flooded Strand, Wasteland, Ponder, Preordain, DRC, Cori-Steel Cutter, Murktide.
  - It has 2 lands, 2 cantrips and 3 threats, but only one blue source and no Force of Will or Daze. Against an unknown 7 it is a clear keep.
  - Against a known combo opponent it is still a keep. Mulliganing to 6 to dig for 4 Force of Will gives roughly a 35 to 40% chance to see one, and the cost is a much worse hand against every slower A draw.
  - The mulligan decision did not matter here. On the draw against a turn-1 Show and Tell, any hand without Force of Will loses, Daze included, because B has no land to return. This loss was variance: A drew a nut 7 with Tomb, Petal, Show and Tell, Aluren and Acererak.
- B's Show and Tell put (Murktide) and its passes with a free DRC on offer (prompts 9 to 17) did not matter. Nothing in B's hand could stop the loop. A land put in (Wasteland on the already tapped Tomb, or Strand) would not have helped either, because the loop needs no mana.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "On the play, turn 1: the opponent has no land, so Daze is impossible and only Force of Will can stop you. If Tomb plus Petal casts Show and Tell (or Aluren) with the other half of the combo in hand, cast it now; waiting adds Daze and Wasteland and finds no protection."
   - Evidence: g35 A prompts 3 to 6.
   - Confidence: high.
   - Mostly duplicates "Look at what the opponent has untapped: with only a Wasteland out, Daze is impossible" and "When the opponent has only Wasteland (no Island), Daze is impossible". It extends them to the no-land case. It does not contradict "Do not cast a raw Show and Tell without a payoff or protection in hand", because here the payoff (Aluren plus Acererak) is in hand. Add it only as a one-line extension.
