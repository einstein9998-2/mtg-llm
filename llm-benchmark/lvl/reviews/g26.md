# Review g26: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 8 (A's 4th turn). Show and Tell for Atraxa on turn 6, then on turn 8 Atraxa attacked (A 5 -> 12, B 29 -> 22), Aluren resolved, and the Acererak Lost Mine loop drained B to 0. B had 0 cards in hand and could not interact.
- **Decider 1, B prompt 78 (turn 7):** B had flipped a second Ajani into Nacatl Avenger (loyalty 3) with 8 creatures and two red Amped Raptors on board, and A was at 6. Avenger's 0 ability makes a Cat, then deals damage equal to B's creature count (9) to any target. That was lethal, and Force of Will cannot counter an ability. B picked `[1] ... ability 1`, which was the +2 (log: "#85 counters Loyalty +2", Cats +1/+1). Without the misclick B wins.
- **Misclick verdict: mostly a harness labelling problem, plus a player misread.** The menu showed only `Activate Ajani, Nacatl Avenger [v85] ability 1` and `ability 2`, with no loyalty cost and no rules text. The numbering skips 0 (index 0 is never offered), so "ability 1" is the first printed ability (+2) and "ability 2" is the 0. The same labels appeared in g11 (B prompt 103, where "ability 2" was the 0) and were flagged in that review, so this is a known issue that is still unfixed. The player could still have worked it out: at loyalty 3 only +2 and 0 are legal, and listed in printed order the second entry has to be the 0. B's report contradicts itself: the Mistakes line says ability 1 was the +2, the Tools line says "ability 1" = 0 ability.
- **Decider 2, A prompt 65 (turn 7, B's turn):** B cast its second Ajani, Nacatl Pariah with the first Ajani in play, 8 creatures on board and its last card in hand. A was at 6 with Force of Will plus Brainstorm and Show and Tell as pitch cards, and passed. Under the legend rule B keeps the new Ajani; the old one is a Cat, so its death flips the new one. This is the standard Boros reach, and B pulled it off in g11. A should have used Force of Will here, pitching Brainstorm, at a cost of 1 life. That leaves B with 0 cards and a board that cannot get through Atraxa (see section 2). The turn-8 win needed only Tropical, Petal, Aluren and Acererak, all still in hand. A won only because B misclicked; A's report does not see the threat ("never used Ajani's 0 burst").
