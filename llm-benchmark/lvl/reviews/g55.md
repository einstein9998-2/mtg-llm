# g55 review: Alurentell (A) vs UR Cutter (B), B wins on game turn 12

## 1. Result and turning point
B won: DRC (3/3 then 4/4 flier with Cutter) hit for 3+3+3+4, Prismari Charm (B 79) took A from 1 to 0. A's life: fetch 1, Tomb 2, FoW 1, Tomb 2, DRC 13, Charm 1.
- T3 counter war (A 27/31, B 29/33-35). A jammed Show and Tell (Aluren put-in, Acererak in hand) with Tomb + Island and no spare; B Dazed (its only land, tapped), A Forced the Daze (pitch Ponder), B Forced Show and Tell (pitch Murktide). B held Daze + FoW + FoW + Murktide at that point.
- T7 (A 53, B 54): A cast Petal + Aluren with Island spare for Daze; B's second FoW (pitch Murktide #2) ended A's last route. A then held Atraxa and Acererak with no black source and never cast another spell that mattered.
- Verdict: mostly variance. B kept three free counters plus two pitch cards off a one-land six. Against that exact hand, waiting on T3 also loses (T5 S&T with spare: Daze paid, FoW, A's FoW, B's FoW #2).

## 2. Judgements asked for, and first real mistake per side
- B mulligan (B 2): zero-lander, clear mulligan. One-land six keep (B 3): right. Mull to 5 is worse and the hand has FoW, Daze, Bauble and a one-drop that are all live with one land against combo. It was punishable (B had one land until T4 and two until T6) but A had no Wasteland. Bottoming Daze (B 4) over Cutter is fine: a second Daze would bounce the only land. Tough-spot value: close keep, worth one Brady answer (B already flagged seq 3).
- A T3 jam vs wait (A 27, A flagged 26): close and defensible. Jamming loses only to two counters; waiting a turn to T5 (Savannah gives the spare) beats Daze + one FoW but costs 3 life to DRC and gives B a draw and a Wasteland window on Tomb. Brady's "jam S&T when you hold protection" and "spare beats Daze, wait a turn" pull in opposite directions here; this hand is a good case for him. Forcing the Daze (A 31) was right: a resolved S&T wins at once.
- A Tomb payments: both were the only way to reach 3 and 4 mana (Island + Tomb on T3; Tomb + Savannah + Petal on T7). Not decisive: at 5 life on B's T12 the 4/4 trample flier plus Charm was still exactly lethal.
- A T7 Petal + Aluren with Island spare: right. No FoW or Veil left, a 3-a-turn flier at 10 life, and B tapped out on its own turn so only free counters remained; nothing better was coming.
- A first real mistake (small): T1 (A 6-8) cracked Strand and cast Brainstorm main phase. Holding the fetch and doing both at B's T2 end step was free (same Island, Wasteland decoy, sees B's turn). Fetch target (A 7): Island over Tropical left Savannah as the only green source, so Aluren was uncastable until a Petal on T7. Neither changed the result against B's counters.
- B first real mistake (small): T2 (B 14) cast Bauble before playing Delta and DRC, so the Bauble cast missed a DRC surveil; then activated it on A (B 24) after Delta was already cracked. Better: Delta, Volcanic, DRC, then Bauble (surveil 1), activate on self or hold for a later DRC trigger.
- B Daze first on the tapped-out S&T (B 29) and pitching Murktide while keeping FoW #2 (B 35) were both right and won the game.
