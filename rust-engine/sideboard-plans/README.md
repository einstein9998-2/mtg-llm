# Sideboard plans (PROVISIONAL, for Brady to review)

One file per deck (`<deck stem>.txt`), one `vs <opposing deck stem>` section per matchup, `-N card`
for cards that leave the main deck and `+N card` for cards that come in from the sideboard. Plans
are applied to the base 75 before games 2 and 3 (never stacked), and the swap is one for one so the
deck stays at 60. `vs *` is a fallback for any opponent. No section means no change.

Written from Legacy knowledge and the card texts, not from tournament data: these are my reading of
what each 15 is for, limited to the 15 cards that were actually in the tournament list. Edit freely;
`bo3 --check` validates every plan against the real lists (cards exist, one-for-one, 60 main,
at most 15 side, at most four copies).

Only matchups with Alurentell are covered so far (it is the current focus). The other 21 pairs of
the eight decks play their main deck in every game until someone writes plans for them.
