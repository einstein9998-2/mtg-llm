## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 5 (its third turn), in one main phase: Aluren, then about 80 free Acererak casts looping Lost Mine of Phandelver, draining B from 20 to 0 through 20 Dark Pool visits. B never had a second land and never cast a spell on A's turn.
- Decided by B's keep (B prompt 2) and its turn-4 tap-out (B prompt 19). B kept a 1-land seven on the draw (Volcanic, Brainstorm x2, Preordain, DRC, Murktide, Prismari Charm) and never found land #2. Main-phase Brainstorm still had about a 70% chance to find the land drop (3 cards, about 17 lands in 49), so the choice was close, not clearly wrong. B flagged it itself.

It held Petal unused (A prompts 17 and 18), drew Acererak on T5, and played Savannah, Petal, then Aluren with Tomb + Maze + Savannah (A prompt 28). That left Petal spare against Daze, which was live: a tapped Island can still be returned. Acererak followed for free. This follows "Spare mana beats Daze" exactly.
