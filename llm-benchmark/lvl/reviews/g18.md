# Review g18: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 6 (A's 3rd turn): Show and Tell for Omniscience, then free Brainstorm, Ponder, Atraxa (took Acererak, Veil, Stock Up), Aluren and the Acererak / Lost Mine loop. 19 Dark Pools drained B from 19 to 0 in the same main phase. A was at 16 (Strand plus 3 combat damage) and was never in danger.
- **Set-up, A turn 2, prompts 10-12:** Ponder showed Show and Tell, Brainstorm and Omniscience. A kept all three on top in that order and did not shuffle. That made the turn-4 Brainstorm (prompt 15) draw Omniscience with Show and Tell already in hand.
- **Decision, A turn 6, prompts 28-30:** A played City of Traitors as its last land and cast Show and Tell with Island + City for Omniscience. B had tapped Plateau and Karakas on its own turn 5 for Voice of Victory, so B had no mana up. Boros has no counterspells, Swords to Plowshares needs W, and Karakas (which bounces a legendary Atraxa or Acererak) was tapped. Voice of Victory stops A's spells only on B's turn, so A had to win on its own turn, and it did. A could not have done better.
- B's loss was matchup and draw, not a misplay. Its seven (five lands, Ajani, Voice) can deal at most 3 damage by turn 5. Its main-deck interaction against Show and Tell plus Omniscience is Karakas, Swords and Wasteland, and with Omniscience in play none of them stops the loop.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A: none that cost anything.** Good plays: Strand fetched a basic Island on turn 2 against a 4-Wasteland deck. On turn 4 A cast Brainstorm before the land, per the playbook. City was saved as the last land. Under Omniscience A cast Brainstorm before Ponder, so Ponder's shuffle cleared the put-backs (Ancient Tomb, Tropical Island).
- A's report calls the turn-4 Hedge Maze (prompt 19) a mistake because B Wastelanded it. I disagree: it surveiled, and it took the Wasteland that would otherwise have hit Tropical Island (A's only green land) or City. That kept Island + City alive for the turn-6 Show and Tell. Flagged as close for Brady.
- Minor deviation, no cost: at the Atraxa reveal (prompt 48) A chose Done with Aluren, Misty and Ancient Tomb still offered. Brady's rule is "do not take 0 cards ... take fewer only to avoid discarding to hand size". A already held two Alurens and won that turn, so it did not matter.
- **B: no clear mistake.** The keep (prompt 1: Mesa, Mesa, Heath, Wasteland, Karakas, Voice, Ajani on the play) is weak against combo but normal for aggro. Flagged as close.
- B turn 5, prompts 22-25 (close): B paid for Voice with Plateau + Karakas and used Wasteland on the tapped Hedge Maze. Paying with Plateau + Wasteland would have kept Karakas untapped through A's turn, at the cost of the Wasteland activation. A second Wasteland was in hand for next turn anyway. Here it changed nothing, because A had Omniscience. Against a Show and Tell for Atraxa alone, an untapped Karakas is B's best answer.
