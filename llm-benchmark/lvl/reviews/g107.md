# g107 review: Alurentell (A) vs UR Cutter (B), B wins on game turn 12 (B's 6th turn, A on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
- A kept Island, Tundra, Ancient Tomb, Ponder, Force of Will, Aluren, Veil (a1). The hand had no green land, no creature and Ponder as its only cantrip, and the only blue card it could pitch to Force was Ponder. Aluren needed two green sources, a creature and a resolved Aluren, so the hand was at least three cards short.
- T1 Ponder (a5-a7) saw Stock Up, Petal and Veil and kept them in that order with no shuffle, which is correct: the look held a cantrip and a green source. Ponder drew Stock Up, Petal came on turn 3 and Veil on turn 5.
- **Turning point, a12/a13 (turn 3):** A played Tomb and cast a bare Stock Up with Island+Tomb, holding Petal "for Aluren GG". B's only land (Volcanic) was tapped, so B could answer only with a free Force or Daze. Two lines beat that: Petal first, which leaves a spare to pay for Daze, or Petal for Veil and then Stock Up, which beats both counters. Saving Petal made no sense, because A had no green land and Petal alone cannot pay GG. B Dazed it (b14, returning its only land) and Wasteland hit the Tomb before A untapped (b18-b20). A never cast another card-drawing spell apart from one Veil. A self-flagged this; it breaks existing rules ("Spare mana beats Daze, Veil beats Force of Will"; "If Daze is live and I cannot keep a spare, wait a turn").
- After that it was mostly the clock and A's draws. A's last six draws were Veil, Tropical, Omniscience (off Veil), Atraxa and Aluren, with no Acererak and no Show and Tell. Meanwhile DRC was a 3/3 flier from B's turn 6 (graveyard: land, instant, sorcery, creature, artifact).
- Life path for A: 20, 18 (Tomb, turn 3), 17 (DRC 1/1), 14, 11 (DRC 3/3 twice), 10 (own Force on Murktide), 7 (Bolt), 4 (DRC), 1 (Bolt, turn 12), then -4 from DRC 3 plus a Monk 2/2 equipped with the Cutter. A paid no fetch life. The Tomb's 2 life did not matter: at 6 life, Bolt plus DRC plus Monk still kills.
- Lethal turn (b86-b100): Cutter first, then Bolt as the second spell, so flurry made a Monk with the Cutter attached (2/2, haste, trample). Bolt took A from 4 to 1, then 5 combat damage. Bolt plus DRC (6) was already lethal and A had no blocker and no relevant answer, because Veil does not stop red spells. The Cutter only added redundancy.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A:** the bare Stock Up at a13 (see above). The keep at a1 was close (route to the combo: none in hand, one cantrip, no green land) and arguably a mulligan under "a keep needs a route to a payoff" and the a30 note. At a59-a62, A cast Aluren with no creature, tapping Petal and every land while Daze and Force were live. B Forced it, pitching Brainstorm. This did not cost the game, but it spent Petal, A's only second green source: the Aluren drawn on turn 11 could not be cast (Tropical was the only G).
- A's Veil in response to B's main-phase Brainstorm (a41) was a fine use of a duplicate Veil: it drew Omniscience. Veil plus Aluren (5 mana including GG) was never castable with Tropical and Petal as the only green sources.
- Holding Force at a65 instead of Forcing back on Aluren was right. With no creature in hand, Aluren would have needed a topdecked Acererak, and Force was needed for the Murktide the next turn.
- Force on Murktide at a76/a77 was mandatory: an 8/8 flier plus DRC 3/3 is 11, exactly A's life. Murktide was 8/8, not 9/9: delve exiled 5 instants and sorceries (Daze, Brainstorm x2, Force, Ponder). Pitching Omniscience and keeping Atraxa was right. Both were dead without Show and Tell, and Atraxa (lifelink, flying blocker) is the better Show and Tell target against a clock.
- **B:** no clear mistake. Daze on the tapped-out Stock Up was correct (B held Delta and Wasteland, so returning the land was cheap). Wasteland on the Tomb before A untapped was correct, even though it left B with zero lands on turn 4. Force pitching Brainstorm on the tapped-out Aluren was correct. The Murktide delve kept a land, creature, artifact and sorcery in the graveyard for delirium, and B kept Misty uncracked to fetch for the Bolt. Murktide into A's free Force (A had 4 cards, lands tapped) was a fine trade: it took A's last counter plus Omniscience. Debatable spots: Preordain bottomed Daze (b57; the note says never, but A had shown Veil and had green, so Daze was weak), and Brainstorm put back Prismari Charm and then cracked Delta, shuffling away B's answer to a resolved Aluren (b49).

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- **R1 (medium):** "Lotus Petal is not 'saved' for Aluren unless you also have a green land: with no green land, spend it as the Daze spare (or on Veil) for the early Stock Up." Evidence: a12/a13. It extends, but does not duplicate, "Spare mana beats Daze, Veil beats Force of Will" (a37-a43): the bot knew that rule but overvalued the Petal.
- **R2 (medium):** "Do not cast Aluren with no castable creature (Acererak) in hand when it spends your second green source (Petal); without Petal the next Aluren is uncastable." Evidence: a62, then the turn-11 Aluren stuck in hand. Related to "Do not tap out for Aluren when Daze may be in hand and I have no spare mana" (a05); this adds the creature and the Petal-as-GG cost.
- **R3 (low):** "Count green sources at the keep: Aluren needs GG. A seven whose only plan is Aluren, with no green land, no creature and one cantrip, is a mulligan." Evidence: a1. Close to "A keep needs a route to a payoff" and the a30 note ("Hands that need two more cards (a creature plus an enabler) are the ones that lose"). Possibly a duplicate; Brady should rule.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
- **N1 (high, low impact):** "When delving for Murktide, exile instants and sorceries (they make counters) but leave one land, creature and artifact in the graveyard so DRC keeps delirium." Evidence: b70-b74 (an 8/8 with the 3/3 flier kept). New.
- **N2 (low):** "Against Alurentell, do not shuffle away Prismari Charm with Brainstorm plus fetch; it is the answer to Aluren with Acererak on the stack." Evidence: b49-b51. It extends the existing "never Brainstorm away Daze, Force or the last pitch card".

## 5. Process and engine flags

- The game used 3 odds calls and 0 sim calls. A's opponent-odds call (61-69% counter) did not change its play. No land drops were skipped (A had no land in hand on turns 9 and 11) and no cantrip was cast in an upkeep.
- A's report calls the Murktide "9/9"; it was 8/8.
