# g45 review: Alurentell (A) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 10 (its 5th turn): Murktide 7/7 + Murktide 4/4 for 11 flying into A at 11 (A paid 1 for its own Force on the Bolt; at 9 without it, dead either way).
- Turn 7, A prompt 72 (decisive): Aluren with all four lands tapped, no spare, Veil uncastable, into B tapped out with 5 cards. B Forced (pitching its other Force). Tapped lands stop neither Force nor Daze (Island and Volcanics on board, so Daze was live too). A's own flag says the jam was "backed by Force pitching 2nd Acererak": Acererak is black. A's hand (Savannah, Force, Boseiju, Veil, Acererak x2) held no blue card, so its Force was dead. The menu showed this: "Cast Force of Will" was offered at prompts 7, 13 and 30 (Stock Up in hand) and missing at 71 and 72.
- Better: hold, take one Murktide hit (19 -> 12, no lethal before turn 9), then on turn 9 Savannah, Aluren with G up, Veil in response to Force or Daze. This was A's own plan at prompt 46. In hindsight it wins. B's hand was Force, Force, Cutter + Murktide drawn turn 8: three blue cards, so one Force at most. Murktide #2 was uncastable on turn 8 without the Force in the graveyard to delve, and B had no burn until turn 10.
- After turn 7 A was dead on board. Turn 9 (5 lands, 12 life, 11 flying power across) offered no win and no blocker. The Stock Up pick at 84 and the Force on the Bolt at 101 did not matter.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: turn 7 prompt 72 (above). It breaks the existing rules "Do not tap out for Aluren when Daze may be in hand and I have no spare mana" and "Daze is live whenever they control any Island-typed land, tapped or untapped". The new part is the misread pitch.
- Keep (prompt 1: Island, Tundra, Tropical, Strand, Boseiju, Stock Up, Force): weak, close to a mulligan. It needs two more cards (enabler + creature), has one cantrip, and its only pitch is the cantrip. It got there (Aluren T3, Acererak + Veil by T5), so it did not lose the game.
- Stock Up picks at 46 (Petal, Ponder, Stock Up, Veil, Acererak): Acererak first was right. Veil second was fine and set up the winning turn-9 line. Stock Up second (dig + a Force pitch) was the close alternative. A's report says Petal was right; Petal would not have beaten B's Force. The picks at 84 (Show and Tell + Omniscience) were moot.
- B: no real mistake. Delve was optimal: at prompt 64 B exiled all three Brainstorms (6/6) and kept a Bauble. At 79 it exiled Force of Will, which grew Murktide #1 to 7/7 and made #2 a 4/4. Tapping out on turn 6 cost nothing because both answers were free Forces. Forcing Aluren with Force + Force (prompt 73) was a correct 2-for-1 with a 6/6 clock. On turn 10, Cutter then Bolt turned on flurry and was lethal in either order. One debatable spot: B put Prismari Charm back with Brainstorm and shuffled it away (prompts 31-34, flagged).

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules

   Evidence: prompt 72 jam based on 'Force pitching Acererak'; the same misread recurred at 101 (A's report). Confidence: high. Extends "Keep one extra blue card as Force of Will pitch fodder" (a40); no conflict.
2. "A clock only argues for jamming if it kills you before your protected turn. Count their damage up to the turn you have Veil + Aluren (5 mana); if you survive, wait."
   Evidence: prompt 72 at 19 life against one 6/6; turn 9 would have had Veil. Confidence: medium. Refines Brady's "Waiting a turn ... with no clock on you it is reasonable"; does not contradict it.

   Evidence: prompt 89, turn 9 main 2. Confidence: medium. New.
