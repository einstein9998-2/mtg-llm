# g38 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 14 (its 7th turn): Show and Tell for Omniscience while B had 0 cards and every land tapped, then 25 Lost Mine laps of free Acererak drained B from 25 to 0. A was at 5 life and facing about 13 power, so B had lethal the next turn.
- **B, prompt 84 (T10 end step):** Elegant Parlor surveil put B's only Wasteland in the graveyard. Atraxa's reveal had just shown B that A held Omniscience, Acererak, Force of Will and Stock Up, and A was on Island plus Ancient Tomb. With Wasteland on Tomb on T11, A has 2 mana on T12 (no Stock Up, so no Show and Tell) and 3 mana on T14 (Stock Up only), and B's board kills first. Keeping Wasteland was clearly better. This decided the game.
- **A, prompt 4 (mulligan bottom):** A bottomed Omniscience, the hand's only Show and Tell payoff, and kept Veil of Summer, which is blank against Boros (no blue or black spells, no counters). With Omniscience kept, T8 Misty to Island plus two Petals casts Show and Tell for Omniscience, then a free Stock Up finds Atraxa or Acererak. That is a likely T8 win instead of T14 at 5 life.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A:** prompt 4, above. The keep at prompt 3 itself (already flagged) was defensible only as a 6 with Show and Tell plus Omniscience: one land away from a win. After bottoming Omniscience, the 6 had no payoff and only one cantrip, which costs 3 mana. It then sat on 0 lands from T3 (Wasteland on Tundra) to T7. That stretch was variance, but the missing payoff was not.
- **B:** prompt 84, above. Earlier lines were good. Turn-2 Wasteland on the lone Tundra (prompt 17, B's flag) was right against a mulliganed one-lander. In B's report, the Swords on Atraxa (prompt 73) is listed as a mistake. It was correct: a 7/7 lifelink, deathtouch blocker beats every Boros attack, and the reveal resolves whatever the timing. B's loss came from flooding (9 lands) plus the Wasteland bin.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- **R1:** "When you bottom after a mulligan, bottom the card that is dead in the matchup first. Against Boros that is Veil of Summer, because they cast no blue or black spells. Never bottom the only Show and Tell payoff from a hand that keeps Show and Tell." Evidence: T1 prompt 4, which cost about 6 turns. Confidence: high. No existing rule covers bottoming. It extends "Veil of Summer is nearly dead against Boros" (a07 to a30 lessons mention Veil only against blue decks).
- **R2:** "A Lost Mine lap is four Acererak casts: Cave Entrance (scry), Goblin Lair, Dark Pool (drain 1), then Temple of Dumathoin (draw 1). Each point drained costs one library card. Start the loop only when library size is greater than the opponent's life, and do not cast free Brainstorm or Ponder during it." Evidence: prompts 135 to 140. Library 34 against 25 life gave 25 drains and 24 Temple draws, ending at library 10. A correctly passed the free Brainstorms at prompts 139 and 140. Confidence: high. It corrects the existing line "about three Acererak casts per point of life" (a06), which should read four casts and one card.
- Nothing else generalizes. The one-land keeps are already covered by Brady's batch 2 mulligan rule.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
- **N1:** "Once Atraxa or anything else shows that the combo player is stuck on two or three lands, a Wasteland in hand or on top of the library is worth more than any other card. Keep it for Ancient Tomb or their only blue source, and do not surveil it away." Evidence: prompt 84. Confidence: high.
- **N2:** "On turn 2 against a combo player who mulliganed and has one land, Wasteland that land before you develop a second threat. Answer a Show and Tell creature with Swords right away, even though it gains them 7 life." Evidence: prompts 17 and 73. A then had no land for three turns and never attacked. Confidence: medium.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- A prompt 131 (T14 Main 1): picked [0] Pass instead of Cast Show and Tell and skipped to Main 2. This is the known `pick 0` slip from the playbook; it cost nothing only because B had no cards.
- A prompt 132: the engine auto-paid Show and Tell with Ancient Tomb (2 life, 7 to 5) although Island, Hedge Maze and Tropical Island were enough. Payment does not prefer painless sources. This matters at low life against aggro. Engine item.
- A prompt 2: 3 sim calls, all unhelpful (A reports odd land counting and 0 results). No decision changed.
- B prompt 122: the pick hung for more than 2 minutes and timed out. B never saw the end and reported the kill as "opponent's turn 14"; it was A's turn 14. B got no window because it had no instants, which is not a bug. B's report also attributes the footer's sim calls to itself, but those were A's.
- The auto macro stopped on its pick budget 3 times (40 and 150 picks) during the loop. That was harmless, and the loop needed about 100 Acererak casts.
- No land drop was skipped by either side (A had no land in hand on T4 and T6). No upkeep cantrips.
