# Cantrips and shuffling

Use when: casting or resolving Brainstorm, Ponder, Stock Up or Mishra's Bauble, cracking a fetchland, or fetching or playing Hedge Maze.

## The governing idea (P2)

- **CS1** [P2] "Brainstorm, then fetch, then Ponder" falls under "see as many new cards as possible". Sometimes you Brainstorm and you like all of the cards, so you do not need to shuffle. (Brady, 2026-10-07, on organizing the playbook)

Every shuffle rule below (CS2 to CS4, CS5, CS6) is an instance of CS1: shuffle when the known cards are worse than a random card, not by habit.

## Fetches and shuffles (P2)

- **CS2** [P2] Fetch cracks shuffle the library, so an unknown top card is just as likely to be replaced by a better one; the cost is 1 life and the real harm is cracking after a Ponder or Brainstorm that put a good card on top (a30 and a33 both lost an Atraxa to an early crack, with no way to know). Crack when I need the land or want to shuffle away a known bad top, not by reflex. (observed)
- **CS3** [P2] Brainstorm put-backs are drawn next turn unless a crack shuffles first; hold the fetch uncracked until their end step so it shuffles before my draw (t25), and never hold Petal/Stock Up on top by accident (t25). Fetch away the cards you put back with Brainstorm if they are bad (Brady, tough spots batch 1).
- **CS4** [P2] Sometimes Brainstorm then Ponder to shuffle; with a fetch in hand, weave it in so Ponder sees fresh cards (Brady, LvL sign-off). Cast Brainstorm before Ponder so Ponder's shuffle clears the put-backs (a43).
- See also mana-and-life.md ML9 (Misty uncracked as a Wasteland decoy) and ML10 (a basic fetched at their end step).

## Ponder (P2)

- **CS5** [P2] Without an enabler in hand, keep a known Aluren or Show and Tell on top; otherwise shuffle any look with no enabler, creature or cantrip. A lone land is not enough unless you are stuck on one land with none in hand (then shuffle any landless look). (Brady, LvL sign-off)
- **CS6** [P2] With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle. (a33)
- **CS7** [P2, P14] Count the cards. The prompt lists identical names once, so a three-card look can show two entries (a33: Aluren, Show and Tell, Show and Tell). If the list has fewer than three entries, assume the missing card is a duplicate of one shown and check it before keeping the order. (a33)

Note: CS6 is the creature-dependent special case; CS5 is the general Ponder rule and is the later, Brady-signed one.

## Brainstorm put-backs (P3)

- **CS8** [P3] Put back duplicates (a second Atraxa or Show and Tell, extra lands), never your only Aluren or Acererak. (Brady, LvL sign-off)
- **CS9** [P3] With plenty of lands (4 across battlefield and hand): put back Lotus Petal, not a land. (Brady, tough spots batch 2)
- **CS10** [P3, P12] Mishra's Bauble lets the opponent see the top card, so put back the card whose identity helps them least. Not Veil of Summer, not Show and Tell. (Brady chose Ponder.) (Brady, tough spots batch 1)
- Never Brainstorm in your own upkeep: turn-sequencing.md TS4.

Note: CS9 puts a Petal back on purpose; CS3's "never hold Petal/Stock Up on top by accident" is about leaving it there without meaning to. They do not conflict.

## Stock Up picks (P3)

- **CS11** [P3] Look at 5, take 2: with enough lands in hand and no Show and Tell or Acererak in hand to complete a pair, take the strong cantrip (the other Stock Up) first, then Atraxa or Force, not another land. (Brady, tough spots batch 2)
- **CS12** [P3] With two or fewer lands and none in hand: take a land first. Take a creature only if Aluren or Show and Tell can cast it next turn; with Aluren and Acererak already in hand, dig for mana. (Brady, LvL sign-off)
- See also protecting-the-combo.md PR6 (Stock Up as Force of Will bait) and vs-boros.md BO1 (Stock Up after a mulligan against Boros).

## Hedge Maze (P2, P7)

- **CS13** [P2, P7] At their end step, fetch Hedge Maze over a dual or basic when either gives the colours you need. Fetched at their end step it enters tapped, untaps on my turn and still surveils (t22, t25). (Brady, LvL sign-off; observed)
- **CS14** [P1, P2] With no Show and Tell payoff on the board or in hand, play the card-selection land first (Hedge Maze) rather than Veil: you need to find something to put in. (Brady, tough spots batch 1)
- **CS15** [P2, P4] From hand, play Hedge Maze on a turn where the untapped land's mana would go unused (play Hedge Maze rather than Boseiju before a Ponder). (Brady, LvL sign-off)
- Under Omniscience, free cantrips find the creature: show-and-tell.md ST9.
