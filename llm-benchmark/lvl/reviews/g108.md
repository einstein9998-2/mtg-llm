# Review g108: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 4 (A's 2nd turn), in main phase 1. A played Misty and fetched Savannah (18 -> 17 life), then cast Aluren with Savannah, Island and 2 Petals, which is exactly {2}{G}{G}. A cast the free Acererak and ran one macro of the Lost Mine loop. The log shows exactly 20 Dark Pool drains. B was at 20: 19 after its Heath, then +1 from Ocelot's lifelink. Summary line: "YOU WON | auto picks 157".
- Decisive, B turn 3, prompt 17: with Plains + Plateau, B cast Amped Raptor and held Goblin Bombardment, Ajani and 2 Voice of Victory. Bombardment was the play. It is the only one of those cards that cannot be cast for free under A's Aluren. Ajani (2 bodies) and both Voices could still be cast free in response to Acererak, which gives Ocelot + Cat token + 4 = 6 bodies. With the enter trigger on the stack (B prompt 26), B sacrifices 5 to kill the 5/5 Acererak, and A has no second Acererak (only 2 Tombs and Stock Up in hand). B could see what was coming: A had Brainstormed and cast both Petals a turn early.
- Should B have delayed Raptor? Yes. Cast free at prompt 26, its discover would have found Swords (Wasteland is a land and is skipped), and Swords exiles Acererak. That is hindsight on the library order. The Bombardment line works whatever the order.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none. Keep (Misty, 2 Tomb, Brainstorm, Stock Up, Atraxa, Acererak): sound. It has a payoff plus two cantrips to find Aluren. It was a little fragile with Misty as the only coloured source, and the Petals covered that. Turn 2: Misty for a basic Island was right against 4 Wasteland and Brainstorm needed U. Cracking Misty first was forced, because Tomb gives no U and Petal was needed for Aluren.
- Brainstorm put-backs (A prompts 9-11), Atraxa then Misty: correct. Atraxa is dead with no Show and Tell (MV 7 is out of Aluren range), and Aluren + Acererak were in hand. Misty on top made A's turn-4 draw a known land, and fetching Savannah gave G for 1 life. Putting back a Tomb would have been equal. Stock Up was not needed.
- Petals a turn early (prompts 12-13): this is the existing Spider-Woman rule (3 in B's list). Boros main deck has no artifact removal, so it cost nothing.
- No Tomb (turn 4): one land drop, so it was Misty to Savannah (1 life) or Tomb (2 life plus a spare Island). A spare does nothing against a tapped-out deck with no counters, and life matters against Bombardment reach. Correct.
- B keep (Plains + Heath, Ocelot, Raptor, Ajani, Voice, Bombardment, on the play): fine, since every spell costs 1 or 2. T1 Ocelot is fine. The Ocelot attack (prompt 19) was free: A had no creatures, and it made a Cat token at end step. First mistake: Raptor over Bombardment (above).
- Swords: B never had it in hand, so W could never have been held for it. The Raptor exile cost nothing by itself, because Swords was 2nd from the top and B never drew again. What it cost was the Raptor cast at sorcery speed. Declining Swords with no target was forced.
- B's free Ajani and 2 Voice (prompts 29-31) were irrelevant. With Bombardment stuck in hand (an enchantment, and B's lands were tapped) there was no sacrifice outlet. Voice only stops spells on B's own turn. The drain is life loss and needs no attack, and the game ended inside the loop. B passed the only window that mattered (prompt 26, Acererak's trigger on the stack) with nothing usable.
