# Review w1g3: Alurentell (A, on the play, mulligan to 6) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B (UR Cutter) won on turn 16 (B's 8th turn): two 8/8 Murktides and two DRCs hit for 22 into A's 10 life. A had B at 3 life with no cards in hand from turn 13 on and could not cast anything.
- A's opening was ideal. On turn 1, A cast Show and Tell (Ancient Tomb + Petal) for Atraxa (prompts 5-8). B had no land, so Daze was impossible, and B held no Force. B put in a 3/3 Murktide, then Wastelanded the Tomb. Atraxa hit twice (B 20 -> 4, A to 39).
- B answered well. On turn 7, B chumped Murktide into Atraxa, which put a creature in its graveyard and turned on delirium. At that end step B cast Unholy Heat for 6 on Atraxa with 3 damage already marked (B prompt 60). A forced it, correctly, because Atraxa was lethal on the next attack. On turn 8, Heat plus Bolt (9 damage) killed Atraxa. B used three cards on it.
- **Decisive error: turn 9, A prompt 77.** A held Show and Tell, Omniscience and City of Traitors, with 4 lands. B was at 4 life with 3 cards and no creature. A cast Show and Tell for Omniscience with nothing to cast for free afterwards.
  - B put in a free Murktide (its hand: Charm, Murktide, DRC).
  - B already knew about Omniscience, because Atraxa's picks are revealed. On its own turn it Prismari Charmed Omniscience back to hand (B prompt 82).
  - Omniscience in play gained nothing over holding it: drawing Acererak or Atraxa later wins the same turn with Show and Tell.
  - Better line: hold both. A drew Veil on turn 11 and Acererak on turn 13. On turn 13: Show and Tell for Omniscience, free Veil (hexproof from blue, so Charm cannot hit Omniscience), then the free Acererak loop drains B from 3. That is a probable win.
  - What A knew then: the playbook names Prismari Charm as B's answer, and Brady says not to cast a raw Show and Tell without a payoff or protection.
- After that A was colour-locked. Its lands were Island, Tundra and two Ancient Tombs, plus City in hand: no green and no black (the deck has no black land at all). Veil, Acererak and Atraxa sat dead in hand from turn 11 to turn 15. The turn-15 Brainstorm needed the last Show and Tell and missed.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A, turn 1, prompt 12 (medium).** For the Atraxa reveal's land, A took City of Traitors over Tropical Island, Savannah or Flooded Strand.
  - A's hand was Island and Tundra, with Tomb in play, so it already had blue and 3 mana for Show and Tell next turn. City only added colourless mana.
  - Strand (dodges Wasteland, fetches Tropical) or Tropical would have made Veil castable from turn 11, and Aluren live.
  - This breaks Brady's existing rule ("the card you pick first is usually the land you want untapped and green ... Forest is better when a Wasteland could hit a dual"). It did not decide the game alone, but it is why the late hand was dead.
- **A, turn 9, prompt 77 (high):** the lone Omniscience, see section 1.
- **A, turns 11 and 13, prompts 90 and 96:** A passed with City of Traitors in hand both turns, against "Play a land every turn you have one". No impact here, because nothing was castable anyway.
- A's other choices were sound:
  - mulligan of the one-land 7 (Brady's rule);
  - turn-1 Show and Tell into a landless opponent;
  - Atraxa picks Show and Tell and Omniscience;
  - Force on the end-step Heat, pitching the spare Show and Tell;
  - basics played first into Wasteland.
- **B, turn 5, prompt 36 (medium).** At 12 life, B did not chump Atraxa with Murktide, then went to 5. Murktide never attacked (Atraxa has vigilance) and chumped on turn 7 anyway, so the non-block bought nothing.
  - Blocking on turn 5 puts a creature in the graveyard. With the end-step Brainstorm (instant) that makes 4 types, so delirium is on for turn 6, when B had two Volcanics for Heat plus Bolt.
  - Atraxa dies a turn earlier, B stays at 12 instead of 4, and A gains 14 less life.
  - Otherwise B played well: Wasteland on A's only land, the end-step Heat using the block damage, and holding Charm for the Omniscience it had seen.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Do not Show and Tell Omniscience by itself. If nothing will be cast for free that same turn (Acererak, Atraxa, Veil, a cantrip that finds one), hold Show and Tell and Omniscience together. Drawing Acererak or Atraxa later wins the same turn, while a lone Omniscience gives them a free creature and a whole turn to bounce it."
   - Evidence: turn 9, prompt 77. Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: extends "Put Omniscience in with Show and Tell when I have Atraxa, Aluren or Acererak in hand" and Brady's "Do not cast a raw Show and Tell without a payoff or protection in hand when you can still dig." Neither covers the no-dig case. No contradiction.
2. "Under Omniscience, cast Veil of Summer for free before starting the Acererak loop: Omniscience then has hexproof from blue, so Prismari Charm cannot bounce it mid-loop. Atraxa's picks are revealed, so assume they hold Charm for the Omniscience they saw."
   - Evidence: turn 10, B prompt 82 (B knew A held Omniscience, and Charmed it), and the turn-13 counterfactual. Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: extends "Veil of Summer also answers their Prismari Charm" (a32) to the Omniscience loop. Not a duplicate.
3. "Lands must supply green: the deck has no black land, so Acererak and Atraxa come only through Show and Tell, Omniscience, Aluren or Petal. From an Atraxa reveal against Wasteland, take Flooded Strand or Misty (fetches Tropical) over City of Traitors when the hand has no green source."
   - Evidence: turn 1, prompt 12, and Veil, Acererak and Atraxa stuck in hand on turns 11-15. Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: sharpens Brady's "Atraxa reveal: ... green to cast Aluren next turn (Tropical Island); Forest is better when a Wasteland could hit a dual". It adds the fetch option and the missing-black point.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "Against Atraxa while holding Unholy Heat, chump-block early if a creature in the graveyard turns on delirium. Then Heat (6) plus Bolt kills her a turn sooner, before lifelink and 7-point hits leave you near dead."
   - Evidence: turn 5, prompt 36. Confidence: medium.
2. "Cast Unholy Heat at the end step of the turn Atraxa took block damage: 6 plus the marked damage kills her with one card, so their Force of Will must trade for a single spell."

RESULT: B (UR Cutter) won on turn 16. A's turn-1 Atraxa took B to 4, but Heat, Heat and Bolt killed it, and A's later hand had no green or black mana.
TOP MISTAKE: A, turn 9, prompt 77: Show and Tell for a lone Omniscience gave B a free Murktide, and B Charmed it away. Holding both wins on turn 13 with Veil, then Acererak.
TOP RULE: Never Show and Tell Omniscience by itself. Hold both until the same turn can cast Acererak, Atraxa or Veil for free.
