# LvL second-night synthesis (reviews g44 to g112, 69 games, live engine, main decks)

Sources: reviews/g44.md to g112.md and their flags.json (69 of 69; g113 was aborted and is not counted), games.json, STATE.md. "L" numbers are lines of /mnt/project-files/llm-player/alurentell-playbook.md as it stands now (L71 to L102 and L106 to L126 are Brady's). P1 to P14 are the principles in restructure/playbook.md; skill files are in restructure/skills/. "Reviews n" counts the reviews that proposed the rule; game ids after it are evidence I checked by grep. Nothing below is already a signed rule unless it says "extends" or "special case of".

## 0. Short answer

- **Win rate.** Alurentell won 43 of 69 (62%): 24 of 35 against Boros (69%) and 19 of 34 against UR Cutter (56%). Night one was 25 of 40 (63%; games.json, the night-one synthesis said 24): 12 of 18 against Boros, 13 of 22 against UR. The total did not move; UR got slightly worse. Design flaw (STATE.md): on night two the seat was tied to the deck, so A was always on the play against UR and always on the draw against Boros.
- **How A won.** All 43 wins were the Acererak Lost Mine drain, all by game turn 11 and 29 of them by turn 7 (turn 2 in g74, turn 3 in g85, turn 4 in g50, g84, g102, g104, g108). Brady's technique worked: room triggers left on the stack, no library loss, no legend-rule death (checked in g47, g75, g82). Most wins came in a window where the opponent had tapped out on its own turn (Boros: g48, g50, g60, g68, g80, g84, g88, g90, g102, g104, g106, g108; UR: g61, g63, g67, g99, g103, g109).
- **How A lost (26), ranked by count, one primary cause per game.**
  1. Mana denial, mostly Wasteland, made worse by A's land handling (9): g53 (fetched a tapped Hedge Maze, lost the Ponder turn), g54 (Atraxa reveal took Tropical over City), g57 (one-land keep, Wasteland on the only dual), g59 (two Wastelands after a pass), g66 (held a land "safe" and skipped the drop), g69 (Brainstorm put back both lands, then double Wasteland), g71 (two Wastelands on green sources, then a menu misread), g76 (spent Petal on an early Ponder, one mana short of Aluren), g83 (cracked a fetch into an untapped Wasteland).
  2. Keeps with no route to a payoff (6): g110, g112 and g94 were lost at the keep; g79 (keep, then Force pitched the only blue card), g70 (close keep, 28 cards with no enabler), g107 (close keep, then a bare Stock Up into Daze).
  3. Variance against a well-timed opponent (5): g55 (three free counters off a one-land six), g56 (18 cards, no enabler), g77 (two mulligans to five), g93 (Daze, Wasteland and Force each on time), g98 (fast clock).
  4. Combo-turn resources thrown away (3): g52 (free Acererak before the land and Brainstorm that found Force number two; Swords after Force on Erode), g72 (discarded the spare Atraxa, then drew Show and Tell with no payoff), g73 (put back the second Acererak; Heat killed the only one).
  5. Force of Will with no pitch or the wrong pitch (2): g45 (jammed Aluren expecting a Force whose only "pitch" was a black Acererak), g95 (Brainstorm left Atraxa as the only blue card, then Forced her away).
  6. Engine (1): g65, the Ancient Tomb auto-pay killed A at 2 life while casting a Show and Tell that would have won. g72 also died to it but was already lost.
- **What changed from night one.** No losses from failing to loop or from the old resolve-everything arithmetic; the loop is now solved. Wins are faster (night one ran to turn 26). New loss types: the engine's Tomb payment (g65), and two broken loops where A had one Acererak and one Force against two answers (g52, g73); night one never lost a loop. Wasteland is still the top cause, but now through A's own land choices (holding lands, end-step cracks, put-backs) rather than unavoidable hits on Tomb. Keep quality is the second cause and is new: night one's bad keeps were one-landers, night two's are payoff-less sevens with plenty of mana. The opponent bots still throw away their own answers (UR binned Charm, Daze or the last pitch card in g51, g65, g81, g97, g99, g101, g109; Boros tapped out its Swords mana in g86, g96, g102).
- Reviewer flags: 223 (A 127: 34 likely mistakes, 93 close; B 96: 45 likely mistakes, 51 close); g112 added none.

## 1. Candidate rules not in the playbook, grouped by principle

### P1 Route to a payoff (skills/opening-hand-and-mulligan.md)
1. **Count cards that win, not cards that dig.** A seven with payoffs or mana but no enabler (no Show and Tell, no Aluren with a castable creature) and at most one cantrip is a mulligan, even with Force of Will or five lands; against Boros on the draw it wins about 1 time in 5. Special case of P1. Games: g70, g79, g94, g107, g110, g112 (all losses). Reviews 6. Confidence medium-high. No Brady conflict, but L88's test ("a 7 with no cantrips and no blue card for Force of Will") did not fire in g79 because Omniscience is blue; L124 needs the number.
2. **Check a keep on the combo assembled by the turn you must win** (Show and Tell plus 3 mana with U by turn 6 on the draw against Boros), not "enabler or cantrip seen"; count Aluren or Omniscience only with a creature. P1, skills/process-and-engine.md. Games: g94 (94% "outs" that were really 21%), g112 (66% vs 21%). Reviews 2. Medium.

### P2 See new cards (skills/cantrips-and-shuffling.md)
3. **In a Ponder look, count a creature or enabler only if your hand can use it** (Atraxa needs Show and Tell; Aluren needs Acererak). Special case of L121 and L41. Games: g64 (correct shuffle of Atraxa, Ponder, Atraxa), g98 (kept Aluren on top with no creature, lost). Reviews 1. Medium.
4. **With no shuffle coming, Brainstorm put-backs are your next draws:** put back what you want to draw next (the lands for your next drops), not Force of Will or its pitch; after putting a land back to redraw it, do not crack a fetch before that draw. Special case of P2 (Brady: shuffle only when the known cards are worse than random). Games: g75, g73, g69. Reviews 2. Low-medium.
5. **Brainstorm does not lower hand size** (cast, draw 3, put back 2); when discarding with Brainstorm in hand, keep a fetch over Ancient Tomb. Games: g80. Reviews 1. Medium.

### P3 Keep what is unique or pays for Force (skills/protecting-the-combo.md, show-and-tell.md)
6. **Only blue cards pitch to Force** (Acererak is black, Aluren and Veil green, Petal colourless). Count your pitches before you rely on Force; when you Brainstorm or pick with Force in hand, keep one blue card per Force; no "Cast Force of Will" in the menu means no backup. Special case of P3; extends L50 ("Keep one extra blue card as Force of Will pitch fodder when discarding to hand size"), which covers cleanup only. Games: g45, g95 (losses), g75, g79, g109 (Force drawn with no pitch), g111 (kept Omniscience as the pitch and won the Force war). Reviews 3. High.
7. **With Aluren as the route, keep both Acererak** through Brainstorm, Ponder and discards; put back Omniscience or a land you will redraw. Games: g73 (loss); a second Acererak beat removal in g58, g86, g92. Reviews 1. High. Clarifies L119, whose duplicate list does not name Acererak.
8. **With no Acererak in hand, keep the spare Atraxa at cleanup** and drop a land or the second Stock Up. Games: g72 (loss). Reviews 1. Medium-high. Narrows L112 ("When discarding to hand size with Aluren in hand, keep Acererak and drop a second Atraxa"), which assumes Acererak is in hand.
9. **Atraxa reveal, rank within a type by what you hold:** sorcery slot Show and Tell over Ponder or Stock Up unless you hold one (g46); with Aluren in hand, Omniscience over a second Aluren, because it pitches to Force and is a second Show and Tell target (g111); against Boros, Brainstorm over Veil in the instant slot (g68). Special case of P3 and P13. Reviews 3. Medium.
10. **Atraxa land pick: take the land that makes next turn's combo castable with your one land drop.** City of Traitors beats Tropical Island when GG is already out (g106) or when next turn is Show and Tell and Wasteland threatens the dual in play (g54, loss); take Omniscience over Aluren when Aluren cannot be hardcast next turn (g44). Special case of P6. Reviews 3. Medium. Refines Brady L87 ("the card you pick first is usually ... Tropical Island"); "usually" leaves room, so no hard conflict.
11. **Under Omniscience with Atraxa already in play, cast the free second Atraxa** for a fresh reveal even without Acererak in hand; the legend rule costs nothing. Games: g91, g92 (Atraxa three and four found Acererak and Force). Reviews 1. Medium. Special case of "take everything free".

### P4 Land every turn (skills/turn-sequencing.md)
12. **Never hold a land in hand to protect it from Wasteland:** you can still play only one a turn, and every turn they lack a Wasteland is a land kept (exceptions: City, or a basic or fetch to play instead); with only nonbasics in hand, play the one you can use this turn. Special case of Brady L95. Games: g66 (loss), g56. Reviews 2. High.
13. **Exception to "cantrip before the land":** when Daze is live, the cantrip needs your only untapped land and the land choice does not depend on the look, play the land first if it gives a Daze spare. Games: g109. Reviews 1. Medium. Narrows Brady L83 ("cast cantrips ... before playing the land").

### P5 Wait while free (skills/turn-sequencing.md)
14. **Stock Up and Ponder are sorceries:** passing the main phase with them and open mana throws the turn's mana away. Special case of Brady L82 ("when waiting costs you something (... mana that would go unused, a turn), cast it"). Games: g105 (cost about 10 life). Reviews 1. High. Belongs as a one-line note under P5.
15. **Wait for a Daze spare only when the wait can make one** (a land, Petal or fetch in hand) and their clock allows it; count their damage up to your protected turn. Cast now when nothing in hand makes mana, when an untapped Wasteland faces your lands, or when they just spent their counters and are tapped out; wait against two Daze or Daze plus Force when no spare is coming. Special case of Brady L126. Wait was right: g45 (lost by jamming), g75, g91, g101. Jam was right: g47, g59 (lost by passing), g63, g85, g97, g109. Reviews 6. Medium. Qualifies observed L60 ("If Daze is live and I cannot keep a spare, wait a turn"); see section 2, item 3.

### P6 Count mana and life (skills/mana-and-life.md)
16. **Assume Ancient Tomb pays every generic cost while it is untapped,** even when a painless payment exists: at low life keep Tomb in hand if your other mana pays, and keep at least 3 life for a combo spell with Tomb on the battlefield. Games: g49, g52, g65 (death), g72 (death), g79, g105, g111. Reviews 3. High. **Conflicts with L23** ("the engine can still offer Tomb-only casts at 2 or 3 life ... (engine-oddities.md item 9, withdrawn as a bug)"): these were not Tomb-only casts; reopen item 9 and fix the payer (section 4).
17. **Count colours, not only amounts:** Veil plus Show and Tell needs G and U; Ancient Tomb and City make neither; Aluren needs two green sources. Hedge Maze always enters tapped, also when fetched in your main phase, so fetch a basic or dual when you want to cast this turn. Extends L110. Games: g53 (loss), g107, g55, g71. Reviews 3. High for the Maze fact, medium for the rest.
18. **Lotus Petal is next turn's combo mana only if the combo is castable with it.** Do not spend it on a cantrip that could wait a turn (g76, g83, losses) or on a hardcast Acererak scry (g69, g79); with no green land it is not Aluren mana, so spend it as the Daze spare or on Veil (g107). Cast it in the window you use it: the engine spends a Petal on the battlefield before an untapped land (g50). Special case of P6. Reviews 5. Medium.
19. **Against Boros, cast Petals the turn before the combo turn whenever that mana is idle,** even before Spider-Woman is seen (they play three; the main deck has no artifact removal). Games: g90, g104; done right in g44, g108. Reviews 2. Medium. Extends L117 ("cast Petals a turn early"), which only covers a visible Spider-Woman.
20. **City of Traitors cannot be floated across your land drop in this engine:** count next turn as City plus untapped lands with no land drop, and at a Stock Up pick take Petal over Veil when Petal is what pays Show and Tell plus a spare. Games: g93, g52. Reviews 1. Medium. Extends L110 ("Spend City of Traitors' mana before the land drop").
21. **"No Aluren at 3 life or less against Boros" counts your life after the Tomb payment, and yields when their board kills you next turn anyway.** Special case of L120. Games: g64 (won from 3), g94 (slip, moot). Reviews 1. Medium-high.
22. **Against UR with Cori-Steel Cutter out, pay no optional life:** shuffle a bad top with Ponder, not a fetch; play the dual from hand. Their two-spell turn deals 9 to 12. Extends L111, which lists no UR reach. Games: g65 (died at exactly 2), g79. Reviews 1. Medium.

### P7 Mana safe from Wasteland (skills/mana-and-life.md)
23. **Against an untapped Wasteland with no basic left to fetch, hold the fetch:** it is a decoy and a Daze spare (crack it in response to Daze); crack it in your main phase on the combo turn and spend the land at once, before they get priority. Games: g83 (loss), g71 (loss), g47, g96. Reviews 2. High. **Exception to Brady L118** ("at their end step fetch it [Hedge Maze] over a dual or basic"); see section 2, item 12.
24. **Against Wasteland, end each turn with a land in hand by putting back dead cards first** (Omniscience without Show and Tell, a second Atraxa) and never fetch one put-back land if that shuffles away the other. Games: g69 (loss). Reviews 1. High. Narrows L119 ("extra lands" are not extra once Wasteland is shown).
25. **Do not channel Boseiju on a dual or any land with a basic type:** they fetch an untapped replacement and the dead land is Murktide delve fuel. Play it as a green land; channel it at Karakas, Cori-Steel Cutter or a lock piece. Games: g45, g81 (Murktide a turn early, second green source lost, 3 extra life paid), g46 (Karakas, good use). Reviews 3. High. New; no Brady line touches it (section 2, item 4).

### P8 Beat free counters (skills/protecting-the-combo.md)
26. **Veil can itself be Dazed:** with your spell on the stack and Daze live, keep the untapped land that pays for it, and cast Veil in response to their Force only if a Daze payment is still left. Special case of P6 and Brady L125. Games: g71 (loss: Force, Atraxa and Veil lost to a 1/1). Reviews 1. Medium-high. See section 2, item 5.

### P9 Show and Tell (skills/show-and-tell.md)
27. **With Show and Tell, Aluren and Acererak in hand and exactly 3 mana into a possible Daze, cast Show and Tell (Aluren in), not Stock Up:** either loses one card to a counter, only Show and Tell wins. Bait with Stock Up only before Daze or Force has been seen or while a piece is missing. Games: g103 (cost two turns), g89 (close), g93. Reviews 1. Medium. Qualifies observed L20 ("a Stock Up into Force of Will plus Daze is a 2-for-1 for me").
28. **Never Force of Will to resolve a Show and Tell when the pitch is your only payoff** and nothing else you would put in wins; let it be countered and keep the payoff. Games: g95 (loss). Reviews 1. Medium-high. Also P3.
29. **Against Boros, Show and Tell gives them something:** a land (a fetch, Karakas or Plains in hand becomes untapped W for Swords on Acererak, g82) or Goblin Bombardment (g88, g50). Count Bombardment sacrifices plus their free MV 3 or less creatures under your Aluren against Acererak's 5 toughness, and hardcast Aluren rather than Show and Tell when you can. Special case of P12. Reviews 2. Medium. **Conflicts with Brady L73** ("What the opponent is doing barely matters"); section 2, item 13.

### P10 Loop (skills/aluren-and-the-loop.md)
30. **Hardcast Acererak only as defence:** when dead on board with no enabler, go Mine Tunnels (the Treasure pays the next cast) and then Fungi Cavern on their biggest attacker; never spend the Petal or Tomb life on a lone scry while it is your Aluren mana. Special case of Brady L96 ("Acererak is plan F"). Games: g93 (made B need two spells for exact lethal), g69, g79. Reviews 3. Medium.

31. **Without the combo in hand, do not Force a creature unless countering it changes the turn you die; but when they tap out on their own turn for a threat that kills you before your combo turn after next, Force it,** so either it dies or they spend counters on their own turn. ### P14 Process (skills/process-and-engine.md)
34. **Under Omniscience the menu lists "Cast X" twice: the first entry is the paid cast** (it taps lands and sacrifices Petals), the later duplicate is free. Games: g86 (lost a Petal); duplicates seen in g44, g48, g58, g78, g87, g89, g91. Reviews 1. High.

Games: g77; same misreport in g69, g83, g97, g109. Reviews 1. Medium.

## 2. Conflicts and questions for Brady

1. **Force on Voice of Victory versus Brady L115** ("Spend Force of Will on what breaks the loop ... not on Swords for a Show-and-Tell Atraxa"). For Forcing Voice on the stack: g66 (lost; spare Show and Tell as the pitch, combo two turns away, Voice dealt 9 of 18 damage and shut every instant window), g76 (lost holding two unused Forces; Voice dealt 6 and was the red permanent for the Ajani kill). Against: g82 (won; the pitch would have been the second Force or Show and Tell), g94 (only pitch was Show and Tell, the only route), g56 and g60 (Ajani, Ocelot Pride: close, would not have saved the game). Reviews recommend: Force Voice while it is on the stack only with a spare blue pitch (a duplicate, never your only Show and Tell or second Force) and when your combo is two or more turns away; on the combo turn L115 stands. Low-medium. Under P11 Voice "breaks the combo" by switching off Force and Veil on their turns.
2. **No-enabler sevens versus the one-lander exception** (L124, Brady: "if you have the combo you do not need cantrips and can keep a one-lander"). The exception held when "the combo" was castable: g74 (one land, won turn 2), g102 and g84 (won turn 4), g85 (one-land six, won turn 3), g50. It did not cover Aluren one-landers: g54 mulliganed one correctly (Aluren needs four mana with GG), g76 kept one and lost a mana short; g57 (one land, every spell costing 3, lost) asks for a cheap second source. g60 got it backwards (mulliganed a one-land combo seven, kept a creature-less six) and still won. The no-payoff keeps that lost were not one-landers: g110 (four lands, two Petals, Ponder), g112 (two Atraxa, Omniscience, Veil, no Show and Tell, no cantrip), g94, g79, g70. Reviews recommend rule 1: count cards that win, not cards that dig. Questions: (a) does the exception extend to Aluren hands, which need four mana with GG? (b) is a seven with payoffs and mana but no enabler always a mulligan, even with Force?
3. **Wait versus jam under Wasteland.** Lines: Brady L96 ("when you hold protection ... jam it now"), Brady L126 (waiting is contextual), observed L60 ("If Daze is live and I cannot keep a spare, wait a turn"). Waiting lost g59: A passed with three lands, Show and Tell, Omniscience, two Force and two Veil into a visible untapped Wasteland and no land in hand, and lost Tropical and Boseiju over the next two turns. Jams were right when Wasteland was coming anyway: g73 and g93 (Tomb certain to be hit), g85 (waiting loses City); in g63 a planned wait lost its Tomb anyway. Waits were right with no Wasteland pressure: g91, g101 (two Daze), g75. Reviews recommend rule 15: a visible untapped Wasteland plus no land in hand means a wait is more likely to lose a mana than gain one, so jam. Medium.
4. **Boseiju channel on a dual.** No Brady line; L118 mentions Boseiju only as a land. Evidence against channeling on a dual: g45, g81. For channeling at the right target: g46 (Karakas). Reviews recommend rule 25. High.
5. **Veil and the Daze land (g71) versus Brady L125** ("play it first to play around Daze; otherwise play it later to surprise the opponent and draw a card"). In g71 A cast Veil after its own Force, tapping its last land, and B Dazed the Veil. The other orders worked when a spare was left: Veil first then Show and Tell with a spare (g87, g111), Show and Tell first with Veil and a spare held (g67, g105). Reviews: not a contradiction but the mana arithmetic behind L125, since Veil can itself be Dazed (rule 26). Recommend adding it under P8.
6. **Omniscience-only loop versus Aluren against Boros, and the g48 question.** Lines: Brady L10 ("Only under Omniscience must the triggers resolve ... check library size against their life"), L120 (Aluren is symmetric). Omniscience-only loops never decked: g68 (library 42 against 20 life, about 23 left), g80 (45 against 21), g92 (30 against 18, about 13 left). Casting Aluren gave Boros free creatures: g48 (a free Voice), g74 (harmless only because of two free Forces); the free Phlage plus Bombardment line could have killed the only Acererak in g50, and Raptor's discover can find Swords (g88, g100). Reviews (g68, g74, g84, g92): with Omniscience in play against Boros, loop without casting Aluren when the library is at least their life + 5; cast Aluren only when it is shorter. That also answers g48: no need to dig for Aluren under Omniscience. Medium.
7. **Show and Tell put-in, Aluren versus Omniscience, with Acererak in hand.** Lines: observed L14 ("Put Omniscience in with Show and Tell when I have Atraxa, Aluren or Acererak in hand") and Brady L122 ("put in Aluren, not Atraxa"), never compared (MAPPING conflict 1). g89 review: with Aluren and Acererak in hand put in Aluren, because the put-in cannot be countered and a later Aluren cast can. The other side: g111 (Atraxa took Omniscience over a second Aluren, and it was the pitch that won the Force war), g44 and g84 (Omniscience right when Aluren could not be hardcast or against Boros). My reading: under Omniscience Aluren does not need to be cast at all (item 6), Force becomes free, and Boros gets no free creatures; Aluren's edge is no draw per lap. Suggested ruling: Omniscience when the library is at least their life + 5, Aluren when Omniscience is not in hand or the library is short. Low-medium; Brady decides.
8. **Dig first before the first Acererak versus Brady L114** ("loop now ... Do not cantrip, attack or take Tomb first"). For digging: g86 (free Brainstorm and Atraxa under Omniscience found Aluren and a second Acererak, which beat the last Swords), g92 (one Force into four open W: Swords then Erode killed Acererak; a later free dig found Acererak and Force and won), g52 (under Aluren, land plus Brainstorm off the untapped Tropical would have found Force two; lost). For loop now: g96 (one answer against one Force, won), and harmless attack-first slips in g44, g46, g105. Reviews recommend keeping L114 as the default, with one exception: when their live answers (W or R open, Quarry plus a creature, an uncracked fetch, cards in hand) outnumber your Forces, dig first under Omniscience with free spells while the library is well above their life; under Aluren only if a land and a cantrip are free to use. Medium-high (seen twice under Omniscience).
9. **Stock Up and Ponder are sorceries (g105).** No conflict: it is the "mana that would go unused" case of Brady L82. Recommend a one-line note under P5 (rule 14).
10. **Petal as the Daze spare (g107) versus Petal as combo mana (g69, g76, g83).** Not a contradiction: L110 counts "a Petal on the battlefield as one coloured mana", which only matters if the combo is castable with it; in g107 there was no green land, so the Petal could never make GG. Recommend rule 18.
11. **One blue pitch card per Force (g95, g99).** A side: L50 covers cleanup only; g95 Brainstormed its blue cards away, g45 relied on a pitchless Force, g79 spent its only pitch. UR side: L131 covers, and g99 adds "do not Force back to save your threat if it leaves your last Force with no pitch" (B lost; g79 and g81 show the mirror). Recommend rule 6 for A and the UR note in section 3. High.
12. **Hedge Maze at their end step versus basics against Wasteland.** Brady L118 ("at their end step fetch it [Hedge Maze] over a dual or basic") and observed L42 ("fetch basics with Misty, Strand and Delta and keep nonbasics for later") pull opposite ways once Wasteland is seen. g99 (fetched Maze; Wasteland took it, about a turn lost) and g83 (end-step crack into an untapped Wasteland, loss) side with basics or holding the fetch; g51, g63, g85, g101 fetched Maze at the end step and won. The night-one question of basic Island versus Tropical first (g55, g67, g68) is still open. Question: when Wasteland has been seen, which line wins?
13. **Hardcast Aluren versus Show and Tell against Boros (g88)** versus Brady L73 ("What the opponent is doing barely matters"). Show and Tell let B put in Bombardment or a white land (g82, g88). Question: is Boros an exception?
14. **Force their Swords on a Show-and-Tell Atraxa when the loop turn is already safe (g100, low)** versus Brady L115. A Forced Swords with B's last white source tapped and a spare Acererak in hand; Atraxa then walled a 7-power board. Low confidence, one game.
15. **Ancient Tomb plus Stock Up over Ponder plus a tapped Maze against a fast clock (g98, low)** versus Brady L74 ("the same card is just good, not a must, against a fast clock"). One game; for Brady only if he wants it.

## 3. Notes for the opponent bots (deduplicated)

UR Cutter:
- Never Ponder-shuffle, Preordain-bottom, surveil or pitch away Prismari Charm: it is the only answer to a resolved Aluren (bounce it in response to Acererak's trigger). Pitch a threat (Murktide, Cutter) to Force first (g81, g107, g109, g71, g91). High; extends L131.
- Pitch order: with your lands tapped, pitch a cantrip you cannot cast, never Daze (g75); with no other blue card, pitch the second Force and keep Daze (g63). Do not Force back to save your own threat if that leaves your last Force with no pitch (g99, g79, g81), nor when they have green open for Veil (g69). High.
