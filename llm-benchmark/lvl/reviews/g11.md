B (Boros) won on turn 10 (B's 5th turn): it cast a second Ajani, Nacatl Pariah, kept the new one under the legend rule so the old one (a Cat) died and flipped the new one into Nacatl Avenger, whose 0 ability dealt 8 to Atraxa; then it swung 17 power into A at 16 with no blockers. - **Decisive: turn 9, A prompt 74.** A started its turn with Aluren AND Acererak in hand (both taken by the turn-7 Atraxa reveal), Island, Hedge Maze, Boseiju and an untapped Lotus Petal: Aluren (2GG) was castable, and the menu offered "[5] Cast Aluren". Aluren, then Acererak for free on a loop (Lost Mine, Dark Pool drains each lap) wins on the spot. A instead cast Brainstorm, cracked Strand, Ponder, played City, Stock Up, and attacked with Atraxa (31 -> 24). It passed up Aluren again at prompts 80, 84 and 85 (City + Boseiju + Tropical still paid for it after Ponder).
  - What A could see: B had 1 card in hand and one untapped land (Lazotep Quarry, which can make W only by sacrificing a creature), so at most one Swords to Plowshares on Acererak was possible. Against that, A was at 10 facing twelve power on board (seven creatures), two Guides with energy and Ajani #2 in B's possible hand: no reason to wait.
  - A's own report says it "never got to cast Aluren" and that the plan was "dig toward Aluren plus Acererak". It was holding both. That is the whole game.
- Verdict: not variance. A's draws were good (Atraxa resolved on turn 7, the combo was in hand on turn 9). B played well but needed A to miss the win.
