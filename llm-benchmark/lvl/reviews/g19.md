# Review g19: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 12 (B's 6th turn). Murktide Regent (cast turn 6) hit for 5 on turns 8, 10 and 12, taking A from 18 to 3. B's main-2 Lightning Bolt was the lethal spell: A Forced it (pitching Omniscience) and B Forced back (pitching Preordain). A never drew a creature in the whole game.
- **Decisive (A, prompts 1 and 7): the keep, then the Ponder.** A kept Tropical, 2 Petal, Ponder, Aluren, Show and Tell, Omniscience. That is one land, one cantrip and no creature, and this playbook already calls it a mulligan. On turn 1, Ponder showed Island, Petal, Petal (listed as 2 entries). A kept the order (prompt 7, "No" to the shuffle). That fixed A's next three cards as Island, Petal and Petal, so no creature or cantrip could arrive before turn 7. A shuffle gave three random cards. 19 of the 53 were a creature or a cantrip (8 creatures, 4 Brainstorm, 3 Ponder, 4 Stock Up), which is about 75% to see at least one by turn 5 and about 39% for a creature itself. The Island was not needed: Tropical plus the two Petals in hand already paid for Show and Tell on turn 3. Petals 3 and 4 did nothing; A ended the game with 5 Petals played.
- **Turn 3 (A prompt 25 / B prompt 26):** A played Show and Tell for Omniscience with nothing to cast afterwards (A had 2 lands and 3 Petals, with 2 Petals spare). B Forced it, pitching Daze. That was correct, because A's spare Petals made Daze dead. On turn 4, B Wastelanded Tropical, A's only green land (B prompt 31). On turn 6, a delved Murktide (B prompt 42) came down against A's one land and empty hand of action.
- A's turn-7 Aluren (prompt 49) came in a correct window. B was tapped out, and its Force of Will had no blue card to pitch: B's hand was Volcanic, Delta, Force and Bolt. After that, A's draws were Force, Ancient Tomb and Ancient Tomb. A had 4 Acererak in 49 cards for an outright win (Aluren plus Acererak loops Dark Pool) and missed them all. The late game was variance, but the first 6 turns were lost to the keep and the Ponder.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, prompt 1 (likely mistake, high; self-flagged): the keep. It breaks two existing playbook rules: "One land with Ponder is a mulligan unless you know the opponent is Storm or Reanimator" and "a creature (Acererak or Atraxa) in the opening seven matters more than extra cantrips". A's sim measured the chance of a second land (about 90%), but the hand's real gap was a creature. Running the sim on lands is what made the keep look safe.
- A, prompt 7 (likely mistake, medium; not flagged): the Ponder no-shuffle above. The playbook already says "With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle." The visible Island tempted A away from that rule.
- A, prompt 25: the turn-3 Show and Tell was acceptable, not a mistake. A had no cantrip to dig with, its next draw was known to be a Petal, and Wasteland was coming. It also cost B two cards (Force plus Daze). Passing on turn 5 (prompt 35) was right: A had exactly 4 mana for Aluren with Daze live, and the playbook says "If Daze is live and I cannot keep a spare, wait a turn." Prompt 82 (Force of Will by alternate cost, pitching Omniscience) was the only line: hardcasting needed both Tombs, which is 4 damage at 3 life.
- B: no real mistake that mattered. The Force on Show and Tell (pitching Daze), the Wasteland on Tropical and the turn-6 Murktide against a one-land opponent were all good. B made one small slip: on turn 12 it skipped its land drop with Wasteland in hand while A had two Ancient Tombs (prompts 77 and 79). On turn 10 it also played Volcanic (prompt 61) before Preordain and Ponder, and Ponder then found that Wasteland. Neither changed the result.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "A Ponder top with the land you want but no creature or cantrip behind it is still a shuffle when the hand already makes three mana with Petals. A third or fourth Lotus Petal counts as a blank card, not as mana."
   - Evidence: A prompt 7. A kept Island, Petal, Petal, and the next two draws were dead. Confidence: medium. Overlap: this extends "Ponder: ... With no creature among the three cards and a creature-dependent hand, shuffle" (a33) so that a visible land does not override it. It contradicts nothing.
2. "When you use the sim on a keep, set the goal to the card the hand is missing (a creature or a cantrip by turn 3), not to lands. A high land number on a hand with no payoff is not a reason to keep."
   - Evidence: A prompt 1. The sim goal was `lands_playable >= 2` (90%), and the hand never found a creature. Confidence: medium. Overlap: the mulligan verdict itself is already a rule ("One land with Ponder is a mulligan ..."; a36 creature note). This rule fixes the tool use that led A to override it.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "Against Show and Tell or Aluren, use Force of Will and pitch Daze whenever they have a spare mana (an untapped Lotus Petal or land). Then Wasteland their only green dual on your next turn: with Petals they can still cast Show and Tell, but Aluren's {G}{G} becomes hard." Evidence: B prompts 26-27 and 29-31. The result was A stuck on Island plus Petals for 4 turns. Confidence: medium. This repeats good play, so it is not a fix.
2. "Play the land drop every turn, and against a combo deck on Ancient Tombs, Wasteland a Tomb. Cast Preordain and Ponder before the land drop so a Wasteland they find can still be played." Evidence: B prompts 61 and 77. Confidence: low; it was harmless here.
