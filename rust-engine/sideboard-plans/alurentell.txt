# Alurentell sideboard plans.
# Sideboard (15): Boseiju, 3 Carpet of Flowers, 2 Consign to Memory, 2 Defense Grid, Dismember,
# Disruptor Flute, 2 Faerie Macabre, Force of Negation, 2 Veil of Summer.
# Common cuts: Stock Up is the slowest card (3 mana, sorcery), Omniscience is win-more when the
# opponent can counter the Show and Tell it needs.

vs ur-cutter
# Islands (Volcanic x4, Island, fetches) make Carpet of Flowers ramp; Defense Grid taxes Daze and
# Force of Will cast on my turn; Faerie Macabre is a free answer to Murktide's delve fuel.
-4 Stock Up
-2 Omniscience
+3 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Defense Grid
+1 Faerie Macabre

vs dimir-tempo
# Underground Sea x4 and Island give Carpet mana. Everything they cast is blue or black, so the two
# extra Veils both protect the combo (uncounterable, hexproof from Thoughtseize and Push) and
# draw. Defense Grid against Daze, Force of Will and Bowmasters on my turn.
-4 Stock Up
-2 Omniscience
-1 Ponder
+3 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Defense Grid
+2 Veil of Summer

vs uwx-control
# Tundra x3, Snow Island, Volcanic: Carpet is live. Stifle and Swords are their answers to
# Acererak and Atraxa, Veil beats the counters and Grid taxes Stifle on my turn.
-4 Stock Up
-2 Omniscience
-1 Ponder
+3 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Defense Grid
+2 Veil of Summer

vs doomsday
# A race between two combos that both fight with Force of Will and Daze: Veil and Force of
# Negation (hits Doomsday, Personal Tutor, Dark Ritual) in, slow cards out. Carpet: Underground Sea
# x4, Snow-Covered Island.
-4 Stock Up
-2 Omniscience
+3 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Veil of Summer
+1 Force of Negation

vs reanimator
# Show and Tell hands them Griselbrand, Archon or Koma for free, so cut two of the four. Faerie
# Macabre (free, instant speed) is the best answer to Reanimate and Animate Dead; Veil stops
# Thoughtseize and Unmask (hexproof from black) and draws; Force of Negation hits Reanimate,
# Animate Dead and Shallow Grave.
-2 Show and Tell
-2 Stock Up
-1 Omniscience
+2 Faerie Macabre
+2 Veil of Summer
+1 Force of Negation

vs boros-aggro
# A creature race: no blue or black spells for Veil to blank, Stock Up is too slow. Dismember and
# Disruptor Flute (name Swords to Plowshares) keep the Aluren / Acererak engine alive, Faerie
# Macabre exiles Phlage (escape), Force of Negation counters Swords, Bombardment and Erode.
-2 Veil of Summer
-2 Stock Up
+1 Dismember
+1 Disruptor Flute
+1 Faerie Macabre
+1 Force of Negation

vs alurentell
# Mirror. Carpet is huge (Tropical Island x2, Hedge Maze x2 and Island x2 are all Islands), Consign
# to Memory counters Acererak's and Atraxa's triggers, Force of Negation hits Show and Tell and
# Aluren.
-4 Stock Up
-1 Omniscience
-1 Ponder
+3 Carpet of Flowers
+2 Consign to Memory
+1 Force of Negation
