# g29 review: A = Alurentell (on the play), B = UR Cutter (mulligan to 6)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 9 (A's 5th turn). Show and Tell for Atraxa on turn 7, then Aluren and the Acererak Lost Mine loop drained B from 10 to 0.
- T7 (A p112, p118): A played Boseiju and cast Show and Tell with Tomb plus Island, keeping Hedge Maze and Boseiju open with Veil and two Force of Will in hand. B Dazed (B p114). A cast Veil in response (p118), so Show and Tell could not be countered and A drew Lotus Petal. The Atraxa reveal (p133-137) took Aluren, Veil #2, Savannah and Ponder. A was at 5 life, so this decided the game.
- T9 (A p157, p161): Wasteland had taken Tomb, so A paid for Aluren with all four lands and kept Petal for Veil. B Dazed and A cast Veil in response. Every spell A cast that turn was then uncounterable, the Acererak loops included.
- T8 (A p151, self-flagged): Atraxa blocked Murktide. Lifelink damage is dealt at the same time, so A went 5 -> 7 and was never in danger.
B never had an out once Atraxa landed. B's real hand on T9 was Island, Volcanic Island and Daze; no burn showed up all game. A at 5 life was not punished, which was luck in A's favour.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, T6 p64 (likely): did not Force of Will the Cori-Steel Cutter. A held FoW x2, Veil, Show and Tell, Atraxa and Acererak, with Hedge Maze untapped to pay for B's possible Daze (B held one). Countering the Cutter (pitching the second FoW) still left FoW + Veil to protect next turn's Show and Tell. Passing let B flurry Baubles and Brainstorm into a 4/4 haste Monk: A went 14 -> 7 that turn and to 5 after the Tomb payment for Show and Tell. A ended the game with both FoW unused. It only worked out because B drew no Bolt.
- A, T3 p19 (close, see flags): played Ancient Tomb before Brainstorm. That goes against the rule "cast cantrips before the land", but Hedge Maze alone paid for Brainstorm, so Tomb left 2 spare mana for Daze, and B did hold Daze. Here the order made no difference, because the following land drops (Island, Boseiju) gave the same mana on T5 and T7.
- A, minor: Brainstorm (p22-23) put back both Acererak with no fetch left to shuffle them away. The T5 draw and one Stock Up card were both Acererak. The cost was small: the alternative put-back was a FoW.
- B: mulligan (p2, a 7 whose only land was Wasteland) was correct. First mistake was T7 p114 (self-flagged): Daze into Show and Tell with two green-capable lands open. A answered with Veil, and Daze cost B a Volcanic Island. T9 p159 repeated it: B Dazed Aluren into an open Lotus Petal after B had seen Veil taken off the Atraxa reveal. Neither choice changed the result. B lost to A's two Veils and Atraxa, not to a misplay.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- A p128: Daze targeted a Show and Tell that Veil had already made uncounterable, and A still answered Yes to "Pay to avoid the effect?". That wasted Boseiju's mana. The prompt is legal (paying is optional), so it is not an engine bug, but players should answer No once Veil has resolved. A's report saw the prompt; it did not notice that it paid.
- A's report says it kept two Force of Will. The opening seven had one; the second came from the T3 draw after Hedge Maze surveiled the other FoW into the graveyard (p18).
- A's opp-odds call at p157 listed FoW, Daze, Bolt and Prismari Charm. B actually held Island, Volcanic Island and Daze. The tool guesses; no decision was hurt.
- Murktide put into play with Show and Tell came in as a 3/3 with no counters, which is the correct rule (it was not cast, so nothing was delved). Opponent creatures showing as tapped on A's turn after attacking is also correct.
- Every land drop was made on both sides (A: Misty, Tomb, Island, Boseiju, Savannah; B: Strand, Tarn, Misty, Wasteland). No upkeep cantrips. A's first pick of one command ran into a timeout and went to the background, but it did apply; the wrong-sequence retry was rejected harmlessly.
- B's process was clean: Brainstorm with Tarn uncracked, then the crack shuffled away the put-backs (p31-34). Brainstorming two Dazes back on top so the Baubles drew them in A's upkeep was neat.
