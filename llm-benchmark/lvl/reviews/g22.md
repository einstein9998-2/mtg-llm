# Review g22: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 10 (A's 5th turn) at 6 life, against a Boros board of six creatures, with B on 36 life. Line: Aluren, then a free Acererak. B cast Erode on Acererak while its ETB trigger was on the stack. A answered with Force of Will (pitching Ponder), then ran 36 Lost Mine laps through the auto macro. A's library went from 35 to exactly 0.
- The deciding decision was quiet: A prompt 85 (turn 8). Swords to Plowshares targeted A's Show-and-Tell'd Atraxa, and A held Force of Will plus three blue pitch cards. A passed.
  - Swords gave A 7 life (9 to 16), and Atraxa's ETB resolved anyway: Aluren, Petal, Brainstorm, Hedge Maze. Brainstorm off the Petal then found Acererak.
  - The Force of Will A kept is the one that countered Erode at prompt 127. Erode was B's last card, and it was the only thing that could break the loop.
  - If A had Forced the Swords, A would have reached B's turn 9 at 8 life with no Force left. B drew Erode that turn. Erode on Atraxa plus the 9-point attack is lethal, and even if A survived, Erode would hit Acererak on turn 10.
  - A's report says it "passed priority ... before I could choose to FoW it". That is wrong: prompt 85 listed `[1] Cast Force of Will`. A chose to pass, and passing was right.
- B's side: its two answers (Swords and Erode) had to beat one Force of Will. Swords went on Atraxa (B prompt 82, already self-flagged), so only one answer was left for Acererak. After that, B had no way to win the race: A was at 16, B's best attack was 9, and Alurentell's combo turn was due.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A: no clear mistake.** A kept making land drops, held Misty uncracked until needed, and found the combo with two Stock Ups, Atraxa and Brainstorm. Inaccuracies:
  - Prompt 21 (turn 4): A spent Lotus Petal on a Stock Up while Ancient Tomb sat in hand. Tomb plus Hedge Maze casts Stock Up and keeps the Petal for a combo turn. Against Boros, Tomb's 2 life argues the other way. Low impact, because the turn-6 and turn-8 mana counts came out the same.
  - Prompt 40 (turn 6): A fetched Tropical Island, not Island, into a deck with 4 Wasteland. This goes against the existing rule "fetch basics ... keep nonbasics for later". Here it cost nothing: Maze and Boseiju are nonbasic too, so turn 7's Wasteland would have hit one of them.
  - The keep (prompt 2) and Force of Will on Ajani pitching a Show and Tell (prompt 11) are self-flagged. That Force left A with no Force until turn 6.
- **B: no clear mistake.** The 2-land keep (self-flagged) is normal for aggro.
  - Turn 5 "miscounted white sources" (B report) is colour screw, not a misplay. Savannah was B's only white source, and Ocelot first was right.
  - Turn 9 (B prompts 98-102): B's report calls the all-in attack a mistake. It was correct. Blockers do nothing against a Dark Pool drain, and B kept the one W (Savannah) open for Erode. 9 damage (16 to 7) was the maximum, and it forced A to combo through Erode on its next turn at 6 life.
  - Holding Swords instead of Erode-plus-attack would have needed Swords left over from turn 8, which is the self-flagged prompt-82 question. With Swords and Erode both in hand on turn 10, one Force does not save Acererak, and B probably wins. That counterfactual is the real story of B's loss.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Against decks with instant removal (Swords, Erode, Bolt, Unholy Heat), keep Force of Will for Acererak's ETB trigger. Removal that hits Acererak with the trigger on the stack ends the loop, and that is where the game is decided. Do not spend Force to save a Show-and-Tell'd Atraxa from Swords when the loop is coming. Swords gives you 7 life, and Atraxa's ETB still resolves."
   - Evidence: A prompts 85 and 127 (B prompt 125). Confidence: medium-high.
   - Overlap: none. It complements "Under Omniscience, cast the redundant spell first as bait".
2. "Count the loop before you start it. Each Lost Mine lap is four Acererak casts: Cave Entrance, Goblin Lair, Dark Pool, Temple of Dumathoin. It drains 1 and then draws 1, so it kills only if their life is at most library + 1. If it is more, stop with at least one card left and attack with the Goblins next turn. Or finish through Tomb of Annihilation: Trapped Entry and Veils of Fear take 3 from an empty-handed opponent and draw nothing."
   - Evidence: A prompts 129-137. Library 35, opponent 36, finished at 0 cards with no margin. The log shows 143 Acererak casts = 35 x 4 + 3. Confidence: high on the count; the Tomb finish is untested in this engine.
   - Overlap: this corrects the playbook line "Cave Entrance (scry), Goblin Lair (blocker), Dark Pool (drain 1) is the three-cast cycle ... about three Acererak casts per point of life". The lap is four casts, and the fourth room draws a card. Entering Tomb rooms does not complete Tomb, so this does not contradict "Default: do not complete Tomb of Annihilation".

## 4. Candidate Boros Aggro notes
1. "Against Alurentell, attack with everything every turn. Blockers do nothing against the Aluren/Acererak drain. Keep only the white mana for instant removal, and fire it at Acererak while its ETB trigger is on the stack, as early in the loop as possible, because each lap draws them a card and may find another Force of Will."
   - Evidence: B prompts 98-102 and 125. Confidence: high.
2. "Count your removal against their Force of Will before spending it on Atraxa. One answer to Acererak loses to one Force, and two win. Swords on Atraxa does not stop its ETB and gives them 7 life."
   - Evidence: B prompt 82 (Swords on Atraxa, A to 16) and B prompt 125 (Erode countered). Confidence: low-medium. A 7/7 lifelink, deathtouch blocker is a real problem, which is why this is not a firm rule.
