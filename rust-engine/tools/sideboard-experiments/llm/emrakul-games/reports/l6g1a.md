RESULT: WON
TURNS: 6
EMRAKUL: Opening seven had Emrakul, Show and Tell, two Aluren, Stock Up and two Misty; kept (two fetches, 70 percent to find a third land by turn 3). On turn 4 I resolved Show and Tell (they Forced it pitching Consign, I Forced back pitching Stock Up) and put Emrakul in (not cast; Emrakul is not in the get_card pool, I used my own knowledge of the card). Their Phelia blinked it for a turn, then Emrakul attacked on turn 5: annihilator 6 made them sacrifice their whole board (3 Tundra, Tamiyo, Phelia, Clue), 15 flying damage took them to 1, and a second swing on turn 6 won. Protection from colored spells made Swords/Ending/Consign dead, and annihilator stopped chump blockers. I would keep it in the main deck against this opponent. They have no hard answer to it, and it is a better Show and Tell target than a second Aluren/Acererak pair. I would cut the City of Traitors slot back only if mana is a problem (see MANA); otherwise cut a second Omniscience (10 mana, nearly dead without Show and Tell, and I bottomed it with Brainstorm).
MANA: I was short of lands and green sources, not of generic mana: kept 2 fetches, hit only Island, Hedge Maze (Wastelanded twice) and Ancient Tomb by turn 4, and never had GG for Aluren. The 19 vs 20 land difference was not the cause here (the fetches fetched mostly nonbasics into Wasteland); City of Traitors would not have mattered for the win, but it would have been a hit-proof-ish extra mana on turn 3 when I was stuck on Island plus one land, so I would not mind having it back in a deck that wants Aluren, but I would not cut Emrakul for it.
