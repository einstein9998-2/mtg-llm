# Show and Tell, Omniscience and Atraxa

Use when: Show and Tell is in hand or castable, Omniscience is in play, or Atraxa's enter trigger is resolving.

Each rule is a default. Read its "Breaks when:" before applying it; if the situation matches, follow the principle instead.

## Whether to cast it (P9)

- **ST1** [P9, P13] Show and Tell versus Aluren: decide by how much mana I have and which other combo pieces are in my hand. What the opponent is doing barely matters. (Brady, tips 2026-10-06)
  Breaks when: the opponent can use a Show and Tell put-in: then casting Aluren is often better (ST17, Brady 2026-10-08).
- **ST2** [P9] Do not cast a raw Show and Tell without a payoff or protection in hand when you can still dig. They may be holding Force of Will for it and get to surveil and turn on delirium. Better sequence (turn 4, Boseiju in hand): play the land, cast Veil of Summer first; if it resolves, Show and Tell for Atraxa; if it does not, Stock Up. If they Daze, pay 1 and draw a card. (Brady, tough spots batch 1)
  Breaks when: you can no longer dig (the rule is for "when you can still dig").
- **ST3** [P9] When you hold protection (Force of Will, Veil of Summer), jam it now; it will not get better by waiting. Never cast Acererak instead of Show and Tell: Acererak is "plan F" because it achieves so little, and a resolved Show and Tell wins the game. (Brady, tough spots batch 2)
  Breaks when: no known exception. (Brady's common Acererak hardcast for a scry, aluren-and-the-loop.md AL9, is not instead of a castable Show and Tell.)
- **ST4** [P9, P8] Jam line with Veil: land (Ancient Tomb), Veil of Summer, then Show and Tell for Atraxa. If Veil resolves, you probably win. If it is Dazed, pay 1 and draw. If it is forced, try again next turn. (Brady, tough spots batch 2)
  Breaks when: no known exception. ST2 and ST4 are the same line from two reviews: if Veil is countered you do not cast Show and Tell that turn. In m01 Brady put Veil before the land drop and the Petal (protecting-the-combo.md PR17); whether ST2 and ST4 should also cast Veil before the land when the lands in play pay for it is open (CHANGES.md, m01 open question 2).
- **ST5** [P9, P4, P8] Land drop vs Atraxa: playing a fetch before Show and Tell plays around double Daze, which is worth slightly more than holding the land drop for a possible Atraxa trigger, because a resolved Show and Tell usually wins anyway. (Brady, tough spots batch 2)
  Breaks when: no known exception (Brady: "slightly more").
- **ST6** [P9, P12] Opponent at 2 cards in hand and I am about to reach 4 mana: Brainstorm first, then Veil plus Show and Tell wins. (Brady, tough spots batch 1)
  Breaks when: no known exception.
- **ST16** [P3, P9] Do not pitch your only payoff to Force of Will to resolve Show and Tell when nothing else you would put in wins: let it be countered and keep the payoff. (SYNTHESIS2 rule 28 with Brady's qualifier, 2026-10-08; g95 loss)
  Breaks when: you are really desperate and you are going to draw with Veil of Summer: then you can pitch your only payoff (Brady, 2026-10-08).
- **ST17** [P9, P12, P15] Show and Tell gives the opponent a put-in too. Against Boros it can be a land (a fetch, Karakas or Plains becomes untapped W for Swords on Acererak, g82) or Goblin Bombardment (g50, g88); count Bombardment sacrifices plus their free MV 3 or less creatures under your Aluren against Acererak's 5 toughness. Against other decks a land can open up Pyroblast or Consign to Memory out of UR, or a fetch then Fatal Push out of UB. So casting Aluren is often better than Show and Tell for Aluren, because they do not get to put something in. (Brady, 2026-10-08, on SYNTHESIS2 rule 29 and conflict 13)
  Breaks when: Show and Tell's upsides matter: it fixes mana, costs 1 less, and lets you play Aluren next turn if the Show and Tell is dealt with (Brady, 2026-10-08).
- With no payoff on the board or in hand, play Hedge Maze before Veil: cantrips-and-shuffling.md CS14.

## What to put in (P1, P10)

- **ST7** [P1] Put Omniscience in with Show and Tell when I have Atraxa, Aluren or Acererak in hand: everything is free afterwards, and the put-in cannot be countered. Cast the free Atraxa second (a04: it blocked and gained 7 life). With Acererak in hand Brady usually prefers Omniscience over Aluren. (observed, a01 to a06; Brady "Usually prefer Omniscience here", 2026-10-08, conflict 7)
  Breaks when: Omniscience is not in hand (then ST8); the opponent shows Static Prison, which Brady names as the Boros counterplay (it is not in the current Boros list, decks/boros-aggro.txt).
- **ST8** [P10] Show and Tell with Acererak in hand: put in Aluren, not Atraxa, then cast the free Acererak and loop. This includes a known Acererak on top with a cantrip and a spare mana to draw it. (Brady, LvL sign-off)
  Breaks when: Omniscience is also in hand: usually put in Omniscience (ST7, Brady 2026-10-08); against Boros with white open, weigh Swords first (Brady, LvL sign-off).
- How the loop differs under Aluren and under Omniscience: aluren-and-the-loop.md AL2 and AL8.

## Under Omniscience (P2, P3, P12)

- **ST9** [P2] Show and Tell for Omniscience, then free Brainstorm, Ponder and Stock Up, finds the creature (a43). Cast Brainstorm before Ponder so Ponder's shuffle clears the put-backs.
  Breaks when: no known exception.
- **ST10** [P12] Under Omniscience, cast the redundant spell first as bait (Aluren, Stock Up); opponents here answered Goblin tokens with Unholy Heat (t22, t24, t26) instead of the loop.
  Breaks when: no known exception. Against Boros you need not cast Aluren at all under Omniscience (AL8).
- **ST11** [P3] Hand size: loops draw many cards. Dump spare cards as free spells, or discard lands and duplicates at cleanup. (observed, a01 to a06)
  Breaks when: no known exception.
- A free Force of Will under Omniscience and the artifact-graveyard catch: protecting-the-combo.md PR12.

## Atraxa's reveal (P3)

- **ST12** [P3] Do not take 0 cards. Take the green source and Force of Will, then Aluren, and win. (Brady, tough spots batch 2)
  Breaks when: you would discard the cards to hand size anyway: that is the only reason to take fewer than allowed (Brady, tough spots batch 2).
- **ST13** [P3] On the combo turn, take every card Atraxa offers, a spare Acererak first (on the combo turn hand size never applies to the reveal). (Brady, LvL sign-off)
  Breaks when: no known exception (it is the combo-turn case of ST12).
- **ST14** [P3] When discarding to hand size with Aluren in hand, keep Acererak and drop a second Atraxa. (Brady, LvL sign-off)
  Breaks when: no known exception.
- **ST15** [P3, P7] The card you pick first is usually the land you want untapped and green to cast Aluren next turn (Tropical Island). (Brady, tough spots batch 1)
  Breaks when: a Wasteland could hit a dual or City of Traitors might die: Forest is better (Brady, tough spots batch 1).
