# g52 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 9 (A's last turn was 8). A lost at 9 life, facing 12 power with no blockers, after B's Swords exiled Acererak in response to his enter trigger.
- **T8, a prompt 66 (decisive):** with Aluren resolved, A cast the free Acererak at once. A was holding one Force of Will, Tropical Island #2 (an unused land drop) and Brainstorm, and B had Plateau plus Quarry (with creatures) open, which is two white. The better line was to play Tropical Island (City was useless here, since it taps only for colourless and no spell could use it), Brainstorm with that U, and only then cast Acererak. The top three cards were Boseiju, Aluren and Force of Will, so the line finds a second Force and Acererak survives Erode plus Swords. With what A could see, the line cost nothing and had 6 outs (Force or a second Acererak) in about 44 cards, roughly 36%. After the free Acererak, A had no U left to respond to Swords.
- **T6, a prompts 44/45:** A passed with City (2) and Petal unused and then discarded Atraxa at cleanup. Stock Up #3 (City + Petal for U) would have seen Atraxa, Boseiju, Aluren, Force and Show and Tell, giving the same second Force for T8. Voice of Victory meant mana left over could not be used on B's turn. Aluren was still castable on T8 without the Petal (Trop + Trop + Tomb), so holding the Petal only guarded against a Wasteland that never came.
- The T8 Aluren at 7 life itself was forced and correct, because a lethal swarm was coming the next turn. Free Phlage under the symmetric Aluren would only have taken A to 2. The engine paying Aluren with Ancient Tomb rather than City (7 to 5) did not matter.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A, T4 prompt 17 (Stock Up picks):** took Aluren + Atraxa from Tomb, Petal, Stock Up, Aluren and Atraxa. Atraxa was dead: Aluren cannot cast her, there was no Show and Tell, and she was discarded unused on T6. Aluren + Lotus Petal was better. On T6 the board would have been Trop, Tomb, City and two Petals (7 mana, GG), enough for Stock Up and Aluren in the same turn. That Stock Up found Acererak, and B had only one white (Quarry) open after auto-pay tapped Plains and Plateau for Raptor, so one Force would have protected the loop at 13 life. This breaks the existing rule "Take a creature only if Aluren or Show and Tell can cast it next turn".
- **Keep (a prompt 2, flagged by A):** 3 lands, Ponder and 3 Stock Up is a fine keep against Boros. It casts Stock Up on turn 2 and digs roughly 13 cards by turn 6, and a six-card hand is worse. The loss came from how the hand was played, not from keeping it. The T2 Ponder shuffle (FoW, City, Veil, flagged by A) follows the playbook's Ponder rule. In hindsight that Force was the missing second answer, which is close but not a mistake.
- **B:** none. Voice on T3, Raptor discovering Ocelot Pride, Erode first and Swords second, and the trigger order were all right. The T5 payment problem was the engine's auto-pay, not a choice B could make (see section 5). In the actual game it cost nothing, because A could not go off on T6.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Under Aluren with Acererak in hand, only one Force and the opponent holding two removal mana (Boros white, Quarry with a creature), play your land and cast your cantrip before the free Acererak. Once he is on the stack you have no mana to answer a second removal spell." Evidence: T8 a66 (Brainstorm would have found Force #2). Confidence: medium. **Contradicts** "With Aluren or Omniscience in play (or castable) and Acererak in hand, loop now ... Do not cantrip, attack or take Tomb first." Suggested scope: only when you cannot answer their removal count.
2. "When Stock Up finds Aluren and no creature Aluren can cast, take the Petal (or Tomb) so next turn holds Stock Up plus Aluren with GG (7 mana). The creature you dig up can then be cast the same turn." Evidence: T4 a17, then T6 Acererak found with one G short. Confidence: medium-high. Extends (does not duplicate) "Take a creature only if Aluren or Show and Tell can cast it next turn".
- Not proposed (duplicate): T6 unused mana is already covered by "when waiting costs you something (... mana that would go unused ...), cast it". Add g52 T6 as evidence: against Voice of Victory, mana left over on your own turn is always wasted.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
1. "Against Acererak with both Erode and Swords, lead with Erode (no life for them) to draw out their Force, and keep Swords for after it." Evidence: b69/b74, which won the game. Confidence: medium. Adds to "Keep W open ... for Swords on Acererak", which does not duplicate it.
2. "Before tapping on your own turn, count two white for their combo turn (Plains plus Quarry with a creature). If you can, pay generic costs with Quarry's colourless rather than Plains." Evidence: b26 (auto-pay left one W on A's T6). Confidence: low, because the engine cannot do this yet.
- B confirmed the existing Ocelot/Voice ordering note at b60: Voice's trigger went on the stack first, so Ocelot resolved first and the Warriors stayed.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Engine auto-pay does not keep colours or life. b26 Raptor ({1}{R}) tapped Plains rather than Quarry's {C}, even though Quarry tapped for {C} on T3. a63 Aluren paid with Ancient Tomb (2 life) while City of Traitors was untapped. Neither player can tap a land manually or float mana, so City's mana cannot be saved through its sacrifice trigger (a82).
- The Quarry menu lists 5 colours times every creature (34 options at b69), which crowds the choice. Attacks take one prompt per creature.
- A's transcript ends at a87 and the engine auto-passed to B's lethal T9 attack (A had no castable response under Voice). A's report calls this "unexpected", but it is not a bug. The engine summary's "odds calls 1" is A's own count, not B's.
- No land drops were skipped and no upkeep cantrips were cast. a9 "Do it?" is Ponder's shuffle question, which is unclear wording.

RESULT: B (Boros) won on game turn 9. A's Acererak was exiled by Swords after A's Force countered Erode (T8).
TOP MISTAKE: T8 a66, A cast the free Acererak before playing Tropical Island and casting Brainstorm, which would have found a second Force of Will.
TOP RULE: Under Aluren against two removal mana with only one Force, play the land and cast the cantrip before the free Acererak.
