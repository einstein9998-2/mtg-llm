## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none found. Two zero-land mulligans to 5 are variance. The 5-card keep (Boseiju, Tomb, Petal, Aluren, Show and Tell) was the best five of that seven (A flagged it, #4). Petal-Brainstorm on t3 (#16-#22) found Atraxa, Misty and City; putting back City and the only Aluren is against "never your only Aluren", but Aluren was dead without Acererak and came back on t5 because Misty was held. Show and Tell for Atraxa on t5 (#41) with Boseiju spare for Daze was right: Aluren had no Acererak to cast, there was no Veil, and B was tapped out with only free counters (A flagged it). Stock Up on t7 (#59) into a tapped-out B was right; Tomb + Acererak (#61-62) was the best pair: Acererak is the only card that makes Aluren a win, and the Tomb gave the t9 spare against Daze (Ponder or Omniscience would not). The t9 Aluren (#76) was forced: B showed 8 + 1 on board with A at 14, and with Bolt plus delirium A was dead on t10 either way; Ponder first was impossible (Tropical was the only U and was needed for GG).
- B: none clearly. The one-land keep on the draw (B #6, flagged by B) with Force, 2 Daze, Ponder and Brainstorm against a 5-card combo hand is a fine keep; Ponder missed lands twice and Brainstorm found Tarn. Brainstorm putting back two Daze and Preordain bottoming both (B #29-30, #35-36) goes against the signed-off note "never Preordain-bottom Daze", but with three Daze, two Force and A always holding Boseiju or another land untapped, keeping one Daze was reasonable. Daze was the right pitch to the first Force (B #43). Murktide on t8 (B #66) used 2 of 3 lands and kept Volcanic up for Bolt with both Forces in hand: textbook. Bolt to face at A's end step (B #81) made exact lethal.
- Verdict: variance. A mulled to 5 against a hand that drew three Force of Will and found Murktide on turn 8.
