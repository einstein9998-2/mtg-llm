# g49 review: Alurentell (A, on the play) beat UR Cutter (B), game turn 9

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 9 (its 5th turn) from B at 16, A at 2. A's turn-5 Show and Tell (A p53) was hit by B's Force of Will, pitching the second Force (B p54-55). B then played Wasteland on Ancient Tomb, a 7/7 Murktide (t6), and Cutter + Bolt to face + attack (t8): A went 16 -> 4. On t9 B was fully tapped out with Daze + Misty in hand. A played Ancient Tomb (p93), cast Veil off Hedge Maze (p94), then Show and Tell for Omniscience (p96, 4 -> 2 from Tomb), a free Atraxa (took Acererak, Force, Show and Tell), and the Lost Mine macro drained 16 -> 0. B's Daze on Acererak (B p116) could not counter through Veil.
Decided by: (1) A drawing the second Show and Tell with Veil already in hand (Stock Up p69-70 took Veil + Atraxa); (2) B's two Forces being gone after t5. Even with a Force kept (pitching Murktide at p55), A's t9 line beat it: 6 mana covers Veil (Forced), Show and Tell and a Daze payment.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A p53 (t5 raw Show and Tell into 7 cards, opponent tapped out): reasonable, not a mistake. A held the payoff (Omniscience + Acererak) and 1 spare for Daze (Petal), so the existing rule "do not cast a raw Show and Tell without a payoff or protection" does not forbid it. It lost A's only Show and Tell but cost B both Forces (2-for-1), which is what made t9 safe. The alternative was Stock Up with the 5 mana (bait the Force, dig for Veil, Show and Tell next turn), close; flagged.
- A p93, first real (minor) mistake, self-flagged: at 4 life A played Ancient Tomb before Veil + Show and Tell. Without it, Maze (Veil) + Island, Tundra, Petal (Show and Tell) paid everything, and once Veil resolved the Daze spare was moot (Veil makes the spell uncounterable by blue). The engine paid Show and Tell with Tomb + Island, so A went to 2 with Tundra and Petal still untapped. Unpunished: B was tapped out. Had the loop stopped, A at 2 died to a Bolt or one Monk hit; at 4 it survived a Bolt.
- A p105 (no impact): stopped Atraxa at Done, leaving a land (Strand/City) and Lotus Petal. Breaks the existing "take every card Atraxa offers".
- B: no clear mistake. The loss came from having no non-blue answer to Veil, not from a misplay. B's report misreads its two key turns:
  - Wasteland on Tomb (B p65) was RIGHT. B's report calls Hedge Maze "their only green source", but Lotus Petal was on the battlefield and casts Veil too. Tomb is 2 mana, and A held a second Tomb, so Maze-Wasteland leaves A with 5+ mana next turn. It matches "Wasteland: Ancient Tomb when Show and Tell is the threat", used on B's own turn before A untapped.
  - t8 tap-out with Bolt to face (B p80-84) was RIGHT. The existing note "hold U (and R) for Prismari Charm ... or Unholy Heat" did not apply: B held only Daze and Misty, with no Charm or Heat. Bolt as the second spell made a hasty Monk, so the turn dealt 12 (16 -> 4). Holding Bolt does not stop Veil + Show and Tell or the Omniscience loop, and A was out of Bolt range anyway (9 -> 7 after its Tomb).
  - B p54-55 (pitch Force over Murktide, self-flagged): Murktide made the clock, and the kept Force would not have won (see 1). Fine.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- (medium) "At 4 life or less, do not play Ancient Tomb before the combo spells when your other untapped mana pays for them: the engine pays generic mana with Tomb first. Cast Veil first; if it resolves, cast Show and Tell off the other lands and Petal. Play Tomb only when the spell cannot be paid without it." Evidence: A p93-96 (4 -> 2 with Tundra + Petal untapped). The Tomb also went first at t5 (p53, Petal left). Narrows Brady's jam line "land (Ancient Tomb), Veil of Summer, then Show and Tell" and "make the land drop before a committal spell" for low life. Extends "Life is mana" and the existing note "check your life yourself" (engine-oddities item 9).

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
- (medium) "The hold-mana rule is only for Prismari Charm or Unholy Heat. If your hand has only Bolt and Daze, cast Cutter and Bolt main phase (the Monk has haste) and take the maximum damage." Evidence: B p80-84. Clarifies "Do not tap out on your own turn for Murktide or Cutter once they can reach 4 mana".
- (low) "Lotus Petal on their battlefield is a green source: Wastelanding Hedge Maze does not stop Veil, so take the Tomb." Evidence: B p65 and B's wrong self-review. This is mostly the existing Wasteland note.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- B's report is wrong: Show and Tell offering Misty Rainforest (B p99) is legal, because Show and Tell puts in lands. The Daze cast through Veil (B p116) was legal but useless.
- A p118: the engine asked A to pay {1} for a Daze that Veil made unable to counter. A paid, harmlessly. Worth checking whether the engine would have countered on "No".
- Auto-payment: generic mana went to Ancient Tomb over Tundra or Petal both times (p53, p96). This is not a bug, but the player cannot choose the payment.
- Lost Mine under Omniscience resolved triggers (library 39 vs 16 laps), correct. No land drops skipped (A t1/3/5/7/9, B t2/4/6/8), no upkeep cantrips.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on game turn 9 on the play from 2 life: Veil off Hedge Maze, then Show and Tell for Omniscience, free Atraxa, and a Lost Mine drain 16 -> 0, after B spent both Forces on A's t5 Show and Tell.
TOP MISTAKE: A p93, playing Ancient Tomb at 4 life before Veil + Show and Tell when Island, Tundra and Petal paid for everything; the auto-payment cost 2 life (4 -> 2).
TOP RULE: At low life, keep Ancient Tomb off the battlefield when the other mana pays; the engine taps Tomb first.
