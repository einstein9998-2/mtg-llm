# Review g63: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 9 (A's 5th turn): it hardcast Aluren with zero spare mana (City of Traitors plus two Hedge Mazes) while B was tapped out on one Volcanic, then cast a free Acererak, and the Lost Mine macro drained B from 19. A was at 9 life.
- Turn 7 (B prompt 50): A cast Show and Tell with one Hedge Maze spare. B cast Daze first, A paid, then B cast Force of Will. The only blue card left to pitch was its second Force (prompt 55), so the line spent three cards and left B with no counter. Force alone, pitching the second Force (dead anyway, since B had no other blue card), keeps Daze. A's turn-9 Aluren had zero spare, so that kept Daze counters it.
- Turn 8 (B prompt 58): B replayed Volcanic and tapped it in main phase for Ponder. B's graveyard had land, instant, sorcery, artifact and creature (delirium), so Unholy Heat did 6 damage. Held up, it kills the 5/5 Acererak with his enter trigger on the stack. A had one Acererak, no Force and no Veil, so the loop stops. Tapping out gave A the zero-risk window, and A took it.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none found. Turn 1 Tomb plus Petal into Stock Up, taking Show and Tell and Acererak, was right: B had no land, so Daze was impossible, and the hand needed a payoff.
- A's turn-3 wait (prompt 13) was reasonable. Jamming meant exactly 3 mana into a tapped Volcanic, and B held Daze (but no Force yet), so a jam would have been countered. The wait was planned around Tomb + dual + City for 2 spare mana on turn 5. B's Wasteland then took the Tomb, the obvious target, and the uncracked Strand decoy could not change that.
- A's turn-5 wait (prompt 34) is close. Maze + City was 3 with no spare, and B held Daze + Force, so both lines failed. Waiting cost 4 life to DRC and gave B a Brainstorm that found the second Force. Under Brady's test it helped both sides about equally. The result was not changed.
- A's turn-7 Show and Tell (prompt 48), rather than an Aluren with no spare, was right. It made B spend Daze + Force + Force, which emptied B's hand of counters. The turn-9 jam (prompt 63) was also right. B was tapped out and had just spent its counters, A was at 9 facing a 3/3 flier, and waiting would let B untap with Heat, Bolt or Charm.
- Fetches: Hedge Maze at both of B's end steps (prompts 32, 46) was the best choice, giving two G/U sources, untapped on A's turn, plus surveil. A's report says green sources earlier would have allowed Aluren a turn sooner. That is wrong: once the Tomb was gone, Aluren (4 mana including GG) needed City plus two lands, and turn 7 was the earliest that was possible.
- B: the first real mistake was turn 7, prompt 50 (Daze first; see 1). B's reason was to bait Veil, but that gains nothing: Veil cast in response to either counter makes Show and Tell uncounterable. The worse mistake was turn 8, prompt 58 (Ponder tapping out). B's report gives the wrong alternative ("FoW pitching Daze, keep FoW#2"); with no other blue card, FoW#2 could not have been cast.
- B's keep (Volcanic, Wasteland, two cantrips, DRC, Bolt, Heat) was fine. Its Wasteland on Tomb on turn 4 was before A untapped and matches the notes. Being stuck on one land from turn 4 to 8 was mostly variance. A turn-2 Wasteland on the Tomb (A's only land) instead of Volcanic + DRC is a close alternative (flagged).

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules

"After the opponent has spent Daze and Force of Will on your Show and Tell and is tapped out on one land, cast Aluren next turn even with no spare mana when you are under a clock; a wait lets them untap with Heat, Charm or a fresh counter." Evidence: turn 9, A prompt 63 (won). Confidence: medium. This qualifies the existing "Do not tap out for Aluren when Daze may be in hand and I have no spare mana (a05)" and extends "When the opponent taps out on its own turn its lands stay tapped through my turn" (t22-t26). It does not duplicate either.
- Considered and dropped: "when planning a wait, count Ancient Tomb as dead against Wasteland" (turn 3). In hindsight, jamming into B's Daze would have lost, so the rule would not have helped here.
