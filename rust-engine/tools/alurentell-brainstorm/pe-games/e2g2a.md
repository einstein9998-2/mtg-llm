RESULT: WON
TURNS: 5
SIDEBOARD: Drew and cast Carpet of Flowers (turn 2, then a second copy went back via Brainstorm), one Orim's Chant, two Lotus Petal and one Veil of Summer (held). Carpet was the best card of the game: their Islands made it 2, then 3, then 4 mana per turn, which paid for Brainstorm/Ponder/Stock Up and for the S&T + Aluren turn. Chant was cast on turn 4 and drawn out Force of Will (pitching Daze); Petal (from Stock Up and Atraxa) was spare mana for the kill turn. Keep all of them in this matchup.
LIST: I mulliganed a zero-land seven and kept six (bottomed Force of Will; it had one blue pitch card). Chants: drew #1 on turn 3 from Brainstorm; held it that turn because Show and Tell could not be cast the same turn (floating Carpet mana went to Brainstorm and I was one mana short of Chant + S&T). On turn 4 I cast it first on my own turn with Show and Tell behind it (their board: Monk token, Cutter, 3 untapped Islands, 5 cards; me at 13). They Forced it (pitching Daze), then Pyroblasted my Show and Tell: so Chant ate Force + Daze and Show and Tell ate Pyroblast, 2 for 2 plus 2 free pitch cards. A second Chant came off Atraxa on turn 5 but was not needed. Petals: drew one off Stock Up on turn 4 and a second off Atraxa's reveal on turn 5; both were extra mana for Daze insurance on the kill turn (Show and Tell + Aluren = 7, with a Petal and Tomb spare). Alurens: only one drawn, off Atraxa's reveal (Show and Tell for Atraxa on turn 5 while they were tapped out with 1 card; 3 Alurens in 38 cards still found it with a 10-card reveal). A second Petal made the turn-5 mana 8 and let me keep Tomb untapped (life 5); a third Chant did not decide anything; having only three Alurens did not matter this game but 4 would have made the Stock Up on turn 4 more likely to hit. I did not miss Prismatic Ending: they never played a Channeler; the Monk tokens from Cutter were the only pressure and Ending cannot hit a token cheaply anyway. I would keep 3 Chant and 2 Petal here; Chant plus Carpet mana is a good combo-turn tool and Petal is a cheap Daze payer.
- Possible mistake, turn 3: I put back Veil and Tropical to Brainstorm, then chose to dig with a second Brainstorm instead of holding mana; I took 6 from Monks across turns 3 and 4 and was at 5 life on turn 5, close to dead to a second burn spell. A blocker or lifegain (I had Atraxa and Acererak in hand but no way to cast them) would have helped; keeping more life by not tapping Ancient Tomb for Stock Up on turn 4 (cost 2 life) was arguable.
- Surprise: they Forced my Chant on turn 4 (instead of saving it for Show and Tell), pitching Daze, and then Pyroblasted my Show and Tell. They also tapped out for a second Cutter plus equip on their own turn 5 with 1 card in hand, which opened the winning window. Pyroblast counters blue spells: it stopped Show and Tell.
- Tool/engine: the odds tool reported 0 unseen Pyroblast for their hand even though they had boarded it in (it only knows the base main), so Pyroblast percentages are understated. The Atraxa/Show and Tell menu and the free Aluren cast worked; the Lost Mine macro needs LIFEMIN below your life total (mine was 5, so I used 2); the macro ran 284 picks to take them from 18 to 0. No real bugs found.
