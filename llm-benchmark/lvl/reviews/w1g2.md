# Review w1g2: Alurentell (A, on the draw, mulligan to 6) vs UR Cutter (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
A (Alurentell) won on turn 18 (A's 9th turn) with Atraxa attacks (7 lifelink a turn, three hits). B never had an answer to a resolved Atraxa.
- Turn 10 (A prompts 70-77) decided it: Ancient Tomb, then Veil of Summer (Hedge Maze), then Show and Tell for Atraxa (Tomb + Tropical), with Lotus Petal spare against a Daze on Veil. B held Force of Will x2, Volcanic and Polluted Delta: one real counter (the second Force is the pitch). Countering Veil would have let Show and Tell resolve anyway, so B correctly passed (B prompts 73/76). The Atraxa reveal took Aluren, Veil, Acererak, Boseiju and Show and Tell.
- After that A missed three wins on the spot, all hidden by Atraxa's dominance:
  - T12 (prompt 115): B forced Aluren (pitching Daze), A cast Veil in response, and B forced Veil (pitching Preordain), going to 0 cards. A passed with Force of Will in hand (Show and Tell to pitch). Forcing back makes Veil resolve, so Aluren cannot be countered, and Acererak is free under Aluren: the drain loop wins against an empty hand.
  - T14 (prompt 139): after Stock Up, A held Show and Tell, Omniscience and Acererak, with Tropical Island and City of Traitors untapped (3 mana; Cast Show and Tell was on the menu). B had 1 card (Lightning Bolt) and no Force left. Show and Tell for Omniscience, then free Acererak recasts, wins. Instead A played Misty (City of Traitors sacrificed) and channeled Boseiju on Cutter.
  - T15 (prompt 156): A forced a Prismari Charm with no target shown (surveil/draw mode), pitching Omniscience. That gave up the T16 Show and Tell + Omniscience + Acererak win to stop a cantrip. A's report calls the Omniscience pick "pointless": A did not see the Acererak loop under Omniscience.
- Also important: B's Wastelands on Hedge Maze (T3) and Tropical Island (T7) held A to 4 mana until turn 10.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, T8, prompt 56 (probable, medium): hand Misty, Ancient Tomb, Veil, Show and Tell, Atraxa, with Hedge Maze and Petal on board. A played Misty and waited. Better: Ancient Tomb, then Show and Tell (Tomb + Maze), holding Petal. Petal pays for a Daze, or casts Veil in response to a Force of Will (Veil makes Show and Tell uncounterable; this works in this engine, t15). That beats any single counter. A's worry was Daze on a Veil cast first, which is a real problem at 4 mana, but casting Show and Tell first removes it. B held exactly Force + Force, so Atraxa would have landed two turns earlier, before B drew Daze (T11). The wait also breaks Brady's "when you hold protection ... jam it now".
- A, T12, prompt 115 (clear): the Force of Will not cast, see 1. This is the costliest error; it only went unpunished because B had no outs.
- A's other choices were sound: the mulligan of a 0-land 7, bottoming Acererak (plan F without Aluren or Omniscience), Stock Up with a Petal spare (T6), taking Atraxa + Ancient Tomb from Stock Up, and keeping Misty uncracked until B's end step (T5, T9).
- B: no mistake that changed the result. This was a FoW/Wasteland-heavy draw with only two threats (DRC, Cutter) and no main-deck answer to Atraxa. Weakest choices: T11 (prompts 95-96), Brainstorm put back B's only land (Delta) and Bolt, skipping the land drop. T15 (prompt 154), Prismari Charm on surveil/draw instead of bouncing Atraxa (see flags).
