RESULT: LOST
TURNS: 4
PROTECT: My main deck has two Veil of Summer (no Chant). I drew one Veil on turn 1 (opening seven) and never cast it. It was a blank against Boros, as the playbook says: they have no blue or black spells, so there was nothing to protect or stop. I put it back with a Brainstorm on turn 2 and shuffled it away with Flooded Strand. My Force of Will was the only real protection and it was stranded: with Acererak on the stack and Swords on it, I was at 1 life (Ancient Tomb paid for Show and Tell) and Force costs 1 life, so I could not cast it. Swords exiled Acererak.
Would I rather have had Orim's Chant in those two slots? Not clearly. Chant would not have stopped Swords on a loop piece in a way that matters here, and kicked Chant costs mana I did not have. The deck needed a land-light, faster, or lifegain-friendly slot, not a Chant. A card that does something against Boros (Prismatic Ending style) would be better than either.
- Mistake: I kept a one-land seven (Tundra, Petal, Brainstorm, two Ponder, Aluren, Veil) on the draw. The playbook (OH5) says a one-lander with Ponder is a mulligan. Ponder on turn 1 found no land, then Wasteland took Tundra and Boseiju/duals lagged. By turn 4 I had spent two turns digging, took 6 and 9 damage, and was at 3 life facing 11 power. Also I did not Force the turn-2 Ajani (Cat token) or the Sand Scout, and the Force I drew later could not be paid at 1 life.
- Opponent surprise: they held Swords with Plateau untapped and fired it on Acererak with his trigger on the stack, exactly the playbook BOo2 line, and put nothing in with Show and Tell.
- Possible engine issues: none seen. Force of Will's 1 life cost being unpayable at 1 life is correct rules, but the menu still offered it ("Cast Force of Will") at 1 life.
