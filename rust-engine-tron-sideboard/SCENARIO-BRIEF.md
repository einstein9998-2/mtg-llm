## Interface facts (how the engine represents things; not a hint about correctness)
- **Eldrazi Confluence** is "choose three, you may choose the same mode more than once". The engine's mode chooser offers each mode once, so the card has TEN modes, one for every multiset of three of the printed modes. A cast chooses exactly one of them (a `choose_mode` step with this index; ask the SCHEMA for the exact form). Each pump or blink instance has its own target slot, in printed order (all +3/-3 targets first, then the exile-and-return targets); Scion instances have none. The indexes: 0 = +3/-3 x3; 1 = +3/-3 x2, blink x1; 2 = +3/-3 x2, Scion x1; 3 = +3/-3 x1, blink x2; 4 = +3/-3 x1, blink x1, Scion x1; 5 = +3/-3 x1, Scion x2; 6 = blink x3; 7 = blink x2, Scion x1; 8 = blink x1, Scion x2; 9 = Scion x3. Test the RULES outcome of the multiset you pick, not the shape of the chooser.
- **Summon: Bahamut** is a Saga creature: Enchantment Creature - Saga Dragon, 9/9 flying, {9}. Chapter triggers behave as for Urza's Saga in the Storm package.
- **Argentum Masticore**: the upkeep "sacrifice unless you discard" is a yes/no then a card choice; ask the SCHEMA / the Storm and Boros scenarios for the shapes of `yes_no` and `choose_cards`. When the controller has no card in hand the sacrifice is the only outcome (no decision). The reflexive trigger's target is chosen when it goes on the stack.
- **Mycosynth Lattice** needs no special syntax.

## Engine simplifications: do NOT write scenarios for these
- Protection from multicolored covers targeting, damage and blocking only (not enchanting or equipping).
- The engine does not model "choose the same mode twice" as a repeated choice (see the ten modes above); do not test the chooser.
- A permanent that is a copy of another, face-down permanents and split second are not in this pool; do not use them.

## Groups
- **A: Eldrazi Confluence and Summon: Bahamut.** Confluence: costs {2}{C}{C} (colorless mana only for the {C}; Trinisphere raises it), instant speed; pump x3 on one creature (-9 toughness, dies), pump on different creatures, +3/-3 kills a 3-toughness creature but a 4-toughness one lives as 7/1; blink returns the permanent tapped under its owner's control as a new object (summoning sick, ETB triggers again, counters gone, an Aura/Equipment falls off, a planeswalker returns with fresh loyalty, a token exiled does not return, an opponent's permanent returns under the opponent's control); blinking a land puts it back tapped; two blink instances on the same permanent (the second does nothing: it is a new object, 400.7); blink in response/without response by Force of Will on the Confluence; Scion x3 makes three tokens that each sacrifice for {C}; mixed multisets (e.g. blink Karn + Scion x2, pump + pump + Scion); a target that becomes illegal (spell still resolves for the other instances; fizzles only if all its targets are illegal); targets chosen per instance (the same creature may be named twice); modes resolve in printed order (pump before blink before Scion) so pump then blink on the same creature in one cast. Bahamut: enters with lore counter and chapter I triggers at once (up to one target, zero targets allowed, nonland only: lands cannot be named); chapter II after your next draw step; chapter III draws two; chapter IV deals damage to the opponent equal to the total mana value of OTHER permanents you control (lands 0, tokens 0, copies not in pool; a Trinisphere 3, a Karn 4; counted when the chapter resolves; excludes Bahamut itself; permanents lost in response are not counted; zero when nothing else), then sacrifice (the Saga is a creature and a 9/9 flyer in the meantime: attacks the turn after it enters if not sick, blocks, is hit by chapter-destroy in response, dies to Swords); counters/Force of Will on the {9} cast; Bahamut cast with Trinisphere out costs {9}; it is not a land. - **B: Argentum Masticore and Mycosynth Lattice.** Masticore: first strike in combat; protection from multicolored (can't be targeted by a multicolored spell such as Lightning Helix / Bloodbraid-style cards from the pool, can be targeted by monocolored and colorless ones, damage from a multicolored creature is prevented, can't be blocked by a multicolored creature, can be blocked by a mono-colored one; a colorless/artifact source is fine; under Mycosynth Lattice nothing is multicolored any more); upkeep: sacrifice unless discard (hand empty: sacrificed; hand non-empty: the controller may decline and sacrifice, or discard); discarding a card puts a reflexive trigger on the stack bounded by the discarded card's mana value (discard a land = MV 0, only MV 0 permanents like tokens/lands-excluded targets: lands cannot be targeted, so tokens and 0-cost nonland permanents such as Lotus Petal); discard a 3-mana card: Trinisphere/MV<=3 targets only; no legal target = the trigger is removed, the Masticore stays; target an opponent's nonland permanent only (not your own); the trigger resolves even if the Masticore left; indestructible target survives; only on your upkeep (not the opponent's); Force of Will etc. cast against the {5}. Lattice affects both players' mana. About 25 scenarios.
