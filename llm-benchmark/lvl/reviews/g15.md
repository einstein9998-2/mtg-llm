# Review g15: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 5 (A's 3rd turn). Prompt 44: Ancient Tomb + Hedge Maze paid for Show and Tell, with Petal unused. A put in Omniscience (B put in Cori-Steel Cutter). A then cast Acererak for free and ran the Lost Mine loop with the auto macro: 20 Dark Pool drains, 20 to 0, on the same turn. B never cast a counterspell all game.
- **Decisive (B, prompt 2): the mulligan.** B shipped Volcanic, Brainstorm, Daze, Force of Will, Bolt, Murktide, Cutter (one land, on the draw). The six it kept (Volcanic, 2 Wasteland, Brainstorm, Murktide, DRC) had no free counter, and none was drawn in the next 6 cards. Force of Will was the only card that beat A's Show and Tell, because A had a spare Petal for Daze both times. B's sim (about 54% to hit a land "in two draws") left out the Brainstorm. On the draw, the turn-2 draw plus Brainstorm sees 4 cards, and 18 lands in 53 gives about 82% for land two (Wasteland counts as a land drop).
- **A's most important decision: turn 3, prompts 12-14 (the wait).** A had Misty on the battlefield, plus Tomb and Petal, and passed. A's own flag 12 says the jam was "exactly 4 mana (Daze kills it)", and flag 1 says "need UU for Show". Both are wrong: Show and Tell costs {2}{U}. Cracking Misty for Island (or Tropical) with Tomb was a turn-3 Show and Tell with the Petal spare against Daze, and A had Acererak for Omniscience. B's Volcanic was tapped (DRC), so only Force of Will could stop it. A's odds tool said 31% for Force of Will, and waiting a turn only raises that, because B draws and Brainstorms. The wait cost the first Tomb (Wastelanded on turn 4) and gave B a turn to dig. It cost nothing in the end only because B had no Force. Prompt 42 showed the true cost: "Cast Show and Tell" was offered with just Maze + Tomb untapped, and the Petal was still in hand.
- Verdict: a sound win, helped by B's hand. With the right cost, A would have won on turn 3.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, turn 3, prompt 13 (likely mistake, high confidence; A self-flagged it as prompt 12 on a wrong premise): A passed instead of cracking Misty and casting Show and Tell with Petal spare. The cause was a card-cost misread, not judgement. A also turned a clear keep at prompt 1 into a "close" flag because of the same misread: Tomb plus Misty is a turn-2 Show and Tell for Omniscience plus Acererak. Everything else was clean. The end-step Misty for Hedge Maze followed the playbook (B had shown Wasteland). Tomb, Petal and Show and Tell on turn 5 were correct, and the free Ponder mid-loop found Force of Will.
- B, prompt 2 (likely mistake, medium; self-flagged): the mulligan above. A one-lander with Brainstorm plus two free counters on the draw is a keep for this deck, and especially so against an unknown opponent whose first land was a fetch.
- B, turn 4, prompt 30 (likely mistake, medium): B cast Brainstorm in its main phase and tapped out, after the land drop (Wasteland) was already used. Nothing else in that main phase needed the mana, and DRC surveils whenever the spell is cast. Cast in A's upkeep or in response to Show and Tell, the same Brainstorm digs for Force of Will at the moment it matters (about 40% for Force or Daze in 3 cards), and Daze can still return a tapped Volcanic.
- B's Wasteland on Tomb (prompt 16/17) was right; A had no other mana.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Show and Tell costs {2}{U}: one blue source plus Ancient Tomb casts it. Tomb plus a fetch is a turn-2 Show and Tell, and Tomb, a blue land and a Petal is Show and Tell with a spare against Daze. If the menu offers the cast, the untapped sources already pay it; count the spare from what is left."
   - Evidence: A prompts 1 and 12 (flags assume UU or 4 mana), 13/14 (passed), 42 (cast offered with 3 sources), and the "Petal stayed untapped" oddity in A's report. Confidence: high (card text). Overlap: none. The playbook gives Omniscience's cost ("{7}{U}{U}{U}") but not Show and Tell's. With the right cost, the existing rules ("Spare mana beats Daze", "When the opponent taps out on its own turn ... t22, t23, t25 and t26 were won in exactly that window") already say jam.
2. "A Lost Mine lap is four Acererak casts: Cave Entrance (scry), Goblin Lair, Dark Pool (drain 1), Temple of Dumathoin (draw). Plan for 4 casts and about 18 auto picks per point of opponent life, so a 20-life kill needs about 370 picks; set the macro budget once for the whole kill."
   - Evidence: about 80 casts for 20 drains; 368 auto picks; 4 budget stops (8, 40, 120 and 150 picks) at A prompts 51, 56, 57 and 58. Confidence: high. Contradicts the playbook line "Cave Entrance (scry), Goblin Lair (blocker), Dark Pool (drain 1) is the three-cast cycle ... about three Acererak casts per point of life". Replace it.
