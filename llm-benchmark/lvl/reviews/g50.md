# g50 review: Alurentell (A, on the draw) beat Boros Aggro (B, on the play), game turn 4

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 4 (its 2nd turn) from B at 21. B tapped Plateau + Mountain for Voice of Victory on its turn 3 (B p9), so its lands stayed tapped through A's turn. A p11-19: Petal, Brainstorm (drew Petal, Stock Up, Strand; put back both Atraxa), Strand -> Tropical, Petal, Show and Tell (Boseiju + Trop + Petal). A put in Aluren (p20), B put in Goblin Bombardment (B p21), free Acererak (p22), Lost Mine macro with room triggers stacked drained 21 -> 0.
Decided by: (1) A's Brainstorm finding both a second mana source and a land; (2) B passing at p24 with Acererak's first enter trigger on the stack, when it held a loop-breaking line (below).

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A keep (p2, self-flagged as seq 2): Boseiju, Petal, Stock Up, Show and Tell, 2 Atraxa, Acererak. The sim said ~59% for a second land by A's 2nd turn (19 lands in 53, two draws: 1 - 34/53*33/52 = 59%). Verdict: right keep, lucky win. It fits Brady's exception (Show and Tell plus a payoff, Petal as the 3rd mana), and a 6 against Boros is worse. But the hand had no Aluren: as dealt, its best turn-4 line was Show and Tell for Atraxa. It drew Aluren on turn 2 (4 in 52), its two natural draws had no land, and only Brainstorm hitting Petal AND Strand made 3 mana. The sim's land-only goal also undercounts the outs (Petal, Brainstorm), so ~59% is a floor for "functions on turn 4", not the true number.
- A p11 (minor): cast Lotus Petal before Brainstorm, with Boseiju untapped. The engine then paid Brainstorm with the Petal, not Boseiju (p12 -> p13: Petal to the graveyard, Boseiju still untapped at p19). No cost here, because all four mana went into Brainstorm + Show and Tell. If Brainstorm had missed, the Petal was gone and Boseiju sat unused. Better: cast Brainstorm off Boseiju and keep Petal in hand.
- A otherwise clean: put back the two Atraxa (dead with Aluren + Acererak + Show and Tell in hand) and shuffled them with Strand. Aluren in with Show and Tell into a tapped-out board follows the playbook. Casting the only Acererak into Bombardment + 3 creatures (p22) was forced: passing gives Boros a free turn under A's own Aluren.
- B keep (p1: Plateau, Strand, Mesa, Ocelot, Voice, Bombardment, Phlage) was a clear keep. The turn-1 Ocelot and turn-3 Mountain + Voice had no alternative that mattered. Bombardment (1R) also taps out, Phlage costs 3, and B drew no Wasteland or Swords, so Boseiju-only A could not be stopped before turn 4.
- B Show and Tell pick (p21, self-flagged): Bombardment over Phlage was RIGHT, for a reason B missed. Under A's Aluren, Phlage (MV 3) is castable free at instant speed anyway (offered at p23-p32), while Bombardment could not be cast tapped out.
- B real mistake, p24 (repeated at p28): Acererak 5/5 on the battlefield, his enter trigger on the stack, and A's hand was 2 Stock Up (only one Acererak). Cast Phlage free: 3 damage to Acererak. Sacrifice Phlage (with its sacrifice trigger pending), Ocelot, Cat and Voice to Bombardment: 4 more, 7 >= 5. Acererak dies, the trigger cannot return him, and the loop ends with A tapped out at 18 holding two Stock Ups. B would still have been an underdog, but it was the only out, and B's report calls Bombardment "irrelevant". Not variance: the existing note says "kill the first Acererak on its enter trigger".

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- (low-medium) "Cast Lotus Petal in the same window as the spell that needs it. Pay cantrips with lands: the engine spends a Petal on the battlefield before an untapped land." Evidence: A p11-13. Narrows the existing "wait as long as possible to cast a spell if waiting is free"; contradicts nothing (Spider-Woman's "cast Petals a turn early" stays the exception).
- (low, question for Brady) "Against Boros with one Acererak: count Bombardment sacrifices plus 3 for a free Phlage under your Aluren against Acererak's 5 toughness. If they reach 5, prefer Show and Tell for Atraxa, or dig for a second Acererak first." Evidence: B p24. Refines "Aluren is symmetric ... free Phlage deals 3". It would not have changed this game: A could not see B's put-in at p20, and B had 3 creatures with Phlage hidden.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
- (high) "Under their Aluren, with Bombardment out, kill Acererak on his first enter trigger: cast Phlage free (3 to Acererak), then sacrifice Phlage before its sacrifice trigger resolves, plus creatures, until he has 5 damage. If he was their only copy, the loop is over." Evidence: B p24/p28. Spells out the existing "kill the first Acererak on its enter trigger" and "Under their Aluren, cast your MV 3 or less creatures free", which B did not apply.
- (medium) "Show and Tell holding Bombardment and Phlage: put in Bombardment. Phlage is free anyway if they put in Aluren, and Bombardment cannot be cast while you are tapped out." Evidence: B p21. Qualifies the g48 candidate "put in Phlage over Voice".

## 5. Process and engine flags
- A's report is wrong: "Cast Acererak" at p19 was payable (Petal for B plus Boseiju and Tropical, {2}{B}). That is not an engine oddity.
- B's report is wrong: "Bombardment damage irrelevant". The engine offered Phlage and Bombardment at every priority window of the loop (p23, 24, 27, 28, 29, 32).
- Engine payment: Brainstorm used the Petal on the battlefield instead of an untapped Boseiju (A p12). Probably the default payment order; worth checking whether the player can choose.
- Lost Mine macro: 364 auto picks, room triggers left stacked as Brady specifies; Dark Pool took A from 18 to 30+. No land drops skipped (A turns 2/4, B turns 1/3), no upkeep cantrips, no wasted tool calls beyond A's 3 keep-time sim calls.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on game turn 4 on the draw: a one-land keep drew Aluren, Brainstorm found Petal + Strand, Show and Tell for Aluren into tapped-out Boros, then a Lost Mine loop from 21.
TOP MISTAKE: B p24, passing with Acererak's enter trigger on the stack: free Phlage (3) plus Bombardment sacrifices (4) killed A's only Acererak and ended the loop.
TOP RULE: Boros note: under their Aluren, free Phlage onto Acererak plus Bombardment sacrifices to 5 damage on his first enter trigger.
