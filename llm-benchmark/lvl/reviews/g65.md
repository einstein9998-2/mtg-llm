# Review g65: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw, mulligan to 5)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won, but on game turn 9 (A's 5th turn, A's own main phase), not on B's turn 8 attack. The attack took A from 11 to 2 (Monk 4 + Monk 2 + DRC 3 = 9). B's report says the attack was lethal; it was not. A then cast Show and Tell (A prompt 96) and died at once.
- **What killed A: Ancient Tomb's 2 damage, from the engine's auto-payment.** The truth log does not print the final life loss. It ends `llm -> 2 Cast Show and Tell` and then `YOU LOST`, with no B prompt in between (B's last prompt is 94). Burn and combat are ruled out: B's only card was Force of Will with no blue card to pitch, B had one untapped Volcanic, and there is no combat on A's turn. A had Island, Hedge Maze, Ancient Tomb and two Tropical Islands untapped, so three Tomb-free ways to pay {2}{U} existed. The menu (Pass / Cast Aluren / Cast Show and Tell) offers no payment or mana-ability choice. There is a precedent in this game: at prompts 62-63, with all four lands untapped, the engine paid Stock Up with Island + Tomb ("2 damage to you") rather than Island + Maze + Tropical. A's report says it confirmed this on "T3". That is wrong: the turn-5 Stock Up (prompt 29) had to use the Tomb, because Island + Maze make only 2.
- Had Show and Tell resolved, A wins: Show in Aluren, then a free Acererak and the Lost Mine loop, and B had no possible response. So the engine decided the game. B's turn-8 Cutter #2 then Bolt as the second spell (B prompts 74, 83) set it up. That made two flurry Monks and forced A's Force of Will.

It held Tropical Island and Ponder at the time. Ponder first, then Tropical from hand, does the same job for no life, and it follows the existing rule "cast cantrips ... before playing the land". A died with exactly 2 life against a 2-damage Tomb tap; one more point leaves it at 1 and winning. (The caveat: the Misty shuffle changed what Stock Up saw.)

- **B's first real mistake: prompts 51-52.** B's Brainstorm put back Ponder (its only blue card) and Bolt, then Delta shuffled them away. Force of Will had no pitch card from turn 6 on, and B had no answer to the turn-9 Show and Tell; only the Tomb saved B. - Milling Wasteland with DRC (B prompt 91) was harmless but pointless. Delirium was already on (instant, sorcery, land, creature in the graveyard, contrary to B's report), and a Wasteland kept on top would only be drawn on turn 10.
