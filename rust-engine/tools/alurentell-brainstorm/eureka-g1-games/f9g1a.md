RESULT: WON
TURNS: 6 (won on my 6th turn, game turn 11, by the Aluren plus Acererak Lost Mine drain loop; opponent 17 to 0, I ended at about 40 life)

EUREKA: Drew one Eureka, on my turn 3. Ponder showed Tundra, Acererak, Eureka and I put Eureka on top, so I drew it right away off Ponder's own draw.
- Turn 4 (my 4th turn): hard cast Eureka with all four lands (Island, Savannah, two Tropical Islands) while the opponent was tapped out. I held Aluren, two Atraxa, Acererak, Tundra. They held 7 cards and no land in hand. Their only possible answer was a free counter, and they used it: Force of Will (pitching Quantum Riddler) countered Eureka. No permanents entered in any round, for me or for them, so Eureka achieved nothing except eating their Force of Will and a Riddler. I was glad I had cast it before Aluren. I planned to put in Aluren, Acererak and both Atraxa, expecting two Atraxa enter triggers even though the legend rule kills one.
- Turn 5: Aluren (hard cast, 4 of 5 lands) resolved. Acererak came in free. They had Consign to Memory ready for the enter trigger: it countered the trigger, so Acererak stayed on the battlefield with no venture. Swords then exiled Acererak on their turn (I gained 5). Phelia attacked and blinked Aluren, which came back at end step.
- Turn 6: Show and Tell resolved (they were tapped out, no counter), I put in Atraxa, they put in nothing. Atraxa's reveal gave Acererak, Force of Will, Show and Tell, Lotus Petal, Ancient Tomb. Free Acererak then looped on Lost Mine, uncountered, about 17 laps for the win.
- Eureka versus Show and Tell here: Eureka is worse in this matchup. They hold a full grip (they had 7 to 9 cards while land-light), so a symmetric put-in is a gift to them, and it is a noncreature spell that Force of Will and Force of Negation both hit. Show and Tell has the same exposure but is 1 mana cheaper and forces nothing good for them since they chose to put nothing in. I would keep 0 Eureka against this UW Phelia deck (maybe 1 as a different-shaped threat if I had to), and I would put the Ponders back. If forced to have it, it is best as bait before the real combo piece when they are tapped out.
