# Review g16: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
B (Boros) won on turn 6 (A's 3rd turn): A cast Aluren at 8 life and B, holding priority, sacrificed 8 of its 9 creatures to Goblin Bombardment in response (B prompts 46-69).
- B's curve decided it: turn 1 Ocelot Pride; turn 3 Amped Raptor, whose energy cast Spider-Woman free from exile; turn 5 a second Ocelot Pride plus Goblin Bombardment, a 6-damage attack (A 17 -> 11), and an end step that hit city's blessing (10 permanents), so the two Ocelot triggers made 4 Cats. That left B with 9 creatures, and Bombardment turned them into 9 instant-speed damage.
- **Turn 6, A prompts 40-45: no line was left.** A had 11 life, Maze + Tomb on the battlefield, Tundra/Strand/Misty in hand, and Aluren, Acererak, Show and Tell, Atraxa. Without Ancient Tomb A had 2 mana, and Show and Tell needs 3 and Aluren needs 4 with GG. Every Tomb line ends at 9 or less (Tundra + Tomb Show and Tell: 9; Misty + Tomb Aluren: 8), and that is at or below B's 9 creatures. Passing loses to the next attack (11+ power plus Bombardment). A's choice (Aluren, self-flag 40) was moot. B played it right: it passed when A cracked Misty at 10 life (prompt 43, only 9 reach) and fired everything only after the Tomb payment.
- **The real margin was life and Tomb-free mana.** A paid 5 life to its own lands (Tomb on turn 4 for Stock Up -2, Misty -1, Tomb -2). Two of those points would have been enough: at 10 after paying for Aluren, A survives the 9 pings at 1, B is left with no creatures, and Aluren plus free Acererak loops drain B out. A Tomb-free 3-mana Show and Tell (putting in Aluren) at 11 life also survives. That needed one more mana that costs no life, the Lotus Petal that A spent on turn 2.
- Verdict: mostly matchup and variance. A was on the draw with a hand two pieces short (needed Show and Tell or Aluren plus Acererak), saw Show and Tell only with the turn-6 draw, and faced a near-nut Boros curve. The one choice that can be argued is the turn-2 Petal (below). Its value cannot be proven in hindsight, because the Ponder shuffle changed every later draw.
