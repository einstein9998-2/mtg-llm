# Two Orim's Chant or two Veil of Summer in the main deck, against Boros, Storm and Tron (2026-10-17)

Follow-up to `REPORT-chant-veil.md`. Brady's point after the first round was that Chant also has uses against non-blue decks where Veil of Summer is lacking, and asked for Boros, Storm and Tron. Same two lists, same layout: game 1 (nobody sideboarded), 4 games per opponent per list (12 per list, 24 games), Sonnet agents on both seats, same seed and same play/draw for game n in both lists.

- **Veil list** (`chantveil-games/arms/cur/alurentell.txt`): the base list, 2 Veil of Summer main.
- **Chant list** (`chantveil-games/arms/brady/alurentell.txt`): 2 Orim's Chant main instead of the 2 Veil, 4 Veil in the sideboard, 1 Faerie Macabre turned into a Forest.
- Opponents: `boros-aggro`, `storm` and `colorless-tron` from `decks-base`. They have no playbook.

## Result
| Opponent | Veil main | Chant main |
|---|---|---|
| Boros Aggro | 3-1 | 4-0 |
| Storm | 4-0 | 4-0 |
| Colorless Tron | 2-2 | 3-1 |
| **Total** | **9-3** | **11-1** |

With the first round added (UR, UB, UW), over all 72 games: Veil main 17-7, Chant main 18-6.

Two games apart over 12 games per list is inside the noise (about ±28 points each), so this is still "no difference found". It is not the pattern the first round suggested either: Chant was not worse against anything, and the only places the two lists differ in what happened (below) are on Boros.

## What the cards did
**Chant list (against non-blue): drawn into hand in 4 of 12, cast in 2, both against Boros, both wins.**
- b1 (Boros, win): in the opening seven. Cast unkicked on turn 4 with the opponent tapped out, right before Aluren. That stopped everything Boros could cast that turn, including the free creatures Aluren gives them, Swords and Amped Raptor's discover, and the whole Lost Mine loop ran with no response.
- b3 (Boros, win): dead for four turns with no white source, then Misty fetched Savannah and Chant was cast on main 1 before Aluren, opponent tapped out. Same effect. Cost: a Tomb activation (2 life) and the Petal.
- b9 (Tron, win): in the opening seven, put back with Brainstorm. Never cast.
- b10 (Tron, win): binned by a Hedge Maze surveil, the second taken from an Atraxa reveal and held, never cast.
- Seen only in Stock Up or an Atraxa reveal and passed over: b6 (Storm, win), b11 (Tron, loss), b12 (Tron, win).

**Veil list: drawn into hand in 4 of 12, cast in 0.**
- c1 (Boros, loss): drawn twice, Brainstormed away, drawn again off Ponder. A blank, and the player says one kicked Chant turn would have bought the Show and Tell turn it was one blue mana short of.
- c3 (Boros, win), c9 (Tron, win), c12 (Tron, loss): drawn, dead.
- Only seen in surveil, Stock Up or an Atraxa reveal: c6 (Storm, took one with Atraxa after the loop was set up), c10 and c11 (Tron).

Chant did real work in 2 of the 4 Boros games; Veil did nothing in any of the 12 games.

## What the players said
Each Alurentell player was asked whether they would rather have the other card.

| | Prefer Chant | Keep what they had (Chant) | Neither / no difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Veil list (12) | c1, c3, c4, c5, c6, c7, c8, c9, c12 | - | c2, c10, c11 |
| Chant list (12) | - | b1, b3, b4, b6, b7, b8, b9, b10 | b2, b5, b11, b12 |

Nobody on the Chant list wanted Veil back.

- **Boros.** Veil is a blank (no blue or black spells, no counters to beat). Chant, unkicked on the combo turn, stops free creatures off Aluren, Swords to Plowshares and the discover from Amped Raptor, which is what c1, c3, c4, b1, b3 and b4 said. In b4 Force of Will did the same job, and in b2 and c2 the opponent was tapped out so neither card mattered.
- **Storm.** All 8 games were won on Alurentell's turn 1 to 3, and Storm only got a Thoughtseize (c5, it took Show and Tell and Aluren plus Acererak won anyway) and Empty the Warrens (c8) off, so nothing needed an answer. Players on the Veil list (c5, c6, c7, c8) said Chant in response to a ritual or on their upkeep stops the storm turn, while Veil stops nothing Storm does. The Chant players (b7, b8) said the same; b5 and b6 said neither was needed.
- **Tron.** Both cards are a blank for the same reason (no counters, discard or targeted removal), and the players said so (c10, c11, c12, b11, b12). Some want Chant slightly more because a kicked Chant stops a Trinisphere or Karn cast on the combo turn; c10 and c11 would rather have a real artifact answer (Prismatic Ending, Boseiju, Force of Negation) than either.

## Losses
- c1 (Boros): lost the race at 7 life to Cats and Guide of Souls. Veil dead in hand twice, a Petal spent on Ponder left it one blue short of Show and Tell.
- c11 and b11 (Tron): Trinisphere on turn 1 or 2 plus Karn, Tezzeret, Ugin and animated artifacts. c11 spent Force of Will (pitching Show and Tell) on the first Trinisphere with no payoff in hand, then found no Atraxa, Aluren or Omniscience; b11 kept a hand with only Show and Tell and had Trinisphere, Karakas and Ugin shut off both routes. The slot did not decide either.
- c12 (Tron): mulligan to six, Show and Tell for Aluren with no mana left for a free cast, then nothing; Trinisphere probably came in (the player never saw the put-in).

Tron wins on the Alurentell side came from Show and Tell into Aluren plus Acererak before Trinisphere or Karakas mattered (c9, b9, b12), the same after Force of Will answered Karn and Ugin (c10, b10, where c10 also won when the opponent's own The One Ring burden killed them). Both Tron-side wins (c11, b11) came from animating a Grim Monolith or The One Ring with Karn.

## Caveats
- 12 games per list; Storm is nearly a fixed 4-0 against both (the opponent usually does nothing on the Alurentell turn), so the real comparison is Boros and Tron, 8 games per list.
- Play/draw and seed are shared per game number, but opening hands are not (the lists differ).
- The Veil and Chant players both saw their own deck and the opponent's main deck by design. One Storm opponent report (b6) says it opened the base-deck folder while checking cards and saw the Alurentell list; the game was a Chant-list win by turn 3, so it is not material.
- Engine and menu items worth a look, each from a single report unless noted:
  - **Lion's Eye Diamond turns** (c6 and b8, both Storm): after casting LED or a 0-cost artifact the engine gave no prompt, so the LED/Echo of Eons line could not be run, and the turn and the opponent's turn were auto-passed. b7's Storm player reports the same thing after a pick. Hold mana abilities for the first prompt.
  - **Karn, the Great Creator and Lotus Petal** (c11, c12 and b10): three reports say Petal could not make mana while Karn was out. Brady ruled this is correct (Karn stops mana abilities too), so it is not an engine bug.
  - **Trinisphere** (c12): after Show and Tell put in Aluren, no more prompts for turns 3 and 4 (the free casts cost 3). Probably correct.
  - A `;` in a pick reason runs as shell text; the pick still goes through (seen in several reports).
  - `get_card` does not know the Tron cards (Karn, Tezzeret, Saga, Ring, Trinisphere), so the Tron players played from memory.
