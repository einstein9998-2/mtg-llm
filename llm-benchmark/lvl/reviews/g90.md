# g90 review: A (Alurentell) beat B (Boros Aggro) on turn 6 (A's 3rd turn, A on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
- A won on T6 by hard-casting Aluren (a38) off Petal, Tropical, Island and a second Tropical (GG), then a free Acererak (a39) and the Lost Mine loop. Life when the loop started: A 11, B 24 (not the other way round; Phlage plus attacks took A from 18 to 11 on T5). The log has exactly 24 Dark Pool picks for B's 24 life, 95 Acererak casts (2 by hand, 93 by macro), Goblin Lair every lap; A ended at about 35. No issue with the kill.
- Deciding factor: on T5 B tapped all three lands (Plateau, Plateau, Karakas) for Phlage, so A's T6 had no possible Swords. B held Windswept Heath x2 and Guide of Souls only: no Swords, Erode, Wasteland or Bombardment all game, so A could not have been punished on T6. The only card that would have slowed A was a T5 Wasteland on a Tropical (Aluren then lacks GG for a turn).
- A's dig: T4 Brainstorm (a17) drew Aluren, Omniscience, Show and Tell; put back Veil (blank vs Boros) and Boseiju; Misty crack (a24) shuffled them away; second Brainstorm (a26) drew Tropical, Tomb, Acererak. That order is the playbook's weave and was right. Brainstorm over Ponder for the second spell was also right: the top three were Tropical, Tomb, Acererak, so Ponder would have left Acererak under the Tomb and T6 would have had no Aluren mana after a Brainstorm.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none clear. Keep (a2, self-flagged): 4 lands with G and U, 2 Brainstorm, Ponder, on the draw. No payoff, but three cantrips and both colours; a keep, not a mulligan.
- A, Ponder shuffle (a9, self-flagged): A saw Show and Tell, Strand, Show and Tell with no enabler or payoff in hand and shuffled. The playbook says "without an enabler in hand, keep a known Aluren or Show and Tell on top". By rough count it is close: keep = a sure Show and Tell plus 5 fresh cards by the T4 Misty shuffle (about 68% to also hold a payoff); shuffle = 8 fresh cards (about 65% to hold both an enabler and a payoff). Keeping was marginally better and matches the rule, but the shuffle is not a real mistake. The payoff, not the enabler, was the bottleneck.
- A, Petal (a10/a11, hindsight): passed T2 holding a castable Lotus Petal. Spider-Woman arrived on T3, so the Petal cast on T4 (a22) entered tapped. Cast on T2, an untapped Petal plus a Tomb land drop after the T4 Brainstorm gives Show and Tell for Omniscience with Aluren in hand a turn earlier, at 19 life. Holding it was normal; against Boros it buys nothing.
- B: no mistake that mattered. Keep (b1) was Plateau, Arid Mesa, Windswept Heath x2, Ocelot Pride, Spider-Woman, Phlage: a standard curve keep with no interaction. Racing was the only plan and was played well: T1 Ocelot, T3 Spider-Woman (correct over Guide; it taxed A's Petal), T5 Phlage to face plus all-out attacks (no blockers). No line kept W open: Phlage needs all three lands, there was nothing to spend W on, and an untapped Karakas does not stop the loop (Acererak returns to hand from his own trigger, and the venture still happens). Tapping Karakas cost nothing. B's own flag (b46) is answered: it did not matter. The loss was A's draws.
- B, small miss: under A's Aluren, B passed four times (b40, b41, b45, b46) instead of casting Guide of Souls for free, which goes against the existing Boros note. It had no effect here.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- "Against Boros, cast Lotus Petal on the turn you draw it unless you need it hidden this turn: three Spider-Woman make a later Petal enter tapped, and their main deck cannot touch an artifact." Evidence: a10/a11 held, a22 entered tapped. Confidence: low to medium. It extends "Spider-Woman makes Petals ... enter tapped (cast Petals a turn early)", which only covers a Spider-Woman already on the battlefield. It does not contradict any rule.
- Nothing else is new. The T6 window ("when the opponent taps out on its own turn...", "count their untapped white ... as Swords before looping") and the Brainstorm, fetch, Brainstorm weave are already rules, and A followed them.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
- "Against Alurentell, Karakas only answers a legendary Atraxa. When it is your third land and the choice is Phlage now or holding it up, tap it for Phlage: 3 damage plus 3 life beats a bounce that cannot stop the Aluren-Acererak loop." Evidence: b31 and b46 (self-flag). Confidence: medium. It qualifies "Keep Karakas untapped for a legendary Atraxa" and does not contradict it.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- No engine bug. Ponder showed three cards as two entries (a7), the known duplicate-name display; A read the known top correctly at a9. B got no priority while Aluren was on the stack (an auto-passed window) and had nothing to cast. Acererak moving "Battlefield -> Hand" before the dungeon prompt is expected.
- No land drops skipped, no upkeep cantrips, no wasted tool calls. The macro finished cleanly in 401 picks.
