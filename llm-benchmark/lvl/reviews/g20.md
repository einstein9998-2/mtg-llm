# Review g20: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs Boros Aggro (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on turn 11 (B's 6th turn). A did nothing after turn 4: stuck on Hedge Maze + Island, it went 20 -> 17 (Tomb, Stock Up) -> 14 -> 9 -> 1 -> 0 to Voice of Victory x2, Spider-Woman and Mobilize tokens.
- Turn 4 (A prompts 14-17): with Maze + Ancient Tomb (3 mana, only one green source), A cast Stock Up and took Acererak + Island over the second Ancient Tomb. Turn 5 (B prompt 23): Wasteland on Ancient Tomb. A was left with 2 lands, 0 lands in hand, and a hand of 3- and 4-mana spells. It drew Acererak, Aluren and Show and Tell and never cast another spell. With Tomb + Island taken instead, the turn-6 land drop gives 3 mana and the second Stock Up, and turn 8 gives 4 to 5 mana. A's own report reaches the same conclusion.
- The untried alternative on turn 4 (A prompt 14): Show and Tell for Aluren (2U, any colours). That puts Aluren in play without needing GG and makes Wasteland irrelevant. After that, any Acererak (mana value 3) is free and loops through Lost Mine (Dark Pool drain) for the win. A drew Acererak on turn 6. The costs: B gets to put a creature in, and Aluren is symmetric, so every Boros creature (all mana value 3 or less) is free for B too. That is close, and it is flagged for Brady.
- Variance: from 44 random cards with 18 mana sources (14 lands, 4 Petal), missing on 3 straight draws is about 20%. The Wasteland on the only fast land was not luck; B had it in its opening hand.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, prompt 16 (Stock Up pick): Acererak over Ancient Tomb or a second land. Acererak could not do anything: no GG for Aluren, and Show and Tell for Acererak only bounces it. A had 2 lands in play, 0 in hand, a hand full of 3- and 4-mana spells, and an opponent known to run 4 Wasteland. Lands were the need. A flagged this itself (g20a seq 16), so it is not repeated in the flags.
- A, keep (prompt 2): Strand, Tomb, Stock Up, 2 Force of Will, Aluren, Veil. A two-lander whose only acceleration is Ancient Tomb, with no creature, no Petal and two Forces that are weak against Boros. It is a close mulligan against a Wasteland deck, but on the draw it is defensible. A flagged it itself.
- A, prompt 9 (no Force of Will on Voice of Victory): close, not a clear error. See flags.
- Sound play by A: Strand on turn 2 with Tomb held back; the fetch at B's end step for Hedge Maze, with surveil binning the third Force of Will; the Veil of Summer discard (Boros has no blue or black spell); the Force of Will discard on turn 8 (Voice made it nearly dead).
- B: no real mistake. Voice first on turn 3 was right: it switched off A's Force of Will on B's turns for the rest of the game, so Spider-Woman and the second Voice resolved without A getting a chance to Force them. On turn 5 Wasteland went on Ancient Tomb, not Hedge Maze, which was correct in hindsight (A held Island; with Tomb + Island A would have had 3 mana and Stock Up). B never played Arid Mesa (turns 9 and 11 had a free drop). That cost nothing.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Stock Up when short of lands (two or fewer in play, none in hand, a 3- or 4-mana hand): take a land first, ideally one Wasteland cannot hit, and take a creature only when you can already cast Aluren or Show and Tell for it next turn."
   - Evidence: A prompt 16-17, turn 4.
   - Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: this is the converse of the batch-2 rule "with enough lands in hand and no Show and Tell or Acererak in hand to complete a pair, take the strong cantrip ... first, then Atraxa or Force, not another land". It does not contradict that rule; it covers the case that rule leaves out (lands not "enough").
2. "With only one green source and Show and Tell in hand, Show and Tell can put Aluren in for 2U. Against decks that cannot remove an enchantment (Boros, game 1), this beats digging when Wasteland threatens the mana. Remember that Aluren makes their small creatures free too."
   - Evidence: A prompt 14, turn 4.
   - Confidence: low (needs Brady).
   - Overlap: it refines Brady's "Show and Tell versus Aluren: decide by how much mana I have and which other combo pieces are in my hand". It does not contradict "Do not cast a raw Show and Tell without a payoff or protection in hand", because Aluren is the payoff.
3. "Against Boros, a two-land seven whose only fast mana is Ancient Tomb, with no Petal and no creature, is a mulligan: one Wasteland strands it, and Force of Will does little against creatures."
   - Evidence: A prompt 2 and the whole game.
   - Confidence: low to medium.
   - Overlap: it fits the a36 note "a creature (Acererak or Atraxa) in the opening seven matters more than extra cantrips" and Brady's Boros tip that Stock Up is "just good, not a must, against a fast clock".

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
1. "Against a Force of Will combo deck, cast Voice of Victory before any other two-drop. Once it is in play they cannot cast spells on your turn, so every later creature resolves, and their Force only works on their own turn."
   - Evidence: B prompt 8 (Voice over Spider-Woman); A had no response window after that.
   - Confidence: high.
2. "Hold Wasteland for the combo player's fast mana. With Ancient Tomb and a dual or Hedge Maze both out, hit Ancient Tomb: a combo player on two lands cannot cast its 3- and 4-mana spells."
   - Evidence: B prompts 21-23, turn 5.
   - Confidence: medium. Killing the only green source (Maze) is the alternative when the combo player is short on colours.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- No wasted calls and no tools used by either side. A skipped no land drop (it had no land in hand on turns 8 and 10, which the engine auto-passed correctly). No upkeep cantrips.
- B skipped land drops with Arid Mesa in hand on turn 9 (it played Plains instead, which is fine) and on turn 11 (prompt 52, passed with Mesa in hand; harmless, since it won that turn).
- Voice of Victory was enforced correctly: A was offered Force of Will while Voice was on the stack (A prompt 9) and got no priority windows with spells on B's turns afterwards.
- Possible engine payment issue (B report, B prompt 30): the auto-payment for Voice used Plains + Plateau and left Lazotep Quarry. B's menus show no plain tap of Quarry, only "Tap Lazotep Quarry for X, sacrificing <creature>". Either Quarry has no free colourless tap, or the auto-payer should have used it for the generic cost. Worth checking the card text against the engine.
- Menu bloat: Lazotep Quarry lists 5 colours times every creature, up to 27 options (B prompt 35). One mis-index sacrifices a creature. It is not a bug, but a menu hazard.
