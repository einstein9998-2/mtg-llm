# Review g5: Alurentell (A, on the play, mulligan to 6) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on turn 9 (A's 5th turn) in the same main phase Aluren resolved: 73 free Acererak casts through Lost Mine of Phandelver drained B 19 -> 1, then one venture into Tomb of Annihilation (Trapped Entry, each player loses 1) finished it. B never had another window after its Daze.
- **Decisive: turn 9, A prompts 58-62.** A played City of Traitors, cast Lotus Petal, then Aluren with City (2) + Island + Savannah + Petal = 5 mana for a 4-mana spell, leaving 1 colorless floating, with Force of Will + Atraxa (blue pitch) in hand. B had tapped both Volcanic Islands on its own turn 8 for Murktide, so only Daze was possible; B Dazed (B prompt 64), A paid with the floating mana (A prompt 68). This is exactly the playbook window ("When the opponent taps out on its own turn its lands stay tapped through my turn"; "Spare mana beats Daze").
- **B's side of it: turn 8, B prompt 50.** B cast Murktide Regent (UU after delving 5) and tapped out. Keeping Volcanic + Volcanic up for Prismari Charm was the only non-Daze interaction available on A's turn: bounce Aluren in response to the first free Acererak (Acererak returns to hand, A has no mana to hardcast it), or bounce Omniscience after a Show and Tell. Here it would not have saved B: A held Force of Will + Atraxa and would have countered the Charm. B self-flagged this decision (g5b #50), framed only around Force of Will.
- Verdict: A played cleanly; B's loss is mostly variance (no Force of Will in 12 cards seen, one Daze, a 2-power clock for most of the game). B's actual hand at the end: Volcanic, Flooded Strand, Bolt, Charm.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none found. Mulligan of the one-lander (Tundra, Petal, Ponder, Aluren, S&T, 2x Omniscience) follows the playbook ("One land with Ponder is a mulligan"); bottoming Omniscience from a hand with no Show and Tell is right. Basic Island before the duals, Strand held as a decoy and cracked at B's end step for Hedge Maze (prompt 33), land drop every turn, no upkeep plays. The Hedge Maze surveil that binned a second Force (prompt 35, self-flagged) is close; in hindsight it was what put Aluren on top for turn 9 (keeping it shifts the draws to FoW, Savannah, City).
- Turn 7 (prompt 44) Ancient Tomb played with nothing to cast, into a deck that had already shown Wasteland: it drew B's second Wasteland (turn 8) and Savannah, A's only green source besides Petal, survived to pay GG for Aluren. Holding the Tomb would have pointed that Wasteland at Savannah. Correct by "Play a land every turn", and a useful decoy example; flagged as close.
- B: the turn-8 tap-out (above) is the nearest thing to a mistake, outcome-irrelevant here. Everything else was standard: DRC/Bauble turn 2, Cutter turn 4, Wasteland on Hedge Maze (turn 6) and Ancient Tomb (turn 8), Daze held for the 4-mana spell instead of the Petal (prompt 61, opp tool said 70% combo in hand). Flooded Strand sat in hand from turn 2 to the end because both Wastelands used the land drops; B stayed on 2 lands all game.
