# g40 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 7 (B's 4th turn). A was on the draw. B had mulliganed to 6 and still curved Karakas + Ocelot Pride (T1), then Lazotep Quarry + Ajani (T3). On T5 it Wastelanded Tropical Island and cast the second Ajani; the legend rule binned one Ajani, the other flipped to Nacatl Avenger, and Avenger +2 let three Cats hit for 7 (A 19 to 12). A's T6 had one usable mana (Boseiju; Hedge Maze entered tapped) with Atraxa, Aluren, Show and Tell and 2 Stock Up stuck in hand. B's T7: Sand Scout, Wasteland on Hedge Maze, +2, lethal.
- **Decider: A's mana, not its cards.** A kept 2 lands + 2 Petals, the T2 Stock Up whiffed on lands, and the mana A did have was spent unevenly (one idle mana on T4, the last Petal on an end-step Brainstorm). On T6 A was two mana short of Show and Tell for Atraxa.
- **Hindsight caveat.** Even a T6 Show and Tell into Atraxa was probably too late against 6 Cats + Avenger at 12 life, so the game was mostly settled by T5. A's errors cost tempo, but they probably did not decide the result. The main causes were variance (the Stock Up whiff, about 11%) and B's very strong six-card hand.

## 2. Judgements requested, then first real mistake per side
- **Keep (A prompt 4): defensible, close, not a mistake.** Hand: Trop, Boseiju, Petal x2, Stock Up x3. The hand casts a turn-2 Stock Up off Trop + 2 Petals. 18 lands remained in 52 cards, so 5 looks miss a land only about 11% of the time. Brady's batch-2 rule covers it almost exactly: "Stock Up on turn 1 that likely finds a permanent mana source is a keep (close)." Against Boros (4 Wasteland, a clock by T7) it is weaker than in the blind case. Three 3-mana cantrips on 2 permanent sources is clunky. Two of the three Stock Ups were never cast, so the hand played as five cards. A flagged it itself (A4); I would lean keep, barely.
- **T2 Petals into Stock Up (A prompts 8, 9, 11): correct.** It was the hand's only proactive play. Holding the Petals would have meant a T2 pass and a T4 with 4 mana and no payoff.
- **Stock Up picks (A prompts 12-15): correct.** Seen: Petal, Brainstorm, Stock Up, Show and Tell, Acererak. No land, and A already held Show and Tell. Acererak is not a payoff without Aluren ("plan F"), and a third Stock Up was uncastable on two lands. Petal (mana) + Brainstorm (1-mana dig) was the most mana-efficient take. A's report calls bottoming Acererak and Show and Tell "losing a payoff". That is wrong: neither was a payoff here.
- **Third Petal (A prompt 25): close, lean hold.** At B's T3 end step A spent the last Petal on Brainstorm. Next turn's lands were Trop (U) and Boseiju (G only), and every spell in hand needed U or 3 mana. With the Petal saved, T4 had Trop + Boseiju + Petal = Stock Up, which uses Boseiju's G as generic. As played, Boseiju's mana sat idle on T4 and both Stock Ups stayed uncastable. The end-step Brainstorm did find Hedge Maze before the T4 land drop, but A then did not play it.
- **First real mistake, A prompt 28 (T4).** A played Boseiju before casting Ponder, and played it instead of Hedge Maze. This breaks Brady's rule: "cast cantrips (Brainstorm, Ponder ...) before playing the land". The land choice was also wrong on its own. Ponder was the only castable spell (Stock Up and Show and Tell need 3, A had 2) and Trop paid for it, so Boseiju's untapped G could not be used. Better: Ponder off Trop, then Hedge Maze (tapped, surveil 1), keeping Boseiju for T6. That gives two untapped sources on T6 after the Wasteland instead of one.
- **Ponder shuffle (A32), self-flagged.** Shuffling S&T/Maze/City was reasonable; the redraw was Atraxa.
- **Brainstorm put-back (A26-27), minor.** A put back both Show and Tells and kept two uncastable Stock Ups. It made no difference, because the T4 draw took one put-back and Ponder shuffled the other.
- **B: no real mistake.** The mulligan of the 1-lander (Wasteland only, B1) and T3 Ajani over Wasteland on the lone Tropical Island (B19, close) are self-flagged. Sequencing on T5 (Wasteland, then Ajani #2 for the legend-rule flip) and T7 (Sand Scout before Wasteland, for the token) was precise.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Play Hedge Maze on a turn when the untapped land's mana would go unused. Boseiju makes only G, and my cantrips need U, so with two lands and only one-mana blue spells castable, the tapped Maze costs nothing and is an extra untapped source next turn."
   - Evidence: T4, A prompt 28. Confidence: medium-high.
   - Overlap: not a duplicate. It complements Brady's "cast cantrips ... before playing the land" (also broken here) and "Hedge Maze fetched at their end step enters tapped, untaps on my turn". No contradiction.
2. "Do not spend the last Lotus Petal on a one-mana cantrip at their end step when next turn's lands can pay for it. Save it to turn two lands (or Boseiju's off-colour G) into a three-mana spell: Stock Up or Show and Tell."
   - Evidence: A prompt 25, then T4 idle G and two dead Stock Ups. Confidence: medium-low.
   - Overlap: in the spirit of Brady's "wait as long as possible to cast a spell if waiting is free"; here waiting was cheaper. No contradiction.
3. Mulligan vs Boros: none. A rule like "mulligan Stock Up-only sevens against Boros" would contradict Brady's batch-2 keep rule, quoted above. One game does not justify it, so it is left to Brady through A's own flag A4.
