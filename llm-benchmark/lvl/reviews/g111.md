# g111 review: Alurentell (A) beat UR Cutter (B) on game turn 7 (A's 4th turn, A on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
- Kill verified: 62 auto Acererak casts and exactly 16 Dark Pool drains; B was at 16 (20, 19 Delta T2, 18 Delta T4, 17 Strand T6, 16 FoW T7). A's life: 20, 19 (Strand T3), 16 (DRC T4), 14 (Tomb for Show and Tell T5), 21 (Atraxa block +7 T6), 20 (Strand T7), 18 (Tomb for Aluren), 17 (FoW), 33 at the end.
- What decided it: T5 (a61-71). Tomb, Petal, Veil of Summer, then Show and Tell for Atraxa with one mana spare. B saw Veil (b67) and could not answer: B's only blue card besides FoW was Daze, so B had one counter, and Veil made Show and Tell uncounterable. Atraxa's reveal (a73-77) took Acererak, Omniscience, Show and Tell, Strand, Petal. T7 (a92-103): Strand to Trop, third Petal, Aluren hardcast with 3 Petals spare against B tapped out with 4 cards; B Forced pitching Daze (b100), A Forced back pitching Omniscience (a103), free Acererak, loop.
- Verified details: keep was Island, Strand, Petal x2, Brainstorm x2, FoW (a1). T1 Brainstorm (put back Petal and Maze); T3 Brainstorm (drew the put-back Petal plus Ponder, Veil; put back Maze and Petal again), then Strand cracked to shuffle them away, then Ponder saw Atraxa, Aluren, Maze, kept, drew Atraxa; Aluren was the T5 draw. B did put Cori-Steel Cutter in off A's Show and Tell (b72). A passed on Forcing B's main-2 Bolt on Atraxa (a91), as the existing rule says (FoW is for what breaks the loop, not to save a Show-and-Tell Atraxa); keeping FoW won T7.
- Omniscience over a second Aluren (a74) was right, not wasted (B's report implies it was): Aluren was already in hand, a second one is green and cannot pitch to FoW; Omniscience is blue (FoW pitch, used at a103) and a second Show and Tell target.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none found. The keep (no payoff, no enabler, two Brainstorms plus a fetch) was close and A flagged it; it dug to Veil, Show and Tell, Atraxa and Aluren by T3. T1 Brainstorm on the play was also the only Daze-proof window (B had no Island yet).
- B, b14 (T2, minor): cast Bauble before DRC and then pointed it at A (saw Hedge Maze). DRC first, then Bauble, gives a surveil and an earlier delirium type; the existing note already says target yourself with a fetch or hold it for a DRC trigger. Nothing B did changed the result: after Veil resolved on T5, B had one counter against A's FoW plus three blue cards.
- B's one-land keep (b2) was not punished (Tarn T1 draw, Delta T4), consistent with Brady's one-lander guidance. DRC attacking into untapped Atraxa was forced (delirium, must attack); equipping Cutter (b82) made it 4/4 so block damage plus Bolt equalled 7, which killed her. That was B's best play.
- B on the first Aluren (b100): FoW pitching Daze was the only counter that mattered (Daze was dead into 3 Petals). The alternative, saving FoW for the free Acererak, meets the same FoW from A. B had no out on T7; the only earlier lever was Daze on A's tapped-out T3 Brainstorm or Ponder (b25, b34), which costs B its only land for a turn.

- Not bugs, though A's report lists them: Flooded Strand finding Savannah and Hedge Maze is legal (Plains/Island types), and A's lands showing tapped on B's turn is correct (they untap in A's untap step). The summary line's odds 1 / sim 2 calls were B's (opp tool at b67, sim at the keep), not A's.
