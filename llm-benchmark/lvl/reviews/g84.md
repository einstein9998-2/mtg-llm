# g84 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B)

1. **Result and turning point.** A won on game turn 4 (its 2nd turn, on the draw). Truth log: 79 Acererak casts and 20 Dark Pool picks, so 20 drains took B from 20 to 0 (A ended at 39 life with about 30 cards left in library). B had no priority prompt after its Show and Tell put-in and could not respond: both its lands were tapped from its own turn and its hand was Savannah, Arid Mesa, Goblin Bombardment and Ajani. The game was decided at A seq 11-13 (Brainstorm kept Omniscience, Petal, City and Show and Tell) and A seq 17-23 (City of Traitors, Show and Tell off Island plus City with the Petal still spare, Omniscience, free Acererak, loop).
2. **First real mistake per side.** A: none. B: none that mattered. B's 7 had no Swords and no Wasteland, so nothing it could draw or hold by turn 4 interacts. This loss was just variance: A kept a 6 that held the whole combo plus the exact mana.
   - A, mulligan (seq 2-4): the first 7 had zero lands, so the mulligan was forced. The 7 it then kept (Strand, City, Brainstorm, Aluren, Show and Tell, Atraxa, Acererak) is a clear keep. Strand into Island plus City on turn 2 is exactly {2}{U} for Show and Tell into Aluren plus a free Acererak. Bottoming Atraxa was correct, since it is the redundant creature and Acererak is the loop.
   - A, Brainstorm put-back (seq 11-13): it put back Aluren and Brainstorm, with Brainstorm on top. That kept Petal, which insures against a Wasteland on the lone Island, and Strand, which is a spare land. Omniscience was the better payoff to keep here. Aluren is symmetric against Boros (free Amped Raptor can discover Swords; free Phlage). A also had no route to GG for a hardcast Aluren. The Omniscience loop needs only about 20 draws against a 50-card library. Note the Show and Tell target was really chosen here: at seq 20 Aluren was no longer an option.
   - A, turn 4 (seq 17-19): it played the land before the committal spell, cast Show and Tell with the Petal held back, and passed with Show and Tell on the stack instead of casting Brainstorm. All of that was correct.
   - B, keep (seq 1): 4 lands, Voice of Victory and two Ajani is a normal game-1 aggro keep.
   - B, turn 1 (seq 5-6): it played Parlor tapped with no 1-drop. Keeping Bombardment on top during the surveil was fine, because A had shown nothing yet.
   - B, tapping out (seq 16): tapping out for Voice cost nothing. B held no Swords, so holding W would not have changed anything. Voice also only stops spells on B's own turn.
   - B, put-in (seq 21): putting in Ajani was the best of four non-answers. A land is legal for Show and Tell but adds no pressure. Bombardment gives at most 3 pings and cannot remove a 5/5 Acererak or stop the drain triggers.
3. **Candidate Alurentell playbook rules.**
   - "Against Boros, when Brainstorm or a hand-size choice forces you to drop one of Omniscience or Aluren and Show and Tell plus Acererak are in hand, keep Omniscience: Aluren gives them free Raptor (Swords) and Phlage, Omniscience gives them nothing." Evidence: seq 11. Confidence: medium. It refines "never your only Aluren or Acererak" (Brainstorm section) and "Show and Tell with Acererak in hand: put in Aluren, not Atraxa". It agrees with "Aluren is symmetric". A did this unprompted, so the rule would not have changed a decision here. It is new only as an explicit exception.
