# Review g23: Alurentell (A) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
A won on game turn 5 (A's 3rd turn) with Aluren + Acererak looping Lost Mine of Phandelver; Dark Pool drained B 19 to 0 in one main phase. B never got a turn 6.
- Turn 1, A prompts 10-12: Ponder showed Ancient Tomb, Boseiju, Lotus Petal; A kept them in play order (Tomb, Boseiju, Petal) and did not shuffle. With Aluren and Acererak already in hand, that locked in a turn-5 Aluren with one spare mana. This was the decisive setup and it was correct.
- Turn 3, A prompts 21-22: Tomb, then Stock Up with exactly 3 mana. B Dazed it (B prompt 23) by returning its only land, so B was stuck on one land for turn 4, which it tapped for Ponder in main 1. On turn 5 B was fully tapped with no Daze or Force left. A played Boseiju and Petal and cast Aluren with 1 mana spare (prompt 36). Nothing could answer it.
- Mostly the matchup and the draw: B's seven had no Force of Will, and Daze was its only answer to the combo.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none found. Both mulligans were close and A flagged both itself: the one-land 7 without Show, and the one-land 6 with Ponder and Stock Up, kept against the playbook's "one land with Ponder is a mulligan" with a sim showing 74% for a second land by turn 2. Every land drop was made, no upkeep spells were cast, and Aluren went down with a spare.
  - One cost was invisible in this game. After Stock Up was Dazed, A had no blue card to pitch to Force of Will. On the Aluren turn A's hand was Force + Acererak, so a Force from B on Aluren could not have been fought. Casting Stock Up was still right: the mana was otherwise unused and it pulled out Daze (see flag).
- B: prompt 31 (turn 4, main 1, before combat). B passed with Mishra's Bauble in hand, as it had at six earlier prompts. B's graveyard already held a land, a sorcery and an instant. Casting and cracking the free Bauble adds an artifact, which turns on delirium: Dragon's Rage Channeler becomes a 3/3 flier and attacks for 3, not 1, and B draws a card. This did not change the result (A won the next turn regardless), but it is a recurring free-value leak.
  - B's Daze on Stock Up (B prompt 23, B self-flagged) was fine: A was tapped out. Holding Daze for Aluren would have failed anyway, because A cast Aluren with a spare.
- B's report is wrong on one point: A was not "tapped-out-looking" on the combo turn. A had 1 mana floating spare after Aluren, plus Force of Will in hand.
