# g79 review: A Alurentell (on the play) vs B UR Cutter. B won on game turn 12

## 1. Result and turning point
- **Result**: B won on game turn 12 (B's 6th turn). Monk Token 2/2 (trample, Cutter-equipped) and DRC 3/3 (delirium, flying) attacked for 5 into A's 3 life. **What killed A: combat, not Ancient Tomb.** The truth log has no damage line again. The cause comes from the transcripts: b88 attack, A at 3 after a84.
- **Life tally for A**: 20, then 18 (t6 Monk), 17 (Force of Will), 14 (t8 Monk + DRC), 12 (t9 Misty + Strand cracks), 10 (Tomb on Aluren), 5 (t10 attack), 3 (Tomb on Acererak), then dead. Self-inflicted total: 7 (Tomb 4, fetches 2, Force 1).
- **Did Tomb damage matter? No.** At 5 (no a84 hardcast), B's 5 power was exactly lethal. At 7 (both Tomb taps avoided), B had exactly 7: it drew Prismari Charm on turn 12 (1 to A, plus prowess on Monk). Only at 8 or more, which also needs skipping the needless Strand crack, would A have lived to turn 13, with Tomb and Acererak in hand and no win.
- **Turning point: a59 (t8), Force of Will pitching Omniscience on a delved 4/4 Murktide.** A held no Show and Tell and no Aluren. That Force, with Omniscience as its only pitch, was the only protection for the combo A was waiting to draw. Countering bought no time: unanswered, Murktide brings the clock to about turn 12, the same turn A died. With Force held, the t9 line wins. B is tapped out from Murktide. A draws Aluren and casts it. B's Force (pitching Murktide #2) meets A's Force (pitching Omniscience). Aluren resolves with B holding only lands. The free Acererak loop (Lost Mine, Dark Pool drain) then kills from about 15 life. A flagged this spot itself (a59), but called it reasonable.
- **b74 decided it on B's side:** Force on Aluren, which B could do only because it declined to Force back at b61.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A: the keep (a1, A flagged it).** The seven had Maze, Misty, Tomb, Petal, Force, Omniscience and Acererak: payoffs but no enabler and no cantrip. Its only routes were natural draws of 8 enablers (about 15% a draw; the sim said 28% in two). Hedge Maze gave one surveil, and Maze #2 was reachable by fetch. A's five draws were Tomb, Strand, Tomb, Aluren, Tomb. A mulligan to six was better. The literal playbook rule ("a 7 with no cantrips and no blue card for Force of Will ... is a mulligan") did not fire, because Omniscience is a blue pitch. "A keep needs a route to a payoff" covers it in spirit.
- **Other A decisions**:
  - a4: surveilling Acererak #2 to the graveyard was fine (a duplicate payoff).
  - Fetches, held from t3 to t8: fetches do not dig, with one exception. Misty or Strand at B's end step could fetch Hedge Maze #2 for a surveil (first chance a16, 1 life), and A never did. That is a small but real miss.
  - Baiting Force: no bait existed. Acererak was the only other spell and the only payoff, and losing it to Force leaves Aluren useless. Jamming Aluren at a73 into a tapped-out B with a spare for Daze was right.
  - a69: the Strand crack was not needed for Aluren (Maze + Trop + Tomb pays it). It cost 1 life.
  - a84: hardcasting Acererak at 5 life for one Lost Mine scry was pointless. A was dead on board to exactly 5, so it changed nothing, but it spent the Petal (A's only black source) and 2 life to Tomb.
- **B: none that mattered.**
  - The b2 keep (5 lands, 2 Murktide, Cutter) is slow but fine.
  - b53: Murktide tapped B out, but B held Force of Will plus Murktide #2 as backup. A's open mana was irrelevant, because A's only instant was the free Force. The play drew out A's Force and its only pitch: two cards for one.
  - b61: not Forcing back was correct and won the game, since that Force countered Aluren next turn.
  - b76: surveilling Wasteland away for delirium (four types in the graveyard) made DRC a 3/3 flier, the lethal damage. That was fine, because Aluren was already countered.
  - b87: B skipped its land drop with Delta and Tarn in hand. It was irrelevant, since the attack was lethal.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Mulligan a seven with payoffs (Acererak, Atraxa, Omniscience) but no Show and Tell, no Aluren and no cantrip, even with Force of Will and enough lands. It needs one of 8 enablers off the top, and Force protects a combo you do not have." Evidence: a1 (no enabler until t9). Confidence: medium-high. This extends the batch-1 mulligan rule, whose "no blue card for Force" condition missed this hand. It does not contradict "A keep needs a route to a payoff."
2. "Without Show and Tell or Aluren in hand, do not spend Force of Will on a creature unless countering it changes the turn you die. Keep Force for the turn you cast the combo." Evidence: a59, then a73/b74. Confidence: medium. This extends "Spend Force of Will on what breaks the loop ... not on Swords for a Show-and-Tell Atraxa." It is new for creature threats.
3. (Repeat of the g65 candidate, still not in the playbook.) "While Ancient Tomb is untapped, assume every spell with a generic cost costs 2 life, even when Tomb-free payment exists. Do not cast a spell that achieves nothing (a lone Acererak hardcast for one scry)." Evidence: a73, a84. Confidence: high. It contradicts the playbook's "item 9, withdrawn as a bug".

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "Against Alurentell, cast your threat into their possible Force of Will when you hold your own Force plus a pitch card, and do not Force back to save it. Their Force on a creature spends their combo protection, and yours stops the combo next turn." Evidence: b53, b61, then b74. Confidence: medium-high. It does not contradict "Do not tap out for Murktide ... hold U (and R) for Prismari Charm", because B held no Charm and Force is free.

- **ENGINE (third case, after g65 and g72): the auto-payer taps Ancient Tomb when a Tomb-free payment exists.** a73 Aluren {2}{G}{G}: Maze, Trop, Trop and Petal could pay it Tomb-free. The engine tapped Tomb #37, Maze and Trop #59, and A went from 12 to 10 (not 14 to 12; the 14 to 12 came from the two fetches). a84 Acererak {2}{B}: Petal (B), Maze and Trop could pay it Tomb-free. The engine tapped Tomb #37 and Petal, and A went from 5 to 3. Both times a painless payment existed, and the menu offered no payment choice. It was not decisive here (section 1). Reopen engine-oddities item 9.
- The truth log again prints no cause for the loss; it should print damage and life-loss events.
- A's report is wrong on two counts. It says "five Ancient Tombs"; A drew three (four seen in all). It says "14 to 10 on Aluren"; Aluren itself cost 2.
- B's report says opponent lands showed "stale tapped" states. That is wrong: A's Tomb, tapped on A's turn 11, stays tapped through B's turn 12.
- The sim calls (2) were A's keep queries, not B's. No menu slips; no upkeep cantrips.

RESULT: B (UR Cutter) won on game turn 12 by combat (Monk 2 + DRC 3 into A at 3); Tomb auto-pay cost A 4 life but did not decide it.
