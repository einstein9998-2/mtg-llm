RESULT: WON
TURNS: 4 (my fourth turn, game turn 8; on the draw)
EMRAKUL: Saw it, never held it. Turn 2 Ponder showed Tropical Island, Force of Will and Emrakul; I kept all three in that order, so Emrakul sat third. Turn 3 Stock Up looked at Brainstorm, Stock Up, Force of Will, Aluren and Emrakul. I took the two cantrips to dig for Show and Tell, and the other three went to the bottom. Emrakul was never cast or put in. With Omniscience in hand it would have been a second Show and Tell target, but I won with Aluren plus Acererak before it mattered. Verdict: it was a dead draw until Omniscience resolves. Against this opponent, who is full of Force of Will, Force of Negation, Stifle and Wasteland, I would not keep it main. If it stays, cut a Hedge Maze, or the 1-of Boseiju, for City of Traitors and leave Emrakul in the board. (Emrakul cannot be countered, which matters against this deck, but only in the Omniscience or Show and Tell lines.)
MANA: Felt short of lands early. I mulliganed a one-lander, and Wasteland killed Hedge Maze on turn 2 and Stifle hit my Flooded Strand trigger (I had to Force it). I was down to Island and Ancient Tomb plus a Tropical Island at 3 to 4 mana until turn 4. The shortage was colored sources (only one or two blue or green), not total mana: Aluren needed Tomb, Tropical Island and a Lotus Petal for exactly GG and 2. I would not have wanted City of Traitors over Emrakul for the colour; it would have added a land but no colour. The 19th land was never the issue, the fetch and Maze losses were.
- Mistake candidate: turn 1 I played Hedge Maze (tapped) into their open Wasteland and lost it on turn 2. A basic or fetch first would have dodged it. Keeping one-lander 7 was not tempting, the mulligan to 6 worked out.
- Surprise: they used Stifle on my own fetchland activation on turn 2 (a real Stifle deck). I paid Force of Will plus Brainstorm to save the fetch, which was probably right with no other land. They also tapped out on their own turn 4 for a warped Quantum Riddler. That left only free counters on my turn: they Forced Aluren (pitching Stifle) and I Forced back, then the loop ran unopposed. Their deck apparently has no Daze.
