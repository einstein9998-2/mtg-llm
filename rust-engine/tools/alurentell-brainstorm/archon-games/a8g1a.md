ARCHON: Never drew it, so it did nothing. I was stuck on two lands (Hedge Maze, Tropical Island) from turn 3 to my turn 7 with Aluren plus two Acererak in hand, and never reached the 4 mana Aluren needs. Archon is not castable for 8 and only Show and Tell or Omniscience gets it in, so it was a dead draw in any hand like this. Against Dimir tempo, which has Daze, Force of Will and 4 Wasteland, I would not keep it in the main. I would cut it for the Ponder it replaced (cheap dig that finds lands). Compared with Atraxa, which is also hard to cast but gives a reveal when it resolves, Archon only matters if Show and Tell resolves. An opponent can answer it with Fatal Push or Snuff Out after it enters, but its enter trigger still takes a creature, a card and 3 life. This game gave no test of that.
