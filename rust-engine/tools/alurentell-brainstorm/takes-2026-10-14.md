# Alurentell brainstorm: what is known, engine support, what to test (2026-10-14)

Sources: earlier reports in sideboard-experiments/ (REPORT-round2, REPORT-llm-emrakul*, REPORT-llm-veil-emrakul), analysis/ (chant-and-petal-postboard-vs-ur, prismatic-ending-vs-tempo/REPORT*.md), and the engine card file `rust-engine/crates/mtg-cards/cards/legacy.cards.ron` (197 definitions). "Noise" means inside the 95% interval.

| Idea | Engine today | What we already know | Take |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chrome Mox | Not in engine (needs imprint) | Petal over Stock Up (1 or 2) moved line-by-T4 from 42% to 44-46% but win rate −3.5 to +3.0 vs UR/UW/UB (round 2, A). Mox only exiles one card, so it fixes Atraxa's hand overflow by one card at most. | Expect the same as Petal: faster, not more wins. A Mox that imprints Atraxa makes any of W/U/B/G, which helps white access, but costs a win condition. Low priority; needs engine work. |
| Emrakul modularity | Delivered as files, not on main | Swap for Ponder +4.6, Veil +4.0, Force +3.6 (sim average over 5 matchups, ±3.7). LLM: 10/12 vs 7/12 (for 2nd Veil), 9/12 (for City), 5/12 (61st card). Never drawn in 8 of 12 games; when cast it won 87-100%. | Your modularity point matches the games: it is live only with Show and Tell or Omniscience, so it is the narrowest of the three payoffs. Nothing says it is worse than what it replaces. |
| More Orim's Chant | In engine | 3rd Chant over Stock Up: −2.9 UR, −5.0 UW, −1.0 UB. Chant → Petal: +6 vs UB only. LLM 1 Ending + 3 Chant 4-2, 0 Ending + 4 Chant 2-4. Chant uncastable (no white) in about a fifth of drawn hands in sims, 7+ of 12 LLM games. | Copies are not the limit, white sources are. A 4th copy adds ~10 points of "Chant in hand" by turn 3 and nothing for white access. |
| Veil cast after the spell | In engine | Already how the playbook plays it (Veil in response to Force/Charm, draws if they cast U/B). Control games: Veil was a dead draw as often as it won the game, nearly always for lack of a spare green source (Veil + Aluren = 5 mana incl. green). | Agree it is the better-timed card vs Chant. The problem is mana, not timing. Veil only gives hexproof from blue and black: Bolt, Swords, Pyroblast still hit Atraxa. |
| Prismatic Ending and white mana | In engine | Cast now beats never by ~7 (UR), 8 (UB), 9 (UW) points of win probability; one turn later gives back 2-4; holding costs 6 points at life ≤5 vs UR. Worse with 3+ creatures out (UR +1.0). Life ≤7 and Show and Tell castable only without Ending: Ending −8.8 (UR) / −4.2 (UB). 2 Ending ≈ 0 Ending + 2 Petal. | The sim does not model cracking a fetch late to dodge Wasteland, so your "wait for the push turn" line is untested, but the sim says waiting is mostly a cost. |
| Other cards like Chant / Veil | Have: Flusterstorm, Consign to Memory, Force of Negation, Cavern of Souls (only helps hard-cast Atraxa). Missing: Silence, Grand Abolisher, Mana Tithe, Autumn's Veil, Spell Pierce, Mental Misstep, Pact of Negation, Teferi, Time Raveler | | Silence is Chant for {W} with no fog, so it is "more Chant". Grand Abolisher is the interesting one (opponents cast nothing on your turn, and Aluren could replay it) but it is a creature that dies to Bolt and needs a turn. All need engine work and all hit the same white limit. |
| Propaganda | Not in engine (no attack tax) | Alurentell's losses are mostly an early tempo clock (Moonshadow, Murktide plus burn, Bowmasters) or a stumbled mana base. | 3 mana, sorcery speed, doesn't stop burn, Forceable. Post-board vs creature decks at best. Low priority. |
