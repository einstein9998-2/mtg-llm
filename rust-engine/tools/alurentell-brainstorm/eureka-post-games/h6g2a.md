SIDEBOARD: Drew and used 2 Carpet of Flowers (turn 2 and turn 4, then 3 more Carpet triggers per turn, the best card of the game: 2 to 3 extra mana a turn at Sea count 1, it paid for Aluren with GG and funded Eureka), 1 Veil of Summer (Dazed, used as bait), 1 Orim's Chant (fetched, never cast, the opponent's hand was empty), 0 Prismatic Ending (seen in an Atraxa reveal, not taken). I would keep all of them in this matchup. With Force of Will boarded out I had no answer to Force of Negation (it countered my first Aluren), so Veil plus Carpet mana for Daze payments mattered.
EUREKA: Yes, drew 1 Eureka (off a turn 5 Brainstorm, three cards deep). Hard cast on my turn 5 main phase 2 for 4 mana: two Carpet triggers (GG floating) plus Boseiju and Tundra, with Tropical Island left up to pay for Daze. The opponent was tapped out apart from an uncracked Flooded Strand, held 2 cards (Underground Sea and one unknown), so they could only answer with free spells and did not. Rounds: R1 me Atraxa, opp Orcish Bowmasters (their only nonland permanent, put in from hand); R2 me Atraxa, opp declined; R3 me Misty Rainforest, opp declined; R4 me Acererak, opp declined; then the process ended (they never put the Underground Sea in). Results: with Atraxa already on the board I now had three Atraxa, the legend rule kept the original (not summoning sick) one, and the two extra Atraxa ETB triggers stayed on the stack. I cracked the Misty in response to shuffle away the known junk on top, and the two reveals (top 10 each, 20 cards) gave me Aluren, Aluren, Veil of Summer, Orim's Chant, Show and Tell, Ancient Tomb, Flooded Strand (hand went from 0 to 8, discarded one at cleanup). Acererak entered and bounced for a free venture scry. For the opponent it gave Bowmasters for free: 1 damage to me plus a 1/1 Orc Army, and it made my later cantrips unattractive. Net: it turned a board of Atraxa plus 1 card in hand into Aluren x2, protection and a lethal setup the next turn; I won on the following turn (Veil bait into Daze, Aluren, Atraxa for 7, then two Acererak laps for the last 2 life). Counterplay they had: Force of Will/Daze/Force of Negation on Eureka (I left a Tropical up for Daze; free counters were the only risk since they were tapped out), or a flash Bowmasters in response. It is a strong card when you have Atraxa on board and 2+ more Atraxa/lands in hand because the legend rule does not stop the ETB triggers. Would keep 2 Eureka in this deck (slightly better than the 2 Ponder it replaced here, since Ponder draws into Bowmasters and Thoughtseize and Eureka gave 20 cards of Atraxa digging in one sorcery), cutting 2 Ponder as tested; I would not cut Show and Tell for it. Eureka is a win-more or a dig card, not a combo-turn card: it needs 4 mana (GG) and some permanents in hand, so it is worse than Show and Tell on a bad hand and best as a second or third play after an Atraxa lands.
