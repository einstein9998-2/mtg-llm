I would leave it out as a 61st card. If it must be in, cut the 4th Ponder or a Hedge Maze for it, not a combo piece. Archon is better than a second Emrakul-style payoff only because it is cheap to cheat in and does not shuffle my deck, but Atraxa is clearly better here, since she also blocks and gains 7 against their fast clock.
