# g89 review: A (Alurentell) beat B (UR Cutter) on game turn 7 (A's 4th turn, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
- Kill verified: B was at 15 when the loop started (20, minus Strand T4, Delta T6, Misty T6, Force T7, Misty T7) and the macro made exactly 15 Dark Pool drains. A finished at 10 life. Aluren was in play, so the room-triggers-left-on-the-stack technique applied.
- Decisive play, A seq 78-83: Show and Tell with Boseiju and Petal open and two Force of Will in hand. B Forced (B seq 80, pitching its other Force, which was B's only blue card) and A Forced back (seq 81-83, pitching Force and keeping Omniscience). Omniscience then made Aluren (seq 87) and Acererak (seq 90) free. A's two Forces had come from the Brainstorm cast in response to its own Misty crack (seq 71-77): it put back Brainstorm and Stock Up, and the fetch shuffled them away. That is the playbook's weave-the-fetch line.
- Setup: A's T5 Show and Tell for Atraxa (seq 29) met no resistance. B had tapped both Volcanics for Cutter on its own turn, and its Force had no blue card to pitch. Atraxa (seq 34-39) took Strand (it can fetch Tropical, so it was picked over Tundra), Petal, Brainstorm, Ponder, Aluren and Acererak, a spare Acererak first, as the playbook says.
- B's report is wrong in three places. The countered Show and Tell was A's second one, not "#1". B paid 4 life to fetches, not about 6. A Daze surveilled during A's combo turn could not have stopped Aluren that turn, because it would still have been in the library.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: none clear. Mulligan: the seven had no lands (Ponder, 2 Force, Aluren, Veil, Atraxa, Acererak), so taking a mulligan was forced. Of the six, it bottomed Acererak and kept 3 lands, Ponder, Show and Tell and Atraxa, which is a sound keep. T1: Strand for a basic Island, then Ponder (kept Tomb, Stock Up, Misty on top). T3 (seq 15): Stock Up instead of Show and Tell into one tapped Volcanic. Daze was live (Volcanic is an Island). In hindsight B had no interaction (no Daze, and a Force with nothing to pitch), so jamming would have won about a turn sooner. With what A could see, digging for protection follows the playbook ("do not cast a raw Show and Tell without ... protection when you can still dig"), and Stock Up did find Force. Close, not a mistake. A already flagged it.
- A cleanup discards T5 (seq 46-47), Strand and Ponder: correct. It kept Misty for the T7 land drop and two Brainstorms as instant-speed pitch cards. The open question is earlier (seq 43): it held the Petal instead of using it to Brainstorm away the two cards it was about to discard (flagged as close).
- B: lost to variance plus A's double Force, not to a clear error. The keep (Strand, Delta, 2 Misty, Force, DRC, Cutter) had no cantrip and no pitch card for Force. It is defensible blind, but it had no live interaction until T7, when B drew its second Force. Tapping out for Cutter on T4 cost nothing because B held no instant. Forcing the T7 Show and Tell by pitching Force was B's only correct play: B knew A held Aluren x2 and Acererak, so letting it resolve loses on the spot, and the 2-for-1 was the price.
- B's put-ins under A's Show and Tells (B seq 32: Polluted Delta; seq 86: Misty Rainforest) were B's own choices. A free fetch is the best of the lands offered, and B had nothing else to put in.
- B's fetch life (4 total) was irrelevant: A's loop does not care about B's life total. The T7 Misty crack in response to Aluren (seq 88) did nothing but cost 1 life.
- Binning Daze (B seq 84, already self-flagged) breaks the existing note "Never ... surveil away Daze ...", but here it was moot: A won that turn.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- R1 (medium): "With Aluren and Acererak in hand, Show and Tell puts in Aluren even when Omniscience is also in hand: the put-in cannot be countered, a cast Aluren can. Put in Omniscience only without Aluren, or when a bounce (Prismari Charm) is the bigger threat than a counter."
  - Evidence: seq 85. A put in Omniscience, then cast Aluren off it (seq 87), which is one more counterable step. B had a single Volcanic left in hand, so it did not matter here.
  - Relation to the playbook: it extends "Show and Tell with Acererak in hand: put in Aluren, not Atraxa". It narrows "Put Omniscience in with Show and Tell when I have Atraxa, Aluren or Acererak in hand". Brady should settle which one wins.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on turn 7 with 15 Dark Pool drains against B's 15 life, after mulling to 6. B's Force on the second Show and Tell was answered by A's Force.
TOP MISTAKE: none decisive. Closest are A's T3 Stock Up bait (B had no interaction, so a jam would have been about a turn faster) and B's interaction-free keep.
TOP RULE: with Aluren and Acererak in hand, Show and Tell in Aluren (uncounterable) rather than Omniscience followed by a counterable Aluren cast.
