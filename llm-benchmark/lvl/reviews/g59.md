# Review g59: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B, on the draw)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 14 (Murktide Regent 8/8 equipped by Cori-Steel Cutter; A 17 to 9, Bolt to 6, then lethal). A's mana was cut by two Wastelands, and it never held a creature payoff.
- Turn 7 to 8 (B 56, 60): Wasteland on Tropical at A's end step, then a second Wasteland on Boseiju. A went from 3 lands to 1 with Show and Tell, Omniscience, two Force of Will and two Veil in hand, and no land.
- Turn 10 (A 88 to 99, B 91 to 101): B delved a 7/7 Murktide. A cast Force (pitching its second Force), B Forced the Force (pitching Preordain), A cracked Misty for Savannah and cast Veil, and B Forced the Veil (pitching the spare Murktide). B's two Forces beat A's one Force and one Veil. Veil first would not have changed this (two Forces against two answers either way). It would only have let A stop and keep its Force pair for Show and Tell once B showed a Force. Close, not a mistake. The Misty crack also shuffled away the Lotus Petal and Aluren that Ponder had left on top (A 71), but Veil needed the green.
- Turn 11 (A 106): Show and Tell for Omniscience with only Veil left in hand. B put in Cutter (B 108, its only permanent, which gave haste and trample). This was the right jam: Murktide was a 3-turn clock and waiting would not help. Omniscience without a creature or cantrip to cast only works if A draws one, and A drew Ancient Tomb. Cutter did not change the death turn (it is lethal on turn 14 without it too).
- Verdict: mostly variance. B drew Daze, two Force of Will, two Wastelands and two Murktides off a one-land keep, and A's 7 never found Acererak or Atraxa. With hindsight, no A line after turn 5 beats B's hand.

## 2. Judgments asked for, and the first real mistake per side
- A's lands into Wasteland: not a mistake. Turn 3: no Wasteland was visible (B played it on turn 4), and Boseiju and Tropical are both nonbasic. Turn 5 (A 38): Tropical was the only land in hand and was needed for the third mana. Holding it means no spell and only delays the Wasteland. The rule against playing a nonbasic into a Wasteland "just to use one more mana" (t18) does not apply here, because A had no basic or fetch in hand.
- A's Stock Up into Daze (A 39, self-flagged as 38): fine. Show and Tell would have been Dazed (no spare), and the playbook calls Stock Up the bait. B's Daze (B 41) was also right: A was tapped out and B knew Murktide was on top.
- A's first real mistake: turn 7 pass (A 54). A had 3 lands, Show and Tell, Omniscience, two Force of Will and two Veil. B had Volcanic tapped, an untapped Wasteland on the battlefield, Daze already spent, and 7 cards. A could only get the fourth mana for Veil plus Show and Tell by drawing a land (none in hand), and the visible Wasteland would take a land back first. The existing rule says "Show and Tell: when you hold protection, jam it now; it will not get better by waiting." Also, once Omniscience is in play, the Forces are free. Hindsight: B held two Force of Will, and A's pair is only one counter (Force pitching Force), so the jam would have been countered. The decision was still wrong; the result would have been the same.
- B's Wasteland timing: B's first real mistake. B held Wasteland from turn 4 through its turn-6 main 2 (B 53), knowing a second Wasteland was on top. It fired at A's turn-7 end step, after A had untapped with 3 lands and Show and Tell. Firing on B's turn 6 would have cost B nothing, because the Wasteland's mana went unused on A's turn 7 anyway, and it would have left A on 2 lands with no Show and Tell window. This breaks the existing note "Use it before they untap". On turn 4, a Wasteland on Boseiju would also have stopped the turn-5 Stock Up. It went unpunished because A passed.
- B's Force backing Force (B 91, 100): correct. Pitching Preordain first and keeping the spare Murktide for the second Force was the right order. B's report reason that Show and Tell was "harmless because Omniscience needs 9 mana" is wrong: Show and Tell puts it in for free. It was harmless only because A had no payoff.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "If they have an untapped Wasteland and you have no land in hand, do not wait for the fourth mana: you are more likely to lose a land than gain one. Jam Show and Tell now with Force backup." Evidence: A 54 (passed, lost Tropical and Boseiju over the next two turns). Confidence: medium. It refines "Waiting a turn instead of jamming is contextual ... only if waiting helps you more than the opponent" and repeats "Show and Tell: when you hold protection, jam it now". It is new only in naming the visible Wasteland plus an empty land hand as the case where waiting never helps.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
None new. B's turn-6 hold breaks the existing "Use it before they untap, to cut the most mana". The flag asks Brady whether B's own land count (Volcanic plus Wasteland) was a reason to hold.
