# g39 review: Alurentell (A) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 10 (B's 5th turn). The final attack was Murktide 6 + delirium DRC 3 + Monk 2 = 11 into A's 10 life. A never found a payoff in 5 turns. A's draws were Stock Up (kept on top by the Hedge Maze surveil), Flooded Strand, Show and Tell #2 and Force of Will. A cast exactly one spell that mattered (Stock Up) and a Force of Will on a Bolt that changed nothing.
- **Decider 1, the keep (A prompt 1).** The seven was Strand x2, Misty, Petal x2, Show and Tell, Acererak: no cantrip, no Atraxa/Omniscience/Aluren, and no black source except Petal. The hand needed two specific cards (a payoff, then protection or a dig) and had one draw step a turn to find them. A flagged this itself.
- **Decider 2, turn 3 Stock Up into Daze (A prompt 30).** B had one Volcanic Island, tapped (Ponder on its turn), so free Daze was the obvious threat. A had Hedge Maze + Island + Petal #1 on the battlefield and Petal #7 in hand. Better: cast Petal #7 first (the menu offered it, [1]), then Stock Up, leaving one sacrificable Petal to pay for Daze. Or wait until turn 5 with three lands + Petal. Stock Up was the hand's only dig. Losing it to Daze left A drawing one card a turn into a turn-3 Murktide.
- **Hindsight caveat.** B held Force of Will from its opening seven and drew a second by turn 6. A resolved Show and Tell into Atraxa was unlikely even with a better draw. The keep made the game mostly unwinnable. The Daze made it certain.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- **A, prompt 1 (keep).** Brady's rule says "a 7 with no cantrips and no blue card for Force of Will, slow even if it finds Acererak, is a mulligan". This hand has Show and Tell as its only blue card, which makes it a near-exact match. Mulligan.
- **A, prompt 30.** The Petal in hand was not a Daze spare. See section 5 for the claim check.
- **A, prompt 57 (smaller).** At B's turn-6 end step A cracked Misty for Tropical Island while Hedge Maze #2 was in the library. Both make G/U. Hedge Maze fetched at end step untaps for A's turn and surveils before the draw. The playbook already notes this (t22, t25). A drew Show and Tell #2, a dead duplicate, the next turn; the surveil could have binned it. The same chance came again at prompt 72 (B's turn-8 end step). Flooded Strand was uncracked and Hedge Maze #2 was still available, but A passed and never cracked that Strand.
- **B, prompt 62 (turn 8).** B cast Dragon's Rage Channeler, then Cori-Steel Cutter, with exactly 3 mana. Flurry only triggers when Cutter is already on the battlefield. Cutter first, then DRC, makes a 2/2 haste trample Monk: 8 damage that turn instead of 6, plus a permanent extra body. B admitted it. It did not matter here.
- B's other decisions were sound. Daze on the tapped-out Stock Up (prompt 31) cost nothing real: B had Misty and Wasteland in hand, so replaying Volcanic cost no land drop. Murktide on turn 6 with delve was strong.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "A Lotus Petal in hand does not pay for Daze: Daze is paid while it resolves, and Petal can only be cast at sorcery speed. To have a Daze spare, cast the Petal before the spell you protect (or count only untapped lands and Petals already on the battlefield)."
   - Evidence: turn 3, prompt 30. Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: sharpens "Spare mana beats Daze ... casting Aluren with a Petal or second Ancient Tomb spare", which never says the Petal must be on the battlefield. No contradiction.
2. "A seven whose only spells are Show and Tell and Acererak is a mulligan, however many lands and Petals come with it: mana does not find Atraxa, Omniscience or Aluren."
   - Evidence: prompt 1. Confidence: medium-high.
   - Overlap: near-duplicate of Brady's "a 7 with no cantrips and no blue card for Force of Will ... is a mulligan". This one covers the case where Show and Tell is the only blue card. Consistent with "Acererak is plan F".
3. "At the opponent's end step, fetch Hedge Maze over a dual whenever both give the colours I need; the surveil before my draw is free. With no payoff in hand, never end their turn with an uncracked fetch while Hedge Maze is still in the library."
   - Evidence: prompts 57 and 72. Confidence: medium.
   - Overlap: partly duplicates "Hedge Maze fetched at their end step enters tapped, untaps on my turn and still surveils (t22, t25)". The new part is the default choice and the no-payoff trigger. It does not contradict "Keeping Misty uncracked until the turn I need the mana costs nothing": that rule is about the turn's own mana, and this one is about the end step before my draw.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- **A's "Daze spare" claim is correct, and it is not an engine bug.** Before Stock Up, A had tapped Maze and Island and sacrificed Petal #1. The board then showed no untapped mana source (prompt 33). Daze's "unless pays {1}" is paid while Daze resolves. Lotus Petal is a non-instant artifact, so it cannot be cast at that point. The engine rightly gave no pay prompt. A's report diagnoses this correctly.
- **Land drops.** None skipped. A had no land in hand on turn 9. B made a land drop every turn.
- **Cantrips.** No upkeep cantrips by either side. A's only selection all game was the Hedge Maze surveil at prompt 24 (kept Stock Up on top).
- **Tool use.** A made 1 odds call, at the mulligan (log line 2), and still kept. No wasted calls otherwise.
- **B's summary confusion.** B's report says the summary printed "odds calls 1" though B made none. The summary line is A's run; not an engine bug.
- **Cosmetic.** "spell #0 countered" in RECENT EVENTS shows #0, not the spell's id (A prompt 33, A prompt 91).
- **Delve UI.** The delve prompts always show "remaining: 1" (B prompts 49-53). This confused B but the result was correct.
