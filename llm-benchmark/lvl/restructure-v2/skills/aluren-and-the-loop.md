# Aluren and the Acererak loop

Use when: Aluren is in play or about to be cast, Omniscience is in play with Acererak available, Acererak could be hardcast, or a dungeon or room choice appears.

Each rule is a default. Read its "Breaks when:" before applying it; if the situation matches, follow the principle instead.

## Loop now (P10)

- **AL1** [P10] With Aluren or Omniscience in play (or castable) and Acererak in hand, loop now and keep choosing Lost Mine until they are dead. Do not cantrip, attack or take Tomb first. Trapped Entry only for the last point. If you do enter Tomb, take Veils of Fear and Sandfall Cell, never Oubliette. (Brady, LvL sign-off)
  Breaks when: a lock piece is out (AL4: go through Tomb); their live answers outnumber your Forces (AL7: dig first, Brady 2026-10-08).
- **AL2** [P10] The Lost Mine technique. Lost Mine rooms: Cave Entrance (scry), Goblin Lair (blocker), Dark Pool (drain 1); Dark Pool is the room that drains. With Aluren in play: cast Acererak at instant speed and leave every room trigger on the stack except the Dark Pool drain, so no cards are drawn and the library is not touched. Only under Omniscience must the triggers resolve (you will eventually draw Aluren); then a lap is four casts, one drain and one draw, so check library size against their life. (Brady, 2026-10-07; this replaces the earlier estimate of "about three casts per point of life", which was wrong.) In a06 I took Lost Mine every time; Mad Mage and Tomb were never needed.
  Breaks when: no known exception. Under Omniscience you are never really going to deck yourself (AL8).
- **AL3** [P10] Default: do **not** complete Tomb of Annihilation. Acererak then bounces to hand every time, and Aluren recasts him for free, so the loop keeps producing rooms (Goblins, Treasures, scry, cards, Dark Pool drain, life). (Brady, 2026-10-01)
  Breaks when: a lock piece is out (AL4).
- **AL7** [P10, P11] Dig first before the first Acererak when their live answers (W or R open, Quarry plus a creature, an uncracked fetch, cards in hand) outnumber your Forces: under Omniscience with free spells while the library is well above their life; under Aluren only if a land and a cantrip are free to use. (Brady "Yes, dig first", 2026-10-08, conflict 8; g86, g92 found the missing pieces; g52 lost without it)
  Breaks when: your Forces cover their live answers: loop now (AL1; g96).
- **AL8** [P10, P12] Against Boros with Omniscience in play, loop under Omniscience without casting Aluren: they cannot cast a free Amped Raptor into an answer, and you are never really going to deck yourself. There is no need to dig for Aluren. (Brady, 2026-10-08, conflict 6; g68, g80, g92 never decked; Aluren gave Boros free creatures in g48, g74)
  Breaks when: they have a huge life total from Guide of Souls plus Ocelot Pride: then you draw your deck and play Aluren, and you presumably have protection by then (Brady, 2026-10-08).

## Hardcasting Acererak (P10, P6)

- **AL9** [P10, P6] Brady's most common Acererak hardcast: you have run out of cantrips, have plenty of lands and are not spending mana this turn, so you turn a Lotus Petal and 2 life (Ancient Tomb) into a scry 1 (Lost Mine, Cave Entrance), and Acererak returns to hand. (Brady, 2026-10-08, on SYNTHESIS2 rule 30)
  Breaks when: the Petal is next turn's combo mana or you are short of lands (ML13; g69, g79 spent it on a scry); you can cast Show and Tell instead (ST3: Acererak is "plan F"); the 2 life puts you within their reach (ML4).

## When to go through Tomb (P10, P12)

- **AL4** [P10, P12] If the opponent has a card that stops the Aluren loop, for example Sphere of Resistance, Damping Sphere, Trinisphere or Disruptor Flute, go through Tomb of Annihilation on purpose. Once the dungeon is completed Acererak stays on the battlefield by himself, and completing it also gives a 4/4 (The Atropal). Check the opponent's battlefield for tax and lock pieces before starting the loop and again whenever one appears. (Brady, 2026-10-01)
  Breaks when: no known exception. It is the stated exception to AL1 and AL3.
- **AL5** [P10] Tomb rooms hurt both players (Trapped Entry: each loses 1; Veils of Fear and Sandfall Cell: lose 2 or discard/sacrifice). Observed in a04: the opponent discarded Force of Will and sacrificed a creature to avoid the loss. (a04)
  Breaks when: no known exception.

## Their side of Aluren and their answers (P11, P12)

- **AL6** [P12] Aluren is symmetric: the opponent casts MV 3 or less creatures free at instant speed (free Phlage deals 3; Amped Raptor can discover Swords, Erode or Bombardment). Do not cast Aluren at 3 life or less against Boros. (Brady, LvL sign-off)
  Breaks when: no known exception. Under Omniscience against Boros you need not cast Aluren at all (AL8).
