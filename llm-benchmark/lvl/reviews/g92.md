# g92 review: Alurentell (A, on the draw) beat Boros Aggro (B) on game turn 6 (A's 3rd turn)

## 1. Result and turning point
- A won with the Lost Mine drain on turn 6. The log has 18 Dark Pool picks, and B was on exactly 18 (20, minus two Arid Mesa cracks; Swords was countered, so A's life gain never happened). Library check under Omniscience alone: at seq 128 the library was 30 and B was at 18. A lap of four casts drains 1 and draws 1 (Temple of Dumathoin), so the kill needed 17 more draws. At seq 132 the library was 17 with B at 5, and it ended at about 13. The old resolve-everything loop was safe with 12 or more cards to spare. The "library 17" in A's report is the seq-132 snapshot, not the final count.
- Decision 1, turn 6, seq 32 to 45. Ponder came before the land (correct order) and kept Show and Tell, then Aluren, on top. Then came Ancient Tomb, Petal, and Show and Tell for Omniscience, with Atraxa in hand (playbook: put in Omniscience when holding Atraxa). The free Atraxa (seq 52 to 56) took all 5 types: Stock Up, Brainstorm, Aluren, Atraxa and Hedge Maze. The free Stock Up (seq 60) took Acererak and a second Aluren.
- Decision 2, seq 72. Correction to the summary and A's report: A did hold a Force of Will (taken at seq 69) when it cast the first Acererak. The problem was one Force against B's 4 cards and 4 untapped W sources. B cast Swords, A Forced it (seq 79), then B cast Erode, and A had nothing left (it slipped at seq 84). The third Atraxa and a free Brainstorm were still in hand, and recasting Atraxa costs nothing. Digging first for a second Force or a backup Acererak loses nothing (library 39, 18 needed), and that is the g86 lesson. The game was saved because Atraxa #3 (seq 102), then Stock Up and Atraxa #4, found Acererak and Force of Will (seq 124/125).
- Aluren (seq 128, A's own flag): casting it was free, and with Brady's technique (leave every room trigger except Dark Pool on the stack) there are no draws, so the library is never at risk. It also gives B free instant-speed creatures (Phlage deals 3, Guide of Souls gains life, Raptor's discover can hit Swords or Bombardment). B actually held Karakas and Lazotep Quarry, but A could not see that. With 30 cards against 18 life, skipping Aluren was correct. Aluren would only be needed if the library were close to B's life.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: seq 72, committing the first Acererak with one Force into 4 open W while free digging was still available (see above). The keep (seq 2: 4 lands, Ponder, Atraxa, Veil, no Show and Tell, Veil blank) was close, and A flagged it itself. Ponder needed to find any one of 4 Show and Tell, and it did on turn 6. The turn-2 Ponder shuffle (seq 9, self-flagged) was defensible, because Show and Tell was the missing piece. Small slips: seq 71, Done left a land, Petal and Aluren in the library (against "take every card", but it cost nothing here). Seq 84, the misclick on Brainstorm cost nothing: Atraxa was not castable at instant speed anyway, and Veil and City went back.
- B: seq 6 (turn 1), surveilling Wasteland into the graveyard. That breaks the existing note "Never surveil Wasteland away while they are short of lands": A had no lands yet. On turn 3, Wasteland plus Parlor still casts Raptor and kills A's only land (Tundra). It probably would not have stopped the turn-6 Show and Tell (Tomb plus Petal paid for it), so this was not the losing play. After that, B's play was good: the Raptor-into-Sand-Scout curve, a fetch-crack token, the attack to put A at 16, and holding Swords and Erode. Putting in Plains gave an untapped W for Swords (Karakas would die to the legend rule). B fired on the first Acererak, Swords first and Erode only after A's Force, so A's single Force was spent. B correctly never used Karakas: Acererak bounces himself, and bouncing Atraxa under Omniscience only gives A a free recast and a new reveal. The loss itself was structural: B's main deck has no enchantment answer, and A had four Atraxas and free Forces under Omniscience.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Under Omniscience, before the first Acererak, count their possible answers (cards in hand, untapped W or R, Quarry with a creature) against your Force of Wills. While a free Atraxa or cantrip is in hand and the library is well above their life, recast and dig for a second Force or a backup Acererak first." Evidence: g92 seq 72 (one Force against Swords plus Erode; saved by a later dig), and g86 seq 56 to 72 (the backup Acererak beat Swords). Confidence: medium-high, now seen twice. It refines "With Aluren or Omniscience in play ... loop now ... Do not cantrip, attack or take Tomb first"; keep that rule for Aluren alone, where cantrips cost mana.
2. "Under Omniscience with Aluren in hand against a creature deck (Boros), skip Aluren when your library exceeds their life by a few cards: loop on Omniscience alone (one draw per lap). Cast Aluren only when the library is short." Evidence: seq 128 (30 cards against 18 life, finished with about 13). Confidence: low-medium. It sits next to "Only under Omniscience must the triggers resolve ... check library size against their life" and "Aluren is symmetric ... Do not cast Aluren at 3 life or less against Boros". It adds the decision between the two, which the playbook does not state.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
1. "With two answers for Acererak, cast one on the enter trigger and hold the second until they Force the first. Never cast both at once." Evidence: B seq 76/81, where Swords drew A's only Force and Erode resolved. Confidence: high. It is new; the notes only say to keep W open and fire on the first cast.
2. "Once Omniscience is in play, never Karakas their Atraxa or Acererak: they recast it free and get another trigger." Evidence: B seq 76 (Karakas offered, correctly declined). Confidence: high. It qualifies "Keep Karakas untapped for a legendary Atraxa", which is right only before Omniscience or Aluren.

## 5. Process and engine flags
- A menu slip at seq 84 (Brainstorm picked by index, meaning Atraxa, which was not on the menu). There were no skipped land drops (A: Tundra, Strand, Tomb; B: Mesa, Karakas, Mesa) and no upkeep cantrips. A held Strand uncracked all game, which was harmless.
- Cosmetic: Erode and Swords show "targeting (gone)" while resolving, and Force of Will's counter logs as "spell #0 countered" (seq 92 to 96). Venturing after Acererak died is correct rules (the bounce fails, the venture still happens). No engine bug.
- Auto macro: about 560 picks, and the budget stopped it twice (seq 130, 132), which needed re-picks. Harmless, but slow.

RESULT: A (Alurentell) won on game turn 6: turn-6 Show and Tell put in Omniscience, the first Acererak was lost to Swords plus Erode, then a free Atraxa and Stock Up dig found Acererak plus Force, and 18 Dark Pool drains finished it (library 30, about 13 left).
TOP MISTAKE: A seq 72 cast the first Acererak with one Force into B's 4 cards and 4 open W while a free Atraxa recast could have found a second Force or Acererak first (B seq 6, surveilling Wasteland away, was B's first error).
TOP RULE: Under Omniscience, count their answers against your Forces before the first Acererak, and dig with free Atraxa and cantrips while the library is well above their life.
