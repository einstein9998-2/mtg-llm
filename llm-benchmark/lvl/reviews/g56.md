# g56 review: Alurentell (A) vs Boros Aggro (B), B won on game turn 7

## 1. Result and turning point
- B won on its 4th turn (game T7): 9 combat (4/3 flying Cat, Guide, Ajani, Voice, two mobilize Warriors) took A from 10 to 1, then Goblin Bombardment (cast precombat, b47) sacrificed a Warrior for the last point. A cast only Ponder, Brainstorm, Stock Up and Lotus Petal.
- Decisive play: b29 (T5) Wasteland on Hedge Maze, A's only nonbasic and only green source, left A on one Island with two Tombs in hand. B then cast Voice of Victory (A cannot cast spells on B's turn, so its Force of Will was dead against Bombardment).
- Mostly variance: A saw 18 cards (opening 7, 3 draws, Ponder, Brainstorm, Stock Up) and none of its 8 enablers (4 Show and Tell, 4 Acererak); about 13% to miss in the 11 new cards. Hand had Aluren and Atraxa but no partner for either. No line to combo existed; no survival line either (no blockers, no removal, one Force, Voice live from T5).

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, keep (a2): Strand, Tomb, Brainstorm, Ponder, FoW, Aluren, Atraxa on the draw. A keep: one card short of a combo (8 outs) with two cantrips and live Force, the "keep (close)" pattern in Brady's batch-2 mulligan rule. Not the cause of the loss.
- A, T4 (a21): with Island untapped, two Tombs and Hedge Maze in hand, Stock Up known on top, A chose Brainstorm then a tapped Hedge Maze (1 mana used). Better: Ancient Tomb + Stock Up (3 mana, sees 5, keeps Brainstorm). Every remaining land was nonbasic, so Wasteland (already on B's battlefield) was going to take one of them anyway; the one exposed should be the one that made mana that turn. This breaks the existing rule "from hand, play [Hedge Maze] on a turn where the untapped land's mana would go unused". Small: with hindsight Stock Up also found nothing (Stock Up, Veil, Aluren, Maze, Misty).
- A's T2 Strand -> basic Island (a10) was right against Wasteland; Brainstorm put-back of the duplicate Aluren and Veil (a23-24, Veil is a blank vs Boros) was right.
- A, Force of Will: never cast. Countering Ajani (a18, T3) would have removed the Cat that dealt 8 of the 20 damage; countering Voice (a32) would not have saved A (it was still dead on T7 to 6 combat + Bombardment sacrifices). Close, flagged for Brady.
- B: no mistake found. Keep (b1, self-flagged) of Marsh Flats, Wasteland, Guide, Ocelot Pride, Ajani, Voice, Bombardment on the play is standard for aggro. Flats -> Plateau + Guide on T1 (Wasteland is the T1 land only with no 1-drop), Wasteland + Ajani T3, Plains, Wasteland, Voice T5, Bombardment T7.
- B's Wasteland timing matches Brady ("before they untap, not after a spell"): Wasteland was tapped for Ajani on T3, so A's T4 end step was impossible; B's T5 main phase was the first window, before A's untap, and Hedge Maze had entered tapped, so no mana of A's was missed. B chose it over Voice + Ocelot Pride (3 mana): correct, the Wasteland cost B one 1-drop and cost A its land drop and its green.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
- None new. The T4 error is covered by the Hedge Maze rule (Rules from LvL reviews) and the t18 rule "Do not play a nonbasic into an untapped Wasteland just to use one more mana (Prefer fetches and basics first)". Possible clarification for Brady (low): "When every land in hand is nonbasic and their Wasteland is out, play the one you can use this turn (Tomb for Stock Up) and keep Hedge Maze for later." It adds to the t18 rule and does not contradict it.
- Force of Will on Ajani against Boros when the hand is not close to combo: evidence a18, confidence low; left as a flag, not a rule.

## 4. Candidate Boros notes
- "With Voice of Victory on the battlefield, cast Goblin Bombardment precombat when combat plus sacrifices is lethal; they cannot answer it on your turn." Evidence b47/b58 (exact lethal, 10 to 0). Confidence medium. Narrows the existing note "Goblin Bombardment: hold it for their combo turn"; it does not contradict it (hold it while they are not in range).
- "Wasteland the combo deck's only nonbasic or only green source even if it costs your extra 1-drop that turn." Evidence b29 (chose Wasteland + Voice over Voice + Ocelot Pride; A stayed on one land and never cast a permanent but Petal). Confidence medium. Extends "Wasteland: ... Hit Ancient Tomb or their only blue or green dual".

## 5. Process and engine flags
- No land drop skipped by A (T2, T4, T6); B had none on T7. No upkeep cantrips. No odds/sim calls by either side (none needed).
- A was never shown the final turn: the game ended while A passed priority (a46), so A's report guesses how it died. Losing players should get the closing event log.
- B's report calls A's tapped lands on B's turn "stale untap state": not a bug. A's Island was tapped for Brainstorm and untaps only in A's untap step; B's Wasteland showed tapped on A's turn because B tapped it for Ajani.
- Ponder showed two entries for three cards (Stock Up x2, a13): the known duplicate-name display, already in the playbook.
- Player flags: a41 (Stock Up pick: Petal + Misty) and b1 (keep) were not duplicated.

RESULT: B (Boros) won on game turn 7 (its 4th turn), 9 combat plus 1 Bombardment from 10; A never found Show and Tell or Acererak in 18 cards (mostly variance).
TOP MISTAKE: A T4 (a21) Brainstorm + tapped Hedge Maze instead of Ancient Tomb + Stock Up; Wasteland then took the Maze (minor, would not have changed the result).
TOP RULE: none new; Boros note: with Voice out, cast Bombardment precombat when combat plus sacrifices is lethal (medium).
