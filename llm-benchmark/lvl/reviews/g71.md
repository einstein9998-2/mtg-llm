# g71 review: Alurentell (A, on the play) vs UR Cutter (B)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 14 (A at 5 after two 3/3 flying DRCs; Bolt at A's T13 end step, Bolt on T14). Correction to the reports: only TWO Wastelands were used (T4 on Boseiju, T6 on Savannah); B drew a third on T13 and never needed it. A ended on 3 lands (Island, Tropical, Tundra) with one green source, so Aluren (GG) was uncastable from T9 on.
- Deciding sequence, T8 (B's turn), a85 to a87. Truth log confirms the misread: a85 menu was [0] Pass, [1] Force of Will, [2] Veil of Summer and A sent 1 (Force on DRC #82, pitched Atraxa at a86). The menu DID shift: at a81 (only a Flooded Strand ability on the stack) Force was not offered and Veil was [1]; at a85 a spell (DRC) was on the stack, so Force appeared at [1] and Veil moved to [2]. Same pattern at a73/a76 vs a81. Harness UX issue: the engine offers Force only when a spell is on the stack, so Veil's number flips between turns.
- a87 made it worse: with its own Force on the stack and Tropical untapped, A cast Veil (tapping Tropical). B answered with Daze on top of Veil; A could no longer pay 1. Passing with Tropical up would have paid Daze, and Veil could still answer a Force in response. Net: A lost Force, Atraxa and Veil to a 1/1 DRC, B spent one Daze.
- T9 a98 naked Show and Tell (3 lands, no spare, B tapped out but a tapped Volcanic made Daze live) was Forced (B pitched Murktide). After T8 nothing better existed: no land in hand, no protection, Veil gone. Jam was defensible (Brady: waiting is contextual; here waiting only gave B untapped Charm/Heat mana and delirium time).
- Had A picked Veil at a85 as intended: Veil draws Tundra, T9 hand = Force, Show, Atraxa x2 (second Atraxa drawn T9), Tundra; Show and Tell with Force backup pitching the spare Atraxa. That was a likely win.

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A: T5 a34/a35. Played Misty before Ponder and cracked it in main 1 for Savannah although the untapped Island alone paid for Ponder, against a deck that had already Wastelanded Boseiju. Savannah was Wastelanded next turn. Keeping Misty uncracked (Wasteland-proof: crack in response) would have left A on 4 mana by T9 (Veil + Show, or Show + Daze spare). Same pattern T7 a59/a60 (Tropical survived). T3 a14 Boseiju over Misty was fine: Stock Up needed the third mana either way and Boseiju was the cheapest Wasteland target.
- Keep (a1, A flagged): reasonable on the play. Two fetches + Petal, Ponder + Stock Up to find one card (Show and Tell), payoff and Veil already in hand. Not a two-missing-pieces hand. T3 tap-out Stock Up into a tapped-out B (only Daze/Force) was fine and resolved.
- B: no real mistake. Wasteland targets were A's only green source each time (Boseiju, then Savannah): best possible. Daze on A's Force when A tapped Tropical for Veil was exact. Force on Show and Tell pitching Murktide (its only other blue card) was forced; letting Atraxa resolve loses. DRC T2 and DRC #2 into A's open mana with Daze/Force backup baited the misread. Minor: T4 a29 Preordain bottomed the deck's only Prismari Charm (answer to Aluren/Atraxa); T12 a118-a123 binned Charm and Heat for delirium speed, safe only because A was stuck.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "When your spell is on the stack and they can Daze, keep the untapped land to pay for it. Cast Veil of Summer only in response to their Force of Will, not first, if Veil would tap the land that pays for Daze." Evidence: g71 a87. Confidence: medium-high. Not a duplicate; refines "Veil timing is situational (Brady): play it first to play around Daze" (that line is about having Veil plus the combo spell mana, not about tapping the Daze payment).
2. "On the opponent's turn, read the label before every pick: Force of Will is listed only when a spell is on the stack, so Veil of Summer is [1] with an ability on the stack and [2] with a spell." Evidence: g71 a81 vs a85. Confidence: high. Sharpens the existing "Keep the menu discipline: indices shift after every action (a06 slip)"; the shift here happened with no action of A's.
3. Not new (evidence of violation, do not re-add): "Keeping Misty uncracked until the turn I need the mana costs nothing and gives Wasteland a decoy" and "cast cantrips (Brainstorm, Ponder ...) before playing the land" (a34/a35, a59/a60), and "A second green source matters" (a36; here Aluren was dead with Tropical as the only G).

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "Against Alurentell, Wasteland the green sources first, even before Aluren is seen; with one green land left Aluren (GG) is uncastable and Veil competes for the same G." Evidence: g71 b32, b56. Confidence: medium. Extends "the green dual once Aluren is seen".
2. "Do not Preordain-bottom Prismari Charm against Alurentell; it is the only answer to a resolved Aluren or Atraxa besides Force." Evidence: b29. Confidence: low (no cost here).

## 5. Process and engine flags
- Menu numbering shift a81 to a85 (above): harness UX, not an engine bug. Suggest fixed slots or always listing Force (greyed) on the opponent's turn.
- a98 pick ran past 120 s and was moved to the background but registered once (log prompt 98 "Cast Show and Tell", then B's prompt 99); the retry's "bad answer" was harmless. A's flag on 98 did register in flags/g71a.tsv.
- Turns 11 and parts of 13 auto-passed: correct, nothing castable (Aluren needed GG with one G source; Show and Tell gone). No land drops skipped; A had no land in hand T11/T13. No upkeep cantrips.
- B's "opponent lands shown tapped on my turn" is correct rules behavior (lands tapped on A's own turn stay tapped until A's untap), not a display bug. No engine bug found. Wall time 10255 s.

RESULT: B (UR Cutter) won on turn 14; A was cut to one green source by two Wastelands, then lost Force, Atraxa and Veil on T8 to a menu misread plus Veil tapping the Daze-payment land, and its naked T9 Show and Tell was Forced.
TOP MISTAKE: A T8 a85/a87: misread Force for Veil (menu shifted), then cast Veil off its last land so B's Daze countered the Force; before that, T5 a35 cracked Misty for Savannah when Island paid for Ponder.
TOP RULE: When your spell is on the stack and Daze is possible, keep the land that pays for Daze; cast Veil only in response to their Force if Veil would tap it.
