RESULT: LOST
TURNS: 4 (dead to combat damage on the opponent's fourth turn; the only spell I cast was Veil of Summer)
ARCHON: Not drawn, so the test is inconclusive. It would not have mattered in this game: Archon costs 8, so only Show and Tell or Omniscience can put it in, and I never saw either. The game was over by turn 4 against Moonshadow, two Nethergoyf and Kaito. As a put-in I would rank it below Atraxa against this opponent: Atraxa is a 7/7 flying vigilance lifelink blocker that stabilises a race, while Archon's enter trigger (edict, discard, lose 3, draw, gain 3) is slower, though an edict would hit a lone Moonshadow or Goyf. I would not keep it main against Dimir tempo. I would put the Ponder back and cut Archon, since digging for Show and Tell is what the deck lacks against this fast start. Like Emrakul-style payoffs it needs Show or Omniscience and is worse than Atraxa when you are the one being raced.
- Mistake (turn 0, mulligan): I mulliganed a seven with Strand, Misty, Tomb, Petal, Force and two Atraxa (no cantrip, no Show) per OH9, and the six I kept (Island, Tomb, Force, Veil, two Atraxa) also had no cantrip or Show and Tell. Against a deck that curves out this fast, the seven with full mana and Force may simply have been the better keep.
- Opponent surprise: Dimir played as a pure aggro deck. Moonshadow turn 1, two Nethergoyf turn 2, Kaito by ninjutsu on turn 3 (7 damage), then Bauble, Ponder and Brainstorm on turn 4 to grow Goyf (land, creature, sorcery, instant) and Moonshadow, for 5+4+4 = 13 damage against my 11 life. They never cast a counterspell, so Force of Will was never relevant.
- Playbook gap: it has no vs-Dimir-tempo guidance, and the main deck has no cheap blockers or interaction (Chant and Prismatic Ending are in the sideboard), so there was no way to interact with an early aggro start.
- No engine bug seen. Veil on their blue Ponder drew a second Ancient Tomb, and Boseiju channel had no useful target.
