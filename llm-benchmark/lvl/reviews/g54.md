# g54 review: A = Alurentell (on the draw, mulligan to 6), B = Boros Aggro (on the play, mulligan to 6)

## 1. Result and turning point
B won on game turn 9; A was at 3 life facing lethal. A went 17 -> 3 in two attacks (T5, T7) with no blocker.
- T4 (A p23-34): Strand -> Savannah, Petal, Show and Tell for Atraxa while B was fully tapped out. B put in Ocelot Pride (B p29). Atraxa gave Tropical Island, Acererak, Show and Tell, Force of Will, Omniscience. The land pick (p30) was Tropical Island; City of Traitors, Tundra and Misty were also offered.
- T5 (B p36-41): B played Wasteland as its land drop, killed Savannah, then Swords'd Atraxa before attacking. A was left on Island plus Tropical Island (2 mana) and never cast another spell. Every card in the hand cost 3 or more.
- Decisive error (A p30): City of Traitors instead of Tropical Island. The plan for next turn was Show and Tell, which needs U + 2 and no GG; Aluren (2GG, 4 mana) could not be cast next turn in either line. City cannot be Wastelanded from hand and makes 2 the turn it is played. After the Wasteland on Savannah, Island + City would still have cast Show and Tell -> Omniscience on T6. B's whole hand then was Lazotep Quarry, so that line wins. With no Wasteland, City gives 4 mana, Tropical 3. The only cost is a sacrifice later if A plays another land, and A held none.

## 2. Judgments on the asked decisions
- A mulligan of the one-lander (p4, self-flagged): correct, though it is a tough spot. Strand, Stock Up, FoW, Aluren, Veil, Atraxa, Acererak "has the combo", but Aluren needs four lands including GG and Stock Up needs three. Veil is a blank against Boros, and the one land is a fetch into a dual against four Wastelands. A's sim gave about 28% to have 3 lands by T3. Brady's "a one-lander is a keep if the hand has the combo" seems to be about combo hands that can be cast; his answer on g54a 4 should say whether it extends to Aluren hands.
- A keep (p5) and bottoming Acererak (p6): correct. Two lands, Petal and Brainstorm give a 3-mana Show and Tell on T4. Without Aluren, Acererak is the weakest card ("plan F"), and Show and Tell only bounces him.
- Savannah fetch (p25): not a mistake. The only basic Island was already out (T2), so Tropical, Tundra, Savannah and Hedge Maze (enters tapped, no Show and Tell this turn) were all equally exposed to Wasteland. Fetching a dual into four Wastelands could not be avoided; the fix was the Atraxa land pick.
- T4 Show and Tell tap-out vs Stock Up first (p27): Show and Tell was right, and A's report is wrong to call it the error. B was tapped out, so nothing could answer on A's turn, and Atraxa drew five. Stock Up first spends the Petal too, B untaps with the same Wasteland, and A is again on 2 lands, a card poorer, facing a bigger board. Brady's Boros tip ("I have to win the turn after Stock Up") also argues against spending T4 on Stock Up.
- B six-card keep (p2-3): clear keep (Plateau, Plains, Wasteland, Guide, Raptor, Swords; bottomed the second Plains). B's own one-lander mulligan (p1, self-flagged) was close but fine.
- B Show and Tell pick (p29): Ocelot Pride over Wasteland was right. Wasteland from hand still hit before A untapped (B's T5 land drop), and Ocelot attacked a turn earlier (+2 counters and flying from Guide, lifelink). B's T5 Wasteland over Quarry (p36) was also right: A had shown Tropical Island in hand, so Quarry first would have let A cast Show and Tell -> Omniscience on T6.

## 4. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "Against a Wasteland deck, when next turn's play is Show and Tell (Aluren not castable next turn anyway), take City of Traitors from the Atraxa reveal over a dual: it is safe in hand and makes 2 the turn you play it, so Show and Tell survives a Wasteland on the dual already in play."
   Evidence: A p30, B p37-38. Confidence: medium. It refines the batch-1 rule "the card you pick first is usually the land you want untapped and green to cast Aluren next turn (Tropical Island); Forest is better when a Wasteland could hit a dual or City of Traitors might die". When Aluren cannot be cast next turn, mana count beats green.

## 5. Candidate Boros notes
1. "When they Show and Tell on their own turn and you hold Wasteland plus a cheap creature, put in the creature: Wasteland played on your next turn still hits before they untap, and the creature attacks a turn sooner." Evidence: B p29, p36. Confidence: medium.
2. "If they show a dual in hand (Atraxa reveal), play Wasteland as the land drop over your untapped land and hit the land that keeps them one short of Show and Tell." Evidence: B p36-38. Confidence: medium; it extends "Use it before they untap".
