# Review w1g4: Alurentell (A, on the draw) vs UR Cutter (B, on the play)

## 1. Result and turning point
B (UR Cutter) won on turn 9 (B's 5th turn), attacking for exactly 5 with Dragon's Rage Channeler (3/3 flier) and a Monk token (prowess from Preordain). A never resolved a spell that mattered.
- Turn 5 (B prompts 41-55): Cori-Steel Cutter, then Brainstorm, made a hasty Monk and turned on delirium. That was 5 damage a turn from turn 5 on, which gave A two turns.
- Turn 6 (A prompt 57): with City of Traitors, A cast Show and Tell for Atraxa with Hedge Maze spare against Daze, into 4 cards and a tapped-out opponent. B's hand was Force of Will, Daze, Bolt and Murktide, with a second Force of Will already Brainstormed onto its library top (B prompt 54). B cast Force of Will, pitching Daze (B 58-59). Turn 8 (A 72): A at 5 life cast the second Show and Tell, B cast the second Force of Will (pitching Murktide), and the board already had lethal.
- Verdict: mostly variance. B kept two Force of Will plus Cutter, and A's seven had no protection. But a single Force of Will in A's hand on turn 6 would have won the counter war: their FoW, my FoW pitching a spare Show and Tell, their Daze paid with Hedge Maze. A's turn-4 sequencing cut its chance of finding that Force (see 2).

## 2. First real mistake per side
- A, turn 4, prompt 25: A played and cracked Misty Rainforest (for a basic Island) before casting anything, then cast Brainstorm and put back both Acererak, then cast Ponder. That Ponder saw Flooded Strand, Acererak and Acererak, which was nothing new, so it was only a shuffle plus a random draw (City of Traitors). The better order was available: Brainstorm off the untapped Hedge Maze, put back the two Acererak, then play and crack Misty (the shuffle removes them), then Ponder off the Island and see 3 fresh cards. That is about 34% to see one of 4 FoW + 2 Veil, against about 12% for the blind draw it got. Force was the card that won this game. This breaks two existing rules: "cast cantrips ... before playing the land" and "Fetch away the cards you put back with Brainstorm if they are bad".
- A, turn 6, prompt 57: Show and Tell or Stock Up first is close, not a clear mistake (flagged for Brady). A held three Show and Tell, so the first one was a fair bait. B would also have held its Force for Show and Tell, so Stock Up probably resolves (about 51% to see Force or Veil in 5 cards), but A would then try at 5 life on turn 8 against FoW + FoW + Daze.
- A's other choices were sound. The keep (3 lands, Ponder, Show and Tell, Atraxa) was fine. On turn 2 Hedge Maze kept Brainstorm on top. Misty fetched a basic against Wasteland. City of Traitors gave the spare against Daze. Turn 8 was all-in by force (5 power on board at 5 life, and Boseiju cannot touch creatures).
- B: no real mistake found. B's report calls holding Murktide a mistake (turn 7, prompts 62/69/71). It was correct: Murktide was the only blue card left to pitch to the Force of Will drawn that turn, and the board was already lethal in two turns. Casting it would have let the turn-8 Show and Tell resolve.

## 3. Candidate Alurentell playbook rules
1. "With one untapped blue source, a fetch, Brainstorm and Ponder: Brainstorm first, then play and crack the fetch to shuffle the put-backs away, then Ponder three fresh cards. A Ponder cast right after a Brainstorm, with no shuffle in between, wastes its look on the cards you just put back."
   - Evidence: turn 4, A prompts 25-35. Confidence: high.
   - Overlap: this sharpens two existing rules, "cast cantrips (Brainstorm, Ponder, ...) before playing the land" and "Fetch away the cards you put back with Brainstorm if they are bad". It partly contradicts a third, "Cast Brainstorm before Ponder so Ponder's shuffle clears the put-backs" (written for the Omniscience case, where every spell is free). The new rule says to let the fetch do the shuffle when one is available, so the Ponder's look is not spent on known cards.
- Nothing else here generalizes. I considered a Show-and-Tell-versus-Stock-Up rule for a no-protection turn against a 2-turn clock, but it would not have changed the result, so it goes to Brady as a flag instead.

## 4. Candidate UR Cutter notes
1. "Against Show and Tell or Aluren, keep one blue pitch card for every Force of Will in hand. Do not cast Murktide when it is the last pitch card and the board already kills in two turns." Evidence: turn 7, B prompts 62/69/71 (held correctly). The turn-8 Force pitching Murktide decided the game. Confidence: medium-high. B's own report mislabels this as a mistake, so the brief should say it outright.
2. "When the combo player leaves one mana up for Daze, Daze is a pitch card: Force of Will pitching Daze, keep the threat." Evidence: turn 6, B prompts 58-59 (A had Hedge Maze untapped). Confidence: medium.

## 5. Process and engine flags

- A prompts 72/76: A cast Show and Tell with City of Traitors and Island first, then played Savannah, so City was sacrificed already tapped. That is 5 mana from 4 lands. Good play, and an allowed exception to "land first"; worth keeping, though it changed nothing here.
- Not bugs, though the reports suggest them:
