CHANT_ENDING: Drew one Orim's Chant (via Brainstorm put-back and redraw, in hand from engine turn 13 to the end) and no Prismatic Ending. I never cast the Chant: it costs W (WW kicked) and my deck had only Tundra and Savannah as white sources, neither drawn, and Carpet makes mana only at the start of my own main phases and it empties, so the only white I could make was on my turn, never at instant speed on their turn. My plan for it was unkicked Chant precombat on my last turn, then Atraxa attack for lethal; they Consign'd both Carpet triggers in my main 1, so I had no white and the Chant was dead anyway. In hindsight 3 Chant main is too many for this build: with 2 white sources and 9 fetch/white-access cards in 60, Chant is a dead card most turns (I was mostly stuck at 3 mana with only Maze for colour). Against Dimir I would play at most 1 or 2 Chant, and only if more white sources are in the main deck (Plains-fetching only helps with Tundra/Savannah); Prismatic Ending is the same colour problem, so I would keep it at 1. I would trade Chant slots for extra Veil of Summer or Force of Will/Petal (the plan cut all 4 Force and 4 Petal; I would at least keep Petals for Daze payment and a second colored source).
